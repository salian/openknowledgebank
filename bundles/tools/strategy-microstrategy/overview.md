# Strategy One Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise data connections and semantic modeling, metrics and attributes, reports, dossiers and dashboards, distribution, mobile, embedded analytics, administration, security and governed self-service.
- Strategy AI and Auto capabilities where licensed, APIs and SDKs, cloud and on-premises deployment distinctions, the MicroStrategy-to-Strategy naming transition, migration, permissions, usage evidence, validation, and controlled analytics publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or expose enterprise data, change semantic objects or metrics, publish analytics, enable AI, embed content, grant access, call APIs, migrate environments, or represent query, metric, forecast, permission, AI output, deployment, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
