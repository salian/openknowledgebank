# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect portal and subscription, contacts and properties, consent and subscriptions, segments, forms and CTAs, emails, workflows, campaigns and channels, integrations, permissions, attribution models, reports, tests, budgets, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the HubSpot Marketing Hub campaign and governance review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before collect contact data, enroll contacts, send email or social content, personalize pages, change consent or subscriptions, activate workflows or campaigns, spend funds, export data, or represent attribution or revenue.
