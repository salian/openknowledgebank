# HubSpot Marketing Hub Overview

## Scope

- Lead capture, forms, CTAs, audience segments, email, personalization, social management, automation, campaigns, analytics, dashboards, and attribution.
- CRM data, properties, workflows, permissions, integrations, AI, subscription controls, evidence, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect portal and subscription, contacts and properties, consent and subscriptions, segments, forms and CTAs, emails, workflows, campaigns and channels, integrations, permissions, attribution models, reports, tests, budgets, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before collect contact data, enroll contacts, send email or social content, personalize pages, change consent or subscriptions, activate workflows or campaigns, spend funds, export data, or represent attribution or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
