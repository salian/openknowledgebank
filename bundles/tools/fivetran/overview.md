# Fivetran Overview

## Scope

- connectors, destinations, managed pipelines, schema handling, database replication, streaming, transformations, dbt orchestration, activations, APIs, governance, private networking, and hybrid deployment
- Administration, permissions, integrations, configuration, audit evidence, validation, and controlled operational use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, edition and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect sources or destinations, replicate or activate data, alter schemas, run transformations, change sync frequency, expose fields, delete data, or represent completeness or freshness without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
