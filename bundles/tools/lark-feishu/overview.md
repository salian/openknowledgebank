# Lark and Feishu Overview

## Scope

- Workplace messaging, groups, meetings, calendars, documents, wikis, base applications, search, workflows, approvals and other configured collaboration capabilities.
- Open Platform applications, bots, events and APIs, identities and roles, Lark versus Feishu regional and service distinctions, data residency, retention, consent, security, validation, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and regional branding, tenant and plan, users and roles, messages and documents, permissions, integrations, retention, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before provision or identify users, send messages, share documents, run approvals, install apps or bots, expose secrets, call APIs, change retention, or represent identity, consent, delivery, confidentiality, residency, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
