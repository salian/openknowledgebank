# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect current product identity and regional branding, tenant and plan, users and roles, messages and documents, permissions, integrations, retention, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Lark and Feishu collaboration, integration, and governance review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before provision or identify users, send messages, share documents, run approvals, install apps or bots, expose secrets, call APIs, change retention, or represent identity, consent, delivery, confidentiality, residency, or compliance.
