# Iterable Overview

## Scope

- Customer profiles and events, segmentation, email, SMS, push and in-app messaging, campaigns, visual journeys, templates, experiments, and analytics.
- Warehouse and CDP connections, APIs, webhooks, real-time decisions, personalization, Nova AI, governance, evidence, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest customer data, define audiences, send messages, activate journeys, run experiments, enable AI decisioning, sync ad audiences, spend funds, or represent attribution or growth without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
