# Constant Contact Overview

## Scope

- Contacts, lists, segments, email campaigns, automation, events, landing pages, and reporting.
- V3 API, OAuth, webhooks, consent, suppression, senders, domains, templates, integrations, and analytics.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and API version, OAuth scopes, contacts and provenance, consent and suppression, lists and segments, sender and domain authentication, content and templates, campaigns and automations, schedules, events, webhooks, reports, attribution, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import or update contacts, change consent or suppression, authenticate senders, create, schedule or send campaigns, activate automation, use OAuth, or represent delivery or conversion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
