# Totango Overview

## Scope

- Customer accounts and contacts, usage and attribute data, health profiles and scores, segments, success programs and journeys, tasks, touchpoints, campaigns, objectives, renewals, dashboards and reports.
- Data and CRM integrations, APIs and automation, AI assistance where available, roles and teams, consent and communications, metric definitions, audit evidence, validation, and controlled customer-success operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify customers, change health scores or segments, send campaigns, create tasks or journeys, alter renewal data, enable AI, connect systems, or represent health, risk, intent, adoption, forecast, retention, expansion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
