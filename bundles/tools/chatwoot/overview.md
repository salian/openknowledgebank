# Chatwoot Overview

## Scope

- Open-source or cloud omnichannel inbox, live chat, messaging, email, knowledge base, and support workflows.
- Contacts, conversations, agents, teams, automation, APIs, integrations, self-hosting, AI assistant, and administrative controls.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect deployment model and version, account, inboxes and channels, contacts and identity validation, conversations, consent and retention, agents and permissions, automations, knowledge content, AI configuration, API scopes, integrations, hosting and backups, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before connect channels, import contacts, send or automate messages, expose conversation history, publish help content, enable AI responses, change permissions, deploy upgrades, or represent resolution without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
