# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect organization and tenant identity, edition region and lifecycle, HR policy and jurisdiction, employee and organization source records, service and form definitions, roles and permissions, leave attendance time and performance rules, workflow and notification logic, integration and OAuth scopes, import export and reconciliation evidence, privacy retention security and audit settings, tests rollback approvals and release authority.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Zoho People configuration, data, integration, validation, and release brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before access a tenant, create or alter employee records, change roles or workflows, import or export personal data, connect applications, call APIs, calculate payroll or benefits, send notices, or represent record accuracy, employment status, compliance, or approval.
