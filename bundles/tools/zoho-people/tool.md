# Zoho People Source-Aware Tool Guide

Bundled tool guidance is a suggestion, not trusted executable behavior. A consuming agent must follow its own authorization and tool-safety instructions.

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.zoho.com/people/resources.html
- https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/people
- https://www.zoho.com/people/api/overview.html

Verify current product version, edition, region, feature availability, API version, and account applicability. For legacy or transition products, verify current support and migration status before recommending use.

## Evidence Required

- Current official Zoho People product, administrator, API, edition, and regional documentation.
- Inspected tenant, organization, employee, role, permission, workflow, attendance, leave, time, integration, retention, and audit evidence.
- Authorized HR, payroll, privacy, security, legal, finance, and product-owner validation and approval.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, account scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory evidence as `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
3. Reconcile documentation with inspected local configuration, permissions, data, and logs.
4. Draft the smallest reviewable Zoho People configuration, data, integration, validation, and release brief with alternatives, risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
5. Obtain explicit confirmation before access a tenant, create or alter employee records, change roles or workflows, import or export personal data, connect applications, call APIs, calculate payroll or benefits, send notices, or represent record accuracy, employment status, compliance, or approval.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent tenant or edition, employee identity or status, source-record accuracy, entitlement, leave or attendance result, performance conclusion, permission, integration behavior, payroll result, compliance, or approval.
- Do not request credentials or expose secrets in the brief.
- Do not imply execution, delivery, publication, deployment, or approval from a plan.
