# Zoho People Overview

## Scope

- Employee and organization records, HR services, forms, leave, attendance, time tracking, performance and other edition-dependent HR workflows.
- Administrator configuration, roles and permissions, automation, reports, imports and exports, APIs, OAuth scopes, integrations, audit evidence, retention, testing, rollback, and controlled release.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect organization and tenant identity, edition region and lifecycle, HR policy and jurisdiction, employee and organization source records, service and form definitions, roles and permissions, leave attendance time and performance rules, workflow and notification logic, integration and OAuth scopes, import export and reconciliation evidence, privacy retention security and audit settings, tests rollback approvals and release authority.

## Boundary

Stop before access a tenant, create or alter employee records, change roles or workflows, import or export personal data, connect applications, call APIs, calculate payroll or benefits, send notices, or represent record accuracy, employment status, compliance, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
