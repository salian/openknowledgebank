# Oracle Sales Overview

## Scope

- Accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, activities, pipelines, forecasts, territories, quotas, partners, products, pricing and incentives where licensed.
- Sales automation, guided selling, analytics, AI recommendations and agents where available, Microsoft integration, mobile, REST APIs, imports, roles, audit evidence, extensibility, and quarterly updates.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify contacts, change leads, opportunities, stages, values, territories, quotas or forecasts, enable AI, send communications, call APIs, change access, or represent pipeline, forecast, incentive, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
