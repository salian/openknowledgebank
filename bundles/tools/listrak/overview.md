# Listrak Overview

## Scope

- Customer profiles, identity resolution, segmentation, email, SMS, RCS, push, web and in-store engagement, journeys, popups, landing pages, and paid audiences.
- Product recommendations, predictive optimization, dynamic content, attribution, analytics, integrations, deliverability, evidence, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before identify or segment people, collect behavior, personalize sites, send messages, activate journeys, sync ad audiences, deploy recommendations, spend funds, or represent attribution, conversion, lifetime value, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
