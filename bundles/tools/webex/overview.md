# Webex Overview

## Scope

- Meetings and webinars, spaces and messaging, supported calling and contact-center capabilities, whiteboards, files, events, rooms and devices according to subscription and deployment.
- Recordings, captions and transcripts, Control Hub administration, identities and roles, bots and integrations, REST APIs and webhooks, retention and compliance controls, consent, security, validation, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before schedule or host meetings, invite or identify participants, record or transcribe communications, send messages, change calling or contact-center routing, install apps, call APIs, or represent attendance, consent, delivery, confidentiality, resolution, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
