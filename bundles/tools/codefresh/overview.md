# Codefresh Overview

## Scope

- GitOps delivery, continuous integration, pipelines, Argo-based deployment, environments, promotion, and observability.
- Accounts, runtimes, repositories, clusters, applications, workflows, secrets, permissions, integrations, and operational controls.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect product edition and version, account and runtimes, repositories and revisions, pipeline and workflow definitions, clusters and namespaces, Argo applications, environments and promotion flows, secrets references, users and permissions, integrations, executions, audit logs, health, and rollback state.

## Boundary

Stop before connect repositories or clusters, change pipelines or applications, expose secrets, run builds, promote or deploy releases, sync or rollback environments, change permissions, or represent delivery health without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
