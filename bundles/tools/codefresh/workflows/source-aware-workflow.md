# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect product edition and version, account and runtimes, repositories and revisions, pipeline and workflow definitions, clusters and namespaces, Argo applications, environments and promotion flows, secrets references, users and permissions, integrations, executions, audit logs, health, and rollback state.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Codefresh configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before connect repositories or clusters, change pipelines or applications, expose secrets, run builds, promote or deploy releases, sync or rollback environments, change permissions, or represent delivery health.
