# Sprinklr Service Overview

## Scope

- Customer conversations across supported digital, social, messaging, voice and other channels, cases, routing, queues, agent desktop, knowledge, self-service, workforce management, quality management and analytics.
- Bots, conversational AI and agent assistance where licensed, CRM and contact-center integrations, automation, roles, recording and consent controls, privacy, audit evidence, validation, escalation, and controlled customer operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change customer records, send messages, record interactions, route or close cases, publish knowledge, deploy bots, enable AI, issue commitments or credits, or represent identity, consent, sentiment, policy, resolution, quality, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
