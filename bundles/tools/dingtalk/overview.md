# DingTalk Overview

## Scope

- Messaging, meetings, contacts, organization, documents, approvals, attendance, and enterprise collaboration.
- Applications, bots, integrations, identity, permissions, workflow, data, security, and administrative operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product edition and region, organization and users, employment status, groups and contacts, chats and meetings, documents and ownership, approval and attendance workflows, apps and bots, credentials and scopes, integrations, retention, permissions, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change users, groups, messages, meetings, attendance or approvals, install apps or bots, access contacts or files, change permissions, export data, or represent delivery or attendance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
