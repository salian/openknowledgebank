# Sanity Overview

## Scope

- Projects, datasets, documents, schema types, fields, references, assets, Studio workspaces, validation, GROQ queries, mutations, transactions, real-time content, history, releases, localization, and visual editing.
- Content Lake APIs, client libraries, webhooks and Functions where available, Canvas and AI-assisted content or Agent Actions where available, roles, tokens, CORS, environments, usage, evidence, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before change schemas or documents, publish releases, upload protected assets, mutate or delete data, deploy Studio or Functions, enable AI actions, expose tokens, trigger webhooks, or represent content, translation, query, publication, rights, or availability without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
