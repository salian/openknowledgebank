# Tidio Overview

## Scope

- Website live chat, inbox, tickets, contacts, visitors, operators, departments, canned responses, supported messaging channels, chatbot Flows, triggers, analytics and integrations.
- Lyro AI Agent and assistance where licensed, knowledge sources, handoff and escalation, limits and billing, identity and permissions, personal-data handling, audit evidence, testing, rollback, and controlled customer communication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before identify visitors, collect or change customer data, send messages, deploy chatbots or AI agents, connect systems, issue commitments, or represent identity, consent, intent, sentiment, policy, response accuracy, resolution, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
