# SAP SuccessFactors Learning Overview

## Scope

- Learning items, curricula, programs, assignments, classes, schedules, instructors, facilities, online content, assessments, evaluations, certifications, learning history, recommendations, and reports.
- Employee and external learner populations, compliance training, content standards and providers where supported, mobile learning, commerce where licensed, roles, workflows, APIs and integrations, audit evidence, release updates, validation, and controlled learning administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before enroll or assign learners, upload protected content, schedule classes, administer assessments, change completion or certification records, approve compliance exceptions, export employee data, call APIs, or represent learning, competency, certification, employment, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
