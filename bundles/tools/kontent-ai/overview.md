# Kontent.ai Overview

## Scope

- Projects, environments, spaces, collections, content models, items, assets, taxonomies, localization, workflows, roles, publishing, and omnichannel delivery.
- Delivery, Preview, Management, Sync and Subscription APIs, webhooks, image transformation, MCP, AI assistance and agents, auditability, and human approval.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before change content models, create or alter content, move workflow state, publish or unpublish, grant API keys, run migrations, deploy agents or MCP actions, or represent compliance or delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
