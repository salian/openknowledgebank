# Zoho Analytics Overview

## Scope

- Data import and live connections, tables and query tables, relationships, formulas and aggregate formulas, reports, charts and dashboards, sharing, scheduling, alerts and data preparation.
- Ask Zia and other AI capabilities where available, embedded analytics, portals, APIs and integrations, cloud and on-premise distinctions, roles and row-level security, audit evidence, usage, migration, validation, and controlled analytics publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or expose data, change relationships or formulas, publish or embed analytics, enable AI, schedule exports, grant access, call APIs, or represent query, metric, forecast, permission, AI output, delivery, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
