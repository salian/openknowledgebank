# Steinberg Cubase Overview

## Scope

- Music recording, MIDI sequencing, audio editing, mixing, effects, scoring, and production.
- Projects, tracks, instruments, plug-ins, licenses, media, collaboration, rendering, export, and delivery workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect installed version and edition, operating system and hardware, project ownership, source recordings and releases, samples and licenses, tracks and routing, tempo and synchronization, plug-ins and versions, MIDI and score data, mix and mastering settings, export specification, renders, and quality-control results.

## Boundary

Stop before record or overwrite media, install untrusted plug-ins, modify shared projects, render or export masters, publish audio, use licensed content, or represent rights or quality approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
