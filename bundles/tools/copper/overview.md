# Copper CRM Overview

## Scope

- Google Workspace-integrated CRM, leads, people, companies, opportunities, projects, tasks, communication, and reporting.
- Pipelines, forms, automations, email, integrations, mobile workflows, permissions, and customer-journey operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, users and roles, leads and contacts, lawful basis and consent, opportunities and stages, projects and tasks, Gmail and Calendar access, forms, email automations, integrations, reports, attribution, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import or update contacts, connect Google Workspace, send automated email, move opportunities, create projects, change permissions, export personal data, or represent pipeline or revenue outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
