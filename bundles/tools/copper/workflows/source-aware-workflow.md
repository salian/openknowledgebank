# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, users and roles, leads and contacts, lawful basis and consent, opportunities and stages, projects and tasks, Gmail and Calendar access, forms, email automations, integrations, reports, attribution, and logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Copper CRM configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before import or update contacts, connect Google Workspace, send automated email, move opportunities, create projects, change permissions, export personal data, or represent pipeline or revenue outcomes.
