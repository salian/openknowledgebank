# SAP Concur Overview

## Scope

- Expense reports, receipts, corporate cards, travel booking and itineraries, requests, approvals, policies, audits, invoices, purchase requests, payments, mileage, tax fields, and reporting.
- Product and country distinctions, SAP integrations, App Center, REST APIs, OAuth, webhooks or event surfaces where documented, identity provisioning, permissions, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled travel and spend operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change traveler, card, expense, receipt, invoice or itinerary data, approve expenses or payments, book travel, change policies, connect systems, expose OAuth credentials, or represent expense, tax, travel, payment, audit, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
