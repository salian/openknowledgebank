# Zoho Books Overview

## Scope

- Organizations, chart of accounts, customers and vendors, estimates, invoices, sales orders, bills, purchase orders, payments, banking and reconciliation, inventory, projects and financial reports.
- Country-specific taxes and e-invoicing where supported, workflows and automation, user roles, accountant access, integrations and API v3, rate limits, import and export, audit trail, reconciliation, validation, and controlled accounting operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post or alter accounting, customer, vendor, invoice, bill, inventory, bank, tax or project records, reconcile accounts, execute payments, file taxes, expose tokens, or represent balances, tax, stock, profit, payment, compliance, or audit conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
