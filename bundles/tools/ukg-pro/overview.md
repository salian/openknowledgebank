# UKG Pro Overview

## Scope

- Core employee records, organizational and compensation data, payroll and tax, benefits, recruiting and onboarding, talent, learning, time and attendance, scheduling and workforce-management capabilities according to licensed modules.
- Employee and manager self-service, reporting and analytics, AI capabilities where available, integrations and APIs, identity and roles, audit evidence, regional and release scope, reconciliation, validation, and controlled workforce operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee, compensation, payroll, tax, time, schedule, benefit or talent data, hire or terminate workers, run payroll, enable AI, call APIs, or make employment, eligibility, performance, tax, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
