# SmartRecruiters Overview

## Scope

- Job and requisition management, career sites, candidate and application records, sourcing, screening, interviews, collaboration, offers, approvals, hiring workflows, communications, and analytics.
- Marketplace applications, webhooks and APIs, HRIS and assessment integrations, automation and AI where available, roles, consent, retention, bias and accessibility review, audit evidence, validation, and controlled hiring operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before process applicant data, post jobs, screen or rank candidates, schedule interviews, send messages, create offers, enable AI, connect HR systems, or make employment, eligibility, compensation, diversity, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
