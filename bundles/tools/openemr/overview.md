# OpenEMR Overview

## Scope

- Patient demographics, appointments, encounters, clinical notes, problems, medications, prescriptions, orders, labs, documents, portals, reports, billing, claims, and practice management.
- FHIR and other interoperability where configured, APIs, roles, audit controls, patient privacy, certified configurations where applicable, modules, upgrades, backups, hosting, validation, and regulated operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change patient records, prescribe, order or document care, exchange protected health information, submit claims, change billing or access, install modules, upgrade systems, or make clinical, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
