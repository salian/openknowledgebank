# Zoom Workplace Overview

Source-aware guidance for Zoom Workplace meetings, recordings, apps, APIs, identity, data governance, and controlled change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes account, plan, release, users, groups, roles, meeting and webinar settings, recordings, transcripts, apps, OAuth scopes, webhooks, retention, consent, data regions, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Plan and account state, effective permissions, participant consent, meeting contents, recording or transcript state, retention, app behavior, and downstream effects. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
