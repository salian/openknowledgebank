# BlackLine Overview

## Scope

- Account reconciliation, transaction matching, journal, close, consolidation, and financial operations workflows.
- Rules, assignments, evidence, exceptions, approvals, audit trails, integrations, reporting, and AI-assisted preparation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and modules, entities and periods, chart of accounts, balances and source systems, matching and reconciliation rules, preparers and approvers, evidence, exceptions, journals, integrations, AI configuration, audit trails, and close status.

## Boundary

Stop before import balances, configure rules, prepare or certify reconciliations, post or approve journals, close tasks or periods, change roles, resolve exceptions, or represent financial assurance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
