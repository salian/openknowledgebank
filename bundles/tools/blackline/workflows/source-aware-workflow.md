# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription and modules, entities and periods, chart of accounts, balances and source systems, matching and reconciliation rules, preparers and approvers, evidence, exceptions, journals, integrations, AI configuration, audit trails, and close status.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the BlackLine configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before import balances, configure rules, prepare or certify reconciliations, post or approve journals, close tasks or periods, change roles, resolve exceptions, or represent financial assurance.
