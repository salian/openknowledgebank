# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect offering and version, cloud and region, deployment and cluster, storage and compute, databases and schemas, tables and formats, data lineage, users and roles, encryption and governance, workloads and queries, backups and recovery, replication, integrations, logs, performance and cost tests, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the IBM Db2 Warehouse architecture and operations review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before provision or scale resources, ingest or expose data, change schemas, permissions or security, run workloads or ML, share data, restore or fail over, delete data, incur cost, or represent performance, availability, recovery, or compliance.
