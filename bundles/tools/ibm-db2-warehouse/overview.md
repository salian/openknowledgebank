# IBM Db2 Warehouse Overview

## Scope

- Hybrid and cloud data warehousing, columnar and in-memory processing, elastic storage and compute, workload management, open formats, governed sharing, BI, and in-database ML.
- SaaS and software deployment, availability, disaster recovery, security, integration, operations, validation, and controlled analytical use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect offering and version, cloud and region, deployment and cluster, storage and compute, databases and schemas, tables and formats, data lineage, users and roles, encryption and governance, workloads and queries, backups and recovery, replication, integrations, logs, performance and cost tests, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before provision or scale resources, ingest or expose data, change schemas, permissions or security, run workloads or ML, share data, restore or fail over, delete data, incur cost, or represent performance, availability, recovery, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
