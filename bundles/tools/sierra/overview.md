# Sierra Overview

## Scope

- Customer-service agents across supported channels, conversational behavior, knowledge and policy grounding, goals, guardrails, escalation, multilingual experiences, personalization, and brand configuration.
- Agent actions and system integrations, testing and simulation, deployment, versioning, monitoring, quality analytics, security and privacy controls, permissions, audit evidence, human supervision, validation, rollback, and controlled customer operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before deploy customer-facing agents, connect systems, access or change customer records, issue refunds or credits, make commitments, send messages, expose credentials, alter guardrails, or represent identity, policy, resolution, transaction, compliance, or business outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
