# Adobe Analytics Overview

## Scope

- Digital experience data collection and analysis.
- Dimensions, metrics, attribution, segmentation, reporting, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect contract and product version, report suites, datastreams, variables, processing rules, identities, consent, segments, metrics, attribution settings, exports, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before change data collection or processing, publish segments, export person-level data, alter attribution, deploy tags, or represent findings as validated without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
