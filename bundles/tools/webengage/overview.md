# WebEngage Overview

## Scope

- User profiles and events, anonymous and identified users, segments, journeys, relays, transactional and marketing campaigns across supported push, web push, in-app, email, SMS and other channels.
- Web and mobile SDKs, data-center-specific APIs, service accounts, integrations, personalization, experiments and analytics where licensed, consent and suppression, roles, rate limits, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before identify users, ingest behavioral data, create audiences, trigger messages or journeys, change consent, expose keys, spend funds, or represent delivery, attribution, conversion, retention, revenue, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
