# CapCut Overview

Source-aware guidance for CapCut projects, media assets, AI features, captions, collaboration, licensing, privacy, and controlled export.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes product surface, app version, device, account and plan, project files, source media, authorship, licenses, consent, music and templates, AI feature inputs and outputs, captions, dimensions, color, audio, export settings, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Product and plan state, project contents, asset rights, consent, AI output provenance, caption accuracy, visual or audio quality, metadata, export fidelity, platform terms, and release authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
