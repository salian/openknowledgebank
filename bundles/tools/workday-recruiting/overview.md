# Workday Recruiting Overview

Source-aware guidance for recruiting workflows, candidate data, permissions, integrations, fairness review, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes tenant, release, subscriptions, security domains, job requisitions, candidate stages, business processes, condition rules, questionnaires, integrations, candidate data, fairness evidence, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Tenant state, subscription availability, candidate facts, effective permissions, workflow outcomes, screening validity, fairness, legal compliance, and hiring authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
