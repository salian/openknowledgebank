# Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement Overview

## Scope

- Prospects, accounts, lists, segmentation rules, forms, form handlers, landing pages, email, templates, engagement programs, scoring, grading, automation rules, campaigns, and reporting.
- Salesforce CRM synchronization, connected campaigns, business units, Einstein features where licensed, API v5, imports, exports, tracking, consent, permissions, recycle bin, evidence, validation, and controlled B2B marketing.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify prospects, change consent, scores or grades, activate programs, send email, deploy tracking, synchronize CRM, call APIs, spend funds, or represent delivery, qualification, attribution, pipeline, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
