# Salesloft Overview

## Scope

- People, accounts, cadences, email, calls, tasks, templates, meetings, conversations and recordings, deal management, pipeline inspection, forecasting, coaching, analytics, and team workflows.
- CRM synchronization, integrations, REST APIs and webhooks, permissions, consent and recording controls, deliverability, Rhythm and AI capabilities or agents where available, evidence, validation, and controlled sales actions.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify contacts, send outreach, place or record calls, enroll cadences, change deal state or forecast, enable AI agents, synchronize CRM, expose API credentials, or represent consent, delivery, intent, pipeline, forecast, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
