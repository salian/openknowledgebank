# Gong Overview

Source-aware guidance for Gong conversations, recordings, integrations, analytics, coaching, privacy, and governed use.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes tenant, subscription, users, teams, data sources, call and meeting capture, recording and consent settings, transcripts, trackers, scorecards, CRM mappings, API credentials, permissions, retention, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Subscription and tenant state, participant consent, recording contents, transcript accuracy, effective access, tracker or score validity, coaching conclusions, retention, and downstream effects. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
