# Dynamics 365 Customer Service Overview

Source-aware guidance for case management, routing, entitlements, knowledge, service controls, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes tenant, environment, release, licenses, roles, case schema, queues, routing, entitlements, SLAs, knowledge, channels, data, integrations, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Tenant state, role access, case values, routing results, entitlement status, SLA compliance, and customer outcomes. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
