# Zoho Projects Overview

## Scope

- Portals, projects, milestones, task lists and tasks, dependencies, Gantt and other views, users and teams, timesheets, issues, documents, forums, calendars, reports and dashboards.
- Budgets and billing where available, workflows and automation, Blueprint, integrations with Zoho and third-party systems, REST API and webhooks, roles and permissions, audit evidence, limits, validation, rollback, and controlled project operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or alter projects, tasks, dependencies, estimates, time or budgets, assign users, run automation, share data, expose tokens, call APIs, or represent effort, cost, resource capacity, status, delivery date, or completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
