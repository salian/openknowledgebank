# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, domain and DNS, site and content, rights, products and inventory, bookings, payments, marketing consent, permissions, analytics, tests, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the GoDaddy website configuration and launch review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before register or transfer domains, change DNS, publish a site, sell products, take payments or bookings, send marketing, connect services, or represent availability or performance.
