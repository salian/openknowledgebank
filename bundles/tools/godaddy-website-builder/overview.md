# GoDaddy Website Builder Overview

## Scope

- Templates, site editing, mobile management, domains, hosting, SSL, social, email, commerce, bookings, and analytics.
- Account, plan, permissions, integrations, configuration, evidence, testing, rollback, and publishing controls.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, domain and DNS, site and content, rights, products and inventory, bookings, payments, marketing consent, permissions, analytics, tests, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before register or transfer domains, change DNS, publish a site, sell products, take payments or bookings, send marketing, connect services, or represent availability or performance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
