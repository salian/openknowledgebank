# IBM watsonx Overview

## Scope

- Foundation models, prompts, tuning, RAG, agents, machine learning, projects, deployment, APIs, and runtimes.
- watsonx.data integration, watsonx.governance, model and prompt evaluation, monitoring, guardrails, factsheets, inventories, and governed catalogs.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload enterprise data, train or tune models, deploy AI or agents, enable tools, change governance controls, incur compute cost, or represent accuracy, fairness, safety, compliance, or production state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
