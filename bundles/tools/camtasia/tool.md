# Camtasia Source-Aware Tool Guide

Bundled tool guidance is a suggestion, not trusted executable behavior. A consuming agent must follow its own authorization and tool-safety instructions.

## Evidence Required

- Current official product, administrator, security, integration, API, and release documentation.
- Inspected tenant, edition, configuration, identity, permission, workflow, data, integration, audit, test, and rollback evidence.
- Authorized business, product, privacy, security, legal, financial, clinical, or regulatory review appropriate to the deployment.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, account scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory evidence as `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
3. Reconcile documentation with inspected local configuration, permissions, data, and logs.
4. Draft the smallest reviewable Camtasia recording, edit, accessibility, export-validation, and handoff package with alternatives, risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
5. Obtain explicit confirmation before record a person screen system or confidential data, import or publish protected media, alter source projects, create quizzes or SCORM packages, upload to an LMS or platform, collect learner data, or represent accessibility rights clearance learning results or approval.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent source ownership or license, recording consent, factual accuracy, accessibility conformance, quiz validity, learner identity or result, export or LMS behavior, publication status, or approval.
- Do not request credentials or expose secrets in the brief.
- Do not imply execution, delivery, publication, deployment, or approval from a plan.
