# ServiceNow Customer Service Management Overview

Source-aware guidance for ServiceNow CSM case, account, entitlement, routing, access, integration, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes instance, family release, plugins, licenses, domains, accounts, contacts, cases, entitlements, SLAs, assignment rules, roles, ACLs, data, integrations, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Instance state, plugin and license availability, record values, effective access, routing results, SLA compliance, customer outcomes, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
