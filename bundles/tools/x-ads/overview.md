# X Ads Overview

Source-aware guidance for X Ads campaign structure, targeting, creative, budgets, measurement, policy review, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes ad account, funding instrument, objective, campaign, ad groups, targeting, creatives, landing pages, budgets, bids, dates, tracking, policies, consent, measurement plan, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Account state, eligibility, audience size, targeting reach, creative approval, policy compliance, delivery, attribution, performance, and cost. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
