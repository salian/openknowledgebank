# Optimizely Feature Experimentation Overview

## Scope

- Feature flags, rules, variations, rollouts, audiences, attributes, events, metrics, experiments, holdbacks, mutual exclusion where supported, results, and recommendations.
- SDKs, datafiles and APIs, Full Stack and Web product distinctions, integrations, data exports, permissions, environments, change history, statistics, quality controls, validation, and safe rollback.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before instrument events, identify users, change flags or allocation, launch or stop experiments, deploy SDKs, expose keys, export data, make product decisions, or represent assignment, metric, significance, causality, or business impact without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
