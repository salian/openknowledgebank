# Apollo.io Overview

## Scope

- People and company search, enrichment, CRM records, and sales intelligence.
- Sequences, email and call workflows, integrations, APIs, MCP, and CLI tooling.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and region, people and company records, data provenance, lawful basis, suppression and consent, sequences, mailboxes, CRM mappings, API scopes, credits, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before search for or enrich people, export personal data, enroll contacts, send messages, place calls, update CRM records, connect mailboxes, or represent consent or intent without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
