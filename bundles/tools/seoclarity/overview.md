# seoClarity Overview

## Scope

- Keyword and rank intelligence, search visibility, competitor analysis, site auditing and crawling, technical SEO, content inventory and optimization, internal links, backlinks, dashboards, reports, and forecasts.
- ClarityAutomate and other automation, Sia and AI capabilities where available, Search Console and analytics integrations, APIs and exports, permissions, evidence history, validation, and controlled recommendations or changes.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before crawl sites, track markets or competitors, generate or publish content recommendations, deploy SEO automation, enable AI, change internal links or pages, export data, or represent rank, traffic, issue severity, causality, forecast, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
