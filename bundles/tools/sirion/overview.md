# Sirion Overview

## Scope

- Contract intake, repository, templates, authoring, review, negotiation, approvals, signatures, obligations, milestones, performance, risk, renewals, analytics, and reporting.
- AI-assisted extraction, drafting and review where available, third-party and enterprise integrations, permissions, audit history, migration, validation, and controlled contract operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload privileged or confidential contracts, generate or accept clauses, change approvals, execute agreements, alter obligations, disclose data, enable AI or integrations, or represent legal interpretation, signature, compliance, savings, risk, or authorization without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
