# Litmos Overview

## Scope

- Courses, modules, content, learning paths, assignments, assessments, surveys, certifications, compliance training, learners, teams, instructors, ecommerce where available, mobile learning, and reports.
- Branding, automation, integrations, APIs, SSO, permissions, audit evidence, content libraries and AI capabilities where licensed, migration and current ownership or naming review, validation, and controlled learning operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before enroll learners, upload protected content, administer assessments, issue certifications, sell courses, change completion or compliance records, enable AI, export learner data, call APIs, or represent learning, competency, certification, payment, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
