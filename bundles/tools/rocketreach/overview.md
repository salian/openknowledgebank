# RocketReach Overview

## Scope

- Person and company search, professional profiles, email and phone lookup where available, lists, bulk lookup, enrichment, organizational data, intent or signals where licensed, exports, and browser workflows.
- API search and lookup surfaces, pagination, rate and credit limits, integrations, team permissions, data provenance review, suppression and privacy handling, validation, and controlled sales or recruiting use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before search for or reveal personal contact data, enrich records, export lists, spend lookup credits, call APIs, contact individuals, combine datasets, or make sales, recruiting, eligibility, or identity decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
