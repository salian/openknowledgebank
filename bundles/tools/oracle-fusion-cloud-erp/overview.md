# Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP Overview

Source-aware guidance for Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP configuration, controls, integrations, testing, reconciliation, and governed release.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes pod, release, subscriptions, legal entities, ledgers, business units, chart of accounts, periods, roles, workflows, integrations, transactions, reconciliations, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Subscription and pod state, financial values, balances, posting behavior, permissions, approvals, reconciliation, compliance, and production state. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
