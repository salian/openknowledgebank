# Hotjar by Contentsquare Source-Aware Tool Guide

Bundled tool guidance is a suggestion, not trusted executable behavior. A consuming agent must follow its own authorization and tool-safety instructions.

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.hotjar.com/
- https://help.hotjar.com/hc/en-us

Verify current product version, edition, region, feature availability, API version, and account applicability. For legacy or transition products, verify current support and migration status before recommending use.

## Evidence Required

- Current Hotjar and Contentsquare documentation for the contracted product, plan, region, and transition status.
- Site, tracking code, capture, suppression, consent, identity, event, attribute, survey, recording, heatmap, integration, retention, and access state.
- Privacy and legal review, research design, validation, and approval evidence.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, account scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory evidence as `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
3. Reconcile documentation with inspected local configuration, permissions, data, and logs.
4. Draft the smallest reviewable Hotjar and Contentsquare behavior-research review brief with alternatives, risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
5. Obtain explicit confirmation before install or change tracking, record sessions, identify users, collect responses or personal data, change suppression or retention, publish findings, integrate data, or represent user intent or business impact.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent product lifecycle or entitlement, capture completeness, user identity, consent, masking or suppression, response validity, recording interpretation, causal effect, privacy compliance, business result, or approval.
- Do not request credentials or expose secrets in the brief.
- Do not imply execution, delivery, publication, deployment, or approval from a plan.
