# Hotjar by Contentsquare Overview

## Scope

- Heatmaps, session recordings, surveys, dashboards, highlights, trends, events, user attributes, and behavior research.
- Current Contentsquare integration and lifecycle, tracking configuration, privacy controls, suppression, permissions, evidence, and validation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect contracted product and lifecycle, sites and tracking, capture and suppression, consent and identity, events and attributes, surveys, recordings and heatmaps, integrations, retention, permissions, research design, validation, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before install or change tracking, record sessions, identify users, collect responses or personal data, change suppression or retention, publish findings, integrate data, or represent user intent or business impact without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
