# Source-Aware Workflow

3. Inspect contracted product and lifecycle, sites and tracking, capture and suppression, consent and identity, events and attributes, surveys, recordings and heatmaps, integrations, retention, permissions, research design, validation, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Hotjar and Contentsquare behavior-research review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before install or change tracking, record sessions, identify users, collect responses or personal data, change suppression or retention, publish findings, integrate data, or represent user intent or business impact.
