# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect offering and version, deployment, namespaces and connections, data lineage, packages and modules, certified models, fields and metrics, filters, forecasts, reports and dashboards, AI and agents, schedules and distribution, permissions, logs, reconciliation, validation, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the IBM Cognos Analytics governed reporting review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before connect or expose data, change models or metrics, generate or distribute reports, publish dashboards, run forecasts or agents, change access, make consequential decisions, or represent compliance or business results.
