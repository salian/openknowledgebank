# IBM Cognos Analytics Overview

## Scope

- Self-service data modeling, advanced reports, interactive dashboards, visualizations, exploration, predictive forecasting, AI Assistant, and reporting agents.
- Certified models, governance, audit trails, access controls, scheduling, distribution, cloud, on-premises and hybrid deployment, validation, and controlled analysis.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect offering and version, deployment, namespaces and connections, data lineage, packages and modules, certified models, fields and metrics, filters, forecasts, reports and dashboards, AI and agents, schedules and distribution, permissions, logs, reconciliation, validation, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or expose data, change models or metrics, generate or distribute reports, publish dashboards, run forecasts or agents, change access, make consequential decisions, or represent compliance or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
