# Avalara Overview

## Scope

- Tax calculation, address validation, transaction, document, filing, exemption, and compliance workflows.
- APIs, SDKs, accounts, companies, nexus, tax configuration, integrations, and reporting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect product and API version, account and company, jurisdictions, registrations and nexus, tax codes, addresses, customers, exemptions, transaction and document state, filing settings, credentials, integrations, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before calculate or commit tax, create or adjust transactions, validate addresses, change nexus or filing configuration, submit returns, handle exemptions, or represent tax liability without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
