# Avid Pro Tools Overview

## Scope

- Audio and MIDI recording and editing, tracks, clips, playlists, routing, automation, mixing, mastering, video, scoring, virtual instruments, plugins, elastic audio, and offline bounce.
- Artist, Studio and Ultimate edition distinctions, immersive audio, cloud collaboration where available, control surfaces, interfaces, session interchange, version compatibility, storage, backups, licensing, validation, and delivery.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before record people, overwrite sessions or media, change routing or sample rate, run third-party plugins, publish or deliver masters, share protected audio, consume licenses, or represent rights, synchronization, loudness, fidelity, or delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
