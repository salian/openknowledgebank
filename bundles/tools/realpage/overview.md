# RealPage Overview

## Scope

- Property and unit records, leasing, applications, screening integrations, resident services, rent and payments, maintenance, facilities, vendors, accounting, budgeting, procurement, and reporting.
- Revenue and pricing products where applicable, marketing, contact center, utility and expense management, affordable housing and compliance support, analytics, data exchange, roles, audit evidence, reconciliation, and controlled property operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change applicant, resident, lease, unit, rent, payment, screening, pricing, maintenance or accounting data, collect funds, change access, or make housing, eligibility, pricing, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
