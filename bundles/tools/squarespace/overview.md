# Squarespace Overview

## Scope

- Website pages, templates and styles, content, blogging, domains, forms, search, analytics, SEO, email and marketing surfaces, memberships, scheduling and commerce features where subscribed.
- Products, inventory, orders, payments and fulfillment integrations, developer APIs and webhooks where supported, code injection and extensions, contributors, privacy and cookie controls, testing, rollback, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before publish websites, change domains or DNS, collect personal data, sell products, process orders, connect payments, inject code, call APIs, grant access, or represent inventory, payment, fulfillment, traffic, conversion, accessibility, rights, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
