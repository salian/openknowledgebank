# RUN Powered by ADP Overview

## Scope

- Employee and contractor records, payroll entry and processing, direct deposit and checks, payroll taxes and forms, deductions, reports, accountant access, time and attendance, and employee self-service.
- HR packages and services, onboarding and benefits integrations where licensed, marketplace applications, permissions, approvals, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled payroll operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee, contractor, pay, tax, time or benefit data, run or approve payroll, initiate payments, file forms, connect applications, grant access, or make employment, classification, tax, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
