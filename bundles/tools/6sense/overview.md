# 6sense Overview

## Scope

- B2B account and intent intelligence.
- Audience, advertising, sales intelligence, workflows, and predictive modeling.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, data sources, identity resolution, audience definitions, model configuration, permissions, consent basis, campaigns, exports, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before activate audiences, launch advertising or outreach, export contact data, change models or workflows, connect systems, or represent buyer intent as fact without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
