# Maze Overview

## Scope

- Prototype tests, website tests, surveys, card sorting, tree testing, panel and link recruitment, screening, moderated interviews, and participant management.
- Heatmaps, paths, usability metrics, clips, AI-assisted summaries and themes, reports, templates, integrations, consent, evidence, and research governance.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before recruit or screen participants, collect recordings or personal data, launch studies, pay incentives, use AI analysis, share reports, or represent sample validity, usability, sentiment, or product decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
