# HaloITSM Overview

## Scope

- Incident, request, problem, change, knowledge, service catalog, assets, IT operations, projects, customer service, billing, automation, and analytics.
- Administration, roles, workflows, integrations, AI, audit evidence, validation, rollback, and controlled service operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect tenant and modules, services and catalog, tickets and SLAs, assets and configuration items, workflows and approvals, knowledge, automation and AI, integrations, roles, logs, tests, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before change or close tickets, approve changes, modify assets or configuration, run automation, publish knowledge, alter SLAs or access, bill customers, or represent service restoration or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
