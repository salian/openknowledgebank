# Kustomer Overview

## Scope

- Customer CRM and timelines, omnichannel support, conversations, routing, workflows, automation, self-service, proactive service, reports, and integrations.
- Concierge, Envoy and Architect AI capabilities, agent studio, contextual data, oversight, permissions, evidence, monitoring, validation, and rollback.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before reply to customers, alter CRM or conversation state, change entitlements, issue commercial actions, deploy workflows or AI agents, export data, or represent resolution, satisfaction, retention, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
