# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect edition and integrations, contract parties and authority, source and negotiated versions, templates and clause library, playbook and policies, redlines and comments, extracted terms, obligations and dates, pricing, approvals, signatures, access controls, retention, AI configuration and outputs, and audit history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Conga CLM configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before generate, modify, approve, send or execute contracts, accept AI redlines, change clause or playbook controls, expose privileged information, trigger signatures, or represent enforceability or compliance.
