# Conga CLM Overview

## Scope

- Contract authoring, templates, negotiation, review, approval, repository, obligations, renewals, reporting, and AI analysis.
- CRM, ERP, procurement and document integrations, clause and playbook controls, permissions, e-signature handoff, and contract data operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect edition and integrations, contract parties and authority, source and negotiated versions, templates and clause library, playbook and policies, redlines and comments, extracted terms, obligations and dates, pricing, approvals, signatures, access controls, retention, AI configuration and outputs, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before generate, modify, approve, send or execute contracts, accept AI redlines, change clause or playbook controls, expose privileged information, trigger signatures, or represent enforceability or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
