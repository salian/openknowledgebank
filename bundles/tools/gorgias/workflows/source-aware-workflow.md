# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, stores and channels, tickets and customers, orders, rules and macros, knowledge, AI Agent skills and actions, integrations, roles, reports, logs, tests, monitoring, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Gorgias support automation and action review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before reply to customers, change or cancel orders, issue refunds or discounts, deploy automation or AI Agent, change routing or access, export data, or represent resolution or performance.
