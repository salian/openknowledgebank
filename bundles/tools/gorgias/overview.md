# Gorgias Overview

## Scope

- Email, chat, social, SMS and voice support, tickets, routing, rules, macros, customer context, help center, reports, and integrations.
- AI Agent, order actions, teams, permissions, SSO, MFA, APIs, validation, monitoring, and controlled automation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, stores and channels, tickets and customers, orders, rules and macros, knowledge, AI Agent skills and actions, integrations, roles, reports, logs, tests, monitoring, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before reply to customers, change or cancel orders, issue refunds or discounts, deploy automation or AI Agent, change routing or access, export data, or represent resolution or performance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
