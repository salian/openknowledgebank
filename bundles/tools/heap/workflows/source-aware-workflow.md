# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect contracted product and lifecycle, workspace and environments, SDKs and capture, schema and event definitions, identities and consent, segments and filters, exclusions and retention, permissions, integrations, data lineage, validation, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Heap and Contentsquare Product Analytics implementation review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before install or change capture, identify users, collect personal data, define events, publish analyses, change retention or access, integrate data, or represent user behavior or business impact.
