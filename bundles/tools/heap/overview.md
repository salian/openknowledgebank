# Heap by Contentsquare Overview

## Scope

- Autocapture, event definition, journeys, funnels, engagement, retention, activation, segmentation, web and mobile product analytics.
- Current Contentsquare integration and lifecycle, data governance, identity, privacy controls, validation, and controlled analysis.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect contracted product and lifecycle, workspace and environments, SDKs and capture, schema and event definitions, identities and consent, segments and filters, exclusions and retention, permissions, integrations, data lineage, validation, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before install or change capture, identify users, collect personal data, define events, publish analyses, change retention or access, integrate data, or represent user behavior or business impact without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
