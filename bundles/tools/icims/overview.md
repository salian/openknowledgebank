# iCIMS Overview

## Scope

- Applicant tracking, candidate CRM, recruitment marketing, career sites, job matching, text engagement, interviews, offers, and onboarding.
- Talent data, workflows, integrations, permissions, analytics, AI, audit evidence, validation, and controlled hiring operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before collect candidate data, source or rank candidates, change applications, schedule interviews, issue offers, onboard hires, send messages, export data, or make employment decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
