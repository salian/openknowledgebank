# Docebo Overview

## Scope

- Learning management, skills intelligence, knowledge, content, coaching, ecommerce, and workforce readiness.
- Learners, audiences, courses, assessments, AI agents, MCP, integrations, reporting, permissions, and automation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and modules, tenant and region, learners and employment status, audiences, content and rights, assignments, assessments and completion, skills taxonomy, knowledge sources, AI agents and action scopes, MCP permissions, ecommerce, integrations, retention, reports, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or publish learning, enroll learners, score assessments, infer skills, deploy AI agents, connect MCP, change records or permissions, sell content, or represent qualification or performance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
