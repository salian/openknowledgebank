# Contentsquare Overview

## Scope

- Experience and product analytics, session replay, heatmaps, journeys, feedback, monitoring, and conversation intelligence.
- Web and mobile capture, AI insights, MCP, integrations, privacy controls, experiments, reporting, and data operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and modules, sites and apps, capture method, consent and privacy settings, masking, identities, sessions, events, journeys, errors, metrics, filters, experiments, integrations, MCP scopes, AI settings, retention, and reports.

## Boundary

Stop before enable capture or replay, change masking or consent settings, connect MCP or data sources, export user data, activate experiments, publish recommendations, or represent behavior or conversion outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
