# 360Learning Overview

## Scope

- Learning management and collaborative course authoring.
- Enrollment, learning analytics, integrations, and administrative workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect tenant edition, users, groups, courses, enrollments, learning records, integrations, AI settings, permissions, retention, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or publish courses, enroll users, change learning records or settings, connect HR systems, process personal data, or represent compliance completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
