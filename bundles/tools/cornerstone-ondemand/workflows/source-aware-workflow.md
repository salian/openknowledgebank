# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect current product and modules, tenant and region, workers and employment status, roles, courses and content rights, assignments, assessments and completion, skills taxonomy and models, performance and talent records, AI settings, integrations, retention, permissions, and audit logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Cornerstone configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or publish learning, assign training, change learner or talent records, score assessments, infer skills, activate AI, export employee data, change permissions, or represent qualification or compliance.
