# Cornerstone Overview

## Scope

- Learning, skills, content, talent, performance, recruiting, and workforce-readiness workflows.
- Users, roles, assignments, assessments, skills intelligence, AI, integrations, reporting, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product and modules, tenant and region, workers and employment status, roles, courses and content rights, assignments, assessments and completion, skills taxonomy and models, performance and talent records, AI settings, integrations, retention, permissions, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or publish learning, assign training, change learner or talent records, score assessments, infer skills, activate AI, export employee data, change permissions, or represent qualification or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
