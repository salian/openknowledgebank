# Crazy Egg Overview

## Scope

- Heatmaps, recordings, traffic analysis, surveys, errors, goals, and A/B testing.
- Site tracking, snapshots, audience and device segmentation, experiment variants, privacy controls, reporting, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, site ownership, tracking code and scope, consent and privacy requirements, masking, snapshots and recordings, traffic sources, goals, segments, survey responses, experiment design and allocation, metrics, sample size, exclusions, integrations, and reports.

## Boundary

Stop before install or change tracking, enable recordings, alter masking, launch or stop tests, collect surveys, change site variants, export visitor data, or represent causality or conversion lift without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
