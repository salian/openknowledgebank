# Deel Overview

## Scope

- Global hiring, employer-of-record, payroll, HR, benefits, contractor, immigration, equipment, and workforce workflows.
- Workers, entities, contracts, compensation, tax, payments, approvals, integrations, AI, compliance, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect contracted services and jurisdictions, employing entity, worker identity and classification, authorization, contracts, compensation and currency, payroll and tax setup, time and leave, benefits, visas, equipment, bank details, approvals, integrations, permissions, payments, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before hire or terminate workers, classify employment, generate or sign contracts, change compensation, payroll, tax, benefits, visa or bank data, approve or send payments, ship equipment, or represent compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
