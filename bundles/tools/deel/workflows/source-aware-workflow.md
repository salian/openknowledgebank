# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect contracted services and jurisdictions, employing entity, worker identity and classification, authorization, contracts, compensation and currency, payroll and tax setup, time and leave, benefits, visas, equipment, bank details, approvals, integrations, permissions, payments, and audit logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Deel configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before hire or terminate workers, classify employment, generate or sign contracts, change compensation, payroll, tax, benefits, visa or bank data, approve or send payments, ship equipment, or represent compliance.
