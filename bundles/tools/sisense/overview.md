# Sisense Overview

## Scope

- Data connections, ElastiCube and live models where applicable, dashboards, widgets, filters, formulas, alerts, exports, embedding, white labeling, and analytics administration.
- Compose SDK, SisenseJS, REST APIs, AI-assisted analytics where available, Linux and Windows lifecycle distinctions, access controls, row-level security, deployment, validation, and monitoring.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect data, publish or embed analytics, change models, formulas or security rules, call APIs, expose credentials, enable AI, export data, or represent query, metric, forecast, access, deployment, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
