# Pylon Overview

## Scope

- Omnichannel B2B support through shared Slack and Microsoft Teams channels, email and chat, tickets, contacts, accounts, routing, SLAs, workflows, views, and collaboration.
- Knowledge base, AI assistance and agents where available, customer portal, integrations, APIs and webhooks, product signals, reports, permissions, audit evidence, validation, and controlled customer communication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before read or send customer messages, create or change tickets, expose shared-channel data, activate workflows or AI, publish knowledge, connect CRM or developer systems, change access, or represent SLA, sentiment, resolution, retention, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
