# Matomo Overview

## Scope

- Web and app analytics, events, goals, funnels, ecommerce, campaigns, segments, dashboards, reports, tag management, and data import.
- Matomo Cloud and On-Premise, JavaScript and server tracking, reporting APIs, consent and cookie controls, anonymization, retention, opt-out, users, and plugins.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before deploy tracking or tags, collect or identify visitors, change consent or privacy settings, import or delete data, grant access, install plugins, or represent compliance, attribution, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
