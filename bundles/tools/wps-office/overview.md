# WPS Office Overview

## Scope

- Word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, PDF viewing and editing, templates, import and export, format compatibility, desktop and mobile applications and cloud document storage or synchronization.
- Team collaboration, sharing and permissions, WPS 365 and regional service distinctions, AI capabilities where available, macros or add-ins where supported, version compatibility, rights, privacy, retention, validation, and controlled document publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload confidential files, share or publish documents, execute macros, enable AI processing, convert or sign PDFs, change permissions, or represent authorship, formula accuracy, compatibility, signature validity, confidentiality, retention, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
