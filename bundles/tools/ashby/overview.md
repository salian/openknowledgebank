# Ashby Overview

## Scope

- Applicant tracking, recruiting CRM, scheduling, analytics, and hiring workflows.
- Job, candidate, interview, offer, automation, integration, and reporting operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, region, jobs, candidates, applications, sources, consent and retention, interview plans, scorecards, calendars, offers, permissions, integrations, automations, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or publish jobs, contact or move candidates, schedule interviews, submit evaluations, issue offers, change retention, export personal data, or represent a hiring decision without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
