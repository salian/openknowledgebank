# Amazon Bedrock Overview

## Scope

- Managed foundation models and generative AI services.
- Agents, knowledge bases, guardrails, evaluation, customization, inference, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect AWS account and region, service and model availability, model version, data classification, IAM, encryption, network controls, prompts, knowledge sources, guardrails, evaluations, quotas, costs, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before enable models, transmit data, create agents or knowledge bases, change IAM or guardrails, deploy inference, incur spend, or represent safety or quality approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
