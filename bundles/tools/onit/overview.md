# Onit Overview

## Scope

- Legal matter and spend management, budgets, invoices, billing guidelines, vendors, analytics, workflow automation, enterprise legal management, and system-of-record reporting.
- Unity ELM, Spend Agent invoice review, Ask Unity, legal AI, contract and service products where applicable, integrations, permissions, audit evidence, human review, and controlled legal operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or alter matters, budgets, invoices, billing rules, vendors or contracts, approve spend, enable AI agents, export privileged data, change access, or represent legal, invoice, savings, compliance, or payment conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
