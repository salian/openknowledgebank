# SAP Datasphere Overview

## Scope

- Spaces, connections, remote tables, replication flows, data flows, transformations, graphical and SQL views, analytic models, semantic definitions, business entities, catalog, lineage, marketplace, and data sharing.
- SAP and non-SAP source integration, BW bridge and data products where applicable, SAP Analytics Cloud integration, users, roles, row-level controls, APIs and CLI surfaces, capacity, monitoring, transport, evidence, validation, and controlled deployment.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect production sources, replicate or transform data, change models or semantic definitions, share data products, grant roles, expose credentials, deploy content, consume capacity, or represent schema, lineage, security, quality, freshness, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
