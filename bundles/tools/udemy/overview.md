# Udemy Business Overview

## Scope

- Curated course libraries according to subscription, search and discovery, learning paths, assignments, custom content, practice and assessment surfaces, certificates, skills and learner experiences.
- Organization and group administration, reports and analytics, SSO and user provisioning, LMS and other integrations, APIs where available, privacy, accessibility, content rights, evidence, validation, and controlled learning operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before provision or identify learners, assign content, upload protected materials, administer assessments, alter completion records, export employee data, connect identity or LMS systems, or represent learning, skill, competency, certification, employment, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
