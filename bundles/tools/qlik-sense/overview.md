# Qlik Sense Overview

## Scope

- Data connections, load scripts, transformations, associative models, fields, dimensions, measures, master items, visualizations, sheets, stories, apps, alerts, subscriptions, and reports.
- Qlik Cloud and client-managed distinctions, spaces and streams, reloads, AutoML and AI-assisted analytics where available, application automation, REST and engine APIs, embedding, section access, roles, governance, monitoring, and migration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or reload production data, change scripts, models, metrics or section access, publish apps, schedule automation, train models, expose API keys, embed analytics, migrate deployments, or represent query, forecast, permission, security, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
