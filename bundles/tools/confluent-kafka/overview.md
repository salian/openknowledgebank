# Confluent Overview

## Scope

- Managed and self-managed data streaming based on Apache Kafka, plus connectors, Schema Registry, stream processing, and governance.
- Confluent Cloud and Platform clusters, topics, producers, consumers, Flink, networking, security, lineage, APIs, CLI, and operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect Cloud or Platform edition and version, organization and environment, clusters and regions, topics and partitions, schemas and compatibility, producers and consumers, connectors, Flink statements, data classifications, identities and ACLs, networking and encryption, quotas and cost, retention, replication, lineage, monitoring, and recovery state.

## Boundary

Stop before provision clusters, create or alter topics or schemas, ingest, consume or export data, deploy connectors or Flink jobs, change ACLs, networking or retention, delete data, or represent delivery or recovery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
