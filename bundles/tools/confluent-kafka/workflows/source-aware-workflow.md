# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect Cloud or Platform edition and version, organization and environment, clusters and regions, topics and partitions, schemas and compatibility, producers and consumers, connectors, Flink statements, data classifications, identities and ACLs, networking and encryption, quotas and cost, retention, replication, lineage, monitoring, and recovery state.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Confluent configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before provision clusters, create or alter topics or schemas, ingest, consume or export data, deploy connectors or Flink jobs, change ACLs, networking or retention, delete data, or represent delivery or recovery.
