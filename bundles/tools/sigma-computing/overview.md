# Sigma Computing Overview

## Scope

- Warehouse connections, datasets and data models, workbooks, tables, pivots, formulas, visualizations, dashboards, metrics, schedules, alerts, exports, embedding, and governed spreadsheet analysis.
- Input tables and writeback, data apps, actions, row and column security, OAuth and SSO, usage and audit logs, APIs, AI Query, formula assistant, Sigma Assistant and agents where available, model providers, token usage, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect warehouses, query, export or embed data, change models or metrics, write data through input tables, trigger actions, grant permissions, enable AI or agents, send data to model providers, or represent query, metric, forecast, writeback, security, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
