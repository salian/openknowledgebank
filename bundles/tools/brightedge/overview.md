# BrightEdge Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise SEO, AEO, keyword research, content optimization, site auditing, and search reporting.
- AI-search visibility, recommendations, automated optimization, integrations, governance, and executive reporting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, sites and markets, keyword and competitor sets, crawl scope, search-engine and AI-search data, metric definitions, attribution, recommendations, automation settings, permissions, integrations, changes, and reports.

## Boundary

Stop before change tracked sites or keywords, activate automated optimization, publish content changes, connect data sources, export data, or represent rankings, visibility, traffic, or revenue impact without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
