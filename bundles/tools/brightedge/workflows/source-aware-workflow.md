# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription, sites and markets, keyword and competitor sets, crawl scope, search-engine and AI-search data, metric definitions, attribution, recommendations, automation settings, permissions, integrations, changes, and reports.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the BrightEdge configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before change tracked sites or keywords, activate automated optimization, publish content changes, connect data sources, export data, or represent rankings, visibility, traffic, or revenue impact.
