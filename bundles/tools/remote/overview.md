# Remote Overview

## Scope

- Employer-of-record services, global payroll, contractor onboarding and management, employee and worker records, contracts, benefits, expenses, time off, payroll inputs, payments, and documents.
- Country and entity workflows, HR management, equity support where available, reports, integrations, API and webhooks, permissions, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled workforce operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before onboard or classify workers, create or change contracts, employment, payroll, benefits, expenses or leave, initiate payments, upload identity or bank data, call APIs, or make employment, tax, eligibility, payment, immigration, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
