# BambooHR Overview

## Scope

- Employee records, time off, benefits, payroll-adjacent, performance, hiring, and reporting workflows.
- APIs, webhooks, fields, permissions, integrations, and administrative operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, company and region, employee records, fields, legal and consent basis, retention, roles and permissions, time-off and benefit settings, payroll mappings, reports, API access, webhooks, integrations, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change employee records, compensation, time off, benefits, performance data, payroll exports, permissions, integrations, or represent an employment decision without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
