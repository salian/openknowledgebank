# Availity Overview

Source-aware guidance for Availity healthcare transactions, APIs, eligibility data, payer connectivity, validation, and controlled operations.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes organization, product, plan, payer, provider identifiers, API or transaction type, credentials, scopes, patient and subscriber data, request fields, response codes, mappings, consent, tests, monitoring, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Product access, payer connectivity, patient or subscriber identity, eligibility or claim facts, authorization, response meaning, transaction acceptance, coverage, and downstream outcomes. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
