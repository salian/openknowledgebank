# OneTrust Overview

## Scope

- Consent and preference management, cookie and tracker discovery, geolocation rules, receipts, data-subject requests, assessments, records, notices, incidents, and data mapping.
- Data discovery and classification, AI governance, third-party and technology risk, compliance automation, ethics, integrations, workflows, permissions, audit evidence, and reporting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before scan systems, collect or signal consent, block trackers, process rights requests, classify sensitive data, assess vendors or AI, change policies or controls, remediate findings, or represent legal compliance or certification without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
