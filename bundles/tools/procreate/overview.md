# Procreate Overview

## Scope

- Canvas creation, brushes, Brush Studio, layers, masks, blending, selections, transforms, color profiles, adjustments, text, drawing guides, gestures, and accessibility settings.
- Animation Assist, Page Assist and 3D painting where supported, time-lapse recording, file formats, imports, exports, backups, device and version limits, asset rights, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import protected assets, record or publish time-lapse, alter or flatten originals, export production artwork, use third-party brushes, overwrite files, or represent originality, color fidelity, rights, recovery, or delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
