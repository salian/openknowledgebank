# Runway Overview

## Scope

- Text, image and video conditioned generation through current models, image and video editing, motion and camera controls, audio or speech surfaces where available, assets, projects, and exports.
- Web application and developer API distinctions, model and feature lifecycle, prompts, API keys, tasks, webhooks where documented, credits, workspaces, permissions, asset rights, safety review, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload protected or personal media, generate likenesses or voices, call paid APIs, expose keys, spend credits, publish outputs, or represent consent, originality, realism, rights, safety, completion, or delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
