# SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting Overview

Source-aware guidance for recruiting configuration, candidate data, workflow, fairness controls, testing, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes tenant, release, modules, jurisdictions, requisition templates, candidate fields, statuses, permissions, workflows, integrations, communications, assessment rules, fairness evidence, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Tenant configuration, candidate facts, permissions, workflow outcomes, ranking validity, fairness, legal compliance, and employment authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
