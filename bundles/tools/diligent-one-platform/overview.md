# Diligent One Platform Overview

## Scope

- Board governance, entities, audit, risk, compliance, controls, investigations, reporting, and market intelligence.
- Users, permissions, evidence, issues, workflows, AI, integrations, secure documents, approvals, and oversight operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and modules, entities and boards, directors and conflicts, risks and controls, audits and tests, evidence, issues and investigations, policies and obligations, meeting materials, permissions, AI settings, integrations, reports, attestations, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before upload or distribute board materials, change entities, risks, controls, tests or issues, launch investigations, collect attestations, activate AI, alter permissions, or represent assurance or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
