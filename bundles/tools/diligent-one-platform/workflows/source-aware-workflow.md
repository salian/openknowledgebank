# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription and modules, entities and boards, directors and conflicts, risks and controls, audits and tests, evidence, issues and investigations, policies and obligations, meeting materials, permissions, AI settings, integrations, reports, attestations, and audit history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Diligent One Platform configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before upload or distribute board materials, change entities, risks, controls, tests or issues, launch investigations, collect attestations, activate AI, alter permissions, or represent assurance or compliance.
