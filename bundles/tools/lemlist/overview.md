# lemlist Overview

## Scope

- Lead database, email and phone finding, verification, enrichment, intent signals, email, LinkedIn, calls, WhatsApp and SMS sequences, and unified inbox.
- Personalization, Deliverability Hub, AI agents, Context Center, API, integrations, MCP, CLI, monitoring, evidence, and controlled outreach.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before source or enrich people, process personal data, connect mailboxes, send outreach, automate social or messaging actions, run AI agents, purchase data credits, or represent consent, deliverability, intent, or pipeline without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
