# Google Workspace Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware collaboration suite administration and content analysis and a reviewable google workspace administration and sharing brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering tenant, edition, and enabled services, users, groups, and organizational units, documents, files, sites, mail, and calendars, sharing and access settings, admin, security, retention, and audit configuration, and export and approval evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer file contents, sharing reach, user membership, mail delivery, retention behavior, and administrator access.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before sending messages, editing or sharing content, changing users or groups, changing admin or security settings, changing retention, or exporting data.
