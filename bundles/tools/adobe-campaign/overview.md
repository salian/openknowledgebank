# Adobe Campaign Overview

## Scope

- Cross-channel campaign orchestration.
- Profiles, segmentation, workflows, personalization, delivery, and reporting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect product generation and build, instance, profiles, schemas, consent, typologies, workflows, templates, deliveries, integrations, permissions, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import profiles, activate workflows, send communications, change personalization or suppression, deploy schemas, or represent consent or delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
