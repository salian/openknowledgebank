# Umami Overview

## Scope

- Website traffic, pages, referrers, devices, geography and other documented dimensions, custom events and properties, sessions, realtime views, goals, funnels, journeys, retention and reports where available.
- Cloud and self-hosted distinctions, tracker configuration, teams and permissions, share links, data import or export, REST API, database and deployment, privacy and consent configuration, upgrades, backup, validation, and controlled analytics use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before deploy tracking, collect identifiers or event properties, change retention or sharing, expose tokens, call APIs, import or export data, upgrade deployments, or represent consent, visitor identity, metric, attribution, privacy, availability, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
