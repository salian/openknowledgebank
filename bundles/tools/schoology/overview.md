# Schoology Learning Overview

## Scope

- Courses, sections, folders, resources, assignments, discussions, assessments, rubrics, submissions, grades, mastery and progress views, calendars, messaging, groups, and mobile access.
- District and institution administration, users and roles, SIS and content integrations, LTI and APIs where supported, analytics, accessibility, privacy, retention, audit evidence, validation, and controlled educational workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change student records, enroll users, publish content, collect submissions, grade or assess learners, send messages, connect SIS or applications, export data, or make educational, eligibility, disciplinary, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
