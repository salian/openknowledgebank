# Melio Overview

## Scope

- Vendor bills, payment scheduling, bank transfer and card funding, checks and electronic delivery, recurring payments, approval workflows, and payment tracking.
- Payment requests, invoices, accountant and team access, accounting-software synchronization, international payments where available, fees, limits, evidence, and reconciliation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect bank or card accounts, create vendors, schedule, approve, cancel or dispute payments, request funds, change accounting data, grant access, or represent payment, settlement, fee, tax, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
