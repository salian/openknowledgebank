# Clari Overview

## Scope

- Forecasting, pipeline inspection, account and opportunity workflows, activity capture, revenue analytics, and sales execution.
- CRM and communication integrations, AI insights, governance, reporting, permissions, and revenue operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and modules, users and roles, CRM objects and mappings, opportunity stages and amounts, forecast hierarchy and categories, activity and communication capture, data freshness, models and AI settings, targets, overrides, integrations, reports, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before connect CRM or communication systems, ingest or export customer data, change forecasts or opportunities, activate AI or automation, send communications, or represent pipeline, forecast, deal, or revenue outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
