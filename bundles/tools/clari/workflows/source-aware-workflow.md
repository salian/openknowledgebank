# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription and modules, users and roles, CRM objects and mappings, opportunity stages and amounts, forecast hierarchy and categories, activity and communication capture, data freshness, models and AI settings, targets, overrides, integrations, reports, and audit history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Clari configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before connect CRM or communication systems, ingest or export customer data, change forecasts or opportunities, activate AI or automation, send communications, or represent pipeline, forecast, deal, or revenue outcomes.
