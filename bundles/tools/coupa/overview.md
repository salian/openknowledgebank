# Coupa Overview

## Scope

- Procurement, sourcing, contracts, suppliers, expenses, invoicing, payments, treasury, and spend analytics.
- Accounts, entities, catalogs, approvals, integrations, AI, controls, reporting, and financial operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and modules, entities and currencies, users and roles, suppliers and bank details, catalogs and contracts, requisitions and purchase orders, invoices and expenses, approvals and policies, tax and accounting mappings, payment state, integrations, AI settings, audit logs, and controls.

## Boundary

Stop before onboard suppliers, change bank details, create or approve requisitions, purchase orders, invoices or expenses, initiate payments, alter controls, export financial data, or represent settlement or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
