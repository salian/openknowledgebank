# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription and modules, entities and currencies, users and roles, suppliers and bank details, catalogs and contracts, requisitions and purchase orders, invoices and expenses, approvals and policies, tax and accounting mappings, payment state, integrations, AI settings, audit logs, and controls.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Coupa configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before onboard suppliers, change bank details, create or approve requisitions, purchase orders, invoices or expenses, initiate payments, alter controls, export financial data, or represent settlement or compliance.
