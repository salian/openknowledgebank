# Bitrix24 Overview

## Scope

- CRM, tasks, chats, catalogs, users, calendars, files, and business workflows.
- REST and REST 3.0 APIs, webhooks, applications, permissions, batching, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect plan and region, REST version, portal, users and structure, CRM records, tasks, chats, files, products and stock, calendars, permissions, webhooks, application scopes, integrations, limits, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or update CRM or workplace records, send messages, change users or permissions, upload files, alter catalog or stock data, install apps, invoke webhooks, or represent completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
