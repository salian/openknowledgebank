# Insider One Overview

## Scope

- Customer data management, identity, segmentation, web and app personalization, journey orchestration, behavioral analytics, reporting, and integrations.
- Email, SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, web push, site search, conversational channels, predictive and generative AI, and autonomous agents.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before identify or segment people, collect behavior, personalize experiences, send messages, deploy journeys or agents, connect data, spend campaign funds, or represent attribution, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
