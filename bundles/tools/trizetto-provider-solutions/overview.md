# Cognizant TriZetto Clearinghouse Overview

## Scope

- Healthcare claims, remittance, eligibility, claim status, prior authorization, patient financial engagement, and clearinghouse workflows.
- EDI transactions, payer connections, practice integrations, acknowledgments, analytics, security, and revenue-cycle operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect contracted services and current product naming, organization and trading partners, patient and member identity, authorization and consent, payer enrollment and connections, claim and eligibility data, codes and formats, EDI mappings, acknowledgments and rejections, remittances and payments, integrations, certifications, security controls, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before access or transmit protected health information, verify eligibility, submit or alter claims, remittances or authorizations, change payer or EDI configuration, collect payments, or represent coverage, adjudication, payment, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
