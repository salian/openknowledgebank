# BILL Overview

## Scope

- Bills, invoices, vendors, customers, approvals, payments, receivables, and cash-flow workflows.
- Developer APIs, organizations, users, bank and payment data, integrations, and reconciliation support.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product and API version, organization, users and roles, vendors and customers, bills and invoices, bank and payment methods, approvals, accounting mappings, sync state, fraud controls, credentials, transactions, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or approve bills or invoices, add vendors or bank details, initiate or void payments, change roles or workflows, sync accounting records, or represent settlement without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
