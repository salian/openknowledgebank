# Yandex Direct Overview

## Scope

- Search and network advertising, campaign and ad-group structures, ads and creatives, keywords and targeting, audiences and retargeting, bids and budgets, strategies, schedules and moderation.
- Yandex Metrica goals and attribution, experiments and reporting where available, Direct API, account and regional scope, billing, policy and restricted-category controls, consent and privacy, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect customer or analytics data, create audiences, launch or change campaigns, bids or budgets, upload ads, call APIs, spend funds, or represent moderation, delivery, attribution, conversion, return, revenue, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
