# Automation Anywhere Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current source material and inspected local evidence. It does not prove local state, authorization, outcomes, or compliance.

## Scope

- Control Room, bot, dependency, credential, schedule, workload, audit, and deployment review

## Key Limitation

- Do not infer bot behavior, credential access, schedule state, queue result, production readiness, execution result, or business outcome.
