# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect workspace and plan, channels and inboxes, contacts and identity, consent and retention, conversations, knowledge sources and rights, AI-agent workflows and permissions, CRM fields, automations, users and roles, integrations, reports, and logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Crisp configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before connect channels, import customer data, send or automate messages, publish knowledge, train or deploy AI agents, change permissions, export conversations, or represent resolution.
