# Crisp Overview

## Scope

- Omnichannel inbox, chat widget, helpdesk, knowledge base, support CRM, analytics, and AI agents.
- Customer profiles, conversations, channels, automations, AI training sources, integrations, APIs, permissions, and support operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect workspace and plan, channels and inboxes, contacts and identity, consent and retention, conversations, knowledge sources and rights, AI-agent workflows and permissions, CRM fields, automations, users and roles, integrations, reports, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before connect channels, import customer data, send or automate messages, publish knowledge, train or deploy AI agents, change permissions, export conversations, or represent resolution without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
