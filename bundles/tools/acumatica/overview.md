# Acumatica Overview

## Scope

- Cloud ERP business workflows.
- Customization, APIs, integrations, reporting, and developer tooling.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect edition and build, tenant, companies and branches, modules, roles, workflows, customizations, endpoints, records, integrations, logs, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before change ERP configuration or records, deploy customizations, call production APIs, post transactions, expose credentials, or represent financial approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
