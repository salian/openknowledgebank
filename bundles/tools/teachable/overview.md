# Teachable Overview

## Scope

- Schools, courses, curriculum, lessons, media, quizzes, coaching and digital products where offered, enrollment, student progress, certificates, communities, pricing, coupons, affiliates and email.
- Checkout, payments, payouts, taxes and transaction fees by plan and region, domains, integrations, webhooks and APIs where available, roles, content rights, accessibility, privacy, analytics, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload or publish protected content, enroll or assess learners, issue certificates, set prices, collect payments, change payouts or taxes, contact students, call APIs, or represent learning, completion, certification, revenue, tax, rights, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
