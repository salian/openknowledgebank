# Adobe Workfront Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise work planning and intake.
- Projects, tasks, resources, proofs, approvals, reporting, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect instance, groups, portfolios, projects, templates, assignments, rates, forms, proofs, approvals, permissions, integrations, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before create or reassign work, change schedules or rates, approve proofs, alter permissions, integrate systems, or represent completion or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
