# Mailshake Overview

## Scope

- Cold-email campaigns, sequence scheduling, personalization, A/B testing, lead catcher, deliverability workflows, and campaign analytics.
- Phone and social outreach, AI-assisted writing, CRM and Zapier integrations, team controls, and API-based recipient and campaign operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or enrich contacts, send outreach, change lead status, use data credits, connect CRM systems, expose API keys, or represent consent, delivery, response, pipeline, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
