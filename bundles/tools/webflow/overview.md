# Webflow Overview

## Scope

- Visual site design, pages and components, styles and interactions, CMS collections and items, forms, assets, publishing, hosting, SEO, localization and ecommerce according to plan and compatibility.
- Data APIs, OAuth apps, webhooks, CLI and code components, Marketplace apps, Webflow Cloud and other developer surfaces where available, workspace permissions, domains, testing, rollback, rights, accessibility, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before publish sites, change domains or DNS, collect personal data, alter CMS or commerce records, install apps, deploy code, expose tokens, or represent content state, payment, localization, accessibility, security, traffic, conversion, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
