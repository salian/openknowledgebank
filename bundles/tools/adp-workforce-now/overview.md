# ADP Workforce Now Overview

Source-aware guidance for ADP Workforce Now workforce and payroll data, APIs, permissions, integrations, controls, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes client account, product generation, subscriptions, company codes, workers, employment and compensation fields, payroll, benefits, time, roles, API product and version, certificates, credentials, scopes, events, mappings, reconciliations, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Product and subscription state, worker identity, employment and payroll facts, effective access, masking, mapping validity, payroll or tax effect, reconciliation, legal compliance, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
