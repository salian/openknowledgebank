# Zoho Cliq Overview

## Scope

- Direct and group chats, channels, threads, files, audio and video calls, meetings, calendar and status, organization directory, guest or external collaboration where supported, search and administration.
- Bots, commands, message actions, widgets, functions, schedulers and databases on the Cliq platform, integrations and APIs, tokens and connections, roles, retention and eDiscovery dependencies, audit evidence, validation, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before provision or identify users, send messages, share files, record calls, create bots or commands, execute functions, connect systems, expose secrets, or represent identity, consent, delivery, attendance, bot result, confidentiality, retention, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
