# Odoo Accounting Overview

## Scope

- Customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes, payments, bank synchronization and statements, reconciliation, chart of accounts, journals, taxes, fiscal positions, and multicurrency.
- Assets, deferred revenue and expenses, budgets, analytic accounting, expense and sales integration, reports, closing, audit trail, localizations, access, imports, APIs, evidence, and controlled posting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create, post, reverse or reconcile accounting entries, connect banks, configure taxes or localizations, register payments, close periods, import data, change access, file or represent tax, financial, payment, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
