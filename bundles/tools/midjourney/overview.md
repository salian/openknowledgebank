# Midjourney Overview

## Scope

- Text-to-image generation, image prompts, style and character references where supported, parameters, variations, upscaling, editing, personalization, moodboards, and organization.
- Web and Discord workflows, image-to-video where available, plans and GPU usage, visibility modes, moderation, asset rights, provenance, safety review, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload protected or personal images, generate likenesses or sensitive media, use compute, publish assets, change visibility, automate access, or represent consent, originality, accuracy, rights, safety, or commercial suitability without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
