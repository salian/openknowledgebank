# Relume Overview

## Scope

- AI-assisted sitemaps, page sections, wireframes and copy drafts, component libraries, style guides, page planning, iteration, collaboration, and project organization.
- Figma and Webflow export workflows, React or code-related outputs where documented, component updates, assets, permissions, plan limits, evidence, accessibility and rights review, validation, and controlled handoff.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before generate or publish website copy, upload protected assets, export to Figma, Webflow or code, overwrite designs, use AI-generated claims, share projects, consume credits, or represent originality, accessibility, responsiveness, SEO, rights, or production readiness without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
