# Procore Overview

## Scope

- Projects, companies, directories, documents, drawings, specifications, RFIs, submittals, meetings, photos, observations, inspections, incidents, daily logs, schedules, and correspondence.
- Budgets, commitments, contracts, change events and orders, invoices, payments, bidding, workforce, timecards, analytics, marketplace integrations, REST APIs, OAuth, permissions, audit evidence, and controlled construction workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or alter project records, contracts, budgets, commitments, changes, invoices, inspections, incidents or timecards, share plans, authorize APIs, approve payments, or represent safety, schedule, cost, quality, contractual, or completion state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
