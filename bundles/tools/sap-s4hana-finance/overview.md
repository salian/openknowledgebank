# SAP S/4HANA Finance Overview

## Scope

- Universal Journal, general ledger, payables, receivables, asset accounting, management accounting, profitability, project and product costing, treasury, cash, credit, collections, tax, group reporting, close, and analytics.
- Cloud public edition, private edition and on-premise distinctions, Fiori apps, business roles, workflows, integrations, APIs, extensions, audit controls, localization, migration, testing, reconciliation, validation, and controlled financial operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post, reverse, settle, consolidate or close financial records, change ledgers, accounts, tax, valuations, allocations, credit or controls, execute payments, deploy extensions, migrate data, or represent balances, profit, tax, treasury, audit, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
