# ThoughtSpot Overview

## Scope

- Data connections, worksheets and semantic models, search-driven analytics, answers and visualizations, Liveboards, metrics, alerts, schedules, exports and sharing.
- Spotter and other AI capabilities where licensed, ThoughtSpot Embedded, SDKs and APIs, actions, cloud and software release distinctions, row and object security, permissions, usage evidence, validation, and controlled analytics publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or expose data, change models or metrics, publish or embed analytics, enable AI, trigger actions, grant access, call APIs, or represent query, metric, forecast, anomaly, permission, AI output, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
