# Tencent Meeting Overview

## Scope

- Online meetings and webinars, audio and video, screen sharing, chat, whiteboard, breakout and participation controls, invitations, registration, rooms, recordings, captions and transcription where available.
- Mainland-China Tencent Meeting and international VooV or regional distinctions, enterprise administration, identities and roles, storage and retention, integrations, APIs, security controls, consent, validation, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before schedule or host meetings, invite or identify participants, record or transcribe communications, share screens or files, grant host controls, call APIs, or represent attendance, consent, identity, recording, delivery, confidentiality, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
