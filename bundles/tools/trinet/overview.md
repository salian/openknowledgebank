# TriNet Overview

## Scope

- Employee and company records, onboarding, payroll processing and tax administration, benefits administration, time and attendance, expenses, documents, reporting and employee self-service according to the contracted service.
- Professional-employer-organization services and HR support distinctions, compliance and risk services, integrations, roles and permissions, data exchange, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled employment operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee, compensation, payroll, tax, benefit, time or expense data, hire or terminate workers, run payroll, initiate payments, change benefits, or make employment, classification, eligibility, tax, compliance, or legal decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
