# Salesforce Marketing Cloud Engagement Overview

## Scope

- Subscribers, lists, data extensions, contact model, segmentation, email, SMS and mobile channels, content, Journey Builder, Automation Studio, personalization, testing, deliverability, and analytics.
- Business units, Contact Builder, synchronized data, Data Cloud and other integrations where licensed, REST and SOAP APIs, installed packages, OAuth, consent and suppression, roles, audit evidence, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest or identify customer data, change consent or suppression, send messages, activate journeys or automations, connect Data Cloud or CRM, expose package credentials, call APIs, spend funds, or represent delivery, attribution, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
