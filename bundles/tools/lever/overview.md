# Lever Overview

## Scope

- Candidate and opportunity records, sourcing, applicant tracking, CRM, job postings, referrals, interviews, feedback, offers, nurturing, and automation.
- Permissions, integrations, APIs, data exports, analytics, audit evidence, privacy, validation, and controlled recruiting decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before collect or enrich candidate data, change applications, schedule interviews, submit feedback, issue offers, automate communications, export records, or make employment decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
