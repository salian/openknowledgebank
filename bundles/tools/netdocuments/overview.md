# NetDocuments Overview

## Scope

- Matter and client workspaces, document and email filing, profiles, search, versioning, check-in and check-out, sharing, collaboration, and Microsoft 365 integration.
- Retention, ethical walls and access controls where configured, audit trails, security, geographic services, APIs and integrations, ndMAX AI capabilities, governance, evidence, validation, and controlled legal work.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload, alter, share, move or delete client documents, change profiles, versions, retention or access, cross ethical walls, export audit data, enable AI, connect integrations, or represent privilege, record status, completeness, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
