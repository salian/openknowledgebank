# Lucidchart Overview

## Scope

- Flowcharts, process maps, org charts, ERDs, UML, architecture and network diagrams, Mermaid import, templates, shape libraries, layers, and revision history.
- Real-time collaboration, comments, data linking, conditional formatting, integrations, AI generation, permissions, evidence, validation, and controlled sharing.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or expose data, create authoritative diagrams, invite collaborators, change access, use AI generation, publish or export diagrams, overwrite revisions, or represent architecture, process, ownership, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
