# Synthesia Overview

## Scope

- AI video creation with scenes, scripts, templates, stock and custom avatars, voices, languages, media, captions, translation, collaboration, review and exports.
- Personal and studio avatar consent, voice and likeness controls, workspace sharing, brand assets, API and integrations where licensed, moderation, accessibility, provenance, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload protected or personal media, create or share likenesses or cloned voices, generate or publish videos, expose API keys, spend credits, or represent consent, identity, authenticity, rights, safety, accessibility, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
