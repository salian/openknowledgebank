# Microsoft Advertising Overview

## Scope

- Search, shopping, audience and multimedia advertising, accounts, campaigns, ad groups, keywords, ads, audiences, budgets, bids, experiments, and recommendations.
- Universal Event Tracking, conversions, product feeds, imports, automated bidding, reports, bulk operations, Campaign Management and Reporting APIs, OAuth, and audit evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change campaigns, ads, audiences, tracking tags, bids or budgets, upload customer or product data, authorize APIs, spend funds, or represent delivery, conversion, attribution, compliance, or return without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
