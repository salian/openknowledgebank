# Oracle Taleo Enterprise Edition Overview

## Scope

- Requisitions, candidates, submissions, screening, interviews, offers, career sections, sourcing, recruiting workflows, communications, user types, configuration, and reporting.
- Integrations, imports and exports, APIs or Connect Client surfaces where applicable, permissions, audit evidence, privacy, accessibility, release documentation, coexistence and migration review with Oracle Recruiting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before collect candidate data, create or alter requisitions or submissions, screen or rank applicants, schedule interviews, issue offers, change workflows or permissions, migrate data, or make employment decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
