# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription and region, connectors and credentials references, datasets and classifications, schemas and transformations, metric definitions, cards and dashboards, filters and time zones, users and permissions, workflows and alerts, AI models and agents, APIs, refresh state, costs, and audit logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Domo configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before connect sources, ingest, transform or export data, change metrics or permissions, activate workflows or AI agents, publish dashboards or apps, delete data, or represent analytics as validated.
