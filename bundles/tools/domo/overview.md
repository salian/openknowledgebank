# Domo Overview

## Scope

- Data integration, transformation, dashboards, analytics, apps, workflows, AI agents, and governed data operations.
- Connectors, datasets, semantic definitions, cards, alerts, permissions, APIs, models, reporting, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and region, connectors and credentials references, datasets and classifications, schemas and transformations, metric definitions, cards and dashboards, filters and time zones, users and permissions, workflows and alerts, AI models and agents, APIs, refresh state, costs, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before connect sources, ingest, transform or export data, change metrics or permissions, activate workflows or AI agents, publish dashboards or apps, delete data, or represent analytics as validated without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
