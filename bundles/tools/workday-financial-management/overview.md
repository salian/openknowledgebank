# Workday Financial Management Overview

## Scope

- General ledger and accounting, organizations and worktags, customers and suppliers, revenue, assets, expenses, procurement, projects, grants and other configured financial processes.
- Business processes and approvals, close and consolidation, reporting and analytics, planning and AI capabilities where licensed, banking and ERP integrations, roles and segregation, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled finance operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post or alter accounting, supplier, customer, asset, expense, procurement or project records, approve transactions, close periods, execute payments, enable AI, connect systems, or represent balances, revenue, expense, tax, forecast, audit, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
