# ChurnZero Overview

## Scope

- Customer health scoring, journeys, plays, onboarding, engagement, renewal forecasting, surveys, and reporting.
- Customer data, integrations, AI agents, automation, communications, account intelligence, and revenue operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, accounts and contacts, identity and consent, product-usage and CRM sources, health-score definitions and weights, segments, journeys and plays, AI-agent permissions, communications, renewal and revenue fields, integrations, reports, attribution, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest or export customer data, change health scores, activate journeys, plays or AI agents, send communications, update CRM or renewal records, or represent churn, retention, expansion, or revenue outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
