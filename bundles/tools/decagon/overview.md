# Decagon Overview

## Scope

- AI agents for customer support, conversations, knowledge, workflows, actions, analytics, and agent assistance.
- Channels, integrations, customer data, tools, policies, evaluation, monitoring, permissions, and deployment operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect contracted capabilities, environment, channels, customer identities and consent, knowledge sources and rights, system instructions, workflows and tools, action scopes, integrations, escalation policy, safety controls, evaluations, monitoring, conversations, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest customer data, deploy AI agents, send messages, call tools or APIs, change accounts or orders, issue refunds or credits, expose knowledge, or represent resolution without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
