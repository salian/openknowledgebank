# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect current Apple app and service availability, device and OS versions, account and subscription state, documents, collaborators, permissions, fonts, media rights, formulas, links, export format, and version history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Apple Pages, Numbers, and Keynote configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before share or publish documents, change collaborators, overwrite files, expose personal or confidential information, use licensed media, or represent calculations as validated.
