# Zoho CRM Overview

## Scope

- Leads, contacts, accounts, deals, activities, products, quotes and sales orders where configured, pipelines and forecasts, campaigns, reports and dashboards, collaboration and mobile workflows.
- Blueprint, workflows and other automation, Zia and AI capabilities where available, portals, marketplace integrations, REST APIs and webhooks, roles, territories and sharing, consent and privacy controls, audit evidence, validation, and controlled sales operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify people, send outreach, change leads, deals, values or forecasts, run automation, enable AI, connect systems, expose tokens, or represent consent, qualification, pipeline, forecast, attribution, revenue, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
