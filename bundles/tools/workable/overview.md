# Workable Overview

## Scope

- Jobs and requisitions, career pages, candidate sourcing and applications, pipelines, screening, interviews, scorecards, collaboration, communications, offers, reporting and recruiting automation.
- Employee records, onboarding and other HR capabilities where subscribed, AI assistance, marketplace integrations, APIs and webhooks, roles, consent, retention, bias and accessibility review, audit evidence, validation, and controlled employment workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post jobs, collect applicant or employee data, source, screen or rank candidates, schedule interviews, send messages, create offers, enable AI, connect HR systems, or make employment, eligibility, compensation, performance, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
