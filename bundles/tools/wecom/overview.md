# WeCom Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise directories, internal chats and groups, customer contacts and groups, meetings, calendars, documents, mail, attendance, approvals and other configured workplace applications.
- WeChat connectivity, third-party and custom applications, bots, webhooks and APIs, identities and roles, regional and language scope, data residency and retention, consent, security controls, audit evidence, validation, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before provision or identify users and customers, send messages, access contacts, run approvals, record meetings, install apps, expose secrets, call APIs, or represent identity, consent, attendance, approval, delivery, retention, confidentiality, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
