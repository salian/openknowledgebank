# Sage 50 Overview

## Scope

- Customers, vendors, invoices, bills, purchase orders, payments, bank reconciliation, chart of accounts, general ledger, sales tax, cash flow, budgets, inventory, job costing, and reports.
- Cloud and desktop product distinctions, multi-company and user plan limits, payroll and payment add-ons, integrations, audit trails, imports, backups, updates, migration, evidence reconciliation, and controlled accounting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create, post, reverse or reconcile transactions, connect banks, change accounts, taxes, inventory or payroll, issue payments, close periods, migrate data, restore backups, or represent balances, tax, payroll, inventory, financial statements, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
