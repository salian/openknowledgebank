# Archer Integrated Risk Management Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise and operational risk, risk and control catalogs, assessments, issues, remediation, third-party governance, audit, compliance, IT and security risk, business resilience, public-sector authorization, and reporting.
- Archer Platform applications, workflows, data feeds, roles, record permissions, calculations, dashboards, REST APIs and SaaS personal access tokens, integrations, quantitative Insight, RMIS AI where licensed, audit evidence, release and API lifecycle, validation, and controlled risk decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change risks, controls, assessments, findings, issues, exceptions, incidents, claims or loss data, accept risk, sign authorizations, enable AI, expose tokens, run data feeds, change permissions, or represent control effectiveness, residual risk, compliance, insurance, or authorization without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
