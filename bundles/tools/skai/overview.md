# Skai Overview

## Scope

- Cross-publisher campaign planning and activation, retail media, paid search, paid social, budget pacing, bulk editing, portfolios, optimization, reporting, measurement, and integrations.
- First- and third-party data connections, automation and algorithms, AI-assisted insights where available, permissions, publisher-specific constraints, audit evidence, validation, and controlled media operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect advertising accounts or customer data, create or change campaigns, audiences, bids or budgets, deploy automation, spend funds, enable AI, or represent delivery, attribution, incrementality, return, revenue, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
