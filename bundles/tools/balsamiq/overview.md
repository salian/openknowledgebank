# Balsamiq Overview

## Scope

- Low-fidelity wireframing, interface sketching, components, links, comments, and review.
- Projects, workspaces, collaboration, assets, exports, and design handoff.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect product and edition, workspace, project and ownership, collaborators and permissions, source requirements, components and assets, links, comments, version history, exports, accessibility review, and approval records.

## Boundary

Stop before create or overwrite wireframes, share projects, invite collaborators, publish links, export confidential designs, use third-party assets, or represent design approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
