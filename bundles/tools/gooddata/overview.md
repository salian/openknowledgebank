# GoodData Overview

## Scope

- Data sources, semantic models, metrics, attributes, visualizations, dashboards, search, embedded analytics, APIs, and SDKs.
- Workspace administration, permissions, governance, deployment, validation, and controlled analytical use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect deployment and edition, workspaces, data sources and lineage, semantic model, metric definitions, filters, permissions, dashboards, embeds, APIs, schedules, logs, validation, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or expose data, change models or metrics, publish dashboards, embed analytics, schedule exports, change access, or represent analytical or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
