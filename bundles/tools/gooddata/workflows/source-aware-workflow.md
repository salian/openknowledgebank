# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect deployment and edition, workspaces, data sources and lineage, semantic model, metric definitions, filters, permissions, dashboards, embeds, APIs, schedules, logs, validation, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the GoodData semantic analytics review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before connect or expose data, change models or metrics, publish dashboards, embed analytics, schedule exports, change access, or represent analytical or business results.
