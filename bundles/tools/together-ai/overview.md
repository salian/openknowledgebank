# Together AI Overview

## Scope

- Hosted access to supported open and other models through serverless and dedicated endpoints, chat and completion, embeddings, image generation and other documented modalities.
- Fine-tuning and training surfaces, model upload or deployment where available, batch and evaluation workflows, API keys, rate and context limits, usage and cost controls, data handling, observability, safety testing, validation, rollback, and controlled release.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload sensitive training or prompt data, expose keys, fine-tune or deploy models, generate content, spend funds, change endpoints, or represent model identity, privacy, safety, evaluation, accuracy, latency, cost, availability, or production readiness without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
