# Seamless.AI Overview

## Scope

- Person and company search, professional profiles, email and phone discovery where available, lists, bulk enrichment, company data, buyer intent and job changes where licensed, and browser workflows.
- CRM integrations, data enrichment, automation, API or developer surfaces where available, team permissions, credits, data provenance review, suppression and privacy handling, validation, and controlled sales or recruiting use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before search for or reveal personal contact data, enrich records, export lists, use buyer-intent or job-change signals, spend credits, synchronize CRM, contact individuals, or make sales, recruiting, eligibility, or identity decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
