# ServiceNow Integrated Risk Management Overview

## Scope

- Policy and compliance management, authority documents, citations, controls, control tests and indicators, risk registers and assessments, issues and remediation, audit management, third-party risk, operational resilience, privacy and ESG surfaces where licensed.
- ServiceNow platform workflows, tasks, approvals, roles, record access, integrations, APIs, continuous monitoring, dashboards, evidence, Audit and Risk agents or other AI where available, release scope, validation, and controlled risk decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change policies, controls, risks, assessments, findings, issues, exceptions or third-party records, accept risk, close remediation, enable AI, run indicators, expose credentials, or represent control effectiveness, residual risk, compliance, audit, resilience, or authorization without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
