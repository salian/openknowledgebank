# Sage Accounting Overview

## Scope

- Customers, suppliers, sales invoices, purchase bills, quotes, payments, bank feeds and reconciliation, cash flow, chart of accounts, journals, VAT and tax workflows, reports, and mobile access.
- Plan and country distinctions, AI productivity assistance where available, automation, accountant collaboration, integrations, APIs, imports, exports, permissions, evidence reconciliation, validation, and controlled accounting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create, post, reverse or reconcile accounting records, connect banks, change tax settings, submit or represent VAT data, issue invoices or payments, authorize APIs, or represent balances, cash flow, tax, financial statements, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
