# Rippling Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current source material and inspected local evidence. It does not prove local state, authorization, outcomes, or compliance.

## Scope

- workforce records, payroll, identity, device, spend, integration, and workflow review

## Key Limitation

- Do not infer worker status, pay result, identity or device state, workflow execution, authorization, compliance, or source-of-record status.
