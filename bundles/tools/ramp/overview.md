# Ramp Overview

## Scope

- Corporate cards, limits and controls, receipts, expenses, reimbursements, travel, bills, vendor payments, purchase requests, procurement, contracts, and spend programs.
- Accounting synchronization, coding and reconciliation, cash and treasury products where available, reporting, savings insights, integrations, APIs, webhooks, permissions, approvals, fraud controls, audit evidence, and controlled finance operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before issue or change cards, limits or spend policies, submit or approve expenses, connect banks or accounting systems, pay bills, move funds, create vendors, expose API credentials, or represent transaction, balance, savings, accounting, tax, payment, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
