# Wix Overview

## Scope

- Sites, pages, visual design, templates, CMS collections, forms, members, blogs, events, bookings, stores and other business solutions according to plan and region.
- Wix Studio and Editor distinctions, payments and commerce, marketing and analytics, automations, apps, Velo code, REST APIs and webhooks, domains and hosting, permissions, rights, accessibility, testing, rollback, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before publish sites, change domains, collect personal data, alter CMS or business records, process bookings or orders, connect payments, install apps, deploy code, or represent inventory, payment, fulfillment, accessibility, traffic, conversion, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
