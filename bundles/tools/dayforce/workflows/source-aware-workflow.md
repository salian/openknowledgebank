# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription, tenant and jurisdictions, worker identity and status, jobs and compensation, schedules and time records, pay rules and tax setup, benefits and eligibility, leave, recruiting and talent data, policies, approvals, integrations, AI settings, permissions, payroll results, and audit logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Dayforce configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before hire or terminate workers, change personal, job, compensation, time, benefits or tax data, run payroll, approve time or leave, schedule workers, export employee data, or represent legal compliance.
