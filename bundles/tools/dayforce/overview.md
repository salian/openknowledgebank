# Dayforce Overview

## Scope

- HR, payroll, time, attendance, scheduling, benefits, talent, recruiting, and workforce analytics.
- Employees, jobs, compensation, tax, policies, workflows, integrations, permissions, AI, and reporting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, tenant and jurisdictions, worker identity and status, jobs and compensation, schedules and time records, pay rules and tax setup, benefits and eligibility, leave, recruiting and talent data, policies, approvals, integrations, AI settings, permissions, payroll results, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before hire or terminate workers, change personal, job, compensation, time, benefits or tax data, run payroll, approve time or leave, schedule workers, export employee data, or represent legal compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
