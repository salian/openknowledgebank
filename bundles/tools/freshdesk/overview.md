# Freshdesk Overview

## Scope

- email, chat, voice, messaging and social support, tickets, cases, assignments, SLAs, knowledge base, portals, automation, AI agents and copilots, integrations, and analytics
- Administration, permissions, integrations, configuration, audit evidence, validation, and controlled operational use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, edition and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect channels, ingest contacts, send replies, deploy AI agents, change routing or SLAs, publish knowledge, export data, or represent resolution or satisfaction without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
