# Agiloft Overview

## Scope

- Contract lifecycle management and repository workflows.
- Intake, templates, clauses, approvals, obligations, integrations, analytics, and AI-assisted review.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect tenant version, contract records, templates, clause library, workflows, permissions, obligations, integrations, AI settings, audit history, and legal approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before create or amend contracts, change clauses or workflows, approve terms, expose privileged records, activate AI processing, or represent legal approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
