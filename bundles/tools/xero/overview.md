# Xero Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware cloud accounting records and reporting analysis and a reviewable xero accounting and reconciliation brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering organization, tenant, region, and accounting period, chart of accounts and tax rates, contacts, invoices, bills, payments, bank transactions, and journals, report definition, filters, and as-of date, lock dates and approval workflow, and API connection and granted scopes.
- Unknowns: do not infer ledger accuracy, payment status, bank match, tax treatment, report completeness, and scope authorization.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before posting or updating transactions, recording payments, reconciling bank items, changing tax or lock dates, changing scopes, or exporting financial data.
