# Brevo Overview

## Scope

- Email, SMS, WhatsApp, transactional messaging, contacts, campaigns, automation, CRM, and commerce workflows.
- APIs, webhooks, templates, domains, senders, consent, suppression, events, and reporting.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and region, plan and API version, credentials, contacts and attributes, lawful basis, consent and suppression, lists and segments, senders and domains, templates, campaigns and automations, transactional events, webhooks, integrations, reports, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import or update contacts, change consent or suppression, authenticate domains, send messages, activate campaigns or automations, use credentials, update CRM records, or represent delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
