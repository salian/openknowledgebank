# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect subscription and catalog, organization and region, learners and employment status, groups, assignments, enrollments, content and credential requirements, assessment and completion records, accessibility, integrations, permissions, retention, and reports.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Coursera for Business configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before invite or enroll learners, assign courses, change learning records, export employee data, connect HR systems, issue or represent credentials, or represent skills or compliance completion.
