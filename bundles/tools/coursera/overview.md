# Coursera for Business Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise learning, course and credential access, academies, skills development, assignments, and analytics.
- Learners, groups, content catalogs, licenses, integrations, completion, assessment, reporting, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription and catalog, organization and region, learners and employment status, groups, assignments, enrollments, content and credential requirements, assessment and completion records, accessibility, integrations, permissions, retention, and reports.

## Boundary

Stop before invite or enroll learners, assign courses, change learning records, export employee data, connect HR systems, issue or represent credentials, or represent skills or compliance completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
