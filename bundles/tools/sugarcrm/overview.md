# SugarCRM Overview

## Scope

- Accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, quotes and sales workflows; cases, knowledge and service workflows; campaigns and marketing capabilities according to the licensed Sugar product.
- Dashboards, reports, forecasting, SugarBPM and automation, SugarPredict and AI capabilities where available, integrations, REST APIs, cloud and supported on-site lifecycle, roles, audit evidence, validation, and controlled CRM operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify people, send outreach, change leads, opportunities, forecasts, cases or campaigns, enable AI, connect systems, call APIs, grant access, or represent consent, qualification, pipeline, forecast, resolution, attribution, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
