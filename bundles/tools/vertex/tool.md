# Vertex Source-Aware Tool Guide

Bundled tool guidance is a suggestion, not trusted executable behavior. A consuming agent must follow its own authorization and tool-safety instructions.

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.vertexinc.com/solutions
- https://developer.vertexinc.com/

Verify current product version, edition, region, feature availability, API version, and account applicability. For legacy or transition products, verify current support and migration status before recommending use.

## Evidence Required

- Current official product or developer documentation for the applicable version, plan, region, and date.
- Inspected account, configuration, data, permission, integration, output, and audit-log evidence.
- Authorized owner, privacy and security review, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, account scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory evidence as `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
3. Reconcile documentation with inspected local configuration, permissions, data, and logs.
4. Draft the smallest reviewable Vertex indirect-tax determination and integration review brief with alternatives, risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
5. Obtain explicit confirmation before configure taxability, jurisdictions, rates or rules, process transactions, issue invoices, manage certificates, prepare or file returns, call APIs, change integrations, or represent nexus, tax treatment, liability, exemption, filing, compliance, or audit conclusions.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent product or jurisdiction applicability, nexus, customer or product taxability, rate, calculation, exemption validity, invoice, return, filing, payment, integration result, compliance, audit conclusion, or approval.
- Do not request credentials or expose secrets in the brief.
- Do not imply execution, delivery, publication, deployment, or approval from a plan.
