# Vertex Overview

## Scope

- Sales, use, value-added and other supported indirect-tax calculation and determination, rates and rules content, jurisdiction mapping, exemption-certificate and returns or compliance workflows according to licensed products and regions.
- ERP, commerce and procurement integrations, APIs and batch interfaces, cloud and deployed-product distinctions, tax data and content updates, e-invoicing capabilities where licensed, roles, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled tax operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before configure taxability, jurisdictions, rates or rules, process transactions, issue invoices, manage certificates, prepare or file returns, call APIs, change integrations, or represent nexus, tax treatment, liability, exemption, filing, compliance, or audit conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
