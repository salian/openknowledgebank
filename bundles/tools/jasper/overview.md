# Jasper Overview

## Scope

- Marketing content generation, chat, documents, Brand Voice, style guides, knowledge assets, campaigns, templates, agents, and collaboration.
- Workspace controls, permissions, content governance, usage reporting, integrations, source evidence, human review, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload proprietary content, create brand profiles, generate or publish claims, deploy agents, crawl sources, share outputs, change workspace access, or represent originality, accuracy, brand compliance, or performance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
