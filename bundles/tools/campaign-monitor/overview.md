# Campaign Monitor Overview

## Scope

- Email campaigns, lists, subscribers, segments, templates, journeys, transactional messaging, and reporting.
- APIs, webhooks, clients, senders, domains, consent, suppression, imports, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and client, API version and credentials, subscriber provenance, consent and suppression, lists and segments, sender and domain authentication, templates and content approval, campaigns and journeys, schedules, transactional events, webhooks, reports, attribution, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import or update subscribers, change consent or suppression, authenticate senders, create, schedule or send campaigns, activate journeys, use credentials, or represent delivery or conversion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
