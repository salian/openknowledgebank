# Kling AI Overview

## Scope

- Text and image conditioned video generation, image generation, sound generation, effects, motion control, storyboards, multimodal Omni workflows, and element libraries.
- Creative Studio, API access, model and feature lifecycle, prompts, assets, rights, safety review, output validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload people or protected assets, generate likenesses or media, synthesize voices or sound, use API credits, publish outputs, make commercial claims, or represent consent, originality, realism, or safety without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
