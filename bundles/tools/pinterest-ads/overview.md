# Pinterest Ads Overview

## Scope

- Ad accounts, campaigns, ad groups, promoted Pins, objectives, targeting, audiences, budgets, bids, catalogs and shopping ads, conversion tracking, experiments where available, and reports.
- Ads API v5, OAuth, bulk operations, product feeds, Conversions API and tag surfaces where documented, permissions, rate limits, policy review, evidence, validation, and controlled spend.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change campaigns, ads, audiences, catalogs, tracking, bids or budgets, upload customer or product data, authorize APIs, spend funds, or represent approval, delivery, conversion, attribution, or return without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
