# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect company and modules, jurisdictions and sites, workers and employment, roles and compensation, time and absence, payroll and benefits, hiring and onboarding, performance and learning, documents and workflows, integrations, permissions, reports, logs, validation, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Bob HR configuration and workforce process review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or alter worker records, hire or terminate, change compensation, time, leave, benefits or payroll, issue documents, run reviews, export data, or represent employment or compliance state.
