# Bob by HiBob Overview

## Scope

- Core HR, employee records, time and attendance, time off, payroll, hiring, onboarding, performance, compensation, learning, engagement, and planning.
- Workflows, documents, permissions, integrations, reporting, privacy, audit evidence, validation, and controlled HR operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect company and modules, jurisdictions and sites, workers and employment, roles and compensation, time and absence, payroll and benefits, hiring and onboarding, performance and learning, documents and workflows, integrations, permissions, reports, logs, validation, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before create or alter worker records, hire or terminate, change compensation, time, leave, benefits or payroll, issue documents, run reviews, export data, or represent employment or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
