# Clio Overview

Source-aware guidance for Clio matters, contacts, documents, billing, APIs, permissions, confidentiality, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes region, account, subscription, API version, users, roles, matters, contacts, documents, communications, calendars, activities, bills, trust data, custom fields, OAuth scopes, retention, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Region and account state, client identity, matter facts, privilege and confidentiality, effective access, billing and trust values, deadlines, legal effect, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
