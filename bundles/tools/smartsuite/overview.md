# SmartSuite Overview

## Scope

- Solutions and applications, tables, records, relationships, fields, views, forms, dashboards, comments, files, templates, permissions, notifications, and collaborative work management.
- Automations, integrations, webhooks and APIs, AI features where available, workspace administration, data import and export, limits, audit evidence, validation, rollback, and controlled operational workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or alter operational records, schemas, permissions or automations, import or export data, connect systems, enable AI, call APIs, or represent record state, calculation, notification, approval, project status, or completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
