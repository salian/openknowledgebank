# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect company and plan, jurisdictions, workers and classification, employment terms, pay rates and hours, deductions and benefits, taxes and filings, bank and payments, integrations, permissions, logs, reconciliations, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Gusto payroll and HR operations review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before hire or terminate workers, classify employment, change compensation or benefits, run payroll, debit accounts, pay workers or contractors, file taxes, sign documents, export data, or represent compliance.
