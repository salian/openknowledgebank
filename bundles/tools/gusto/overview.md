# Gusto Overview

## Scope

- Employee and contractor payroll, tax filing, payments, benefits, time and PTO, hiring, onboarding, documents, performance, and reporting.
- Embedded Payroll API, company administration, permissions, accounting integrations, evidence, validation, and controlled processing.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect company and plan, jurisdictions, workers and classification, employment terms, pay rates and hours, deductions and benefits, taxes and filings, bank and payments, integrations, permissions, logs, reconciliations, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before hire or terminate workers, classify employment, change compensation or benefits, run payroll, debit accounts, pay workers or contractors, file taxes, sign documents, export data, or represent compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
