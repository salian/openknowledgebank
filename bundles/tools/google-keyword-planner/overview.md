# Google Ads Keyword Planner Overview

## Scope

- Keyword discovery, historical search data, location, language and network filters, bid estimates, budget planning, and forecasts.
- Google Ads account access, campaign assumptions, export review, evidence reconciliation, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and access, billing eligibility, seed terms and URLs, match assumptions, market, location, language, network, date range, historical export, forecast inputs, policies, budget, measurement plan, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change campaigns, set bids or budgets, target sensitive audiences, publish forecasts as commitments, spend funds, or represent traffic, conversion, cost, or return without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
