# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and access, billing eligibility, seed terms and URLs, match assumptions, market, location, language, network, date range, historical export, forecast inputs, policies, budget, measurement plan, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Google Ads keyword research and forecast review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or change campaigns, set bids or budgets, target sensitive audiences, publish forecasts as commitments, spend funds, or represent traffic, conversion, cost, or return.
