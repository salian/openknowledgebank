# RingCentral Overview

## Scope

- Business phone, numbers, extensions, calling, SMS and MMS, team messaging, video meetings, fax, voicemail, routing, recordings, administration, and analytics.
- Contact center products, AI transcription and summaries where available, bots and integrations, REST APIs, SDKs, webhooks and subscriptions, OAuth, permissions, emergency services, compliance recording, evidence, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before place or route calls, send messages, record or transcribe communications, change numbers or emergency addresses, access voicemail, enable AI, expose OAuth credentials, call APIs, or represent consent, delivery, identity, emergency, quality, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
