# Bloomreach Engagement Overview

## Scope

- Customer data collection, profiles, analytics, predictions, recommendations, campaigns, and channel automation.
- Accounts, projects, environments, imports, APIs, SDK tracking, identity unification, consent, activation, and exports.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current Bloomreach Marketing and Engagement naming, account, tenant type and region, projects and environments, customer identifiers, event schemas, consent, tracking, imports, identity merge rules, segments, campaigns, channels, predictions, recommendations, exports, RBAC, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before collect or import customer data, merge identities, activate segments, send messages, retarget users, change tracking or consent, export profiles, or represent predictions or attribution without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
