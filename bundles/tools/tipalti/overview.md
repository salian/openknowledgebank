# Tipalti Overview

## Scope

- Supplier onboarding and identity or payment details, tax-form collection and validation surfaces, invoice capture and coding, approval workflows, purchase orders, expenses, payment methods and reconciliation.
- Multi-entity and multi-currency operations, ERP integrations, payment APIs and reporting where applicable, sanctions or fraud controls, roles and segregation, audit evidence, regional and product scope, validation, exception handling, and controlled finance operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before onboard or verify payees, collect tax or bank data, approve invoices or expenses, initiate payments, alter procurement or accounting records, connect ERP systems, or represent identity, tax, fraud, approval, payment, reconciliation, compliance, or savings without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
