# Snapchat Ads Overview

## Scope

- Campaigns, ad squads and ads, objectives, placements, creative formats, audiences, budgets, bids, schedules, delivery, optimization, reporting, experiments, catalogs and commerce surfaces where available.
- Snap Pixel, Conversions API and measurement integrations, custom and lookalike audiences, account roles, billing, platform policy, regional and age constraints, privacy, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect customer data, create audiences, launch or alter campaigns, upload creatives, deploy tracking, change bids or budgets, spend funds, or represent consent, delivery, attribution, lift, conversion, return, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
