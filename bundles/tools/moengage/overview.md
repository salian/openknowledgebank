# MoEngage Overview

## Scope

- Customer profiles, events, segmentation, email, SMS, push, in-app, web and messaging channels, campaigns, journeys, personalization, recommendations, and experiments.
- Behavior analytics, funnels, cohorts, retention, attribution, AI-assisted creation and decisioning, APIs, SDKs, connectors, consent, permissions, audit evidence, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest or identify customer data, change consent or segments, send messages, activate journeys, run experiments, enable AI decisions, connect destinations, spend funds, or represent delivery, conversion, attribution, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
