# Paylocity Overview

## Scope

- Payroll, tax services, employee records, onboarding, time and labor, scheduling, benefits, compensation, recruiting, performance, learning, expenses, surveys, communication, and self-service.
- Workflows, analytics, reports, marketplace integrations, APIs, permissions, mobile access, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled workforce operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee, candidate, pay, tax, time, benefit or performance data, run or approve payroll, schedule workers, reimburse expenses, call APIs, change access, or make employment, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
