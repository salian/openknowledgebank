# eClinicalWorks Overview

## Scope

- EHR, clinical documentation, scheduling, practice management, patient engagement, revenue cycle, and interoperability.
- Patient records, orders, results, medications, referrals, interfaces, exchange networks, APIs, permissions, billing, and clinical workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect licensed modules and version, organization and facilities, clinician credentials and assignments, patient identity and consent, encounters and documentation, medications and allergies, orders and results, codes and problem lists, referrals, schedules, interfaces and exchange partners, payer and billing data, roles, audit logs, downtime and recovery procedures, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change patient records, place or sign orders, prescribe, release results, exchange health data, submit claims, schedule care, change permissions or interfaces, or represent clinical or payment outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
