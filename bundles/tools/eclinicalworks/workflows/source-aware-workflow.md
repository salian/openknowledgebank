# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect licensed modules and version, organization and facilities, clinician credentials and assignments, patient identity and consent, encounters and documentation, medications and allergies, orders and results, codes and problem lists, referrals, schedules, interfaces and exchange partners, payer and billing data, roles, audit logs, downtime and recovery procedures, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the eClinicalWorks configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before access or change patient records, place or sign orders, prescribe, release results, exchange health data, submit claims, schedule care, change permissions or interfaces, or represent clinical or payment outcomes.
