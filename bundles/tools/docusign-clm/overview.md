# Docusign CLM Overview

## Scope

- Contract generation, review, negotiation, routing, approval, repository, obligations, renewals, analytics, and AI.
- Templates, clauses, playbooks, workflows, integrations, permissions, agreement data, signatures, reporting, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect product and modules, contract parties and authority, source and negotiated versions, templates and clause library, playbook and policies, redlines and comments, extracted terms and AI outputs, obligations and dates, pricing, approvals, signature handoff, access controls, retention, integrations, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before generate, modify, approve, send or execute contracts, accept AI clauses or redlines, change playbooks or workflows, expose privileged information, trigger signature, or represent enforceability without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
