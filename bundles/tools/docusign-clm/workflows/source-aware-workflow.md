# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect product and modules, contract parties and authority, source and negotiated versions, templates and clause library, playbook and policies, redlines and comments, extracted terms and AI outputs, obligations and dates, pricing, approvals, signature handoff, access controls, retention, integrations, and audit history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Docusign CLM configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before generate, modify, approve, send or execute contracts, accept AI clauses or redlines, change playbooks or workflows, expose privileged information, trigger signature, or represent enforceability.
