# PandaDoc Overview

## Scope

- Documents, templates, variables, pricing tables, content blocks, recipients, roles, approvals, comments, negotiation, electronic signatures, payments, forms, rooms, and analytics.
- Document generation and status APIs, embedded signing, webhooks, OAuth, CRM integrations, workspaces, permissions, audit trails, certificates, evidence reconciliation, validation, and controlled sending.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before generate, alter, send, approve, sign, void or delete documents, collect signatures or payments, expose tokens, activate webhooks, share confidential data, or represent contract formation, signer identity, payment, or legal effect without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
