# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect current product naming and migration status, subscription, modules, framework and control mappings, risks, tests, evidence, issues, owners, permissions, integrations, AI settings, and audit history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Optro (formerly AuditBoard) configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before change controls or risk records, request or upload evidence, execute or sign off tests, close issues, publish reports, alter permissions, or represent compliance.
