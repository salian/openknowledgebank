# Optro (formerly AuditBoard) Overview

## Scope

- Audit, risk, compliance, controls, and evidence-management workflows on the current Optro platform.
- Projects, issues, testing, reporting, integrations, access, and AI-assisted GRC operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product naming and migration status, subscription, modules, framework and control mappings, risks, tests, evidence, issues, owners, permissions, integrations, AI settings, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before change controls or risk records, request or upload evidence, execute or sign off tests, close issues, publish reports, alter permissions, or represent compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
