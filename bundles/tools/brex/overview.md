# Brex Overview

## Scope

- Cards, expenses, travel, reimbursements, budgets, vendors, payments, accounts, and financial workflows.
- Developer APIs, users, departments, locations, transactions, statements, webhooks, integrations, and administrative controls.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect Brex product and API version, organization, users and roles, departments and locations, cards and limits, budgets, policies, expenses and receipts, vendors, bank details, payments, accounts, transactions, statements, credentials, webhooks, integrations, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create users or cards, change limits or budgets, submit or approve expenses, add vendors or bank details, initiate payments, move funds, use credentials, or represent settlement without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
