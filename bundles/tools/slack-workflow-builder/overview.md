# Slack Workflow Builder Overview

## Scope

- No-code workflow creation with supported triggers, forms, messages, variables, conditional logic and connector steps, templates, collaborators, publishing, and activity review.
- Developer-built custom workflow steps and functions where supported, app installation and authorization, channel and workspace scope, plan availability, rate and execution constraints, testing, rollback, and controlled automation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before publish workflows, send messages, collect or disclose data, install apps, authorize connectors, invoke custom steps, change access, or represent trigger delivery, execution, notification, approval, or business completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
