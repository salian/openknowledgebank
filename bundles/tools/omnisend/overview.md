# Omnisend Overview

## Scope

- Contacts, tags, segments, forms, landing pages, email, SMS and push campaigns, automation workflows, templates, A/B tests, and product recommendations.
- Ecommerce integrations, events, attribution, reports, deliverability, consent, APIs, webhooks, account permissions, evidence, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify contacts, change consent or segments, send campaigns, activate automations, connect stores, use API keys, spend funds, or represent delivery, attribution, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
