# Oracle Eloqua Marketing Automation Overview

## Scope

- Contacts, accounts, custom objects, segments, campaigns, email, forms, landing pages, lead scoring, personalization, campaign canvases, programs, and analytics.
- CRM and AppCloud integrations, REST and Bulk APIs, imports, exports, web tracking, consent and preferences, deliverability, roles, audit evidence, validation, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify contacts, change consent or scores, activate campaigns or programs, send email, deploy tracking, connect CRM, call APIs, export data, spend funds, or represent delivery, attribution, pipeline, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
