# Kit Overview

## Scope

- Subscribers, custom fields, tags, segments, forms, landing pages, broadcasts, sequences, visual automations, recommendations, and creator commerce.
- App Store, REST API, OAuth, webhooks, plugins, MCP, permissions, integrations, evidence, validation, and controlled messaging.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify subscribers, change tags or consent, send broadcasts, enroll sequences, activate automations, sell products, grant OAuth access, deploy apps or MCP actions, or represent delivery or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
