# Vanta Overview

## Scope

- Framework and control mapping, automated and manual tests, evidence collection, policies, personnel and access workflows, risk registers, vendor or third-party risk, security questionnaires, trust centers and audit collaboration.
- Integrations and APIs, continuous monitoring, remediation workflows, AI capabilities where available, roles and permissions, scope and framework-version control, auditor independence, evidence freshness, exception review, validation, and controlled compliance operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect systems, collect employee or security evidence, change controls or tests, accept risks, close findings, publish trust claims, answer questionnaires, enable AI, grant access, or represent control effectiveness, certification, audit, compliance, or security without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
