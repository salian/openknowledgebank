# Sitecore XM Cloud Overview

## Scope

- Content items and templates, visual authoring in Pages, components, presentation, sites, languages, workflows, publishing, personalization and analytics surfaces where available.
- Headless delivery, Experience Edge, SDK and API integrations, search, deployment automation, environments, roles, permissions, content migration, testing, release scope, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before change content models, author or publish content, alter personalization, connect data or delivery services, deploy code, grant access, expose tokens, or represent content state, audience, experiment, publication, performance, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
