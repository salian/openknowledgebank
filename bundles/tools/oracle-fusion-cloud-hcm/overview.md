# Oracle Fusion Cloud HCM Overview

## Scope

- Global HR, workforce structures, person and employment records, journeys, recruiting, onboarding, talent, learning, performance, compensation, benefits, workforce planning, time, labor, absence, and payroll where licensed.
- Redwood experiences, embedded AI where available, analytics, roles and data security, approvals, audit, HCM Extracts, REST and bulk integrations, environments, testing, quarterly updates, and controlled HR operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee records, employment, pay, benefits, time, absence, performance, recruiting or compensation, run payroll, deploy integrations, enable AI, change roles, or make employment, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
