# SysAid Overview

## Scope

- Incident, request, problem, change and service-catalog workflows, self-service, knowledge, assets and discovery, automation, SLAs, reports, dashboards, notifications and administration.
- Copilot and agentic AI capabilities where available, AI chatbot and assistance, integrations, APIs, identity and email connections, permissions, audit evidence, release scope, validation, rollback, and controlled service operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access user or asset data, create or alter tickets, changes or approvals, run discovery or automation, enable AI agents, connect systems, expose credentials, or represent identity, priority, SLA, resolution, change success, security, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
