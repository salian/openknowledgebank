# Zammad Overview

## Scope

- Tickets, customers and organizations, groups and roles, email and supported messaging channels, tags, text modules, knowledge base, calendars, SLAs, reports and search.
- Triggers, schedulers and automation, integrations, REST API and webhooks, cloud and self-hosted distinctions, authentication, permissions, data import, backup and restore, upgrades, audit evidence, validation, rollback, and controlled support operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change customer data, send messages, route or close tickets, alter SLAs or automations, grant access, expose tokens, call APIs, upgrade or restore systems, or represent identity, priority, SLA, delivery, resolution, security, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
