# Oracle Analytics Cloud Overview

## Scope

- Data connections, datasets, preparation, semantic models, calculations, workbooks, visualizations, dashboards, pixel-perfect reporting where applicable, sharing, and scheduling.
- Augmented analytics, natural-language queries, machine learning, AI assistance, maps, mobile, embedding, REST APIs, roles, row-level security, usage tracking, backups, migration, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect production data, run queries, change models or metrics, train models, enable AI, share or embed content, grant access, schedule exports, migrate catalogs, or represent query, forecast, security, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
