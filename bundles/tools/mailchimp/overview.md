# Mailchimp Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware email marketing and audience operations analysis and a reviewable mailchimp campaign and audience brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering account and plan, audience, contact, consent, and subscription status, segments and tags, campaign, template, and content versions, journeys and automations, and domains, senders, reports, attribution, and integrations.
- Unknowns: do not infer consent, contact status, segment membership, delivery outcome, attribution, and domain authentication.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before sending or publishing campaigns, modifying contacts or consent, changing audiences or automations, changing domains, or committing spend.
