# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, store and integrations, customer identities and provenance, consent and suppression, events and orders, segments, forms, senders and domains, templates, campaigns and workflows, personalization, schedules, reports, revenue attribution, and logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Drip configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before import or update customers, connect stores, change consent, launch forms, campaigns or workflows, send messages, export personal data, or represent delivery, conversion, or revenue.
