# Drip Overview

## Scope

- Customer profiles, ecommerce events, segmentation, email, onsite messaging, workflows, personalization, and analytics.
- Store integrations, consent, suppression, forms, campaigns, automations, revenue attribution, reporting, and data operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, store and integrations, customer identities and provenance, consent and suppression, events and orders, segments, forms, senders and domains, templates, campaigns and workflows, personalization, schedules, reports, revenue attribution, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import or update customers, connect stores, change consent, launch forms, campaigns or workflows, send messages, export personal data, or represent delivery, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
