# Microsoft Viva Engage Overview

## Scope

- Communities, conversations, questions, polls, praise, announcements, storylines and stories, events, leadership and campaign experiences, Answers and analytics according to license and tenant configuration.
- Microsoft 365 and Teams integration, network and community administration, corporate communicators and delegates, external and multitenant participation where supported, retention and eDiscovery dependencies, roles, privacy, moderation, validation, and controlled employee communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create communities or campaigns, publish employee communications, identify or invite members, post as delegates, moderate content, export analytics, change admin roles, or represent identity, sentiment, endorsement, reach, engagement, employment, compliance, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
