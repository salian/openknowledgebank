# Lusha Overview

## Scope

- Verified business contacts and companies, search, lists, enrichment, buying and intent signals, browser extension, CRM integrations, and data workflows.
- API and AI-agent access, credit use, matching, field mapping, governance, privacy requests, evidence, validation, and controlled go-to-market use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before look up, enrich or export people, process personal data, infer buying intent, sync CRM records, grant API or agent access, purchase credits, launch outreach, or represent accuracy, consent, intent, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
