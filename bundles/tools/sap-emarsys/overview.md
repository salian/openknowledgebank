# SAP Emarsys Customer Engagement Overview

## Scope

- Contacts, fields, segments, email, SMS, mobile and web channels, campaigns, automation programs, templates, personalization, recommendations, loyalty, deliverability, testing, and analytics.
- Commerce and SAP integrations, data onboarding and activation, APIs, webhooks, permissions, consent and suppression, AI-assisted or predictive capabilities where available, evidence, validation, and controlled engagement.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest or identify customer data, change consent or segments, send messages, activate programs, publish personalization, enable AI, connect commerce systems, call APIs, spend funds, or represent delivery, prediction, attribution, conversion, loyalty, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
