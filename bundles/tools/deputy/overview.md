# Deputy Overview

## Scope

- Employee scheduling, timekeeping, attendance, HR, payroll, hiring, communication, tasks, and compliance workflows.
- Workers, locations, demand forecasts, pay and break rules, leave, permissions, integrations, AI, analytics, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and jurisdiction, worker identity and status, roles and locations, availability and qualifications, schedules and forecasts, time clocks and timesheets, pay, overtime and break rules, leave, payroll mappings, communications, AI settings, integrations, permissions, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before hire workers, create or publish schedules, change time, attendance, pay, leave or employee data, approve timesheets, run payroll, send communications, or represent compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
