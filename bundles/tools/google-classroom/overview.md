# Google Classroom Overview

## Scope

- Classes, classwork, assignments, rubrics, grading, feedback, rosters, guardian communication, originality reports, analytics, and APIs.
- Education tenant administration, SIS integration, permissions, privacy, accessibility, evidence, and validation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect tenant and edition, classes, rosters and roles, assignments and rubrics, submissions, grades and feedback, guardian settings, originality reports, integrations, permissions, logs, policies, validation, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before create classes, enroll or remove users, publish assignments, return submissions, change grades, contact guardians, run originality checks, connect an SIS, export student data, or represent learning outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
