# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect site and plan, theme and code, content and rights, members and consent, Stripe and email configuration, integrations, permissions, analytics, tests, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Ghost configuration and publishing review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before publish or delete content, change themes or code, import or export members, charge subscribers, send newsletters, connect services, or represent delivery or revenue.
