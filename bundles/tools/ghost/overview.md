# Ghost Overview

## Scope

- Publishing, themes, headless APIs, members, paid subscriptions, newsletters, analytics, and integrations.
- Administration, permissions, configuration, evidence, validation, and controlled operational use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect site and plan, theme and code, content and rights, members and consent, Stripe and email configuration, integrations, permissions, analytics, tests, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before publish or delete content, change themes or code, import or export members, charge subscribers, send newsletters, connect services, or represent delivery or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
