# Qualtrics XM Overview

## Scope

- Survey design, logic, quotas, translations, contact directories, email and link distributions, response collection, panels, incentives, dashboards, reports, text analysis, and statistical research tools where licensed.
- Customer, employee, product and brand experience products, workflows, integrations, REST APIs, webhooks, permissions, anonymization and confidentiality settings, data retention, audit evidence, sampling, validation, and controlled research.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before collect personal or sensitive responses, identify participants, distribute surveys, pay incentives, change anonymity or retention, trigger workflows, export data, call APIs, analyze employee feedback, or represent sample validity, sentiment, causality, employment, or business conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
