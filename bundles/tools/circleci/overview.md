# CircleCI Overview

## Scope

- Configuration-driven pipelines, workflows, jobs, executors, artifacts, caches, tests, and deployments.
- Projects, organizations, contexts, secrets, runners, orbs, APIs, webhooks, policies, insights, and administrative operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect CircleCI version and plan, organization and projects, repositories and revisions, configuration, orbs and dependencies, executors and runners, contexts and secret references, permissions, caches and artifacts, test results, deploy targets, policies, webhooks, insights, audit logs, and rollback state.

## Boundary

Stop before connect repositories, modify configuration or contexts, expose secrets, approve, rerun or cancel workflows, deploy artifacts, change runners or permissions, or represent build, test, security, or deployment success without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
