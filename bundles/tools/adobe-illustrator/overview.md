# Adobe Illustrator Overview

Source-aware guidance for Illustrator documents, vector production, assets, color, accessibility, automation, licensing, and controlled export.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes application version, operating system, source files, artboards, dimensions, color mode and profiles, fonts, linked assets, authorship, licenses, consent, layers, effects, accessibility criteria, export settings, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Document contents, asset and font rights, color accuracy, appearance, accessibility, metadata, export fidelity, compatibility, and release authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
