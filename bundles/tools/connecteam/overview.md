# Connecteam Overview

## Scope

- Employee scheduling, time tracking, communication, tasks, forms, training, documents, and workforce operations.
- Public API, users, jobs, shifts, time clocks, permissions, integrations, webhooks, and administrative workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, users and employment status, roles and permissions, jobs and locations, schedules and availability, time clocks and timesheets, pay rules and breaks, forms and documents, communications, training records, consent and retention, API scopes, integrations, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change employee records, schedules, shifts, time or attendance, send communications, assign tasks or training, publish documents, change permissions, export personal data, or represent payroll or employment outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
