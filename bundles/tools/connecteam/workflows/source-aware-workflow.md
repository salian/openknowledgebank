# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, users and employment status, roles and permissions, jobs and locations, schedules and availability, time clocks and timesheets, pay rules and breaks, forms and documents, communications, training records, consent and retention, API scopes, integrations, and audit logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Connecteam configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or change employee records, schedules, shifts, time or attendance, send communications, assign tasks or training, publish documents, change permissions, export personal data, or represent payroll or employment outcomes.
