# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account and plan, users and roles, channels, messages, files, permissions, integrations, retention, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Zoom Team Chat communication, integration, and governance review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create channels, invite or identify people, send or delete messages, share files, install apps or bots, call APIs, change retention, or represent delivery, identity, consent, confidentiality, retention, or compliance.
