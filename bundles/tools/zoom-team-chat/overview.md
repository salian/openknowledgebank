# Zoom Team Chat Overview

## Scope

- Direct and group messaging, public and private channels, files, search, presence, collaboration, notifications, and supported meeting or workflow connections.
- Administration, users and roles, retention and compliance settings, apps, bots, webhooks and APIs where available, permissions, privacy, security, validation, and controlled communications.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account and plan, users and roles, channels, messages, files, permissions, integrations, retention, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create channels, invite or identify people, send or delete messages, share files, install apps or bots, call APIs, change retention, or represent delivery, identity, consent, confidentiality, retention, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
