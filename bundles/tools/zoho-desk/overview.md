# Zoho Desk Overview

## Scope

- Tickets, contacts and accounts, departments, email and supported messaging channels, assignment and teams, statuses and SLAs, knowledge base, community, self-service, reports and dashboards.
- Workflows, macros, blueprints and other automation, Zia and AI capabilities where available, telephony and CRM integrations, extensions, REST API and webhooks, roles and data sharing, privacy, audit evidence, validation, and controlled support operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change customer records, send messages, route or close tickets, alter SLAs or automation, publish knowledge, enable AI, connect systems, call APIs, or represent identity, priority, SLA, sentiment, response, resolution, compliance, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
