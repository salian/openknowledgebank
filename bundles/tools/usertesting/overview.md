# UserTesting Overview

## Scope

- Moderated and unmoderated research, participant targeting and recruitment, tasks and questions, prototypes and experiences, recordings, transcripts, notes, clips, highlights, surveys, card sorting and tree testing where licensed.
- UserTesting and UserZoom product-surface distinctions, research repositories and analytics, AI-assisted summaries or insights where available, panel and bring-your-own-participant workflows, consent, sensitive-data handling, roles, validation, and controlled research.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before recruit or identify participants, collect recordings or sensitive responses, expose prototypes, offer incentives, enable AI analysis, share clips, or make product, employment, eligibility, accessibility, or demographic conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
