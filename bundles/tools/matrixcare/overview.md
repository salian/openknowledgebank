# MatrixCare Overview

## Scope

- Electronic health records, clinical and care workflows, medication and order documentation, scheduling, referrals, family engagement, and interoperability.
- Billing, revenue cycle, staffing, quality, analytics, integrations, access controls, audit evidence, validation, and regulated post-acute operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change patient records, document care, alter medication or orders, schedule services, exchange protected health information, submit claims, change billing, or make clinical, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
