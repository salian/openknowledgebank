# Gainsight Overview

Source-aware guidance for Gainsight customer data, health models, playbooks, integrations, automation, analytics, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes tenant, product and release, licenses, users, roles, customer records, objects, fields, health scores, rules, CTAs, playbooks, journeys, integrations, credentials, API versions, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Tenant and license state, customer facts, effective access, model validity, score meaning, rule and playbook outcomes, communications eligibility, attribution, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
