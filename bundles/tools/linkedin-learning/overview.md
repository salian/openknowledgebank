# LinkedIn Learning Overview

## Scope

- Enterprise learning content, collections, assignments, learner access, recommendations, custom content, administration, and activity reporting.
- Catalog sync, AICC and SSO launch URLs, xAPI completion statements, Reporting APIs, OAuth, LMS and LRS integrations, evidence, and validation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before provision learners, assign content, expose learner activity, configure SSO or OAuth, grant API keys, send xAPI statements, import or export records, or represent completion, skill, or qualification without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
