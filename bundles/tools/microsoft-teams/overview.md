# Microsoft Teams Overview

Source-aware guidance for Teams collaboration, meetings, apps, data access, governance, and controlled change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes tenant, licenses, policies, teams, channels, memberships, guests, meetings, apps, permissions, retention, sensitivity, data, integrations, audit evidence, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Tenant configuration, membership, permissions, message contents, policy enforcement, retention, recording consent, and downstream effects. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
