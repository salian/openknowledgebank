# Ortto Overview

## Scope

- Customer profiles, activities, attributes, audiences, forms, capture widgets, email and SMS, journey orchestration, personalization, templates, and experiments.
- Dashboards, reports, funnels, attribution, AI assistance, data sources and destinations, integrations, APIs, webhooks, consent, permissions, evidence, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest or identify customer data, change consent or audiences, send messages, activate journeys, enable AI, connect systems, export data, spend funds, or represent delivery, attribution, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
