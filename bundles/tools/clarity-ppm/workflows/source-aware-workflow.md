# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect current product name and release, tenant and modules, portfolios and investments, projects and tasks, resources and employment data, calendars and rates, budgets and actuals, benefits, risks and issues, workflows, permissions, integrations, reports, audit history, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Clarity by Broadcom configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or change investments, projects, staffing, time, budgets, forecasts, benefits, risks, workflows or permissions, post financial data, or represent delivery or value outcomes.
