# Clarity by Broadcom Overview

## Scope

- Strategic portfolio, investment, project, resource, financial, roadmap, and value-stream management.
- Plans, ideas, staffing, time, cost, benefits, risks, workflows, reports, integrations, and administrative controls.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product name and release, tenant and modules, portfolios and investments, projects and tasks, resources and employment data, calendars and rates, budgets and actuals, benefits, risks and issues, workflows, permissions, integrations, reports, audit history, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change investments, projects, staffing, time, budgets, forecasts, benefits, risks, workflows or permissions, post financial data, or represent delivery or value outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
