# GEP Quantum Intelligence Overview

## Scope

- spend analysis, savings, sourcing, contracts, suppliers, procure-to-pay, invoices, payments, supply chain, data fabric, agent orchestration, policies, guardrails, audit trails, integrations, and studio
- Administration, permissions, integrations, configuration, audit evidence, validation, and controlled operational use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, edition and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before ingest spend or supplier data, launch sourcing, recommend or award suppliers, change contracts or purchase orders, approve invoices or payments, deploy agents, or represent savings or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
