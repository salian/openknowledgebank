# HubSpot Service Hub Overview

## Scope

- Omnichannel help desk, tickets, routing, views, SLAs, knowledge base, customer portal, AI assistance and agents, feedback, customer success, and analytics.
- CRM data, properties, workflows, permissions, integrations, APIs, privacy, evidence, validation, and controlled support operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect portal and subscription, customers and CRM records, tickets and entitlements, channels and routing, SLAs, knowledge and portal, AI sources and outputs, workflows, integrations, permissions, reports, logs, tests, monitoring, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before reply to customers, change tickets or CRM records, publish knowledge, expose a portal, deploy AI, alter routing or SLAs, grant access, export data, or represent resolution, satisfaction, or retention without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
