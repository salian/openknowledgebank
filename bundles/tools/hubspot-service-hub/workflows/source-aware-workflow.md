# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect portal and subscription, customers and CRM records, tickets and entitlements, channels and routing, SLAs, knowledge and portal, AI sources and outputs, workflows, integrations, permissions, reports, logs, tests, monitoring, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the HubSpot Service Hub support and retention review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before reply to customers, change tickets or CRM records, publish knowledge, expose a portal, deploy AI, alter routing or SLAs, grant access, export data, or represent resolution, satisfaction, or retention.
