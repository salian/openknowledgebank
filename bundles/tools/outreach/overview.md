# Outreach Overview

Source-aware guidance for Outreach sequences, prospects, integrations, automation, messaging, compliance, and controlled activation.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes org, subscription, users, roles, mailboxes, prospects, accounts, sequences, steps, schedules, templates, opt-outs, territories, CRM mappings, OAuth scopes, API version, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Subscription and org state, prospect facts, contact eligibility, consent or opt-out status, effective permissions, sequence behavior, message approval, delivery, attribution, and downstream effects. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
