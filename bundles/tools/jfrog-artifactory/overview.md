# JFrog Artifactory Overview

## Scope

- Local, remote and virtual repositories, package formats, metadata, builds, proxy caching, promotion, federation, replication, and APIs.
- Permissions, identity, tokens, certificates, Xray integration, retention, backup, migration, audit evidence, validation, and rollback.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload, promote, copy, move or delete artifacts, change repositories or permissions, expose tokens, replicate or federate content, alter retention, restore backups, or represent provenance or security without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
