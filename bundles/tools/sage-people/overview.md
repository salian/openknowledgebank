# Sage People and Sage HR Overview

## Scope

- Employee profiles, organizational data, onboarding, documents, absence, time and attendance, scheduling, expenses, performance, goals, surveys, recruitment and applicant workflows, with product-specific scope.
- Salesforce-platform Sage People and small-business Sage HR distinctions, payroll integrations where available, reports, workflows, roles, APIs, webhooks, imports, audit evidence, privacy, validation, and controlled HR operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee or candidate data, attendance, leave, expenses, compensation, performance, applications or offers, connect payroll, call APIs, change roles, or make employment, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
