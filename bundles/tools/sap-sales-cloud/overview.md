# SAP Sales Cloud Overview

## Scope

- Accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, activities, pipelines, forecasts, territories, visits, guided selling, sales planning, analytics, and mobile workflows.
- Version 2 and legacy Cloud for Customer distinctions, Joule and AI features where available, SAP S/4HANA and other integrations, extensibility, APIs, roles, audit evidence, quarterly releases, validation, and controlled sales operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify contacts, change leads, opportunities, stages, values, territories or forecasts, enable AI, synchronize ERP or CRM, call APIs, change roles, or represent qualification, pipeline, forecast, order, incentive, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
