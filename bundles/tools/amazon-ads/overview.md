# Amazon Ads Overview

## Scope

- Amazon advertising campaign management.
- API-based campaign, targeting, budget, creative, and reporting workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect advertiser and marketplace, account permissions, API version, profiles, campaigns, targeting, budgets, creatives, product data, attribution settings, and logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or modify campaigns, change bids or budgets, upload audiences or creatives, use credentials, launch ads, or represent performance as validated without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
