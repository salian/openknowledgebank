# GoTo Meeting Overview

## Scope

- Meeting scheduling and hosting, audio, video, screen sharing, chat, drawing, remote control, breakout rooms, recording, and transcription.
- APIs, integrations, account administration, permissions, security, evidence, validation, and controlled collaboration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, organizers, meetings and invitations, participants and access, audio and video settings, recordings and transcripts, integrations, retention, logs, consent, accessibility, tests, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before schedule or start meetings, invite participants, grant remote control, record or transcribe, share recordings, change retention, connect apps, or represent attendance or consent without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
