# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, organizers, meetings and invitations, participants and access, audio and video settings, recordings and transcripts, integrations, retention, logs, consent, accessibility, tests, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the GoTo Meeting configuration and meeting-control review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before schedule or start meetings, invite participants, grant remote control, record or transcribe, share recordings, change retention, connect apps, or represent attendance or consent.
