# Intuit ProConnect Tax Overview

## Scope

- Online professional federal, state and local return preparation, client documents, data import, diagnostics, K-1 workflows, signatures, invoicing, and e-filing.
- QuickBooks integration, client onboarding, SmartReturn, Tax Advisor planning, user access, workflow status, evidence reconciliation, and controlled filing.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before enter or change taxpayer data, classify tax treatment, calculate or sign returns, transmit e-files, debit fees, issue advice, share documents, or represent filing, acceptance, payment, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
