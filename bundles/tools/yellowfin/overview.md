# Yellowfin Overview

## Scope

- Data-source connections, metadata views, reports, charts and dashboards, filters, broadcasts and alerts, stories and presentations, collaboration and governed self-service analytics.
- Signals and assisted analysis where available, embedded analytics, APIs and integrations, deployment and version distinctions, roles and row-level security, usage and audit evidence, migration, validation, and controlled analytics publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect or expose data, change views or metrics, publish or embed analytics, enable signals, schedule broadcasts, grant access, call APIs, or represent query, metric, anomaly, forecast, permission, delivery, or business results without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
