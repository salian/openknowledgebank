## Evidence Required

- Current official product or developer documentation for the applicable version, plan, region, and date.
- Inspected account, configuration, data, permission, integration, output, and audit-log evidence.
- Authorized owner, privacy and security review, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, account scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory evidence as `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
3. Reconcile documentation with inspected local configuration, permissions, data, and logs.
4. Draft the smallest reviewable Zoho Workplace collaboration, AI, and information-governance review brief with alternatives, risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.

Obtain explicit confirmation before provision users, send email or messages, share files, create or publish documents, record meetings, change retention, enable AI or third-party agents, migrate data, grant access, or represent identity, consent, delivery, document correctness, confidentiality, retention, compliance, or approval.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent edition or app applicability, user identity, message delivery, file or document state, formula accuracy, sharing permission, meeting or recording consent, AI output, retention or deletion, confidentiality, compliance, or approval.
- Do not request credentials or expose secrets in the brief.
- Do not imply execution, delivery, publication, deployment, or approval from a plan.
