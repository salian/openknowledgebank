# Zoho Workplace Overview

## Scope

- Zoho Mail and Calendar, Writer, Sheet and Show, WorkDrive, Cliq, Meeting and other applications according to Workplace edition, with email, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, files, chat and conferencing.
- Directory and identity, MFA and device controls, sharing and external collaboration, eDiscovery and retention features where licensed, Zia and AI across supported apps, third-party AI data flow, migration, integrations, roles, audit evidence, validation, and controlled collaboration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before provision users, send email or messages, share files, create or publish documents, record meetings, change retention, enable AI or third-party agents, migrate data, grant access, or represent identity, consent, delivery, document correctness, confidentiality, retention, compliance, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
