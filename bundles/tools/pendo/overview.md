# Pendo Overview

## Scope

- Product analytics, tagged features and pages, paths, funnels, retention, segments, dashboards, session replay, in-app guides, polls, NPS, feedback, roadmaps, and mobile analytics.
- Agent and AI capabilities where available, data and metadata, installation, APIs and integrations, visitor and account identification, permissions, privacy, consent, validation, and controlled publication.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before install tracking, identify visitors or accounts, record sessions, change masking or consent, publish guides or polls, collect feedback, enable AI, export data, or represent user intent, adoption, causality, satisfaction, or business impact without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
