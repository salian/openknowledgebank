# Oracle Health Overview

Source-aware guidance for Oracle Health EHR interoperability, FHIR apps, clinical data, authorization, validation, and governed implementation.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes organization, platform and release, environment, endpoint, supported FHIR resources, app registration, authorization scopes, patient and encounter context, clinical workflow, mappings, consent, tests, monitoring, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Platform and endpoint state, supported resources, patient identity, clinical facts, authorization, consent, mapping validity, app behavior, safety, and implementation approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
