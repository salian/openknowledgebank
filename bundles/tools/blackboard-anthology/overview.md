# Anthology Blackboard Overview

## Scope

- Anthology Learn and related API-backed teaching, course, content, roster, assessment, and integration workflows.
- REST applications, users, courses, memberships, grades, content, accessibility, and administrative operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current Anthology product and legacy Blackboard naming, deployment and version, institution, courses, users and roles, rosters, content, assessments, grades, accommodations, accessibility, application registration, scopes, integrations, retention, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change courses, rosters, content, assessments, grades, accommodations, users, applications, or permissions, expose student data, or represent academic outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
