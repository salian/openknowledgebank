# TallyPrime Overview

## Scope

- Ledgers, vouchers, receivables and payables, invoicing, inventory, orders, banking, reconciliation, cost centers, budgets, financial statements, GST and applicable tax workflows, and payroll where configured.
- TallyPrime release and edition scope versus legacy Tally.ERP 9, multi-company and multi-user operation, remote access, synchronization, import and export, extensions, backup and restore, security controls, audit evidence, validation, and controlled accounting operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post or alter transactions, invoices, inventory, payroll or tax records, reconcile banks, file returns, execute payments, import data, grant access, migrate companies, or represent balances, tax, payroll, stock, profit, filing, compliance, or audit conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
