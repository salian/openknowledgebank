# monday CRM Overview

## Scope

- Lead capture, contacts, accounts, deals, customizable pipelines, activities, email synchronization and tracking, templates, sequences, quotes, and documents.
- Automations, integrations, dashboards, goals, forecasting, team workload, permissions, connected boards, imports, exports, API access, evidence, and controlled sales operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify prospects, send or track email, change deal stages or values, automate assignments, create quotes, forecast revenue, grant access, call APIs, or represent consent, pipeline, forecast, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
