# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, mailboxes and channels, conversations and customers, workflows and tags, profiles, Beacon and Docs, AI sources and answers, Messages, integrations, roles, reports, logs, tests, monitoring, rollback, and approvals.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Help Scout support configuration and automation review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before reply to customers, change conversation state, edit profiles, publish Docs, deploy Beacon or Messages, enable AI Answers, change access, export data, or represent resolution or performance.
