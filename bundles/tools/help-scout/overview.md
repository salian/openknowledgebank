# Help Scout Overview

## Scope

- Shared inbox, conversations, workflows, tags, customer profiles, Beacon, Docs knowledge base, live chat, proactive Messages, AI Answers, and reports.
- Mailboxes, teams, permissions, integrations, APIs, privacy, evidence, validation, and controlled support operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, mailboxes and channels, conversations and customers, workflows and tags, profiles, Beacon and Docs, AI sources and answers, Messages, integrations, roles, reports, logs, tests, monitoring, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before reply to customers, change conversation state, edit profiles, publish Docs, deploy Beacon or Messages, enable AI Answers, change access, export data, or represent resolution or performance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
