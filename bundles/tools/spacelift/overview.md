# Spacelift Overview

## Scope

- Infrastructure-as-code stacks and runs, plans and applies, state management, modules, contexts, dependencies, drift detection, scheduling, private and managed workers, spaces, audit trails and notifications.
- Terraform, OpenTofu, Pulumi, CloudFormation, Kubernetes and other supported workflows, VCS and cloud integrations, OPA/Rego policies, approvals, access controls, APIs, secrets and OIDC, validation, rollback, and controlled infrastructure delivery.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before connect cloud or source-control accounts, expose credentials, plan or apply infrastructure, alter state, approve runs, reconcile drift, change policies, execute tasks, or represent plan accuracy, policy result, infrastructure state, security, availability, cost, or deployment success without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
