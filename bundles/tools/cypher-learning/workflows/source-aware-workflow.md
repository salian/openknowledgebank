# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect tenant and plan, organizations and audiences, learners and status, courses and content rights, assignments, assessments and completion, skills taxonomy, AI-agent knowledge and actions, ecommerce, integrations, accessibility, permissions, retention, and reports.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the CYPHER Learning configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or publish courses, enroll learners, score assessments, infer skills, deploy AI agents, sell training, change records or permissions, or represent qualification or compliance.
