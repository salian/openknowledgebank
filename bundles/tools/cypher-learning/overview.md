# CYPHER Learning Overview

## Scope

- Course creation, delivery, personalization, skills development, assessments, gamification, reporting, and extended-enterprise training.
- Learners, organizations, automation, ecommerce, AI agents, content, integrations, permissions, and administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect tenant and plan, organizations and audiences, learners and status, courses and content rights, assignments, assessments and completion, skills taxonomy, AI-agent knowledge and actions, ecommerce, integrations, accessibility, permissions, retention, and reports.

## Boundary

Stop before create or publish courses, enroll learners, score assessments, infer skills, deploy AI agents, sell training, change records or permissions, or represent qualification or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
