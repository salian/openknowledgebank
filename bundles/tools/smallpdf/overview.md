# Smallpdf Overview

## Scope

- PDF compression, conversion, merge, split, rotation, editing, organization, scanning, OCR, viewing, sharing, password and signature workflows across supported web, desktop and mobile surfaces.
- AI-assisted chat, summaries or questions where available, team and business controls, file transfer and retention behavior, encryption and security claims, plan limits, rights review, validation, and controlled document handling.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload confidential or personal documents, remove passwords, convert or alter records, request or apply signatures, share links, enable AI processing, or represent document authenticity, completeness, signature validity, confidentiality, compliance, or deletion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
