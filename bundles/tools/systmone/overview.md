# SystmOne Overview

## Scope

- Longitudinal health records and applicable primary, community, mental-health, social-care, urgent-care, hospital, pharmacy and other configured clinical or administrative modules.
- Appointments, documentation, prescribing and medicines, referrals, results, tasks, care coordination, reporting, patient-facing and interoperability surfaces where commissioned, role-based access, audit, validation, downtime and clinical-safety controls.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change health records, document clinical care, prescribe or administer medicines, book appointments, refer patients, share data, configure decision support, or make diagnosis, treatment, eligibility, safeguarding, or clinical-safety decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
