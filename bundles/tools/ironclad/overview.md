# Ironclad Overview

Source-aware guidance for Ironclad contract workflows, repository records, APIs, permissions, approvals, and governed change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes instance, subscription, environment, workflow schemas, template and workflow IDs, repository records, clauses, documents, entities, roles, permissions, OAuth scopes, webhooks, retention, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Subscription and environment state, contract contents, privilege, clause meaning, effective permissions, approval or signature authority, workflow outcome, retention, and legal effect. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
