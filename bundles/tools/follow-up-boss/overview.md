# Follow Up Boss Overview

## Scope

- Lead-source intake, assignment, routing, stages, action plans, tasks, calls, texts, email, reporting, and edition-dependent team workflows.
- Account discovery, role and permission review, consent and suppression controls, integration mapping, deduplication, migration, attribution validation, testing, rollback, and release evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account edition and lifecycle, business and team structure, lead sources and ownership rules, consent opt-out and do-not-call evidence, users roles and permissions, routing stages action plans and communication settings, integrations API fields and mappings, duplicate merge migration and attribution rules, privacy retention audit tests rollback approvals and release authority.

## Boundary

Stop before access an account, create alter route merge import export or delete lead records, call text or email people, change roles workflows or integrations, attribute revenue, or represent consent lead status conversion compliance or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
