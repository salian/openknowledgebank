# Papaya Global Overview

## Scope

- Global payroll, employer-of-record services, contractor management, workforce payments, onboarding, employee and worker records, benefits support, invoices, funding, and payment tracking.
- Country and entity workflows, compliance support, reports, analytics, integrations, permissions, document handling, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled payroll operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before onboard or classify workers, change employment or payroll data, calculate or fund payroll, initiate payments, manage benefits, upload identity or bank data, approve invoices, or make employment, tax, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
