# Reply.io Overview

## Scope

- Prospect lists, segmentation, multichannel sequences, email, LinkedIn tasks and calls where supported, personalization, A/B tests, inbox and reply handling, meetings, team workflows, and analytics.
- Email account health and deliverability, data enrichment, AI-assisted writing and SDR agents where available, CRM and Zapier integrations, APIs, permissions, evidence, and controlled outreach.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or enrich contacts, send outreach, automate social or call tasks, change lead status, enable AI SDR, connect mailboxes or CRM, expose API keys, spend credits, or represent consent, delivery, reply intent, pipeline, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
