# Basecamp Overview

## Scope

- Projects, messages, to-dos, schedules, files, chats, check-ins, and client collaboration.
- Accounts, teams, permissions, notifications, integrations, exports, and administrative workflows.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect account and plan, projects, participants and client access, permissions, messages, to-dos, dates, files, confidential data, integrations, notifications, retention, exports, and activity logs.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change projects, assign work, invite people or clients, send messages, upload or delete files, change access, export data, or represent completion without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
