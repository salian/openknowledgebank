# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect account and plan, projects, participants and client access, permissions, messages, to-dos, dates, files, confidential data, integrations, notifications, retention, exports, and activity logs.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Basecamp configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or change projects, assign work, invite people or clients, send messages, upload or delete files, change access, export data, or represent completion.
