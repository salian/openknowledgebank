# ZoomInfo Sales Overview

Source-aware guidance for ZoomInfo Sales data use, enrichment, integrations, outreach controls, privacy review, and governed operations.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes subscription, product modules, user roles, data sources, records, fields, confidence and freshness indicators, suppression requirements, territories, CRM mappings, integrations, credentials, usage limits, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Subscription and module availability, identity or contact accuracy, data provenance, permissions, suppression status, lawful basis, outreach eligibility, and integration outcomes. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
