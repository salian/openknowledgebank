# Wave Overview

## Scope

- Bookkeeping, chart of accounts, transactions, invoices, estimates, customers, vendors, bills, receipts, bank connections, reconciliation, financial reports and supported payment or payroll services.
- Country, plan and service availability, payment processing, developer APIs and integrations where available, users and permissions, import and export, tax settings, audit evidence, validation, and controlled accounting operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post or alter transactions, invoices, payroll or tax data, connect banks, reconcile accounts, initiate or refund payments, expose API credentials, or represent balances, tax, payroll, payment, profit, compliance, or audit conclusions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
