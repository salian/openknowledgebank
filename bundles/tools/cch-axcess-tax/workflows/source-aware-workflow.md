# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect licensed modules and release, firm and office, taxpayer identity and authorization, entity and jurisdictions, source documents and workpapers, elections, forms and diagnostics, calculations, carryovers, review status, signatures, e-file acknowledgments, permissions, integrations, and audit trail.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the CCH Axcess Tax configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before enter or change taxpayer data, calculate or finalize returns, clear diagnostics, make elections, transmit filings, request signatures, change permissions, or represent tax liability or filing acceptance.
