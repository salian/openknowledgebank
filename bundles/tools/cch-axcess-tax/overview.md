# CCH Axcess Tax Overview

## Scope

- Cloud tax preparation, return workflow, diagnostics, calculations, e-filing, research links, and firm operations.
- Entities, jurisdictions, forms, workpapers, imports, reviews, permissions, signatures, filings, integrations, and audit evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect licensed modules and release, firm and office, taxpayer identity and authorization, entity and jurisdictions, source documents and workpapers, elections, forms and diagnostics, calculations, carryovers, review status, signatures, e-file acknowledgments, permissions, integrations, and audit trail.

## Boundary

Stop before enter or change taxpayer data, calculate or finalize returns, clear diagnostics, make elections, transmit filings, request signatures, change permissions, or represent tax liability or filing acceptance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
