# Yardi Voyager Overview

## Scope

- Property and unit records, owners, tenants and residents, leasing, charges and receipts, payables and general ledger, budgets, procurement, maintenance, inspections and operational reporting according to configured markets and modules.
- Commercial, residential, affordable, senior and other real-estate product distinctions, portals and payments, ancillary Yardi solutions and integrations, roles and permissions, privacy and fair-housing controls, audit evidence, reconciliation, validation, and controlled property operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change tenant, applicant, lease, property, maintenance, accounting or payment records, screen applicants, post charges, execute payments, alter housing decisions, or represent occupancy, eligibility, rent, balance, valuation, compliance, or approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
