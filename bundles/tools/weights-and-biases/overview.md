# Weights & Biases Overview

## Scope

- Projects, runs, configurations and metrics, system data, tables and media, artifacts and lineage, registries, models, sweeps, reports, dashboards, alerts and automations.
- Weave tracing and evaluation, inference or deployment surfaces where available, SDKs and APIs, cloud and self-managed distinctions, teams and permissions, secrets, data rights, retention, cost, validation, and controlled ML operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before upload proprietary datasets, prompts, models or outputs, expose keys, launch sweeps or jobs, register or deploy models, enable tracing, spend compute, or represent lineage, reproducibility, evaluation, safety, performance, cost, or production readiness without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
