# Kajabi Overview

## Scope

- Courses, communities, coaching, podcasts, newsletters and downloads, lessons, assessments, progress, websites, landing pages, email, and automations.
- Offers, checkout, subscriptions, payments, refunds, upsells, affiliates, mobile delivery, customer data, reports, evidence, and controlled commerce.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before publish products, enroll customers, assess learners, set prices, take payments, issue refunds, send marketing, change automations, export customer data, or represent learning, sales, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
