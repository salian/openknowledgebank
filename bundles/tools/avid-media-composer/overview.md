# Avid Media Composer Overview

## Scope

- Nonlinear video editing, media organization, effects, color, audio, titles, and finishing workflows.
- Project, bin, sequence, collaboration, storage, interchange, render, export, and delivery operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect installed version and edition, hardware and codecs, project and bin ownership, source media, licenses and releases, sequence state, color and audio settings, effects, linked storage, collaboration locks, export specification, renders, and quality-control results.

## Boundary

Stop before modify shared projects or sequences, relink or delete media, render or export masters, publish content, overwrite files, or represent rights or quality approval without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
