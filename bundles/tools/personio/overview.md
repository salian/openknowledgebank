# Personio Overview

## Scope

- Employee profiles, organizational data, onboarding and offboarding, documents, attendance, absence, recruiting, applications, interviews, offers, performance, compensation, surveys, and payroll preparation.
- Workflows, approvals, reports, roles, integrations, APIs, webhooks where documented, data imports and exports, audit evidence, privacy, validation, and controlled HR operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change employee or candidate data, attendance, leave, compensation, performance, applications or offers, prepare payroll, send communications, call APIs, change roles, or make employment, eligibility, payment, or compliance decisions without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
