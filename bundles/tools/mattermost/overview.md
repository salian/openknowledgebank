# Mattermost Overview

## Scope

- Channels, direct messages, search, files, notifications, calls, collaborative playbooks, incident workflows, boards, bots, webhooks, plugins, and integrations.
- Cloud and self-hosted deployment, high availability, authentication, permissions, compliance exports, retention, audit logs, administration, APIs, and upgrades.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before send or delete messages, expose files or credentials, initiate calls, run playbooks or bots, install plugins, change retention or permissions, perform exports, deploy upgrades, or represent incident or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
