# Planview Overview

## Scope

- Strategy, objectives, portfolios, programs, projects, products, roadmaps, scenarios, capacity, resources, dependencies, risks, work intake, financials, and performance reporting.
- Enterprise architecture, value-stream management, agile planning, connected work, product development, integrations, APIs, permissions, audit evidence, forecasts, and controlled portfolio decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before create or alter portfolios, projects, roadmaps, resources, budgets, forecasts, priorities or architecture records, connect systems, automate workflows, or represent delivery, capacity, financial, strategic, or business outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
