# Adobe Marketo Engage Overview

## Scope

- Marketing automation and lead management.
- Programs, campaigns, scoring, personalization, analytics, and integrations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect subscription, workspaces, partitions, person fields, consent, programs, smart campaigns, scoring, channels, integrations, permissions, and activity logs.

## Boundary

Stop before import or update people, change scoring, activate campaigns, send communications, sync CRM data, export records, or represent consent or attribution without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
