# MailerLite Overview

## Scope

- Subscribers, groups, custom fields, signup forms, double opt-in, campaigns, newsletter editors, A/B tests, visual automation, websites, landing pages, surveys, and analytics.
- Digital products, subscriptions, bookings, transactional email, integrations, REST API, webhooks, MCP, permissions, evidence, validation, and controlled messaging.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before import or identify subscribers, change consent or groups, send campaigns, activate automations, publish sites or forms, sell products, grant API or MCP access, export data, or represent delivery, conversion, or revenue without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
