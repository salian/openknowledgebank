# SideFX Houdini Overview

## Scope

- Procedural and direct modeling, animation, rigging, particles, dynamics, simulation, effects, lighting, look development, rendering, and compositing.
- Digital assets, USD and Solaris, Houdini Engine, plug-ins, batch processing, pipeline integration, validation, and controlled production.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect version and edition, project and scene, node graphs and parameters, assets and rights, geometry and caches, simulations, materials and lighting, renderer and color settings, plug-ins and dependencies, licenses, farm and resources, outputs, logs, tests, rollback, and approvals.

## Boundary

Stop before modify production scenes or assets, run simulations or renders, distribute jobs, install plug-ins, consume licenses or compute, publish media, overwrite outputs, or represent quality or delivery without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
