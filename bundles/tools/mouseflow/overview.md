# Mouseflow Overview

## Scope

- Session recordings, click, movement, scroll, attention and geographic heatmaps, funnels, form analytics, feedback campaigns, friction scoring, filters, and segments.
- Web installation, masking and excluded content, consent controls, retention, users, integrations, exports, account settings, evidence reconciliation, and controlled analytics use.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before install tracking, record sessions, collect or reveal user data, change masking, consent or retention settings, launch feedback, invite users, export or integrate data, or represent behavior, causality, conversion, privacy, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
