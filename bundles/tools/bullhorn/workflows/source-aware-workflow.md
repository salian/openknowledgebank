# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record objective, account or project scope, date, constraints, and authorized owner.
2. Verify current official sources, edition, region, API or feature version, and lifecycle status.
3. Inspect edition and region, candidates and consent, clients and contacts, jobs and submissions, placements, employment and compensation data, communications, source provenance, retention, fields, roles, automations, integrations, reports, and audit history.
4. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
5. Reconcile conflicts among documentation, configuration, permissions, data, integrations, and logs.
6. Draft the Bullhorn configuration and use review brief with options, dependencies, privacy and security risks, validation, rollback, and stop conditions.
7. Require explicit confirmation before create or change candidate, client, job, submission or placement records, contact candidates, submit profiles, change status, export personal data, automate decisions, or represent hiring outcomes.
