# Bullhorn Overview

## Scope

- Applicant tracking, recruiting CRM, candidate, client, job, placement, and staffing workflows.
- Search, automation, communication, reporting, integrations, permissions, and data operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect edition and region, candidates and consent, clients and contacts, jobs and submissions, placements, employment and compensation data, communications, source provenance, retention, fields, roles, automations, integrations, reports, and audit history.

## Boundary

Stop before create or change candidate, client, job, submission or placement records, contact candidates, submit profiles, change status, export personal data, automate decisions, or represent hiring outcomes without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
