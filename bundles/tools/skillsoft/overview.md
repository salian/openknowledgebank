# Skillsoft Percipio Overview

## Scope

- Learning content, channels, paths and journeys, assignments, audiences, assessments, practice, certification preparation, coaching, skills, recommendations, and learner experiences.
- Enterprise administration, content curation, reports and analytics, integrations and APIs where licensed, AI capabilities where available, roles, privacy, accessibility, evidence, validation, and controlled learning operations.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before access or change learner records, assign content, administer assessments, alter completion or skill records, enable AI, export employee data, connect HR systems, or represent identity, learning, competency, certification, employment, or compliance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
