# Box Overview

## Scope

- Files, folders, versions, sharing, collaboration, search, metadata, classifications, workflows, and APIs.
- Authentication, users, groups, events, webhooks, AI, retention, legal holds, governance, and enterprise administration.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect Box API version, enterprise and storage zone, app and OAuth scopes, users and groups, files and folders, ownership, collaborations and shared links, classifications, retention and legal holds, integrations, webhooks, AI settings, events, and audit logs.

## Boundary

Stop before upload, download, share, move, overwrite or delete content, invite collaborators, change classifications or retention, apply legal holds, use AI, alter users or scopes, or represent governance without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
