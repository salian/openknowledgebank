# Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance, Supply Chain Management, and Business Central Overview

## Scope

- Finance, accounting, budgeting, tax, cash, fixed assets, projects and reporting in Dynamics 365 Finance and Business Central, with product-specific scope.
- Supply chain, inventory, procurement, manufacturing, warehouse and asset workflows, Dataverse and Power Platform integration, extensions, environments, roles, audit evidence, testing, and release management.

## Evidence Contract

Product documentation can establish documented capabilities. It cannot establish the local edition, configuration, data, access, integration state, execution result, or approval. Inspect current product identity and lifecycle, account or deployment, plan and region, users and roles, configuration, source data, permissions, integrations, logs, controls, validation, rollback, and approval evidence.

## Boundary

Stop before post or reverse transactions, change ledgers, tax, inventory, vendors, customers or master data, approve purchases or payments, deploy extensions, change access, migrate data, or represent financial, inventory, tax, operational, or compliance state without evidenced authority, validation, and explicit confirmation.
