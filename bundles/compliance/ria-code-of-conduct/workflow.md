# RIA Code of Conduct Review Workflow

1. Identify legal entity, registration number/status or exemption context, entity form, person/role, service, client type, product/security, and conduct date.
2. Capture the IA Regulations and circulars operative on that date; separate current consolidation, historical source, guidance, firm policy, records, and orders.
3. Decide whether regulation 15(9), clause 8 personnel scope, or clause 9 body-corporate scope is relevant. Record rationale or `Needs verification`.
4. Map the issue to one or more of the nine principles, then identify each detailed regulation/circular separately.
5. Record only provided and authorised firm/client evidence, its population and period, provenance, completeness, and confidentiality limits.
6. Distinguish policy design, implementation evidence, event facts, outcome evidence, and reviewer conclusion.
7. Reconcile Verified/Provided/Assumed/Needs verification facts and state only the narrow supported gap or conclusion.
8. Escalate legal interpretation, client harm, conflict, fee, confidentiality, breach, remediation, complaint, inspection, enforcement, or regulator communication to an accountable reviewer.
9. Recheck SEBI's regulations, current Master Circular, later circulars, registration facts, and event scope before reuse.
