# Overview

Regulation 15(9) of the `Securities and Exchange Board of India (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013` requires an investment adviser to abide by the Code of Conduct in the Third Schedule. The current SEBI consolidation is amended through 25 November 2025. The original regulations took effect on 21 April 2013; historical conduct requires the version operative on the event date.

The Third Schedule contains nine principles. Clause 8 expressly addresses an investment adviser and its partners, principal officer, and persons associated with investment advice. Clause 9 assigns primary conduct and procedure responsibility to senior management of a body corporate registered as an investment adviser. Do not generalise those clauses to every financial professional, exempt person, or entity.

The current Master Circular dated 6 February 2026 consolidates detailed IA directions. Later circulars remain separate and must be checked. Regulations, circulars, firm policy, client records, and enforcement orders are different evidence classes.

No firm policy or conduct event is known unless provided and verified. This bundle does not turn a broad principle into an invented control or breach finding.
