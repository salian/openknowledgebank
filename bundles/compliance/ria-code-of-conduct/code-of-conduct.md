# SEBI IA Code of Conduct Evidence Contract

## Source Note

Use the then-operative IA Regulations for regulation 15(9) and the Third Schedule. Use the 6 February 2026 Master Circular and later circulars for detailed directions. Firm policy and records show local design or conduct only when actually provided; enforcement orders are fact-specific and are not universal policy.

## Applicability And Version

Record legal entity, SEBI registration number/status, claimed exemption, individual/firm/body-corporate form, person and role, service, client type, product or security, conduct event, event date, regulation version, circular version, and source rationale.

- The original IA Regulations took effect 21 April 2013.
- The current consolidated text is amended through 25 November 2025.
- Current clause 6 and clause 8 wording reflects amendments effective 30 September 2020.
- Historical review uses the text and circulars effective when the conduct occurred, not automatically today's consolidation.

If registration, exemption, role, service, event date, or source version is absent, mark applicability `Needs verification`. Do not infer that all financial advice, public commentary, research, distribution, insurance, pension, legal, accounting, or other professional activity is covered identically.

## Nine-Principle Map

| Clause | Review focus | Evidence question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Honesty and fairness | client interest and market integrity | What complete, dated conduct evidence supports the representation and treatment? |
| 2 Diligence | skill, care, analysis, alternatives | What analysis and review record existed when advice was given? |
| 3 Capabilities | resources and procedures | What authorised evidence shows capacity and operation for the relevant period? |
| 4 Information about clients | relevant client information and confidentiality | What lawful, current information and confidentiality controls applied? |
| 5 Information to clients | material information disclosure | What was required, provided, understood, and retained for this event? |
| 6 Fair and reasonable charges | fees and applicable ceilings | Which client category, fee rule, agreement, period, and calculation apply? |
| 7 Conflicts of interest | avoidance, disclosure, and fair treatment | What conflict existed, how was it identified, and what dated treatment evidence exists? |
| 8 Compliance | applicable regulatory requirements and covered persons | Which exact regulation/circular applied to the entity, person, service, and date? |
| 9 Senior management responsibility | registered body-corporate standards and procedures | Is the entity a registered body corporate, and what governance evidence is actually provided? |

The table is navigation, not a replacement for the Third Schedule or detailed requirements.

## Conduct-Event Ledger

For each issue record clause, exact regulation/circular and effective date, entity/person/role, client/service/product scope, event date, evidence population and period, source status, firm-policy status, observed fact, owner, reviewer, gap, and recheck trigger.

Statuses: `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`.

A written policy does not prove implementation or outcome. A sample does not establish the full population without a documented basis. Missing evidence does not itself prove breach. Do not invent client facts, advice quality, fee fairness, conflict handling, firm controls, remediation, or SEBI/IAASB position.
