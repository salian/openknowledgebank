# Quality Check

Qualitative only; no measured score. Rate Meets/Partially/Does not/Not assessed:

1. Registration/exemption, entity form, person/role, service, client, product, and event date are explicit.
2. The then-operative regulation, Master Circular, and later circulars are versioned and distinct.
3. Regulation 15(9), clause 8 personnel scope, and clause 9 body-corporate scope are applied narrowly.
4. Nine principles are not substituted for detailed requirements or firm-specific policy.
5. Clause mappings trace to complete, dated, authorised evidence and disclose population limits.
6. Policy design, implementation, event facts, outcomes, and reviewer conclusions remain distinct.
7. No invented firm policy, client fact, breach, remediation, or regulator position appears.
8. Sensitive evidence and consequential communication/action require authority and accountable review.

Publish no aggregate score without approved tasks, config, matched outputs, and qualified reviewer results.
