# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory entity establishment respondent and signatory authority, official survey notice identifier channel and contacts, current law survey instructions scope period definitions units and deadlines, field-to-ledger-and-operational source map, calculation translations and reconciliations, confidentiality access and transfer controls, missing-value and explanation log, legal accounting and management review, submission timestamp receipt correspondence correction and retention records.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the IBGE survey applicability and submission evidence record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to give unlicensed Brazilian legal advice, respond to unverified requests, invent values, expose confidential data, sign or submit without authority, pay penalties, claim acceptance, or declare compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
