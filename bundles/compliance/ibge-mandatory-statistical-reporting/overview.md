# IBGE Mandatory Statistical Reporting Overview

Verify entity, establishment, respondent authority, official survey notice and IBGE channel; determine applicability from current survey and Brazilian law under qualified review; inventory requested period, units, definitions and deadlines; map fields to approved accounting and operational records; preserve Portuguese source wording and translations; protect confidential statistical and personal data; validate totals and explanations; obtain authorized sign-off; submit only through verified channels; retain receipt and correspondence; distinguish submission from acceptance or compliance.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity establishment respondent and signatory authority, official survey notice identifier channel and contacts, current law survey instructions scope period definitions units and deadlines, field-to-ledger-and-operational source map, calculation translations and reconciliations, confidentiality access and transfer controls, missing-value and explanation log, legal accounting and management review, submission timestamp receipt correspondence correction and retention records. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish entity scope, survey legitimacy, notice authenticity, field value, definition, confidentiality, deadline, penalty, submission acceptance, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
