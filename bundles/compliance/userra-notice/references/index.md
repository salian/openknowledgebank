# Official Reference Index

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/userra/resources
- https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-IX/part-1002

The poster is one notice method and does not exhaust USERRA obligations. Verify employer and service coverage, notice facts, cumulative service, timeliness, escalator position, qualifications, accommodations, benefits, documentation limits, exceptions, state protections, and current DOL rules.

## Evidence Required

- Current authoritative source and edition.
- Objective, scope, local records, constraints, decision criteria, and accountable owner.
- Conflicts, assumptions, approvals, validation evidence, and stop conditions.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable USERRA notice, leave, and reemployment review brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before make legal conclusions, certify compliance, file or amend a submission, issue a notice, alter controls, or represent approval.
