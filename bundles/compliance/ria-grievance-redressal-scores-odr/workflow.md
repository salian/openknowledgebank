# Review Workflow

1. Freeze the review date, entity and registration context, complaint scope, relevant dates, channels, and source versions.
2. Inventory direct-IA, SCORES, Designated Body or IAASB, SEBI, ODR, website, mobile-app, and internal evidence without changing any record.
3. Build a pathway map and label every fact with an evidence state.
4. Create separate direct-IA and SCORES timeline rows; calculate no live deadline when a trigger or calendar rule is unverified.
5. Review entity ATR, first review, second review, escalation, and disposal evidence without treating an ATR as adjudication.
6. Review ODR prerequisite, eligibility, limitation, pending-proceeding, stage, and status evidence using the current operative source.
7. Reconcile SCORES and ODR states and identify conflicts as gaps, not conclusions.
8. Reconcile monthly disclosure data and seventh-day publication evidence to the current Annexure C format.
9. Record findings, source conflicts, missing evidence, owners, and accountable review needs.
10. Stop before any complaint submission, portal action, communication, disclosure change, settlement, arbitration action, remediation, or regulator contact.

Use the [grievance guide](grievance-redressal.md) for controls and the [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md) before relying on the review record.
