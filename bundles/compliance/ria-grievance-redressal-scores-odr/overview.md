# Overview

This bundle supports a bounded review of investment adviser grievance evidence across direct handling, SCORES 2.0, SMART ODR, and monthly complaint disclosure. Start with the current authoritative source and the actual case or firm evidence. Label absent facts `Needs verification`.

## Source hierarchy

Use the current Investment Advisers Regulations and Master Circular for the adviser duty, the operative SCORES circular and current FAQ for SCORES workflow, the operative ODR Master Circular for dispute routing, and the current Investor Charter for client-facing grievance and disclosure duties. A consultation paper is not an operative requirement.

Historical conduct must be tested against the source effective on the relevant date. Portal screens and FAQs can change and must not silently replace a circular.

## Evidence states

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Record the source, effective date, evidence owner, and reviewer for each material conclusion.

## Boundaries

The bundle does not determine complaint merits, give legal or investment advice, submit or alter a complaint, calculate a live filing decision, access a portal, draft a client response, or determine an arbitral outcome. See the [grievance guide](grievance-redressal.md) and [workflow](workflow.md).
