# Grievance Redressal, SCORES and ODR Guide

## 1. Establish applicability

Record the adviser registration and service, complainant relationship, complaint subject, cause-of-action date, direct-receipt date, SCORES-receipt date, current channel, pending proceeding, and source versions. Check whether the issue falls within securities-law matters administered by SEBI and whether an exclusion applies. Do not infer eligibility from a portal status alone.

## 2. Keep the clocks separate

The Investor Charter states that a complaint raised directly with an investment adviser should be redressed immediately and no later than 21 days from receipt. The SCORES framework separately gives the entity 21 calendar days from receipt on SCORES to resolve the grievance and upload its Action Taken Report. Record both triggers independently; never substitute one receipt date for the other.

For SCORES, record the entity ATR date, the 15-calendar-day first-review window, the Designated Body or IAASB first-review trigger and 10-calendar-day ATR period, the 15-calendar-day second-review window, and any SEBI second review. Verify current portal evidence before concluding that a review window opened, expired, or escalated.

An ATR upload is not automatically proof of satisfactory resolution or immediate final disposal. SCORES facilitates redress and does not adjudicate a dispute.

## 3. Reconcile SCORES and ODR

The investor first raises the grievance with the market participant. If it is not satisfactorily resolved, the operative ODR framework permits escalation through SCORES and, subject to the framework, initiation through the ODR portal. Check current eligibility, limitation, other pending proceedings, fees, documents, and status before describing the route.

Record whether the matter is in pre-conciliation, conciliation, arbitration, closed, or unknown only when supported. Moving to ODR or another remedy can change the SCORES disposition; reconcile both systems rather than reporting concurrent unresolved statuses without evidence. Do not promise a result or prescribe portal interactions.

## 4. Review monthly complaint disclosure

The current IA Master Circular requires investment advisers to display complaint data on their website and mobile application, if any, by the seventh day of the succeeding month in the prescribed Annexure C format. Reconcile opening, received, resolved, and pending data to source records and preserve the publication evidence.

Do not invent zero complaints, resolution dates, average resolution time, complaint categories, or impersonation treatment. Record definitions and exceptions from the current source and identify unexplained differences.

## 5. Evidence and escalation

Maintain a timeline ledger with event, channel, source record, event date, receipt date, applicable clock, due-date method, status, evidence location, and reviewer. Keep personal, financial, allegation, settlement, and arbitration material access-controlled and minimize public extracts.

Escalate source conflicts, uncertain applicability, missing receipt dates, missed or disputed windows, unresolved status differences, and disclosure variances to an accountable Indian securities compliance or legal reviewer.
