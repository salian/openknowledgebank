# Singapore PDPA Do Not Call Provisions Source-Aware Compliance Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/do-not-call-registry
- https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/about-do-not-call-registry/do-not-call-registry-for-organisations/organisations-guide-to-singapores-do-not-call-dnc-provisions
- https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012

PDPC and statutory sources do not establish sender or recipient scope, message purpose, registry status, consent validity, exception, identification sufficiency, lawful transmission, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, statute, rule, circular, manual, edit file, FAQ, and form sources with effective date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected entity, sender, adviser, provider, claim, notice, transaction, record, consent, calculation, submission, and exception evidence.
- Named owner, qualified local legal or regulatory review, control validation, signatory, submission, retention, and approval authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable Singapore DNC screening and message authorization record with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to send calls texts or faxes without authority, fabricate checks or consent, ignore withdrawal, misuse exceptions, hide sender identity, share lists, bypass suppression, or declare compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent sender scope, recipient scope, marketing purpose, DNC status, consent, withdrawal, exception, identification sufficiency, lawful transmission, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
