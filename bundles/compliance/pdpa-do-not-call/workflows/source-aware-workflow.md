# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory organization sender vendor and campaign authority, recipient number source Singapore nexus purpose and channel, current statute regulations advisory guidelines and effective dates, relevant DNC register query timestamp result and validity, consent language capture scope date and provenance, withdrawal and suppression history, ongoing-relationship facts and legal review, sender identification callback and content approval, vendor contracts list synchronization tests message delivery complaint incident retention and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the Singapore DNC screening and message authorization record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to send calls texts or faxes without authority, fabricate checks or consent, ignore withdrawal, misuse exceptions, hide sender identity, share lists, bypass suppression, or declare compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
