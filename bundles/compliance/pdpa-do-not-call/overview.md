# Singapore PDPA Do Not Call Provisions Overview

Verify sending organization, message purpose, recipient number, Singapore nexus and voice, text or fax channel; classify marketing scope under current PDPA and regulator guidance; check the relevant DNC register through approved channels within the applicable validity period; rely on consent or ongoing-relationship provisions only with documented legal basis and withdrawal handling; include required sender identification and contact information; synchronize suppression across vendors and systems; test controls; retain screening, consent, message, delivery, complaint and incident evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes organization sender vendor and campaign authority, recipient number source Singapore nexus purpose and channel, current statute regulations advisory guidelines and effective dates, relevant DNC register query timestamp result and validity, consent language capture scope date and provenance, withdrawal and suppression history, ongoing-relationship facts and legal review, sender identification callback and content approval, vendor contracts list synchronization tests message delivery complaint incident retention and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish sender scope, recipient scope, marketing purpose, DNC status, consent, withdrawal, exception, identification sufficiency, lawful transmission, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
