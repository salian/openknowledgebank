# Federal-State Enrollment Screening Contract

## Source Note

Use current and point-in-time eCFR for codified federal requirements. Use the CMS Medicaid Provider Enrollment Compendium as subregulatory guidance. Inspect current state law, State plan, provider manuals, applications, agreements, bulletins, managed-care materials, notices, and appeal rules for implementation. Do not treat one state's process as national.

## Applicability Record

Record state/program, FFS or managed care, provider type/category, individual/entity, billing/ordering/referring/prescribing role, locations, ownership/control and managing roles, enrollment event, effective date, source versions, and accountable reviewer. Unresolved fields are `Needs verification`.

## Screening and Reliance Ledger

For each activity record citation, state source, subject/person, provider category/risk, trigger, required activity, evidence source/date, result status, reliance source and identity match, outstanding activity, reviewer, decision owner, and recheck trigger.

Permitted statuses: `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable - rationale pending review`, `Not assessed`.

Reliance on Medicare or another state is not a blanket substitute. Verify the same provider, current approved status, screening date/scope, required activity, and any state-added or outstanding screening. Do not infer a database result or reliance eligibility.

## Decision Boundary

- Missing evidence is a gap, not automatically denial, termination, or exclusion.
- A name match is not a confirmed identity or exclusion determination.
- A federal minimum does not displace a valid stricter state method.
- Risk category, fee, moratorium, fingerprint/site requirement, exception, notice, deadline, appeal, and final status require current authoritative evidence.
- Do not access systems, submit information, pay fees, contact agencies, collect fingerprints, conduct visits, disclose records, or take adverse action without explicit authorization.
