# Medicaid Provider Enrollment and Screening Quality Check

Qualitative rubric only; not a benchmark score or enrollment decision.

Rate each `Meets`, `Partially meets`, `Does not meet`, or `Not assessed`.

1. **Applicability:** State/program, provider type/role, entity/person, delivery system, locations, event and dates are explicit.
2. **Federal/state separation:** State-plan duties, CMS guidance, state implementation, managed-care terms and provider actions remain distinct.
3. **Current authority:** Exact sections, versions, effective dates and state sources are captured.
4. **Risk and activity:** Risk/category, adjustments, license, database, site, fingerprint, fee and moratorium requirements are evidence-backed.
5. **Reliance:** Medicare/other-state identity, status, timing, scope and outstanding activities are verified; no blanket reliance.
6. **Evidence and status:** Sources, dates, affected persons, reviewer, result status, conflicts, gaps and triggers are traceable.
7. **Decision discipline:** No invented match, exclusion, risk, fee, deadline, denial, termination, appeal, status or compliance claim.
8. **Safety/authority:** Protected data and live access/contact/submission/fingerprint/site/adverse actions require explicit authority and qualified review.

Record reviewer qualifications, candidate version, context, sources/dates, ratings, findings, corrections, and disposition. Publish no aggregate score without approved reproducible evaluation evidence.
