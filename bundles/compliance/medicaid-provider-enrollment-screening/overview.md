# Overview

Use this bundle to identify which federal Medicaid provider screening rules and state implementation materials govern a specific enrollment event.

## Federal Baseline

42 CFR Part 455 Subpart E sets State-plan and State Medicaid agency requirements involving enrollment, screening, license verification, revalidation, denial/termination, reactivation, appeals, site visits, criminal-background checks, federal databases, categorical risk, fees, and moratoria.

## State and Program Boundary

Providers act through state-specific applications, agreements, manuals, categories, systems, notices, managed-care arrangements, and appeal procedures. Section 455.452 permits additional or more stringent state screening. Every state/program detail must be verified; a federal baseline is not a universal provider instruction.

## Not For

- Live database, exclusion, identity, license, fingerprint, or site-visit screening
- Fee calculation/payment, portal use, application submission, agency contact, adverse action, or appeal
- Legal advice or a conclusion about enrollment, exclusion, risk, denial, termination, or compliance

## Risk Boundary

Use minimum-necessary protected data and explicit authority. Qualified state/program, legal, compliance, credentialing, and program-integrity reviewers own interpretations and decisions.
