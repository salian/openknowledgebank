## Current-Source Note

A donor generally needs a contemporaneous written acknowledgment for each contribution of $250 or more; organizations have a separate disclosure duty for quid pro quo contributions over $75. Verify cash/noncash, goods or services, timing, valuation boundaries, vehicles, appraisals, and current IRS guidance.
