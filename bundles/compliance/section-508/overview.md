# Overview

This bundle supports a bounded review of Section 508 evidence across federal ICT development, procurement, maintenance, and use. It separates the statute, regulatory standards, incorporated WCAG requirements, FAR acquisition rules, agency and contract overlays, and product evidence.

## Authority hierarchy

Use 29 U.S.C. 794d for legal coverage, 36 CFR part 1194 for the Revised 508 Standards, the current FAR for acquisition obligations, and the incorporated WCAG 2.0 Recommendation for referenced success criteria and conformance requirements. Section508.gov provides federal implementation guidance but does not replace those authorities.

Agency policy and contract language can add scope, evidence, testing, delivery, and acceptance requirements. Record those separately rather than presenting them as universal Section 508 text.

## Evidence states

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Record the source, edition, effective date, ICT version and configuration, evidence owner, test method, and reviewer. With no project evidence, all project-specific conclusions remain `Needs verification`.

## Boundaries

The bundle does not give legal advice, determine an exception, certify a product, select test tools, conduct an acquisition, accept an ACR, make an award, file a complaint, or authorize a claim. See the [Section 508 guide](section-508.md) and [workflow](workflow.md).
