# Review Workflow

1. Freeze the review date and collect the statute, current 36 CFR standards, current FAR, agency policy, acquisition documents, and contract terms.
2. Inventory each ICT component, lifecycle activity, user group, product version, configuration, and evidence source.
3. Determine the potential federal and acquisition context without making a final legal-coverage conclusion.
4. Build a component-level standards matrix covering WCAG-incorporated and non-WCAG provisions.
5. Record any safe harbor, exception, exemption, best-meets, or equivalent-facilitation assertion with exact authority and evidence.
6. Build a conformance ledger for ACR assertions, test methods, results, user evidence, defects, versions, and unresolved differences.
7. Trace accessibility requirements from need and market research through solicitation, evaluation, contract, acceptance, maintenance, and alteration.
8. Separate legal compliance, technical conformance, procurement acceptability, user accessibility, and remediation status.
9. Apply the quality check and record missing evidence, source conflicts, owners, and accountable review needs.
10. Stop before exception approval, solicitation or contract change, award, acceptance, public claim, complaint, or remediation deployment.

Use the [Section 508 guide](section-508.md) for controls and the [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md) before relying on the record.
