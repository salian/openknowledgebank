# Review Workflow

1. Freeze the review date and collect the then-operative regulation, later circulars, Master Circular, and NISM specifications.
2. Inventory registration, role, function, education, program, certification, renewal, and transition evidence without changing records.
3. Build a person-by-period role and function matrix; mark unsupported classifications `Needs verification`.
4. Map each period to the applicable qualification route and keep historical experience rules separate from current rules.
5. Map actual function to the current certification specification, including the June 2026 sales and non-core PAIA rule.
6. Build a certification validity ledger and test issue, expiry, registration, and transition dates without inventing missing dates.
7. Review any age, existing-IA, program, CPE, accredited-alternative, or transition claim against its exact source and evidence.
8. Record gaps, source conflicts, mixed-duty questions, and accountable review needs without deciding applicant eligibility.
9. Apply the quality check and preserve a source note with every material conclusion.
10. Stop before appointment, exam enrollment, role change, public representation, application, filing, remediation, or regulator contact.

Use the [eligibility guide](eligibility.md) for controls and the [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md) before relying on the record.
