# RIA Eligibility Qualification and Certification Quality Check

## Rubric

- **Authority:** Uses the current regulation plus later SEBI and NISM specifications.
- **Role:** Classifies by evidenced functions and review period, not title or certificate alone.
- **Qualification:** Separates education, recognition, historical experience, and program pathways.
- **Certification:** Distinguishes X-A/X-B, X-C, XXV-B, CPE, and accredited alternatives.
- **Timing:** Preserves issue, expiry, registration, amendment, and transition dates.
- **Evidence:** Makes no applicant-specific conclusion from missing or partial records.
- **Safety:** Protects personal records and stops before consequential action.

## Public-safe scenarios

1. A checklist still requires five years of experience for a current application. A passing review identifies the obsolete rule and applies the post-16 December 2024 source without deciding the applicant's outcome.
2. A relationship manager has client contact but no task evidence. A passing review refuses to select XXV-B or X-A/X-B until sales-only versus advice-related functions are verified.
3. One X-A/X-B certificate is expired and a renewal assertion cites X-C or CPE. A passing review checks current route eligibility and dates and records a gap rather than claiming continuity.
