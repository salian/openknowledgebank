# Eligibility, Qualification and Certification Guide

## 1. Identify the person and function

Record whether the review concerns an individual investment adviser, principal officer, person associated with investment advice, partner engaged in providing advice, or another person. For PAIA, document whether duties are only sales and other non-core services or involve investment-advice-related aspects. Titles alone do not establish function.

Record the review date, registration date, role start date, actual tasks, reporting line, client contact, advice involvement, and supporting records. Do not infer a role from a certificate held.

## 2. Separate qualification and experience

From 25 November 2025, regulation 7(1)(a) provides a route based on a recognized graduate degree or equivalent educational qualification, or CFA Charter, together with relevant certification. Regulation 7(1)(c) separately identifies specified NISM postgraduate programs. Recognition, equivalence, program identity, completion, and applicability require evidence.

The prior general experience requirement was removed effective 16 December 2024. Do not require current experience merely because an older regulation, circular, checklist, or application record did. Experience can remain relevant to a historical review or another fact-specific provision, but its role and source must be stated.

The current Master Circular says a person qualifying through the specified regulation 7(1)(c) NISM program route does not need initial entry-level certification, but must obtain relevant renewal certification within three years from the registration certificate. Do not extend that treatment to another program by analogy.

## 3. Map current certification by function

For individual advisers, principal officers, and PAIA involved in investment-advice-related aspects, verify the current SEBI certification specification and the relevant NISM X-A and X-B evidence. For a PAIA who performs only sales and other non-core services, the SEBI circular effective 24 June 2026 specifies NISM Series XXV-B.

A sales or non-core PAIA who already held X-A and X-B on 24 June 2026 was not required to obtain XXV-B immediately; the circular requires XXV-B before expiry of the existing certifications. Record both certificate identities and expiry dates before applying that transition.

Do not treat XXV-B as sufficient for a person involved in advice-related aspects. Do not assume every client-facing employee is a PAIA or sales-only. Escalate mixed duties and changing roles.

## 4. Review continuity and renewal

Regulation 7(2), effective 25 November 2025, requires fresh relevant NISM certification before expiry of existing certification or within three years from the registration certificate, as applicable. Establish which branch applies and check later SEBI/NISM specifications.

NISM X-C is a renewal examination for candidates holding valid X-A and X-B certificates under its current eligibility conditions. Series XXV-B has its own renewal rule. CPE and accredited alternatives have route-specific conditions; do not present them as universally interchangeable or available after expiry.

Create a ledger with person, function period, qualification route, certificate series, issue date, expiry date, renewal route, registration date, transition rule, evidence status, and reviewer. A passed examination, certificate copy, or registration certificate does not by itself prove all other requirements.

## 5. Review exceptions and evidence

Treat existing-IA age relief, program-based initial-certification treatment, PAIA transition, and any accredited alternative as narrow source-dependent provisions. Record the exact qualifying date and evidence. Do not combine separate provisions into a broader exemption.

Protect personal and application records. Escalate uncertain equivalence, mixed duties, expired or mismatched certificates, source conflicts, transition ambiguity, and historical-period questions to an accountable Indian securities compliance or legal reviewer.
