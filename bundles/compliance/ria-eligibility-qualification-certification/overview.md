# Overview

This bundle supports a bounded review of current investment adviser qualification and certification evidence. It starts with the person's actual function and the relevant date, then separates educational qualification, certification, renewal, transition, and registration records.

## Source hierarchy

Use the current IA Regulations for legal scope and qualification rules, later SEBI circulars for function-specific certification requirements, the current IA Master Circular for consolidated implementation guidance, and current NISM materials for the specified examination or renewal route. Historical conduct requires the then-operative source.

The February 2026 Master Circular predates the June 2026 sales and non-core PAIA circular. Read them together; do not use the older circular alone to classify a current PAIA certification requirement.

## Evidence states

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Record source, effective date, evidence owner, and reviewer for each material conclusion. With no applicant evidence, all applicant-specific fields remain `Needs verification`.

## Boundaries

The bundle does not give legal advice, recognize a degree, classify actual duties conclusively, validate NISM records, select an exam, enroll a person, make an application, or decide eligibility. See the [eligibility guide](eligibility.md) and [workflow](workflow.md).
