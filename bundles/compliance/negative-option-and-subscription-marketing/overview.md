# Negative Option and Automatic Renewal Marketing Overview

Record that the 2024 amended FTC rule was vacated, then identify currently operative federal and state sources and inspect offer, consent, billing, cancellation, record, and communication evidence for qualified review.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, covered entity and activity, transaction or communication facts, consumer and consent evidence, disclosures, records, controls, exceptions, retention, filings, remediation, and approval evidence. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish operative legal requirement, federal or state coverage, offer clarity, informed consent, billing authorization, cancellation effectiveness, refund duty, violation, liability, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
