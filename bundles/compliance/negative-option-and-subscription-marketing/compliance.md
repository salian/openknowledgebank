# Negative Option and Automatic Renewal Marketing Source-Aware Compliance Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/11/15/2024-25534/negative-option-rule
- https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/restore-online-shoppers-confidence-act
- https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/negative-option-rule

The FTC's 2024 amended Negative Option Rule was vacated in 2025; linked historical rule materials do not establish an operative universal click-to-cancel duty, local coverage, facts, state-law obligations, compliance, or legal advice.

## Evidence Required

- Current controlling text, amendments, effective dates, agency interpretations, orders, and applicable jurisdiction.
- Inspected entity, product, transaction, communication, consumer, consent, disclosure, record, control, and filing evidence.
- Qualified legal or compliance reviewer, accountable owner, remediation, retention, validation, and approval evidence.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable negative-option offer, consent, cancellation, and legal-status review brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before launch or alter offers, enroll or bill consumers, renew subscriptions, change cancellation, contact consumers, issue refunds, rely on the vacated rule, or represent legal compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent operative legal requirement, federal or state coverage, offer clarity, informed consent, billing authorization, cancellation effectiveness, refund duty, violation, liability, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
