Federal Reserve Regulation WW requires an NSFR of at least 1.0 and has separate shortfall notification, remediation-plan, reporting, and public-disclosure provisions. Verify effective dates, transition rules, entity scope, official forms or portals, and later amendments before relying on the guidance.
