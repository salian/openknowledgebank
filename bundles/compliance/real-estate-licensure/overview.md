# Real Estate Licensure Overview

This bundle supports evidence-grounded review of Real Estate Licensure. It separates current authoritative requirements from proposals, guidance, local facts, and professional conclusions.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes jurisdiction, role and activity, entity structure, regulator materials, education and examination records, application and renewal status, sponsorship, disclosures, discipline, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish applicability, legal conclusions, thresholds, deadlines, exemptions, filing status, control effectiveness, compliance, approval, or authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
