# RTO 2025 Regulator and Transition Contract

## Source Note

Legislation supplies binding text. ASQA, DEWR and TAC materials explain policy/regulatory practice. Preserve exact instrument title, version, regulator and decision/event date.

## Applicability Matrix

| Context | Starting source | Date boundary |
|---|---|---|
| ASQA/NVR RTO | F2025L00354, F2025L00355 and Credential Policy | Commenced 1 July 2025; standards in effect when decision made; verify continuing regulatory activity. |
| TAC/WA RTO | Current WA Regulations and TAC Registration Standards 2025 documents | Commenced 1 Oct 2025; transition 1 Oct–31 Dec; verify r.64 preserved pending matter. |

Record legal entity, RTO code, regulator, registration/scope, locations/third parties, activity, application/audit, evidence period, decision date, instrument/version and transition rationale. Mark gaps `Needs verification`.

## Exact Naming

- Commonwealth Outcome instrument: `National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Outcome Standards for NVR Registered Training Organisations) Instrument 2025`.
- Commonwealth compliance instrument: full title shown above; numbered sections are Compliance Requirements, with Fit and Proper Person and NRT Logo schedules.
- TAC: `Outcome Standards - Registration Standards 2025` and `Compliance Standards & Fit and Proper Person Requirements - Registration Standards 2025`.
- Credential Policy remains a separate enforceable component.

## Evidence Ledger

Map exact requirement to regulator/source/version, scope, evidence population/period, owner, reviewer, `Verified`/`Provided`/`Assumed`/`Needs verification`/`Not assessed`, gap, action authority and recheck trigger.

Annual declaration reviews must verify regulator, Compliance Standard 15 text, approved form, reporting period, full scope/third parties/locations, accountable signatory, due date and submission evidence. Do not infer TAC instructions apply to ASQA or vice versa.

Missing evidence is not automatically non-compliance. Do not invent transition eligibility, audit finding, declaration status, deadline, rectification, notification or regulator decision. No live action without explicit authority.
