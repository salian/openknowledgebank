# Overview

The 2025 Standards framework has Outcome Standards, compliance requirements and a Credential Policy. For ASQA-regulated NVR RTOs, the Commonwealth instruments commenced 1 July 2025. The exact compliance instrument is the `National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Compliance Standards for NVR Registered Training Organisations and Fit and Proper Person Requirements) Instrument 2025`.

TAC uses WA `Registration Standards 2025` documents. WA substantive amendments commenced 1 October 2025, with a transition through 31 December 2025 and continued pre-commencement treatment for specified pending matters. Resolve regulator and event/decision status before applying any date or clause.

This is not a universal checklist or authority to declare, notify, submit, contact, disclose, or change operations.
