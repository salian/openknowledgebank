# Quality Check

Qualitative only; no measured score. Rate Meets/Partially/Does not/Not assessed:

1. Regulator/entity/scope are explicit.
2. Instrument/document names and versions are exact.
3. ASQA and TAC commencement/transition are not conflated.
4. Legislation, Credential Policy and guidance remain distinct.
5. Requirements trace to complete evidence populations/periods.
6. Annual declaration scope/form/signatory/deadline are regulator-specific.
7. No invented compliance, audit, transition, notification or decision claim.
8. Protected data and external/change actions require authority and qualified review.

Publish no aggregate score without approved tasks, config, matched outputs and reviewer results.
