# RTO 2025 Compliance Review Workflow

1. Identify entity/RTO code, ASQA or TAC, registration/scope, locations, third parties, activity, application/audit and decision dates.
2. Capture exact current/historical legislation, regulator documents, Credential Policy and guidance; keep them distinct.
3. Determine operative date: ASQA 1 July 2025 boundary or WA 1 October 2025/r.64 transition. Record rationale; do not assume.
4. Map applicable requirements to authorised evidence, population/period, owner, reviewer, status and gap.
5. For declarations/notifications, verify regulator-specific standard, form, reporting period, scope, signatory and deadline without submitting.
6. Reconcile verified/provided/assumed/needs-verification facts and give only the narrow supported answer.
7. Escalate legal, audit, registration, transition, non-compliance, rectification and regulator communication to accountable reviewers.
8. Monitor instrument amendments, guidance, registration/scope, third parties, training products and regulator dates.
