## Current-Source Note

Use the current tax-year revisions and instructions. For 2026, the IRS directs employers to the March 2026 Form 941 revision, while W-2/W-3 amounts should reconcile to Forms 941, 943, or 944. Verify employer type, filing assignment, wages, deposits, corrections, and SSA submission separately.
