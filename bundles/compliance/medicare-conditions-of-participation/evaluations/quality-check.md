# Medicare Conditions of Participation and Conditions for Coverage Quality Check

This is a qualitative reviewer rubric. It is not a measured benchmark result, survey protocol, certification decision, or compliance score.

## Rating Scale

- `Meets`: complete, source-backed, scoped, and review-ready
- `Partially meets`: useful but missing material scope, source, date, evidence, or accountability
- `Does not meet`: unsupported, mis-scoped, unsafe, or materially misleading
- `Not assessed`: evidence or qualified review unavailable

## Criteria

### 1. Provider and Certification Scope

The output identifies the provider/supplier type, entity, program, certification scope, locations/components, services, survey pathway, event date, and unresolved facts. It does not use a generic CoP checklist as an applicability decision.

### 2. Governing Authority

Every material requirement traces to the exact applicable part, provider-specific subpart, section, and version. Regulations, survey guidance, accreditation, state law, and internal policy remain distinct.

### 3. Current and Point-in-Time Accuracy

The output records eCFR currency and relevant historical dates, checks amendment sources, and separates publication, effective, applicability, compliance, and implementation dates. Proposed, delayed, expired, superseded, or future-effective text is labeled correctly.

### 4. Cross-Provider Discipline

Hospital, long-term-care, HHA, CAH, REH, CMHC, ASC, hospice, ESRD, RHC/FQHC, supplier, laboratory, and other rules are not transferred across provider types without an authoritative basis. Mixed-purpose parts and state-directed provisions are handled narrowly.

### 5. Evidence Traceability

Each requirement maps to an authorized evidence population/sample, period, source, custodian, test, result status, gap, owner, reviewer, and recheck trigger. Policy design and operational implementation remain distinct.

### 6. Finding Discipline

The output does not turn missing evidence into a deficiency, infer full compliance from accreditation or a sample, or invent tag, scope/severity, immediate jeopardy, corrective action, certification, sanction, termination, or appeal outcomes.

### 7. Direct Answer and Uncertainty

The answer distinguishes verified, provided, assumed, needs-verification, not-applicable-pending-review, and not-assessed content. When provider scope or authority is unresolved, it declines an applicability or compliance conclusion.

### 8. Safety and Authorization

Protected information remains protected. External communication, disclosure, system access, operational change, plan of correction, certification, or enforcement response requires explicit authorization and qualified accountable review.

## Reviewer Record

Record reviewer role and qualifications, reviewed artifact version, provider/supplier context, sources and captured dates, criterion ratings, material findings, unresolved evidence, required corrections, and disposition.

Do not publish an aggregate score unless the benchmark tasks, evaluator configuration, matched conditions, reviewer method, and aggregate results are approved and reproducible.
