# Provider-Type and Point-in-Time Compliance Contract

## Source Note

Current codified requirements come from the exact eCFR part, subpart, and section applicable to the provider/supplier and event. The eCFR is continuously updated and authoritative but unofficial. Use its point-in-time tools and cited Federal Register amendment to resolve historical or transitional requirements. CMS State Operations Manual material supplies survey procedures and interpretive guidance; it does not replace the regulation. State law, Medicaid, accreditation, provider-agreement, and organization requirements need separate verification.

## Applicability Record

Do not assess a requirement until this record is complete or each gap is marked `Needs verification`:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Entity and program | Legal entity, Medicare/Medicaid participation, provider agreement or supplier approval |
| Provider/supplier type | CMS enrollment/certification classification and governing definition |
| Certification scope | CCN or equivalent, locations, campuses, departments, branches, distinct parts, services, excluded components |
| Survey pathway | State Survey Agency, CMS, CMS-approved deeming program, or other applicable process and scope |
| Event and review dates | Service, incident, policy, survey, amendment, and evidence periods |
| Governing authority | Exact part, subpart, section, version, source URL, and captured currency date |
| Additional layers | State/local law, Medicaid, accreditation, CMS memorandum, provider agreement, organization policy |

## Provider/Supplier Starter Map

This map is a navigation aid, not an exhaustive applicability determination.

| Provider or supplier context | Starting federal source | Boundary to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Ambulatory surgical center | 42 CFR Part 416 | Resolve the applicable ASC CfC subpart; do not treat payment provisions as the CfC set. |
| Hospice | 42 CFR Part 418 | Resolve the applicable hospice CoP and program provisions for the event date. |
| PACE organization | 42 CFR Part 460 | Use PACE-specific requirements and program agreement context; do not import hospital CoPs. |
| Hospital, including applicable specialty provisions | 42 CFR Part 482 | Confirm the certified hospital and every included/excluded location or component. |
| Long-term-care facility or ICF/IID | Applicable Part 483 subpart | Separate provider/facility requirements from provisions directed to states or state agencies. |
| Home health agency | Part 484 participation subparts | Keep HHA CoPs distinct from payment and value-based purchasing provisions. |
| CORF, REH, CAH, outpatient therapy provider, or CMHC | Provider-specific Part 485 subpart | Never transfer a requirement between the separate provider types. |
| Portable X-ray, OPO, or home infusion supplier | Service-specific Part 486 subpart | Resolve supplier/service scope and any distinct accreditation process. |
| RHC or FQHC | 42 CFR Part 491, Subpart A | Preserve the rule's certification/CfC terminology and different program pathways. |
| Clinical laboratory | 42 CFR Part 493 | Treat CLIA requirements and processes as a distinct regime. |
| ESRD facility | 42 CFR Part 494 | Apply ESRD CfCs to the covered facility and services. |

Also inspect Parts 488 and 489 when survey, certification, enforcement, provider agreements, supplier approval, effective dates, termination, or related process is at issue.

## Point-in-Time Rule Record

For every relied-on provision, capture:

- provider/supplier type and certification scope;
- exact citation and heading;
- current eCFR URL and displayed currency date;
- historical/event date and point-in-time text;
- Federal Register citation for relevant amendments;
- publication, effective, applicability, compliance, and implementation dates, each separately;
- proposed, final, delayed, expired, superseded, stayed, waived, or current status as supported;
- CMS manual appendix/transmittal or memorandum version, if used;
- reviewer and date.

If any date or version is unresolved, state `Needs verification` and do not assert the requirement applied.

## Requirement-to-Evidence Ledger

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Requirement | Quote minimally or paraphrase; cite exact section and version |
| Applicability | Provider, location/component, service, period, and rationale |
| Evidence | Named artifact, population/sample, period, system/source, and custodian |
| Test | What was inspected and the acceptance criterion authorized by the reviewer |
| Result | `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable - rationale pending review`, or `Not assessed` |
| Gap | Missing, conflicting, stale, incomplete, or out-of-scope evidence without converting it into a deficiency |
| Accountability | Owner, qualified reviewer, decision, due date, and recheck trigger |

## Finding and Decision Boundary

- Missing evidence is a gap, not automatically noncompliance.
- A policy statement is not proof of implementation.
- A sample does not prove the unsampled population.
- Accreditation does not establish deemed compliance unless the CMS-approved program, provider type, scope, and relevant status are verified.
- An interpretive guideline or survey tag does not replace the regulatory text.
- A survey finding, scope/severity, immediate-jeopardy determination, plan of correction, certification action, sanction, termination, or appeal outcome must come from authorized official evidence and accountable review.

## Safeguards

Use minimum-necessary access. Do not place PHI, PII, personnel, credentialing, peer-review, complaint, survey, accreditation, legal, security, or other protected content into public outputs. Do not access systems, contact authorities, change policies or care, submit a plan of correction, certify compliance, disclose records, or take an enforcement-related action without explicit authorization.
