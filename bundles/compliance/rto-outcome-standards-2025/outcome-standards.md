# RTO 2025 Outcome Standards Evidence Contract

## Source Note

Legislation supplies binding text. ASQA, DEWR and TAC materials explain policy or regulatory practice. Preserve exact instrument/document title, version, regulator, activity, and event/decision date.

## Applicability Matrix

| Context | Starting source | Date boundary |
|---|---|---|
| ASQA/NVR RTO | F2025L00354 | Commenced 1 July 2025; verify standards effective when the decision is made and any continuing regulatory activity. |
| TAC/WA RTO | Current WA Regulations and TAC `Outcome Standards - Registration Standards 2025` | Commenced 1 Oct 2025; transition ran 1 Oct-31 Dec; verify regulation 64 treatment for a pending matter. |

Record legal entity, RTO code, regulator, registration/scope, training product, cohort, mode, location, third party, activity/application/audit, evidence period, decision date, instrument/version, and transition rationale. Mark unresolved facts `Needs verification`.

## Component Boundary

- Outcome Standards address four quality areas and the outcomes an RTO is expected to achieve.
- Compliance Requirements are a separate instrument or regulator document covering administrative and integrity obligations.
- Credential Policy is a separate component for trainer, assessor, and validation credentials.
- Where one operational topic engages multiple components, create separate source-to-requirement rows. Do not import a compliance or credential obligation into an Outcome Standard.

## Four Quality Areas

1. Training and assessment: training is engaging and aligned; assessment is fit for purpose, fair, valid and supported by systematic validation.
2. VET student support: information, diversity and inclusion, wellbeing, progression, feedback and complaints are addressed for the relevant cohort and context.
3. VET workforce: the workforce is sufficient, capable, current and appropriately supported; apply the separate Credential Policy where credentials are at issue.
4. Governance: accountable governance, risk management, integrity, monitoring and continuous improvement support sustained quality outcomes.

Use the exact Standard and performance indicator in the operative source. These summaries are navigation aids, not substitutes for the instrument.

## Outcome-to-Evidence Ledger

For each row record exact Standard/performance indicator, regulator/source/version, training product/cohort/mode/location/third party, evidence population and period, intended outcome, observed measure, owner, reviewer, status, gap and recheck trigger.

Statuses: `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`.

A policy or process can show design, not that the intended outcome occurred. A sample cannot establish whole-population performance unless its basis and limits are documented. Missing evidence does not automatically establish non-compliance. Do not invent learner outcomes, audit findings, transition eligibility, rectification, regulator decisions, or required action.
