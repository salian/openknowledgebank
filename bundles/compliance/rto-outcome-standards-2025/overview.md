# Overview

The Commonwealth Outcome instrument is the `National Vocational Education and Training Regulator (Outcome Standards for NVR Registered Training Organisations) Instrument 2025`. Schedule 1 groups Standards 1.1-4.4 into training and assessment, VET student support, VET workforce, and governance.

Outcome Standards are one component of the 2025 framework. Compliance Requirements address separate administrative and integrity matters; the Credential Policy separately sets trainer, assessor and validation credential requirements. A topic may cross components, but its source and requirement must remain distinct.

For ASQA-regulated NVR RTOs, the Commonwealth instrument commenced 1 July 2025. TAC uses WA `Outcome Standards - Registration Standards 2025`: WA substantive amendments commenced 1 October 2025, transition ran through 31 December 2025, and regulation 64 preserves specified pending matters. Resolve regulator and activity/decision status before selecting a source or date.

This is not a universal checklist or authority to disclose, contact, submit, decide, or change operations.
