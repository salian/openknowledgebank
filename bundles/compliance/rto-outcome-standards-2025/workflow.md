# RTO 2025 Outcome Standards Review Workflow

1. Identify entity/RTO code, ASQA or TAC, registration/scope, training products, cohorts, modes, locations, third parties, activity and decision dates.
2. Capture exact current and relevant historical legislation, regulator documents and guidance; preserve source type and version.
3. Determine the ASQA 1 July 2025 boundary or WA 1 October 2025/regulation 64 position. Record rationale; do not assume.
4. Classify each issue as Outcome Standard, Compliance Requirement, Credential Policy, or another obligation; create separate rows when it crosses components.
5. Map each exact Standard/performance indicator to authorised evidence, population/period, intended outcome, observed measure, owner, reviewer, status and gap.
6. Test whether evidence shows design, implementation, or outcome; state sampling and coverage limits.
7. Reconcile Verified/Provided/Assumed/Needs verification facts and give only the narrow supported answer.
8. Escalate legal, audit, registration, transition, learner-safety, non-compliance, rectification and regulator communication to accountable reviewers.
9. Monitor instrument amendments, regulator guidance, registration/scope, training products, third parties, cohorts and decision dates.
