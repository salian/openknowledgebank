# Quality Check

Qualitative only; no measured score. Rate Meets/Partially/Does not/Not assessed:

1. Regulator, entity, registration/scope, training context, and dates are explicit.
2. Instrument/document names, versions and operative dates are exact.
3. ASQA and TAC commencement/transition are not conflated.
4. Outcome Standards, Compliance Requirements, Credential Policy and guidance remain distinct.
5. Exact Standards/performance indicators trace to scoped evidence populations and periods.
6. Evidence distinguishes design, implementation and achieved outcome, with sample limits stated.
7. No invented compliance, outcome, audit, transition, rectification or regulator-decision claim.
8. Protected data and external/change actions require authority and accountable review.

Publish no aggregate score without approved tasks, config, matched outputs and qualified reviewer results.
