# SEBI IA Disclosure Evidence Contract

## Source Note

Use the IA Regulations operative on the event date for regulations 15 and 18. Use the 6 February 2026 Master Circular and any later circular for its detailed direction. Firm policy, disclosure text, delivery records, client facts, and orders are separate evidence classes.

## Applicability And Version

Record legal entity and registration status, client or prospective-client audience, service, product/security, recommendation or conduct event, event and delivery dates, regulation/circular version, and source rationale.

- The current IA Regulations are consolidated through 25 November 2025.
- Regulation 18(2)-(3) were omitted effective 30 September 2020.
- Regulation 18(8)-(9) were inserted effective 16 December 2024.
- A historical event uses the text and circulars effective at that time.

If audience, event, date, source, or underlying fact is absent, mark it `Needs verification`.

## Current Duty Map

| Source | Trigger and audience | Evidence focus |
|---|---|---|
| 18(1) | prospective client deciding whether to use the adviser | verified adviser business, disciplinary history, service terms, affiliations, and other decision-relevant facts |
| 18(4) | advice concerns a financial product or security | adviser's verified holding or position, including an evidenced absence where appropriate |
| 18(5) | actual or potential conflict from issuer connection/association | connection, conflict, facts affecting objectivity/independence, content, timing, and delivery |
| 18(6) | investment advice is made | material key-feature facts, particularly the supported performance track record, and delivery evidence |
| 18(7) | recommended product has warnings/disclaimers in documents or advertising | identified warning/disclaimer and evidence that attention was drawn to it |
| 18(8) | advisory service concerns products/services outside SEBI purview | scope determination, required no-SEBI-purview/no-recourse disclosure, and current circular evidence |
| 18(9) | AI tools are used in providing investment advice | verified extent of use and disclosure evidence |
| 15(1), 15(5) | conflicts arise in the stated fiduciary/activity context | exact conflict, timing, audience, and disclosure evidence |
| Current circular | agreement, cost, consent, other-activity, channel, or other specified trigger | exact circular section, effective date, scope, content, and evidence |

The table is navigation, not substitute text. Do not revive omitted 18(2)-(3) for a current event. Do not infer that a historical compensation duty is absent from every other current source.

## Materiality Issue Record

Record the exact source phrase, audience and decision, verified fact, potential significance, qualitative/quantitative context where relevant, information available at the time, contrary evidence, timing, reviewer, rationale, and unresolved question.

The regulations do not supply one universal materiality threshold for every disclosure. Do not label a fact material or immaterial solely because it appears on a generic list.

## Disclosure-Event Ledger

For each duty record source/effective date, trigger, audience, deadline/timing, content basis, local evidence, approval/reviewer, delivery method/evidence, version, status, gap, and recheck trigger.

Statuses: `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`.

A template or policy does not prove accurate content or timely delivery. A delivery record does not prove understanding. Missing evidence does not itself prove breach. Do not invent firm, client, conflict, holding, disciplinary, product, performance, warning, AI, cost, consent, delivery, remediation, or regulator facts.
