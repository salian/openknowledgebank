# Quality Check

Qualitative only; no measured score. Rate Meets/Partially/Does not/Not assessed:

1. Entity/registration, audience, service, product/security, event, and dates are explicit.
2. The then-operative regulation, Master Circular, and later circulars are versioned and distinct.
3. Current regulation 18(1), (4)-(9), regulations 15(1)/(5), and circular duties retain separate triggers.
4. Omitted regulation 18(2)-(3) is not presented as current; current cost duties trace to current sources.
5. Materiality rationale uses verified context and accountable review, not an invented universal threshold.
6. Content, approval, delivery, receipt, and understanding evidence remain distinct and population limits are stated.
7. No invented firm/client fact, conflict, holding, disciplinary history, AI use, disclosure, breach, or regulator position appears.
8. Sensitive evidence and consequential communication/action require authority and professional review.

Publish no aggregate score without approved tasks, config, matched outputs, and qualified reviewer results.
