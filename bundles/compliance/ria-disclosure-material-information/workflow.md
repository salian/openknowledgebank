# RIA Disclosure Review Workflow

1. Identify entity/registration, audience, service, product/security, recommendation or conduct event, and event/delivery dates.
2. Capture the IA Regulations and circulars operative on the event date; separate current, historical, guidance, firm policy, disclosure text, records, and orders.
3. Map each issue to the exact regulation 15/18 or circular trigger. Do not treat omitted 18(2)-(3) as current.
4. Record the verified underlying fact and whether the duty is prospective-client, current-client, advice-time, conflict-time, onboarding, annual, or channel-specific.
5. For a materiality issue, document audience/decision context, significance, evidence, counterevidence, timing, rationale, reviewer, and uncertainty without inventing a threshold.
6. Compare required content and timing with authorised disclosure and delivery evidence; distinguish template, approval, delivery, receipt, and understanding.
7. Reconcile Verified/Provided/Assumed/Needs verification facts and state only the narrow supported gap or conclusion.
8. Escalate legal interpretation, client harm, conflict, disciplinary, performance, outside-SEBI, AI, breach, remediation, complaint, or regulator communication to an accountable reviewer.
9. Recheck SEBI regulations, the current Master Circular, later circulars, registration, source version, and local facts before reuse.
