# Overview

Current regulation 18 of the `Securities and Exchange Board of India (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013` contains sub-regulations (1) and (4)-(9). It addresses adviser information for prospective clients, holdings or positions, issuer-related conflicts and objectivity, product/security key features, warnings/disclaimers, outside-SEBI services, and the extent of AI-tool use.

Former sub-regulations 18(2)-(3), concerning specified compensation connected to distribution/execution services and intermediary recommendations, were omitted effective 30 September 2020. They are historical, not current regulation 18 duties. Outside-SEBI and AI-use provisions 18(8)-(9) were inserted effective 16 December 2024.

Regulations 15(1) and 15(5), the current Master Circular dated 6 February 2026, and later circulars may supply separate disclosure duties. Each requires its own trigger, audience, timing, and evidence row.

`Material` is context-dependent. No firm fact, threshold, disclosure content, delivery, client understanding, or breach is assumed without verified evidence and accountable review.
