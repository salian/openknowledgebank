## Current-Source Note

Map each purpose and data category to a current LGPD basis; do not use one basis for an undifferentiated processing inventory. Article 7 and Article 11 differ, and legitimate interest does not apply to sensitive data. Document purpose, necessity, expectations, balancing and safeguards where applicable, transparency, rights, retention, sharing, and changes.

Verify source status, effective dates, amendments, jurisdiction, entity scope, and local facts before relying on this bundle.
