# Overview

Use this bundle to review Medicaid claim readiness, submission evidence, adjudication, payment reconciliation, and unresolved compliance questions without inventing billing or claim facts.

## What This Bundle Is For

- Separate current federal Medicaid baselines from state, program, plan, fiscal-agent, provider-type, service, and payer implementation rules.
- Organize source-backed evidence for enrollment, eligibility, coverage, authorization, TPL, EVV, documentation, coding, submission, adjudication, and payment.
- Distinguish transport acknowledgement, payer acceptance, adjudication, payment, and reconciliation.
- Produce review-ready gaps, decisions, owners, and source records.

## Federal Baseline

Federal sources establish selected state-plan claims-processing requirements, national transaction standards, NPI, TPL/COB, Medicaid NCCI, EVV, and program-integrity context. They do not by themselves establish that a particular provider claim is complete, timely under the applicable provider rule, covered, accepted, payable, paid, or compliant.

## Needs Verification: State and Program

Verify the current state, program, plan, fiscal agent, managed-care or fee-for-service arrangement, provider and claim type, service, location, dates, effective sources, companion instructions, edits, deadlines, rates, attachments, EVV implementation, denial handling, correction, appeal, refund, and overpayment process. Treat every unresolved item as `Needs verification`; do not infer it from a federal baseline or a different payer's instructions.

## What This Bundle Is Not For

- It is not legal, coding, clinical, reimbursement, fraud, or payer-contract advice.
- It does not replace current state Medicaid agency, plan, fiscal-agent, clearinghouse, companion-guide, code-set, contract, or qualified professional review.
- It does not submit, change, correct, void, appeal, refund, disclose, or check a real claim, eligibility record, authorization, or payment.

## Risk Boundary

Protect PHI, PII, credentials, claim identifiers, clinical documentation, financial information, and payer communications. Require explicit authority and qualified review before any live submission, disclosure, payer contact, correction, appeal, refund, or record change.
