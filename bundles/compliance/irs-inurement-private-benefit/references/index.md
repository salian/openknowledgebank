Current-source note: IRS guidance updated in June 2026 distinguishes inurement to insiders from private benefit to insiders or outsiders. Any inurement can jeopardize exemption, while non-insider private benefit requires qualitative and quantitative incidental-benefit analysis; reasonable fair-value payments require evidence. Verify effective dates, transition rules, entity scope, official forms or portals, and later amendments before relying on the guidance.
