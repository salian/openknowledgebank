# Investment Adviser Advertisement Code and Prior BASL Approval Source-Aware Compliance Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/apr-2023/advertisement-code-for-investment-advisers-ia-and-research-analysts-ra-_69798.html
- https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/sep-2024/1727418139473.pdf
- https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/jun-2026/sebi-proposes-common-advertisement-code-for-specified-regulated-entities_102337.html

SEBI materials do not establish adviser registration, current future-rule applicability, claim truth, testimonial consent, warning sufficiency, BASL approval, approval number, suspension status, publication, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, statute, rule, circular, manual, edit file, FAQ, and form sources with effective date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected entity, sender, adviser, provider, claim, notice, transaction, record, consent, calculation, submission, and exception evidence.
- Named owner, qualified local legal or regulatory review, control validation, signatory, submission, retention, and approval authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable BASL advertisement approval and publication evidence record with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to give investment advice, publish unsupported claims, use testimonials without consent, omit warnings, fabricate approval numbers, submit or publish without authority, continue during suspension, or declare compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent registration, category, current-rule applicability, claim, performance, testimonial consent, warning sufficiency, prior approval, approval number, suspension status, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
