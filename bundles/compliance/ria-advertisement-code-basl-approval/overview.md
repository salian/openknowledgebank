# Investment Adviser Advertisement Code and Prior BASL Approval Overview

Verify adviser identity, SEBI registration, category, services, brand and publication authority; distinguish enacted rules from the June 2026 proposal and monitor its disposition; classify every covered advertisement and channel; substantiate fees, credentials, performance, comparisons and testimonials with required periods and limitations; include current warnings and disclosures; route final content through the applicable BASL or successor approval process; display the verified approval number; check suspension restrictions; preserve versions, approval, publication and withdrawal records.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes adviser entity registration category and publication authority, current enacted SEBI BASL and successor requirements with effective dates, proposal-versus-effective status record, advertisement inventory channel audience and version, service fee credential performance comparison and testimonial substantiation, consent disclosures warnings and risk statements, media and brand rights, BASL submission queries approval and approval number, suspension check, publication monitoring withdrawal retention and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish registration, category, current-rule applicability, claim, performance, testimonial consent, warning sufficiency, prior approval, approval number, suspension status, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
