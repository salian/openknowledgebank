# Home Mortgage Disclosure Act and Regulation C Source-Aware Compliance Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1003/5/
- https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/documentation/fig/

Controlling and agency sources state general requirements and interpretations; they do not establish local coverage, facts, exceptions, compliance, privilege, filing status, legal advice, or approval.

## Evidence Required

- Current controlling text, amendments, effective dates, agency interpretations, orders, and applicable jurisdiction.
- Inspected entity, product, transaction, communication, consumer, consent, disclosure, record, control, and filing evidence.
- Qualified legal or compliance reviewer, accountable owner, remediation, retention, validation, and approval evidence.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable HMDA applicability, data, filing, and disclosure review brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before classify an institution or transaction, submit or correct a loan application register, publish disclosures, expose applicant data, certify, or represent HMDA compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent institution or transaction coverage, reportable field values, filing frequency or deadline, data accuracy, disclosure duty, exception, violation, filing status, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
