# Investment Advice and Distribution Segregation Source-Aware Compliance Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2024/annual-compliance-certificate-for-client-level-segregation-by-non-individual-investment-advisers-and-timeline-for-submission-of-periodic-reports_87975.html
- https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/aug-2024/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-investment-advisers-regulations-2013-last-amended-on-august-01-2024-_85362.html
- https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/sep-2024/1727418139473.pdf

SEBI sources do not establish local adviser category, group scope, client identity, advisory or distribution relationship, segregation, exception, signatory authority, submission, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, statute, rule, circular, manual, edit file, FAQ, and form sources with effective date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected entity, sender, adviser, provider, claim, notice, transaction, record, consent, calculation, submission, and exception evidence.
- Named owner, qualified local legal or regulatory review, control validation, signatory, submission, retention, and approval authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable client-level segregation certificate support and evidence matrix with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to give investment advice, invent client relationships, hide distribution or conflicts, alter source records, approve exceptions, sign or submit certificates, change business activities, or declare compliance without authority.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent adviser category, group scope, client identity, advisory relationship, distribution activity, segregation, exception, certificate accuracy, signatory authority, submission, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
