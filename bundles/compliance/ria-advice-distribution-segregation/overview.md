# Investment Advice and Distribution Segregation Overview

Verify non-individual adviser, registration, group entities, products, clients, review period and current requirements; map each client and related persons across advisory and distribution systems; define identifiers, dates and exceptions under qualified Indian review; reconcile agreements, advice records, commissions, referrals and transaction evidence; investigate conflicts and data gaps; prepare the prescribed certificate and periodic reports from evidence; verify signatory authority and deadlines; submit only through approved channels; retain receipts and remediation records.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes adviser registration entity category and review authority, current regulations circular forms effective and transition dates, group entity product and service inventory, client and related-person identifiers, advisory agreements advice and fee records, distribution referral transaction commission and consideration records, source-system mappings deduplication and reconciliation, exception conflict and remediation log, certificate form calculations and signatory authority, periodic reports submission receipts correspondence retention and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish adviser category, group scope, client identity, advisory relationship, distribution activity, segregation, exception, certificate accuracy, signatory authority, submission, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
