# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current controlling and interpretive sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, applicability, exceptions, and local procedures.
3. Inventory adviser registration entity category and review authority, current regulations circular forms effective and transition dates, group entity product and service inventory, client and related-person identifiers, advisory agreements advice and fee records, distribution referral transaction commission and consideration records, source-system mappings deduplication and reconciliation, exception conflict and remediation log, certificate form calculations and signatory authority, periodic reports submission receipts correspondence retention and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the client-level segregation certificate support and evidence matrix, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to give investment advice, invent client relationships, hide distribution or conflicts, alter source records, approve exceptions, sign or submit certificates, change business activities, or declare compliance without authority.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
