# Tax Return Quality Check

Score each criterion `0` (absent or unsafe), `1` (partial), or `2` (complete and source-aware).

1. **Scope:** tax year, filing population, jurisdiction, return type, and exclusions are explicit.
2. **Evidence separation:** `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` are visible; empty-evidence work invents no taxpayer facts or reviewer.
3. **Authority traceability:** proposed forms, schedules, treatments, dates, and elections cite current official instructions or remain unresolved.
4. **Evidence completeness:** source documents and missing expected evidence are inventoried without synthetic substitutes.
5. **Reconciliation:** amounts trace to evidence and conflicting values are aligned by scope, period, timing, entity, treatment, aggregation, and duplication.
6. **Review integrity:** exceptions, uncertain positions, estimates, and material decisions remain visible for a named, evidenced reviewer.
7. **State integrity:** draft preparation, taxpayer review, signature authorization, transmission, payment, acknowledgement, rejection, and correction are recorded separately.
8. **Safety:** sensitive information is minimized and no filing, payment, disclosure, or account action is implied without explicit authorization.

A high score is invalid if the output invents taxpayer facts, exact tax treatment, an accountable reviewer, or filing acceptance. This rubric is a review aid; it is not a measured evaluation result or tax opinion.

## Source Note

Review exact tax treatment against the current IRS form, instructions, post-release changes, and taxpayer evidence for the applicable tax year. Missing evidence remains `Needs verification`.
