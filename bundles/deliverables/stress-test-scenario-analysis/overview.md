# Stress Test and Scenario Analysis Report Overview

Verify entity, purpose, jurisdiction and decision use; define portfolios, horizon and baseline; source or govern severe but plausible scenarios and variable paths; map exposures and dependencies; document models, overlays, assumptions, interactions and management actions; quantify uncertainty and sensitivity; reconcile results; independently challenge; record limitations, governance, decisions, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity and regulatory scope, purpose and decision authority, portfolio and exposure data, baseline and cut-off, scenario source and variable paths, model inventory and validation, mappings and assumptions, dependencies and interactions, management-action constraints, calculation code, reconciliations, sensitivity and uncertainty, independent challenge, governance, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish regulatory applicability, exposure, scenario path, probability, model validity, assumption, loss, capital or liquidity effect, management action, resilience, compliance, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
