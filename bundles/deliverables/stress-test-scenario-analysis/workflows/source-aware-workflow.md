# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory entity and regulatory scope, purpose and decision authority, portfolio and exposure data, baseline and cut-off, scenario source and variable paths, model inventory and validation, mappings and assumptions, dependencies and interactions, management-action constraints, calculation code, reconciliations, sensitivity and uncertainty, independent challenge, governance, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the stress test and scenario analysis report, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access restricted financial data, alter scenarios or models, assume management actions, declare resilience or failure, change capital or liquidity plans, report to regulators, or approve decisions.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
