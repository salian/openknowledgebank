# Stress Test and Scenario Analysis Report Source-Aware Deliverable Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-stress-test-scenarios.htm
- https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d450.htm

Federal Reserve 2026 scenarios and Basel principles apply to specific supervisory and banking contexts; they do not establish local applicability, exposures, scenario severity, model validity, losses, capital or liquidity effects, resilience, compliance, or required action.

## Evidence Required

- Current authoritative or originator sources with edition, date, scope, and definitions.
- Inspected local inputs, records, assumptions, constraints, alternatives, calculations, and outcomes.
- Named owners, validation evidence, qualified review where required, approval, and distribution authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable stress test and scenario analysis report with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access restricted financial data, alter scenarios or models, assume management actions, declare resilience or failure, change capital or liquidity plans, report to regulators, or approve decisions.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent regulatory applicability, exposure, scenario path, probability, model validity, assumption, loss, capital or liquidity effect, management action, resilience, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
