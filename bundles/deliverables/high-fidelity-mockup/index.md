# High-Fidelity Mockup

Source-aware deliverable bundle for preparing a visually complete digital interface mockup while separating visual polish from interaction coverage, accessibility evidence, usability evidence, and production implementation.

## Required Response Contract

Every substantive response must contain:

1. **Direct answer** - state what can be concluded or prepared now.
2. **Evidence status** - list `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately, writing `None` where a category is empty.
3. **Verification plan** - name source category, scope, date or version, and conflict checks.
4. **Confirmation boundary** - identify the accountable reviewer and actions prohibited without explicit approval.
5. **Source note** - name sources used and material limitations.

Do not call a mockup implemented, usable, accessible, approved, or production-ready without the corresponding evidence.
