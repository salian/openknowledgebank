# Board and Executive Product Update Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware executive product reporting and decisions analysis and a reviewable board or executive product update.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering audience, meeting, product scope, strategy, and requested decisions, customer problem, research, adoption, retention, and outcome evidence, roadmap, delivery, quality, reliability, and dependency status, financial, commercial, capacity, and investment evidence, risks, assumptions, options, tradeoffs, and forecast ranges, and owners, approvals, prior commitments, and follow-up.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer outcome, delivery status, metric, forecast, risk, and executive decision.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before making board representations, committing roadmap, spend, headcount, forecasts, or customer claims without accountable review.
