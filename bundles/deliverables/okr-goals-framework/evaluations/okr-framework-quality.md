# Quality Check

Review a completed [OKR framework brief](../deliverables/okr-framework-brief.md). A high-quality artifact may be incomplete when evidence is missing, but it must label the gap and identify the exact next check.

## Adversarial Checks

- With no local evidence, does it avoid invented strategy, metrics, targets, owners, dates, cadence, and approvals?
- With conflicting metric sources, does it compare definitions, scope, time, version, calculation, source-of-record, and ownership?
- Does it preserve prompt-supplied facts under `Provided`?
- Does it avoid treating initiative completion as outcome proof?
