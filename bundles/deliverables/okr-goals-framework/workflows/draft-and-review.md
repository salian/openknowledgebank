# Draft and Review

1. State the deliverable, decision, audience, scope, and time horizon.
2. Inventory sources and label each item `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
3. Extract strategy and priorities; if absent, stop at a framework skeleton.
4. Draft objectives as concise outcome-oriented directions.
5. Draft key results as measurable evidence. Record definition, baseline, target, unit, segment, source, owner, and review date; mark missing values.
6. Separate initiatives, dependencies, and risks from results.
7. Check whether results support the objective and whether any is only an activity.
8. Apply local commitment, scoring, and review policy; otherwise leave fields unconfirmed.
9. Present conflicts and the smallest decision needed.
10. Request confirmation before publishing commitments, changing live goals, communicating employee-impacting decisions, or linking goals to compensation.

Use [OKR framework brief](../deliverables/okr-framework-brief.md). When sources disagree, compare definition, population, identifier, time window, version, calculation, processing state, owner, and permissions before choosing a value.
