# OKR Framework Brief

## Direct Answer

State the proposed framework, decision requested, scope, and time horizon.

## Evidence Status

### Verified

- Public framework facts and inspected local sources with date/version.

### Provided

- Facts explicitly supplied by the requester or local artifacts.

### Assumed

- Temporary drafting assumptions only, each with an expiry for verification.

### Needs verification

- Missing or conflicting strategy, metric, ownership, cadence, scoring, policy, and approval facts.

## Initiatives and Dependencies

Separate proposed work from the results it is intended to influence. Record dependency owner, decision needed, and evidence.

## Review Logic

Record local check-in cadence, status method, scoring rules, source-of-record, escalation threshold, reviewer, approver, and next review date. Use `Needs verification` where policy is not supplied.

## Risks and Confirmation Boundary

Include attribution, metric gaming, data quality, dependency, privacy, employee sensitivity, and commitment risks. State `Accountable reviewer: Needs verification` when no reviewer is evidenced. Do not publish commitments, change live goals, send employee-facing communications, or connect goals to compensation without confirmation.
