# Overview

Use this bundle when the requested output is a concrete organizational or team goals artifact, not merely an explanation of OKRs.

## Inputs Required

- Planning decision, audience, scope, and time horizon.
- Strategy or priority evidence with source date/version.
- Metric definitions, baselines, targets, units, segments, and data owners.
- Objective/KR owners, reviewer, approver, check-in cadence, and status method.
- Local policy for committed or aspirational goals and any performance-management relationship.

## Quality Bar

Objectives describe meaningful outcomes or direction. Key results make progress measurable and verifiable. Initiatives remain separate from results. Unknown local facts remain visibly unverified.
