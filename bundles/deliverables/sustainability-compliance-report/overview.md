# Sustainability Compliance Report Overview

Determine entity, group, listing, size, jurisdiction and reporting period from evidence; verify enacted requirements, transition dates and current standards rather than relying on proposals; document materiality process and reporting boundaries; trace every metric and narrative to systems, methods, factors and owners; separate estimates and unavailable value-chain data; reconcile financial links; assess controls and prior findings; preserve governance approvals and assurance evidence; distinguish preparation, assurance, compliance and filing acceptance.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes reporting entity group listing size jurisdiction and period, enacted current requirements transition and standard versions, scope and applicability legal memo, governance and materiality process, value-chain and consolidation boundaries, metric definitions source systems owners methods factors estimates and controls, narrative and target substantiation, financial reconciliations and prior-period changes, gaps limitations remediation, assurance requests findings and report, approval filing receipt and publication record. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish entity scope, effective requirement, materiality, boundary, metric, estimate, target, control effectiveness, assurance, compliance, filing acceptance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
