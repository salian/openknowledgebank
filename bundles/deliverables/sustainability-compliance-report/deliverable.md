# Sustainability Compliance Report Source-Aware Deliverable Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://finance.ec.europa.eu/financial-markets/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/corporate-sustainability-reporting_en
- https://finance.ec.europa.eu/regulation-and-supervision/financial-services-legislation/implementing-and-delegated-acts/corporate-sustainability-reporting-directive_en
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2464/oj/eng

EU sources do not establish a local entity's scope, effective obligations, materiality, reporting boundary, metric accuracy, assurance conclusion, compliance, or filing acceptance.

## Evidence Required

- Current controlling, standards-owner, regulator, platform, contract, research, accounting, tax, or assurance sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected source records, definitions, lineage, calculations, assumptions, exclusions, uncertainty, reconciliations, and approvals.
- Named preparer, owner, qualified reviewer, signatory where required, retention, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable sustainability compliance report and evidence index with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to assume proposed rules apply, fabricate metrics or estimates, suppress material impacts, change source records, claim assurance or compliance, sign file or publish reports, or represent regulator acceptance without authority.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent entity scope, effective requirement, materiality, boundary, metric, estimate, target, control effectiveness, assurance, compliance, filing acceptance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
