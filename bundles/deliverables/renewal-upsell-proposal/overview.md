# Renewal and Upsell Proposal Overview

Verify customer, account team, contract, renewal date and proposal authority; reconcile licensed scope, usage, support and adoption from defined systems; substantiate outcomes and testimonials; distinguish attributed value from causal ROI; identify customer-stated priorities, stakeholders, risks and unresolved issues; scope expansion against product availability and security or legal requirements; use current approved pricing, tax and concession rules; document options and assumptions; require product, finance, legal and commercial approval before delivery or negotiation.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes customer account and proposal authority, executed contract amendments renewal notice and current entitlements, stakeholder map and customer-provided priorities, usage adoption support and outcome records with definitions, testimonial and logo consent, ROI inputs assumptions and limitations, expansion use-case product availability security privacy and integration review, approved price packaging tax discount and concession matrix, options risks redlines delivery and negotiation record, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish contract term, entitlement, usage, adoption, customer outcome, ROI, stakeholder support, product fit, price, concession, renewal likelihood, expansion, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
