# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory customer account and proposal authority, executed contract amendments renewal notice and current entitlements, stakeholder map and customer-provided priorities, usage adoption support and outcome records with definitions, testimonial and logo consent, ROI inputs assumptions and limitations, expansion use-case product availability security privacy and integration review, approved price packaging tax discount and concession matrix, options risks redlines delivery and negotiation record, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the source-linked renewal and expansion proposal, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent usage or outcomes, expose customer data, misstate product availability, offer unauthorized price or concessions, change contracts, send proposals, negotiate or sign, or guarantee renewal or expansion.
