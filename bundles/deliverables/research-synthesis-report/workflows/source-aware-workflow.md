# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory sponsor question intended use and protocol, search and source inventory, inclusion exclusion and screening record, study population method date and limitation extraction, quote statistic and artifact provenance, coding framework reviewer agreement and exceptions, quantitative definitions transformations and reconciliation, contradictory and negative evidence, confidence criteria and sensitivity, privacy consent and de-identification, evidence-to-finding and recommendation map, limitations and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the traceable research synthesis report and evidence matrix, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to fabricate sources quotes or participants, expose research data, combine incomparable measures, overstate prevalence or causality, suppress contradictions, publish findings, or trigger decisions without authority.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
