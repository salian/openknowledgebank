# Incident Postmortem Report Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware incident learning and corrective action analysis and a reviewable incident postmortem report.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering incident scope, severity, dates, systems, and stakeholders, timestamped timeline and source records, user, business, data, security, and service impact, detection, escalation, mitigation, recovery, and communication evidence, trigger, contributing conditions, control gaps, and uncertainty, and corrective actions, owners, priorities, due dates, verification, and closure evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer timeline event, impact, root or contributing cause, individual intent, remediation effectiveness, and closure status.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before assigning blame, making legal, security, employment, or public claims, closing actions, or publishing the report without accountable review.
