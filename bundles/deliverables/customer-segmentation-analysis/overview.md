# Customer Segmentation Analysis Overview

Define purpose and prohibited uses, population and period, consent and lawful basis, identity resolution, variables, missingness, method, number of segments, stability, sensitivity, labels, uncertainty, fairness, validation, monitoring, retention, access, and decision governance.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes decision purpose and prohibited uses, jurisdiction and lawful basis, population and period, data dictionary and provenance, consent and identity rules, missingness and bias, feature transformations, method and parameters, stability and sensitivity, fairness review, validation, retention, access, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish customer identity, consent, lawful basis, trait, segment membership, segment meaning, stability, fairness, causal behavior, value, targeting eligibility, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
