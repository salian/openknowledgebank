# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory decision purpose and prohibited uses, jurisdiction and lawful basis, population and period, data dictionary and provenance, consent and identity rules, missingness and bias, feature transformations, method and parameters, stability and sensitivity, fairness review, validation, retention, access, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the customer segmentation analysis report, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before identify or profile individuals, infer sensitive traits, activate audiences, personalize prices or eligibility, exclude customers, disclose segment membership, change retention, or represent causality.

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
