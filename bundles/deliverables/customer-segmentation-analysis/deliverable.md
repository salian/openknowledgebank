# Customer Segmentation Analysis Source-Aware Deliverable Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/automated-decision-making-and-profiling/what-is-automated-individual-decision-making-and-profiling/

ICO guidance explains profiling and automated-decision considerations under UK GDPR; it does not determine local legal applicability, consent, identity, customer traits, segment validity, fairness, stability, commercial value, or permission to target individuals.

## Evidence Required

- Current authoritative or originator sources with edition, date, scope, and definitions.
- Inspected local inputs, records, assumptions, constraints, alternatives, calculations, and outcomes.
- Named owners, validation evidence, qualified review where required, approval, and distribution authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable customer segmentation analysis report with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before identify or profile individuals, infer sensitive traits, activate audiences, personalize prices or eligibility, exclude customers, disclose segment membership, change retention, or represent causality.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent customer identity, consent, lawful basis, trait, segment membership, segment meaning, stability, fairness, causal behavior, value, targeting eligibility, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
