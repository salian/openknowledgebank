# Purchase Order Overview

Verify legal entities, supplier and purchasing authority; link approved requisition, quote and agreement; define items, quantities, specifications, delivery, acceptance, price, currency, taxes, freight, payment, invoicing, funding, coding, terms, changes, contacts, privacy and security obligations; validate totals and approvals; preserve a clear draft-to-issue boundary.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes buyer and supplier legal records, delegated purchasing authority, approved requisition and budget, quote and sourcing record, governing agreement and terms, item specifications and quantities, delivery and acceptance rules, prices and currency, tax and freight basis, accounting codes, privacy and security review, calculations, approvals, and issuance record. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish party identity, purchasing authority, supplier selection, item or quantity, price, tax, funding, term, delivery, acceptance, signature, contract formation, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
