# Value-at-Risk Analysis Overview

Confirm entity, portfolio, decision use and jurisdiction; reconcile positions and valuations; define confidence level, horizon, method, lookback, weighting, dependencies, nonlinearities and missing data; govern market data and transformations; calculate reproducibly; quantify sampling and model uncertainty; backtest and independently validate; pair VaR with stress tests and tail limitations; document exceptions, limits, changes, governance and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes portfolio and legal-entity scope, position and valuation reconciliations, approved market-data sources, model version and code, confidence level horizon and history, distribution and dependency assumptions, mappings and proxies, nonlinear treatment, missing-data controls, reproducible outputs, uncertainty and sensitivity, backtesting and exceptions, independent validation, stress comparisons, limits, governance, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish position, valuation, market input, distribution, dependence, model validity, VaR result, maximum loss, backtest outcome, exception, capital effect, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
