# Customer Satisfaction (CSAT/NPS) Report Overview

Customer Satisfaction (CSAT/NPS) Report is a reviewable artifact. Its audience, purpose, source material, scope, required sections, decision criteria, approvals, and release conditions must be evidenced rather than inferred.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes the governing source and version, objective, audience, scope, local records, definitions, inputs, calculations or mappings, permissions, conflicts, validation results, approvals, and reviewer authority. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish source versions, applicability, local records, measurements, calculations, findings, owners, approvals, dates, permissions, compliance status, or outcomes. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
