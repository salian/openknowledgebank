# Statement of Work Overview

Verify parties, governing agreement and authority; define objectives, in-scope work, exclusions, deliverables, specifications, dependencies, assumptions, responsibilities, milestones, acceptance criteria and procedure, service levels, fees and expenses, invoicing, intellectual property, data and security, changes, termination, risks, signatures, and version control.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes legal entity and authority records, governing agreement and order documents, approved objectives and scope, requirements and solution evidence, delivery capacity and dependencies, deliverable specifications, acceptance criteria and authorized approvers, schedule basis, pricing and tax approvals, IP and data terms, security review, change and termination terms, and signatures. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish party identity, authority, scope, deliverable, feasibility, dependency, acceptance criterion, schedule, fee, tax, legal term, signature, contract formation, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
