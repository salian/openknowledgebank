# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory legal entity and authority records, governing agreement and order documents, approved objectives and scope, requirements and solution evidence, delivery capacity and dependencies, deliverable specifications, acceptance criteria and authorized approvers, schedule basis, pricing and tax approvals, IP and data terms, security review, change and termination terms, and signatures.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the statement of work draft and approval record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to bind a party, commit scope, personnel, dates or fees, accept terms, transfer rights, process data, waive requirements, sign, issue, or represent acceptance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
