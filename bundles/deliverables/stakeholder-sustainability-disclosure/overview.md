# Stakeholder Sustainability Disclosure Overview

Define audience, purpose, jurisdiction and reporting basis; identify entities, value chain, period, materiality lens and topics; substantiate stakeholder engagement, impacts, risks, opportunities, governance, metrics, methods, estimates, comparatives and targets; control omissions, claims, consistency, accessibility, assurance status, publication, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes authorized audience and purpose, jurisdiction and current standards, reporting entities and value chain, period and comparatives, materiality process, stakeholder records and consent, impact and financial evidence, metric definitions and source data, methods and estimates, controls, omissions, target governance, claims review, assurance scope, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish standard applicability, stakeholder view, boundary, material topic, impact, risk, opportunity, metric, estimate, performance, target progress, compliance, conformance, assurance, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
