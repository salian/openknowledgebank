# Creative Brief Source-Aware Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/creative-brief

Use these sources to locate and verify the governing method or requirements. Do not reproduce protected source material, assume an edition remains current, or treat general guidance as proof of local facts or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current governing or authoritative source, edition, effective date, and applicability.
- Objective, audience, scope, exclusions, definitions, and acceptance criteria.
- Local source records, inputs, calculations or mappings, permissions, conflicts, and validation evidence.
- Named owners, approvals, release conditions, exceptions, and rollback evidence when actually documented.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable Creative Brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before approve, sign, publish, distribute, file, implement, or use the artifact for consequential decisions.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent source versions, applicability, local records, measurements, calculations, findings, owners, approvals, dates, permissions, compliance status, or outcomes.
- Do not infer access, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
