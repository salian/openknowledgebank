# Contract Compliance and Obligations Tracker Overview

Inventory executed instruments and hierarchy, extract clauses with citations, distinguish obligations, rights, conditions and dependencies, record parties, triggers, dates, notices, evidence, status, disputes, confidentiality, owners, review, and change history.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes complete executed contracts and amendments, governing law and hierarchy, clause citations, parties, obligations and rights, conditions and dependencies, calculation and date rules, notice provisions, performance evidence, disputes and waivers, confidentiality, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish contract completeness, hierarchy, enforceability, party, obligation, trigger, due date, amount, performance, breach, remedy, waiver, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
