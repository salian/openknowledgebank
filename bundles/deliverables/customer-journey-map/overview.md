# Customer Journey Map Overview

Define the actor, scenario, goal, scope and time horizon; trace stages, actions, channels, touchpoints, thoughts and emotions only from evidence; show pain points, needs, accessibility barriers, backstage dependencies, measures, confidence, gaps, and opportunities.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes research objective and consent, participant and segment criteria, interviews and observations, analytics and service records, accessibility evidence, journey scope and scenario, channels and touchpoints, backstage processes, measures, contradictions, confidence, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish customer identity, behavior, thought, emotion, need, pain point, segment representativeness, channel performance, opportunity value, process owner, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
