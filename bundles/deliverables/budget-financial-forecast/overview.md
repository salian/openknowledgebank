# Budget and Financial Forecast Overview

Define purpose, entity, period, currency, accounting basis, baseline, drivers, assumptions, scenarios, dependencies, cash and financing, uncertainty, sensitivity, reconciliation, controls, ownership, and approval cadence.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes approved historical actuals, chart of accounts, entity and period, revenue and cost drivers, staffing and contract plans, tax and financing assumptions, cash timing, scenario definitions, dependencies, reconciliations, model controls, sensitivity, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish historical balance, driver value, growth rate, timing, tax treatment, liquidity, financing, scenario probability, forecast outcome, budget authority, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
