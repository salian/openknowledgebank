# Bank Covenant Compliance Report Overview

Verify borrower, lender, facility, reporting period and complete executed loan-document set; establish amendment precedence and extract exact covenant definitions, thresholds, testing dates and certificate form under counsel review; map each input and permitted adjustment to closed financial records; calculate ratios reproducibly and reconcile totals; show headroom and sensitivity; disclose defaults, exceptions and waivers without resolving legal effect; preserve signatory and delivery requirements; require treasury, accounting and counsel approval before certification.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes borrower lender facility and preparer authority, complete executed credit agreement guaranties and amendments with precedence, reporting period certificate form deadlines and signatory requirements, covenant definitions thresholds and permitted adjustments, ledger financial statements and source schedules, calculation workbook control totals and independent recalculation, headroom sensitivity exceptions defaults and waiver documents, accounting and legal decisions, signature delivery receipt and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish agreement completeness, precedence, defined term, input, adjustment, ratio, headroom, breach, default, waiver, signatory authority, compliance, lender acceptance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
