# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory borrower lender facility and preparer authority, complete executed credit agreement guaranties and amendments with precedence, reporting period certificate form deadlines and signatory requirements, covenant definitions thresholds and permitted adjustments, ledger financial statements and source schedules, calculation workbook control totals and independent recalculation, headroom sensitivity exceptions defaults and waiver documents, accounting and legal decisions, signature delivery receipt and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the bank covenant compliance certificate support report, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to interpret agreements without counsel, change ledger records, invent adjustments or waivers, conceal breaches, sign or deliver certificates, contact lenders, claim compliance, or represent acceptance without authority.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
