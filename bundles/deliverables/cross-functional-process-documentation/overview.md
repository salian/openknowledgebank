# Cross-Functional Process Documentation Overview

Define process purpose, trigger, boundaries, participants, roles, steps, decisions, handoffs, inputs and outputs, systems, data, controls, exceptions, service expectations, measures, risks, variants, validation, ownership, and versioning.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes process objective and boundaries, observed work and interviews, roles and decision rights, inputs and outputs, systems and data, handoffs, business rules, controls, exceptions, timing and measures, variants, accessibility, validation walkthroughs, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish process step, sequence, role, handoff, rule, system state, control operation, exception, timing, performance, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
