# Campaign ROI Report Overview

Define campaign, objective, period, currencies and decision; reconcile spend, fees and credits to source systems; define conversion and revenue recognition; separate observed platform attribution from modeled incrementality; document attribution model, windows, identity limits, refunds, taxes and offline joins; use LTV and margin assumptions only with owners; calculate ROAS, ROI and payback transparently; compare like periods and cohorts; run sensitivity analysis; report uncertainty and data gaps; require marketing and finance approval before budget action.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes campaign owner and report authority, objective period channels currencies and decision, invoices platform spend fees credits and finance reconciliation, conversion event and revenue definitions, attribution model windows and identity settings, order refund cancellation tax margin and offline records, experiment or incrementality evidence, LTV and payback assumptions with owners, reproducible formulas and sensitivity cases, exclusions limitations comparability review and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish cost, conversion, revenue, attribution, incrementality, LTV, margin, payback, causal impact, ROI, comparability, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
