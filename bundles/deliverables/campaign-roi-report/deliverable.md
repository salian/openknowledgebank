# Campaign ROI Report Source-Aware Deliverable Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12198930
- https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11080067
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing

Analytics and FTC sources do not establish local cost completeness, revenue, attribution accuracy, incrementality, LTV, causal impact, return, or approval.

## Evidence Required

- Current controlling, standards-owner, regulator, platform, contract, research, accounting, tax, or assurance sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected source records, definitions, lineage, calculations, assumptions, exclusions, uncertainty, reconciliations, and approvals.
- Named preparer, owner, qualified reviewer, signatory where required, retention, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable campaign ROI report and reproducible calculation workbook with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to change tracking or budgets, invent conversions or revenue, double count, present attributed results as causal, suppress costs or refunds, claim return, publish, or recommend spend without authority.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent cost, conversion, revenue, attribution, incrementality, LTV, margin, payback, causal impact, ROI, comparability, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
