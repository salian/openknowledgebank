# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory campaign owner and report authority, objective period channels currencies and decision, invoices platform spend fees credits and finance reconciliation, conversion event and revenue definitions, attribution model windows and identity settings, order refund cancellation tax margin and offline records, experiment or incrementality evidence, LTV and payback assumptions with owners, reproducible formulas and sensitivity cases, exclusions limitations comparability review and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the campaign ROI report and reproducible calculation workbook, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to change tracking or budgets, invent conversions or revenue, double count, present attributed results as causal, suppress costs or refunds, claim return, publish, or recommend spend without authority.

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
