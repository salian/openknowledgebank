# SLA Compliance Report Overview

Verify governing agreement, service, customer, period and amendments; translate each service level into versioned indicators, sources, clocks, time zones, calendars, pause and exclusion rules, targets and remedies; reconcile events and changes; reproduce calculations; document missingness, incidents, disputes, uncertainty, review, credits and approval separately.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes executed agreement and amendments, service and customer scope, measurement period and calendar, indicator and target definitions, source-system versions, event and ticket records, clock and pause logic, approved exclusions, incident and maintenance records, reproducible calculations, reconciliations, disputes, remedy terms, reviews, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish contract term, target, event timestamp, clock state, exclusion, measurement, result, breach, compliance, service credit, dispute outcome, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
