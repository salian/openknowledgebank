# System Requirements and Functional Specification Overview

Confirm authority, stakeholders and operational concept; define system boundary, context and terminology; elicit and trace stakeholder needs; write atomic, necessary, feasible, unambiguous and verifiable functional and quality requirements; specify interfaces, data, states, errors, security, privacy, accessibility and safety; identify assumptions, dependencies, priorities, verification methods, baselines, changes, review, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes approved scope and operational concept, stakeholder research and authority, current systems and process evidence, glossary and models, architecture and interface versions, data definitions and ownership, functional and quality needs, legal and policy constraints, security privacy accessibility and safety analyses, feasibility evidence, traceability, verification plan, change records, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish stakeholder need, system boundary, requirement, priority, interface, data meaning, constraint, feasibility, verification result, acceptance, baseline, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
