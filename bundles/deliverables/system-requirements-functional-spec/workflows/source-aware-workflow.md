# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory approved scope and operational concept, stakeholder research and authority, current systems and process evidence, glossary and models, architecture and interface versions, data definitions and ownership, functional and quality needs, legal and policy constraints, security privacy accessibility and safety analyses, feasibility evidence, traceability, verification plan, change records, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the system requirements and functional specification, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to set requirements without authority, expose sensitive architecture or data, commit design or schedule, approve interfaces, waive constraints, claim verification, baseline the specification, or accept the system.

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
