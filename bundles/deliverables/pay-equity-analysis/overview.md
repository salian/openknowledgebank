# Pay Equity Analysis Report Overview

Verify purpose, jurisdiction, privilege and access; define population, period and compensation components; inspect data and job comparability; preregister descriptive and statistical methods, legitimate factors, interactions, uncertainty and sensitivity; protect identities; separate patterns from causes and legal findings; document limitations, independent review, remediation governance, retention, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes legal purpose and jurisdiction, counsel and privilege decision, population and period, compensation definitions, HR and payroll lineage, job architecture and work evidence, legitimate factors, missingness and corrections, statistical plan and code, sample sizes and uncertainty, sensitivity and validation, access and retention controls, review, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish employee identity or protected trait, data completeness, job comparability, legitimate factor, model validity, disparity cause, discrimination, legal compliance, liability, remediation, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
