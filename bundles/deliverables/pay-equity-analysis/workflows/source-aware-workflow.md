# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory legal purpose and jurisdiction, counsel and privilege decision, population and period, compensation definitions, HR and payroll lineage, job architecture and work evidence, legitimate factors, missingness and corrections, statistical plan and code, sample sizes and uncertainty, sensitivity and validation, access and retention controls, review, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the pay equity analysis report, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or expose employee data, infer protected traits, classify comparable work, change pay, identify individuals, allege discrimination, waive privilege, notify employees or regulators, or decide remediation.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
