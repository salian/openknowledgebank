# Pay Equity Analysis Report Source-Aware Deliverable Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.eeoc.gov/statutes/equal-pay-act-1963
- https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ofccp/faqs/compensation

EEOC and OFCCP sources describe United States legal and federal-contractor contexts; they do not establish local coverage, employee comparability, data accuracy, pay disparity, causation, discrimination, privilege, required remediation, or liability.

## Evidence Required

- Current authoritative or originator sources with edition, date, scope, and definitions.
- Inspected local inputs, records, assumptions, constraints, alternatives, calculations, and outcomes.
- Named owners, validation evidence, qualified review where required, approval, and distribution authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable pay equity analysis report with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or expose employee data, infer protected traits, classify comparable work, change pay, identify individuals, allege discrimination, waive privilege, notify employees or regulators, or decide remediation.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent employee identity or protected trait, data completeness, job comparability, legitimate factor, model validity, disparity cause, discrimination, legal compliance, liability, remediation, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
