# Sprint Planning Document Overview

Verify current Product Goal, ordered backlog and Definition of Done; facilitate the team's Sprint Goal, selection and delivery plan; record evidence for value, acceptance, dependencies, risks, capacity and leave; preserve the Developers' ownership of the forecast and adaptation; distinguish forecast from guarantee; link decisions, changes, review, and approval where locally required.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current Product Goal and backlog order, item descriptions and acceptance evidence, Definition of Done, prior delivery and quality data, team availability and leave, dependencies and environments, risks and incidents, Sprint Goal discussion, Developers' selection and plan, changes, and review record. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish priority, value, item readiness, estimate, capacity, assignment, dependency status, feasibility, completion, delivery date, commitment, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
