# Product Launch Readiness Checklist Overview

Define launch scope, audience, environments and decision authority; specify evidence-backed criteria for product behavior, quality, accessibility, security, privacy, legal, data, reliability, capacity, observability, support, documentation, commercial operations, communications, measurement, incident response and rollback; record exceptions, owners, decisions, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes approved scope and release identity, acceptance criteria and test results, accessibility evidence, security and privacy reviews, legal and commercial approvals, data migration and reconciliation, load and reliability results, observability and alerts, support and incident plans, documentation, communications, measurement, rollback test, exceptions, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish release scope, test result, defect status, capacity, security, privacy, compliance, support readiness, customer commitment, rollback success, go-live decision, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
