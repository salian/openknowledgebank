# Sales Pipeline and Forecast Report Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware deliverable bundle for opportunity scope, stage, forecast category, amount, currency, close date, probability, owner, change, risk, coverage, and forecast review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.pipeline_inspection_drive_revenue_using_parent.htm&type=5
- https://resources.docs.salesforce.com/latest/latest/en-us/sfdc/pdf/forecasts.pdf

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- CRM and report version, included teams and opportunity population, stage and forecast-category definitions, exit criteria, amount and currency rules, probability source, close-date policy, snapshot and comparison dates, exclusions, duplicates, ownership, product and region segmentation, source query, changes and risk evidence, quota and coverage formula, approvals, and limitations

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer opportunity state, amount, probability, close date, forecast category, owner, risk, coverage, commit, or revenue outcome.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that change opportunity, stage, amount, probability, close date, category, owner, forecast, or quota data; contact customers; publish forecasts; or make revenue commitments.
