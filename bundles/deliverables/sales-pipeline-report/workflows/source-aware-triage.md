# Sales Pipeline and Forecast Report Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for CRM and report version, included teams and opportunity population, stage and forecast-category definitions, exit criteria, amount and currency rules, probability source, close-date policy, snapshot and comparison dates, exclusions, duplicates, ownership, product and region segmentation, source query, changes and risk evidence, quota and coverage formula, approvals, and limitations.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that change opportunity, stage, amount, probability, close date, category, owner, forecast, or quota data; contact customers; publish forecasts; or make revenue commitments.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
