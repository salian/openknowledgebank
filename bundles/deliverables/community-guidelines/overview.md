# Community Guidelines and Code of Conduct Overview

Define community purpose and surfaces, participants, expected and prohibited conduct, reporting routes, urgent escalation, confidentiality limits, moderation authority, proportionate responses, conflicts, appeals, accessibility, transparency, retention, versioning, and review.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes community purpose and channels, user groups and accessibility needs, platform capabilities, applicable law and contracts, reporting and emergency routes, moderation roles, enforcement options, conflict and appeal process, privacy and retention controls, transparency commitments, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish participant identity or intent, incident fact, policy authority, legal classification, confidentiality, enforcement proportionality, safety outcome, appeal result, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
