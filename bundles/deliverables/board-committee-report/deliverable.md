# Board and Committee Report Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware deliverable bundle for concise board or committee reporting with evidenced mandate, decisions, performance, risk, finance, compliance, alternatives, and governance boundaries.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- board or committee mandate, meeting, audience, and decision rights
- agenda item, requested decision, and prior decisions
- performance, financial, operational, risk, and compliance evidence
- definitions, periods, comparators, sources, and uncertainty
- options, tradeoffs, recommendation, dissent, and consequences
- management owner, legal privilege, confidentiality, approvals, and follow-up

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Reconcile conflicting definitions, dates, versions, scopes, filters, owners, and calculation or processing rules.
- Do not infer governance mandate, decision right, metric, risk status, recommendation support, and approval.
- Require accountable confirmation before making board representations, distributing confidential or privileged material, committing resources, or recording decisions without authorized review.
