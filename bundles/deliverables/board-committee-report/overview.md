# Board and Committee Report Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware board and committee decision support analysis and a reviewable board or committee report.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering board or committee mandate, meeting, audience, and decision rights, agenda item, requested decision, and prior decisions, performance, financial, operational, risk, and compliance evidence, definitions, periods, comparators, sources, and uncertainty, options, tradeoffs, recommendation, dissent, and consequences, and management owner, legal privilege, confidentiality, approvals, and follow-up.
- Unknowns: do not infer governance mandate, decision right, metric, risk status, recommendation support, and approval.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before making board representations, distributing confidential or privileged material, committing resources, or recording decisions without authorized review.
