# Wireframes Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware interface structure and interaction planning analysis and a reviewable wireframe set and annotation brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering audience, user tasks, scenarios, and research evidence, information architecture and content hierarchy, screen, flow, navigation, state, error, and edge-case scope, responsive breakpoints and device constraints, accessibility, localization, privacy, and content requirements, and annotations, open questions, test findings, ownership, and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer user task, content priority, flow behavior, responsive behavior, accessibility need, and design approval.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before presenting wireframes as validated design, publishing content or assets, committing implementation, or making usability claims without testing.
