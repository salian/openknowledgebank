# IT Strategic Plan Overview

Confirm planning authority, horizon and mission; assess current capabilities, demand, technical debt and obligations; define outcomes, principles, target capabilities, initiatives, dependencies, sequencing, funding assumptions, workforce, sourcing, measures, risks, governance, review, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes mission and business strategy, planning mandate and horizon, stakeholder demand, portfolio and architecture inventory, service performance, technical debt, workforce and supplier capacity, obligations, financial assumptions, initiative evidence, dependencies, measures, risks, governance, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish strategic priority, baseline capability, demand, capacity, cost, funding, benefit, feasibility, dependency, target, delivery date, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
