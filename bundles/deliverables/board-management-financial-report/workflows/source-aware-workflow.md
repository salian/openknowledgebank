# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory reporting entity and basis, ledger close status, trial balance, consolidation and eliminations, accounting policies, actuals, budget and forecast versions, cash and debt records, covenant definitions, reconciliations, controls, assurance, narrative, and board requests.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the board and management financial report package, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before post entries, close books, consolidate, change accounting policy, issue forecasts, certify controls, publish statements, approve distributions, borrow, spend, or make board representations.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
