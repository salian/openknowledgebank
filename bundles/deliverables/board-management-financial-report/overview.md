# Board and Management Financial Report Overview

Define audience and decisions, reporting basis, entities, period, currency, actuals, budget and forecast versions, reconciliations, material movements, liquidity, covenants, risks, controls, assurance, and approvals.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes reporting entity and basis, ledger close status, trial balance, consolidation and eliminations, accounting policies, actuals, budget and forecast versions, cash and debt records, covenant definitions, reconciliations, controls, assurance, narrative, and board requests. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish entity scope, accounting basis, balance, variance, cash position, covenant compliance, forecast, control effectiveness, audit status, board decision, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
