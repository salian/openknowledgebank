# Quarterly Business Review Presentation Overview

Define audience, relationship and period; confirm objectives and metric definitions; reconcile actuals to governed sources and comparatives; separate observations from causal hypotheses; document customer-confirmed views, delivery and support evidence, financial assumptions, risks, forecast scenarios, decisions needed, commitments, owners, dates, access, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes meeting purpose and authorized audience, confirmed objectives, metric dictionary and source systems, period and comparative data, reconciliations and adjustments, delivery and support records, customer-confirmed feedback, financial records and forecast assumptions, risks and dependencies, prior commitments, decisions, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish objective, metric definition, result, comparator, cause, customer view, value, forecast, risk, commitment, decision, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
