# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory account and contract scope, customer-confirmed objectives and success criteria, stakeholder roles and consent, usage and support records, implementation state, commitments and dates, risks and dependencies, value evidence, renewal and commercial records, communication history, access controls, owners, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the customer success and account plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before contact customers, disclose account data, promise outcomes, commit resources or dates, change health scores, forecast renewal or expansion, offer commercial terms, or represent customer agreement.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
