# Customer Success and Account Plan Overview

Define plan audience and account scope, distinguish customer-confirmed outcomes from internal hypotheses, record current state, stakeholders and authority, adoption and support evidence, commitments, risks, dependencies, measures, value validation, review cadence, access, and approvals.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes account and contract scope, customer-confirmed objectives and success criteria, stakeholder roles and consent, usage and support records, implementation state, commitments and dates, risks and dependencies, value evidence, renewal and commercial records, communication history, access controls, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish customer objective, stakeholder authority, adoption, health, value, satisfaction, commitment, risk, renewal intent, expansion, forecast, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
