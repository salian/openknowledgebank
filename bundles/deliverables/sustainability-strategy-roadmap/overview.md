# Sustainability Strategy and Roadmap Overview

Confirm mandate, reporting context and material topics; establish controlled baselines and boundaries; define principles, outcomes, governed goals and target methods; evaluate initiative options, dependencies, transition and social impacts, resources, financing and risks; specify measures, controls, claims, milestones, accountability, stakeholder engagement, review, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes organizational mandate and governance, applicable standards and obligations, materiality evidence, organizational and value-chain boundary, controlled baseline inventories, target method and eligibility, initiative options and technical evidence, financial and workforce assumptions, dependencies, transition and human-rights impacts, measures and controls, claims review, stakeholder records, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish material topic, baseline, boundary, target eligibility, target validation, initiative feasibility, cost, benefit, dependency, impact, compliance, claim, milestone, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
