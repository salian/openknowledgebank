# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory organizational mandate and governance, applicable standards and obligations, materiality evidence, organizational and value-chain boundary, controlled baseline inventories, target method and eligibility, initiative options and technical evidence, financial and workforce assumptions, dependencies, transition and human-rights impacts, measures and controls, claims review, stakeholder records, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the sustainability strategy and roadmap, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to select topics or targets without governance, submit targets, commit capital or dates, contact stakeholders, make environmental claims, claim validation or compliance, publish a roadmap, or approve initiatives.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
