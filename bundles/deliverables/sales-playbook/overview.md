# Sales Playbook and Battlecards Overview

Define offer, audience and permitted uses; ground problems and qualification in research; verify positioning, capabilities, limitations, proof and customer references; date and source competitor statements; control pricing, discounts, privacy, outreach, claims and regulated topics; map process, roles, handoffs, objections and escalation; test, version, govern, and approve.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes approved offer and audience, customer research, current product documentation and roadmap boundaries, claim substantiation, approved references and consent, dated competitor public sources, pricing and discount authority, sales process and CRM definitions, privacy and marketing rules by jurisdiction, training and testing, owners, versions, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish customer need or intent, qualification, capability, roadmap, claim truth, customer result, competitor capability, price, discount, consent, legal compliance, win rate, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
