# Bill of Lading and Shipping Documentation Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware shipping documentation and cargo records analysis and a reviewable bill-of-lading review brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering transport mode, route, ports, dates, jurisdiction, and applicable form, shipper, consignee, notify party, carrier, forwarder, and authority, booking, bill, container, seal, vessel, voyage, and reference identifiers, cargo description, classification, packages, marks, quantity, weight, measure, and value evidence, freight terms, carriage terms, Incoterm if applicable, originals, negotiability, and endorsements, and dangerous goods, customs, sanctions, filing, release, signatures, corrections, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer party identity, cargo description, quantity, route, document status, and customs acceptance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before issuing, signing, endorsing, filing, amending, releasing cargo, or making customs, sanctions, legal, title, or dangerous-goods determinations without qualified authorization.
