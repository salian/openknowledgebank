# Tax Provision and Accrual Schedule Overview

Verify entities, jurisdictions, period and reporting basis; reconcile pretax book income and current tax; inspect tax bases and temporary differences; document enacted rates, recognition and measurement, loss and credit carryforwards, valuation allowances or recognition limits, uncertain positions, withholding and other taxes, currency, return-to-provision adjustments, entries, disclosures, controls, review, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity and jurisdiction records, reporting framework and period, signed returns and prior provision, trial balance and pretax book income reconciliation, tax-basis workpapers, temporary-difference rollforwards, enacted tax law and rates, carryforward schedules, forecast and realizability support, uncertain-position analysis, currency sources, return-to-provision adjustments, journal entries, disclosures, controls, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish entity or jurisdiction, reporting basis, tax law or rate, tax base, temporary difference, realizability, uncertain position, provision amount, journal entry, disclosure, compliance, filing, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
