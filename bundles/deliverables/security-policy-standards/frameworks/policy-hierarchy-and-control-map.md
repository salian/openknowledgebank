# Policy Hierarchy and Control Map

| Layer | Purpose | Quality question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy | Approved intent, scope, principles, accountability | Is direction clear and authorized? |
| Standard | Specific, testable requirements | Can conformance be assessed without guessing? |
| Procedure | Repeatable implementation steps | Can the team perform and evidence it? |
| Guideline | Recommended practice where judgment is expected | Is advice distinguished from requirements? |

## Mapping Record

| Requirement | Source and edition | Reference | Applicability | Evidence | Gap/exception | Owner/approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supplied requirement | Inspect official source | Exact reference after inspection | Provided or Needs verification | User-supplied artifact | Unresolved issue | Evidence or Needs verification |

Do not treat a mapping row as proof of implementation, effectiveness, certification, or legal compliance. Add source note, inspection date, scope, and evidence limits.
