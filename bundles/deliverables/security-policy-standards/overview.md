# Security Policy & Standards Overview

This bundle supports formal security policy and standard deliverables. It turns supplied governance evidence and selected authoritative sources into a reviewable document without inventing local owners, controls, dates, thresholds, or compliance status.

## Source Categories

- Official frameworks and control catalogs: NIST CSF 2.0 and NIST SP 800-53.
- Management-system standard: ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- Practical public examples: SANS/CRF policy library.
- Local evidence: governance records, scope statements, contracts, risk decisions, inventories, control evidence, exceptions, and approvals.

## Boundaries

A framework mapping is not proof of compliance or control effectiveness. Inspect current editions and local obligations before exact claims. Route approval, enforcement, disclosure, risk acceptance, and certification claims to accountable reviewers.
