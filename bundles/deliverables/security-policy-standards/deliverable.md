# Security Policy and Standards Source-Aware Guide

Use this bundle to draft and review information and application security policies and standards while keeping local facts, source requirements, assumptions, and missing evidence distinct.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of implementation, control effectiveness, compliance, certification, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources

- https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20
- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final
- https://www.iso.org/standard/27001?browse=tc
- https://www.sans.org/information-security-policy

Name the source and edition used in each Source Note. Inspect current source material and local obligations before citing exact requirements or claiming alignment.

## Evidence Required

- decision, audience, scope, systems, data, jurisdictions, and obligations
- selected sources, editions, access dates, and applicable requirements
- governance records, owners, approvers, exception authority, and review cadence
- existing controls, implementation evidence, gaps, exceptions, and risk decisions
- document status, effective date, distribution, change history, and supersession record

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, document layer, audience, scope, and evidence boundary.
2. Inspect selected sources and record edition, date, scope, and applicability.
3. Label facts Verified, Provided, Assumed, or Needs verification.
4. Draft requirements with rationale, applicability, evidence, and source mappings.
5. Record gaps and exceptions without inventing owners, approvals, or risk acceptance.
6. Route the review copy to accountable security, legal, compliance, audit, and business reviewers as applicable.

## Guardrails

- Do not infer obligations, local controls, control effectiveness, approval, implementation, compliance, or certification.
- Treat a framework mapping as traceability, not evidence that a requirement is implemented or effective.
- Minimize sensitive security, customer, credential, architecture, vulnerability, and incident data.
- Require explicit confirmation before approval, enforcement, publication, disclosure, risk acceptance, or live-system changes.
