# Security Policy & Standards Quality Check

Score each dimension from 0 to 12.

- **Empty-evidence integrity:** Avoid inventing scope, owners, approvers, controls, dates, obligations, effectiveness, or compliance status, and clearly list Needs verification.
- **Policy structure:** Distinguish policy, standard, procedure, and guideline and include scope, requirements, evidence, exceptions, review, and approval boundaries.
- **Source and control traceability:** Name source categories, edition/date, local evidence, mapping limits, expected differences, unresolved gaps, and the next inspection step.

A high score requires a direct readiness conclusion, not just a polished template. Unsupported security details or a fabricated accountable reviewer should materially reduce the score.
