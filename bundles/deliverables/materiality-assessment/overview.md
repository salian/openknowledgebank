# Materiality Assessment Overview

Verify applicable reporting objective and standards; define boundary, value chain, period and lenses; identify actual and potential impacts, sustainability-related risks and opportunities, stakeholders and evidence; document methods, severity, likelihood, time horizon, uncertainty and thresholds separately; validate, govern, approve, disclose, and review changes.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes jurisdiction and selected standards, reporting objective and entities, activities and value chain, stakeholder and expert evidence, actual and potential impacts, risks and opportunities, severity and likelihood support, financial effects and time horizons, methods and thresholds, uncertainty, governance records, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish standard applicability, boundary, stakeholder view, impact, severity, likelihood, risk, opportunity, financial effect, score, threshold, material topic, compliance, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
