# Variance Analysis Report Overview

Define decision, entity, period, currency and comparison basis; reconcile governed actual and baseline versions; normalize scope, classifications, timing and rates; decompose volume, price, mix, timing and other effects with reproducible methods; distinguish arithmetic attribution from causal explanation; investigate material items, uncertainty and offsets; document forecast implications, actions, owners only when evidenced, controls, review and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity and decision scope, reporting basis and period, approved budget forecast or standard version, ledger and operational actuals, mappings and reconciliations, currency and rate sources, decomposition method and code, materiality rules, transaction and operational support, management explanations labeled as provided, uncertainty and sensitivity, forecast bridge, actions, controls, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish baseline, actual, classification, mapping, rate, variance, decomposition, causal driver, materiality, accountability, forecast, corrective outcome, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
