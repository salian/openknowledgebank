# Source-Aware Policy Brief

## Direct Answer

State whether the policy can be drafted, reviewed, approved, or adopted now. Name the exact decision blocked by missing evidence.

## Policy Architecture

Summarize purpose, scope, principles, governance, taxonomy, appetite/capacity, limits, treatment, monitoring/reporting, breaches, exceptions, assurance, review, and change control. Mark unsupported sections as `Needs verification`.

## Verification Plan

Name each source category, entity/jurisdiction scope, date or version, source of record, and conflict check for definitions, periods, metrics, thresholds, owners, and approval rights.

## Confirmation Boundary

Name the evidenced accountable reviewer. If not evidenced, write `Needs verification`. Do not adopt the policy, accept risk, change limits or controls, or make regulatory/certification claims without explicit approval.

## Source Note

Name the official or authoritative framework sources, local evidence inspected, and material limitations.
