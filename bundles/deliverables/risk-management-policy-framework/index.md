# Risk Management Policy / Framework

Source-aware deliverable bundle for drafting and reviewing an enterprise risk management policy or framework.

## Required Answer Contract

Every substantive response must include:

1. **Direct answer** - state what can be drafted or concluded now.
2. **Evidence status** - separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`; write `None` where empty.
3. **Verification plan** - name source category, scope, date or version, and conflict checks.
4. **Confirmation boundary** - identify the evidenced reviewer, or `Needs verification`, and actions prohibited without approval.
5. **Source note** - name authoritative sources used and limitations.
