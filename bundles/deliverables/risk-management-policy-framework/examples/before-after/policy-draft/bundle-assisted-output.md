# Bundle-Assisted Output

## Direct answer

A reviewable policy structure can be drafted now, but adoption or a compliance conclusion is blocked by missing organizational and jurisdiction-specific evidence.

## Evidence status

- **Verified:** ISO 31000:2018, COSO ERM, FSB, and Basel source concepts can inform the structure; applicability must be checked.
- **Provided:** None.
- **Assumed:** None.
- **Needs verification:** Entity, jurisdiction, authority, scope, risk taxonomy, appetite/capacity, limits, owners, approval rights, controls, review cadence, and effective date.

## Draft structure

Use document control; purpose and scope; strategy relationship; governance; taxonomy and method; appetite/capacity/limits; treatment and controls; monitoring/reporting; breaches and exceptions; records; assurance; review and change control.

## Verification plan

Inspect current official authorities and internal source-of-record documents for entity, jurisdiction, effective date, definitions, metrics, thresholds, owners, delegation, and approval conflicts.

## Confirmation boundary

Accountable approver: **Needs verification**. Do not adopt the policy, accept risk, set limits, change controls, or make regulatory or certification claims without explicit qualified approval.

## Source note

Use ISO, COSO, FSB, and Basel sources for framework context; they do not establish this organization's requirements or implementation status.
