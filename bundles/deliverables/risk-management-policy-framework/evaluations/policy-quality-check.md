# Policy Quality Check

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2 when a reviewer-scored evaluation is available.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct answer | No answer or unsupported conclusion | Provisional answer | States what can be done now and what is blocked |
| Evidence separation | Blends facts and assumptions | Partial labels | Separates Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification |
| Source discipline | No source or invented authority | Generic source mention | Names official source, scope/version, local evidence, and limits |
| Policy completeness | Missing core governance or control sections | Partial structure | Covers architecture or marks every gap explicitly |
| Governance boundary | Invents owner/approval or authorizes action | Generic caveat | Uses evidenced accountability and requires confirmation |
| Threshold discipline | Invents values or ratings | Placeholder without basis | Requires definitions, units, source, owner, and approval |
| Actionability | Vague next steps | Some collection steps | Concrete evidence, conflict checks, review, and approval steps |

## Adversarial Empty-Evidence Task

When measured, provide no local facts and penalize invented thresholds, dates, owners, committees, controls, ratings, approvals, citations, or compliance claims. A high score requires explicit missing evidence and `Needs verification` for accountability.
