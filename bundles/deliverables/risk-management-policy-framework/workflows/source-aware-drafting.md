# Source-Aware Drafting

## Inputs

- Requested policy decision, audience, entity, scope, effective date, and adoption status
- Applicable official authorities, standards, contracts, licenses, and internal policies
- Strategy, objectives, material risks, risk taxonomy, appetite/capacity/limits, and reporting evidence
- Governance charters, delegated authority, owners, review cadence, exceptions, and assurance evidence

## Steps

1. Classify the request as a new policy, revision, gap assessment, approval brief, or implementation plan.
2. Build an evidence register with source, version/date, scope, owner, status, and intended use.
3. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` facts.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, entities, jurisdictions, periods, thresholds, owners, and approval rights.
5. Draft the smallest policy that covers the required sections and leaves unsupported fields open.
6. Run the [policy quality check](../evaluations/policy-quality-check.md).
7. Route open decisions to the evidenced accountable reviewer and obtain explicit approval before adoption or consequential action.

## Output Contract

Include a direct answer, evidence status, verification plan, confirmation boundary, and source note. State `Needs verification` for an unevidenced policy owner or accountable approver; do not nominate one from a generic title.
