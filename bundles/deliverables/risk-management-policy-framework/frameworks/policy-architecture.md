# Policy Architecture

Use this as a completeness map. It is not a mandatory legal structure.

| Section | Decision it supports | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document control | Who owns, approves, and changes the policy? | Charter, delegation, approval record, dates |
| Purpose and scope | Which entities, activities, risks, and exclusions are covered? | Legal structure, strategy, process inventory |
| Principles and strategy | How does risk management inform objectives and performance? | Strategy, planning, performance, and framework sources |
| Governance and roles | Who identifies, owns, oversees, assures, escalates, and accepts risk? | Org chart, charters, RACI, delegated authority |
| Taxonomy and method | How are risks described and assessed? | Definitions, method, scales, aggregation rules |
| Appetite and capacity | Which risk types and levels are acceptable within capacity? | Approved appetite, capacity basis, metrics, constraints |
| Limits and triggers | What boundaries and warning signals apply? | Metric definitions, units, periods, data, threshold owner |
| Treatment and controls | How are responses selected and tracked? | Control inventory, action plans, owners, due dates |
| Monitoring and reporting | What is reported, to whom, when, and with what evidence? | Reports, data lineage, cadence, distribution rules |
| Breaches and exceptions | What happens when a boundary is exceeded or policy is not followed? | Escalation procedure, authority, issue records |
| Review and assurance | How is the policy tested, reviewed, and changed? | Review plan, audit/assurance scope, change history |

## Evidence Status Rules

- `Verified`: supported by an inspected authoritative or local source.
- `Provided`: supplied by the user but not independently verified.
- `Assumed`: a clearly labeled drafting assumption.
- `Needs verification`: missing or conflicting evidence that blocks reliance.

Do not convert a blank or placeholder into an approved value.
