# Risk Management Policy / Framework Overview

This bundle helps produce a structured first draft and review plan for an enterprise risk management policy or framework.

## What It Is For

- Turning supplied governance, strategy, risk, control, and approval evidence into a reviewable policy structure
- Making appetite, capacity, limits, escalation, monitoring, exceptions, and review explicit
- Preserving the distinction between framework guidance and local obligations or facts

## What It Is Not For

- It does not provide legal, regulatory, audit, certification, or professional advice.
- It does not set risk values or authorize acceptance, limit changes, control changes, or disclosures.
- It does not establish that an organization has implemented a framework.
