# Compensation Structure and Pay Band Documentation Overview

Define jurisdiction and workforce, job architecture, compensable factors, market sources and dates, range methodology, currencies and geographies, placement and progression rules, incentives, exceptions, transparency, privacy, fairness analysis, governance, monitoring, and approvals.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes jurisdictions and covered workforce, current job descriptions and architecture, compensable factors, market data and licenses, pay records, currencies and locations, range methodology, placement and progression criteria, incentive plans, exceptions, fairness analyses, retention rules, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish employee identity, protected status, job equivalence, market rate, lawful factor, range value, individual placement, pay fairness, discrimination, legal compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
