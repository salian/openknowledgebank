# Compensation Structure and Pay Band Documentation Source-Aware Deliverable Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.eeoc.gov/equal-paycompensation-discrimination
- https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/section-10-compensation-discrimination
- https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/recordkeeping-requirements

EEOC sources describe United States federal discrimination and recordkeeping considerations; they do not establish local coverage, job equivalence, lawful factors, market rates, pay equity, range design, individual placement, or compliance in any jurisdiction.

## Evidence Required

- Current authoritative or originator sources with edition, date, scope, and definitions.
- Inspected local inputs, records, assumptions, constraints, alternatives, calculations, and outcomes.
- Named owners, validation evidence, qualified review where required, approval, and distribution authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable compensation structure and pay band documentation with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before collect or expose employee pay data, classify jobs, set ranges, place employees, change compensation, communicate individual pay, infer discrimination, or represent legal compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent employee identity, protected status, job equivalence, market rate, lawful factor, range value, individual placement, pay fairness, discrimination, legal compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
