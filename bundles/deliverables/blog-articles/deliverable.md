# Blog Posts and Long-Form Articles Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware deliverable bundle for audience, purpose, claim, source, outline, headline, accessibility, originality, voice, disclosure, review, publication, and measurement review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://developers.google.com/style
- https://developers.google.com/style/headings

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- commissioning brief and audience, publication and brand standards, topic and search intent, primary sources and claim ledger, interviews and consent, product or legal substantiation, outline and draft, originality and rights checks, accessibility and inclusive-language review, author and editor, disclosures, links, publication approval, distribution plan, and metric definitions

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer audience need, claim truth, source authority, authorship, originality, rights, endorsement, publication approval, search performance, or business impact.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that publish or distribute content, use names, quotes, images, or copyrighted material, make product, legal, health, financial, or performance claims, contact sources, spend promotion budget, or alter live pages.
