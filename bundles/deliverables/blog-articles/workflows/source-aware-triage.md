# Blog Posts and Long-Form Articles Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for commissioning brief and audience, publication and brand standards, topic and search intent, primary sources and claim ledger, interviews and consent, product or legal substantiation, outline and draft, originality and rights checks, accessibility and inclusive-language review, author and editor, disclosures, links, publication approval, distribution plan, and metric definitions.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that publish or distribute content, use names, quotes, images, or copyrighted material, make product, legal, health, financial, or performance claims, contact sources, spend promotion budget, or alter live pages.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
