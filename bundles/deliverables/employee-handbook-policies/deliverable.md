# Employee Handbook and HR Policy Package

## Output Contract

1. **Document control:** identify employer entity, covered workforce and locations, languages and accessibility needs, version, effective date, superseded material, owner, reviewers, approval authority, and status only when evidenced.
2. **Source note:** distinguish external authority from local evidence: laws, regulations, agency guidance, decisions, wage orders, contracts, collective bargaining, benefit plans, offers, existing policies, actual practices, systems, channels, training, and approvals.
3. **Evidence status:** list `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`. With no local evidence, set the first three to `None` except facts explicitly supplied in the request.
4. **Applicability matrix:** for each policy module record jurisdiction, employer/workforce coverage, location, headcount or other trigger, sector or contract condition, authority and effective date, affected population, owner, evidence, conflicts, and open review issue. A U.S. federal source is not a substitute for state, local, international, sector, contract, or collective-bargaining analysis.
5. **Handbook architecture:** organize document control and purpose; employment relationship and classifications; equal opportunity, harassment, accommodation, and retaliation; wage, timekeeping, scheduling, and payroll; leave and benefits; safety and injury reporting; conduct and conflicts; technology, communications, privacy, and records; complaints and investigations; discipline and separations; acknowledgements; and jurisdiction/workforce addenda only as applicable.
6. **Policy-module contract:** each module should state purpose, scope, policy, defined terms, responsibilities, procedures, usable reporting or escalation paths, rights and non-retaliation safeguards, privacy and record handling, exceptions, contacts, source/owner, effective date, version, and review cycle where relevant.
7. **Reporting and response:** provide practical alternative channels when a supervisor, manager, policy owner, or executive may be implicated. Describe intake, escalation, interim safeguards, impartial review, corrective-action authority, and records using only processes the employer can operate. Qualify confidentiality to the extent possible and consistent with a fair response; do not guarantee secrecy or an outcome.
8. **Rights-preservation review:** check conduct, confidentiality, communications, social media, recordings, logos, solicitation, wage discussion, safety reporting, government contact, complaint, investigation, discipline, and separation language against current nonwaivable and protected rights. Narrowly tailor restrictions to an evidenced legitimate purpose.
9. **Employment terms and conflicts:** reconcile handbook language with offers, employment agreements, collective bargaining, benefit plans, wage/time rules, leave programs, privacy notices, safety programs, and actual practice. Do not insert at-will, noncontract, modification, or reserved-rights language without jurisdiction-specific review.
10. **Accessibility, distribution, and training:** identify readable language, translation, accessibility, delivery channels, notice/posting requirements, manager and employee training, questions, and version access. Keep a handbook from being the sole control when law or operations require separate notice, process, or training.
11. **Acknowledgement contract:** state what acknowledgement proves, such as receipt of a named version, and what it does not prove. Do not use it to waive statutory, contractual, collective, reporting, participation, accommodation, or protected concerted rights, or to claim understanding or consent without lawful design and evidence.
12. **Change and status record:** preserve source review, drafting, conflict resolution, legal/functional review, approval, publication, distribution, receipt, acknowledgement, training, enforcement, exception, review, and supersession as distinct states with accountable owners and evidence.

## Reconciliation Rule

When laws, agreements, plans, policies, or practices disagree, none is automatically controlling without applicability analysis. Align employer and workforce, jurisdiction, location, classification, represented status, source hierarchy, effective date, subject, benefit plan, contract, policy version, actual channel, and practice. Record the governing determination and qualified reviewer evidence, or keep the conflict unresolved; do not silently merge incompatible terms.

## Source Note

The linked U.S. federal sources identify baseline discrimination, accommodation, retaliation, wage/time, leave, posting, protected concerted activity, and safety-reporting surfaces. Coverage and exact obligations vary. State, local, international, sector, contract, collective-bargaining, plan, court, board, and current agency sources plus employer evidence must be inspected before authoritative drafting.

## Safety Boundary

This package contains no complaint intake, investigation, case decision, disciplinary action, policy publication, distribution, signature collection, monitoring configuration, employment-term change, or HR-system instructions. Consequential action requires explicit authorization, approved systems, privacy safeguards, and accountable qualified review.
