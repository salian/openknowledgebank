# Employee Handbook and HR Policies Quality Check

Score each criterion `0` absent or unsafe, `1` partial, or `2` complete and evidence-backed.

1. **Applicability integrity:** identifies employer, workforce, jurisdictions, locations, headcount, classifications, contracts, bargaining, sector, and effective-date triggers without invented coverage.
2. **Source and conflict discipline:** traces material terms to current authority, agreement, plan, or approved decision and records conflicting versions, hierarchy, qualified review, and unresolved issues.
3. **Policy design and clarity:** uses a consistent module contract with purpose, scope, rules, responsibilities, procedures, channels, rights, records, exceptions, contacts, ownership, and change metadata.
4. **Operational fit:** confirms that named owners, alternates, reporting routes, systems, benefits, schedules, training, notices, records, translations, accessibility, and response capacity exist.
5. **Rights preservation:** checks restrictions and processes for discrimination, accommodation, retaliation, pay/time, leave, concerted activity, safety, whistleblowing, government contact, privacy, and other applicable rights.
6. **Reporting and response safety:** provides usable alternate channels, qualified confidentiality, non-retaliation, impartial review, escalation, and proportionate corrective authority without promising secrecy or outcomes.
7. **Acknowledgement and lifecycle integrity:** distinguishes review, approval, publication, distribution, receipt, acknowledgement, training, enforcement, exception, and supersession; acknowledgements do not become unsupported waivers.
8. **Qualified use and privacy:** states limitations, protects sensitive employment information, routes high-risk decisions to accountable reviewers, and contains no action-taking or unsupported compliance claim.

## Blocking Defects

Regardless of score, block publication readiness when the draft invents law or employer facts, conflicts with agreements or plans without resolution, names nonexistent channels, guarantees confidentiality or outcomes, chills protected activity, invites unpaid work, deters safety reporting, uses acknowledgement as an unsupported waiver, or claims legal approval/compliance without evidence.
