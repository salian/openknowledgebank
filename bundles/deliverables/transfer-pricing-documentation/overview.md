# Transfer Pricing Documentation Overview

Verify entities, jurisdictions, periods and current local requirements; reconcile controlled transactions and agreements; document functions, assets and risks from evidence; select and justify methods; govern searches, comparables and adjustments; reconcile tested results to accounts; address intangibles, services, financing, restructurings and uncertainty as applicable; control consistency across master, local and country-by-country records, review, filing and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity and ownership records, jurisdiction and current local rules, intercompany agreements and invoices, transaction and ledger reconciliations, functions assets and risk-control evidence, business strategy and intangibles, method-selection analysis, database search protocol and licensed outputs, comparable screening and adjustments, financial segmentation and calculations, prior filings and rulings, review, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish entity relationship, transaction, function, asset, risk control, method, comparable, adjustment, arm's-length result, tax liability, compliance, filing, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
