# Environmental, Social, and Governance Report Overview

Verify jurisdiction, reporting objective and current standards; define entities, value chain, period, materiality lens, stakeholder and governance processes, topics, disclosures, metrics, methods, estimates, comparatives, omissions, controls, consistency with financial reporting, claims, assurance, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes jurisdiction and applicable requirements, reporting objective and standards, entities and value chain, period and comparatives, materiality process and governance approval, stakeholder and impact evidence, risks and opportunities, disclosures and metrics, methods and estimates, controls, omissions, financial-report links, claims, assurance, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish standard applicability, reporting boundary, material topic, impact, risk, opportunity, metric value, estimate, completeness, compliance, conformance, assurance, performance, target progress, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
