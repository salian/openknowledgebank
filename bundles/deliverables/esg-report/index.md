# Environmental, Social, and Governance Report

Use this bundle to prepare a reviewable **ESG report and disclosure index** without inventing local facts, qualifications, records, results, permissions, or authority.

## Required Response Contract

Every substantive response must contain:

1. **Direct answer** - what can and cannot be concluded now.
2. **Evidence status** - separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.
3. **Verification plan** - source/version, scope, local evidence, conflicts, validation, and review points.
4. **Confirmation boundary** - the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.
5. **Source note** - official sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.

Prompt-supplied facts belong under `Provided`, not `Assumed`. Never invent standard applicability, reporting boundary, material topic, impact, risk, opportunity, metric value, estimate, completeness, compliance, conformance, assurance, performance, target progress, or approval.
