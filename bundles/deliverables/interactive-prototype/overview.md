# Interactive Prototype Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware interactive experience prototyping analysis and a reviewable interactive prototype specification.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering research question, audience, scenario, and decision, prototype fidelity, platform, device, and scope, flows, screens, states, interactions, errors, and edge cases, content, assets, rights, sample data, and privacy, design-system, accessibility, and usability requirements, and test plan, participants, observations, limitations, ownership, and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer user need, interaction behavior, content approval, accessibility, technical feasibility, and test outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before presenting a prototype as production, collecting sensitive data, publishing assets, committing implementation, or making usability claims without valid testing.
