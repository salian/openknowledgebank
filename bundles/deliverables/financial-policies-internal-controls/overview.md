# Financial Policies and Internal Controls Documentation Overview

Define entity, jurisdiction, objectives and reporting basis; map risks to preventive and detective controls, roles and segregation, thresholds, approvals, procedures, systems, evidence, access, exceptions, monitoring, deficiencies, remediation, change control, retention, testing, governance, and assurance.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity and jurisdiction, objectives and reporting basis, current policies and processes, risk assessment, role and access records, transaction flows, control descriptions and evidence, thresholds and approvals, system configuration, exceptions, monitoring and testing, deficiencies and remediation, retention, governance, assurance, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish applicability, financial objective, risk, control owner, segregation, threshold, control design, operating evidence, effectiveness, deficiency, compliance, assurance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
