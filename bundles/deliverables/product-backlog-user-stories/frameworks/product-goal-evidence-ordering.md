# Product Goal, Evidence, And Ordering

Use four passes:

## 1. Goal

What future state or outcome is the Product Backlog intended to advance? Quote or link the supplied Product Goal; otherwise mark it `Needs verification`.

## 2. Evidence

For each item, record the user or stakeholder, problem evidence, expected outcome, constraints, and source. Separate `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`.

## 3. Ordering

Explain the relative order using the team's selected basis: value, risk, dependency, learning, urgency, or a named framework. State definitions, inputs, scope, time/version, and uncertainty. Do not present an optional score as objective truth.

## 4. Inspection

State what evidence would change the order, who confirms it, and when the list will be reviewed. Keep the output transparent enough for stakeholders and the team to inspect and adapt.

## Guardrails

- Product Owner accountability does not mean the Product Owner must personally author every item.
- User-story syntax is optional.
- A refinement label does not prove readiness.
- A score without defined inputs is not evidence.
- An unverified owner, date, estimate, metric, dependency, or customer claim remains `Needs verification`.
