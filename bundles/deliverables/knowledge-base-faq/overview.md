# Knowledge Base and FAQ Articles Overview

Define audience and verified questions; inspect current product, policy and support sources; write task-oriented answers with prerequisites, steps, outcomes, exceptions, safety and escalation; test findability, comprehension and accessibility; assign owners, versions, review triggers, analytics, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes search and support demand, audience and task research, current product and policy sources, interface and version evidence, eligibility and regional rules, tested procedures, screenshots and rights, accessibility review, escalation routes, analytics, owners, review dates, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish user intent, product state, interface label, policy, eligibility, availability, procedure result, accessibility conformance, support commitment, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
