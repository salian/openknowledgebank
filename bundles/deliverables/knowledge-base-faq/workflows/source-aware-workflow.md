# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory search and support demand, audience and task research, current product and policy sources, interface and version evidence, eligibility and regional rules, tested procedures, screenshots and rights, accessibility review, escalation routes, analytics, owners, review dates, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the knowledge base and FAQ article set, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to change product or policy, access accounts, promise eligibility or outcomes, expose personal data, publish content, claim accessibility conformance, or replace support escalation.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
