# Web and Digital Analytics Report Overview

Define decision, audience, properties, channels and period; govern metric and dimension definitions; inspect tag, event, identity, consent, filtering, attribution and retention settings; reconcile source coverage, missingness, bot and internal traffic, timezone and currency; distinguish observed associations from causes; report uncertainty, segments and comparatives with privacy controls; document limitations, reproducibility, decisions and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes approved business question and permitted use, property and stream identities, platform version and configuration, metric dictionary, tag and event implementation, consent and identity settings, filters and exclusions, timezone currency and attribution settings, source and campaign taxonomy, raw or exported data provenance, quality and reconciliation checks, bot and missingness analysis, calculations and code, uncertainty, privacy review, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish user identity, consent, implementation completeness, event, metric definition or value, channel, attribution, conversion, causal effect, revenue, return, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
