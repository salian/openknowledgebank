# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory approved business question and permitted use, property and stream identities, platform version and configuration, metric dictionary, tag and event implementation, consent and identity settings, filters and exclusions, timezone currency and attribution settings, source and campaign taxonomy, raw or exported data provenance, quality and reconciliation checks, bot and missingness analysis, calculations and code, uncertainty, privacy review, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the web and digital analytics report, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to change tracking consent identity retention or attribution, access or expose user data, activate audiences, alter campaigns or spend, publish performance claims, or represent causality or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status

- Provided:

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
