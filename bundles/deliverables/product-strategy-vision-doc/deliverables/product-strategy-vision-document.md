# Product Strategy and Vision Document

Use the smallest structure that supports the decision. Replace every bracketed field with evidence or mark it `Needs verification`.

## 1. Decision summary

- **Direct answer:** [What direction or decision is proposed?]
- **Why now:** [Evidence-backed trigger; otherwise `Needs verification`]
- **Decision owner and date:** [Provided or `Needs verification`]
- **Decision requested:** [What must be confirmed, rejected, or deferred?]

## 2. Evidence status

- **Verified:** [Source, scope, date/version, and fact]
- **Provided:** [User-supplied evidence, attributed to the provider]
- **Assumed:** [Working assumption and why it is useful]
- **Needs verification:** [Unknown, source needed, owner, and due point]

## 3. Product vision

Describe the durable future state and the change customers or users should experience. Keep it aspirational but concrete enough to guide choices. Do not smuggle in a target, market claim, or delivery commitment.

## 4. Strategic context

- **Audience and exclusions:** [Who is served and who is not targeted]
- **Problem and desired outcome:** [Evidence and unresolved questions]
- **Business or portfolio alignment:** [Objective or source; do not infer alignment]
- **Current context and horizon:** [Date, product state, constraints, and planning horizon]

## 5. Strategic choices

| Choice | Rationale and evidence | Alternative considered | Tradeoff or constraint | Revisit signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Choice] | [Evidence status] | [Alternative] | [What is not pursued] | [What would change it] |

## 6. Objectives and outcomes

| Objective | Customer/product outcome | Metric definition and baseline | Target/time horizon | Owner/status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Objective] | [Outcome] | [Provided or needs verification] | [Provided or needs verification] | [Provided or needs verification] |

## 7. Initiatives and sequencing

Describe initiatives as coherent bets or workstreams, not an unqualified feature list. For each, state the outcome supported, evidence, dependency, expected learning, and stop or revisit condition. Keep detailed requirements in the appropriate downstream document.

## 8. Risks, assumptions, and open questions

Separate risks from assumptions. Name the evidence needed to retire each uncertainty and the accountable reviewer for consequential decisions.

## 9. Review and change log

Record the context date, sources reviewed, decisions made, decisions deferred, conflicts found, and next review trigger. A strategy should be revisited when evidence or constraints materially change.

## Quality bar

A reviewer can identify the desired future, the audience and problem, the choices and tradeoffs, the outcomes and evidence, what is unknown, who must confirm, and what would cause revision without relying on implied local facts.
