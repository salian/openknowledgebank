# Bundle-Assisted Output (Illustrative)

## Direct answer

The available evidence is insufficient to choose a product direction or commit targets. The next useful artifact is an evidence-ready strategy draft with explicit decision points.

## Evidence status

- **Verified:** None.
- **Provided:** None.
- **Assumed:** The request concerns a digital product; confirm scope before using this draft.
- **Needs verification:** target audience, problem, desired change, business objective, market context, competitors, current product state, measures, targets, owners, horizon, constraints, and decision authority.

## Vision

Describe the durable change the product should create for a named audience. Keep this statement aspirational but avoid market leadership, revenue, adoption, or delivery claims until evidence is supplied.

## Strategy choices to resolve

| Question | Evidence needed | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who is the priority audience and who is excluded? | Segmentation and customer evidence with scope/date | Product owner confirms target segment |
| Which problem matters most? | Research, usage, support, or outcome evidence | Product and customer evidence owner confirms |
| What differentiates the product? | Current market and competitor evidence | Strategy owner confirms the claim |
| Which outcomes define progress? | Metric definitions, baseline, target, horizon, owner | Accountable business/product owner confirms |

## Initiatives

Do not select features from the empty evidence set. Once choices are confirmed, describe initiatives as bets with expected outcome, dependency, learning, tradeoff, and stop or revisit signal.

## Confirmation boundary

No roadmap, funding, staffing, customer promise, public claim, or live-system change is authorized by this draft.
