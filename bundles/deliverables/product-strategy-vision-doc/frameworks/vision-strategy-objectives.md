# Vision, Strategy, Objectives, and Initiatives

| Layer | Answers | Evidence needed | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vision | What better future should exist for whom? | User/customer problem and desired change | Vague aspiration or hidden promise |
| Strategy | Which choices will move us toward it, and what will we not do? | Context, evidence, constraints, alternatives | Feature list without tradeoffs |
| Objective | What outcome will show progress in a defined period? | Metric definition, baseline, target, horizon, owner | Unsupported target or output-only measure |
| Initiative | What coherent bet or workstream will test or advance the choice? | Rationale, dependency, expected learning, stop signal | Roadmap commitment presented as certainty |

## Use

1. Write the vision without pretending the path is known.
2. Make the strategy a set of choices and exclusions, not a catalog of work.
3. Translate choices into outcome-oriented objectives only when measures are defined.
4. Link initiatives to expected learning and outcomes, while keeping implementation detail in downstream artifacts.
5. Revisit each layer when evidence, constraints, or the desired customer change materially changes.

## Evidence status

The layer definitions are general guidance. Product-specific statements require `Verified` or `Provided` evidence; missing facts remain `Needs verification`.
