# Product Strategy and Vision Source-Aware Quality Check

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2. A high score requires evidence discipline, not just polished prose.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct answer and task fit | Missing or evasive | Partly answers the decision | Clear proposed direction and decision boundary |
| Evidence separation | Invents or blurs local facts | Labels some uncertainty | Separates Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification |
| Vision/strategy distinction | Collapsed or contradictory | Distinction implied | Desired future is distinct from choices and path |
| Strategic coherence | Feature list or unsupported claims | Some links between problems and choices | Audience, problem, choices, tradeoffs, objectives, and initiatives connect |
| Outcomes and measurement | Vague outputs or invented metrics | Outcomes named but definitions incomplete | Measures, baselines, targets, horizons, and owners are evidenced or explicitly pending |
| Reconciliation and learning | Ignores conflicts | Notes uncertainty without plan | Reconciles source scope/version conflicts and names next evidence |
| Safety and confirmation | Makes commitments or claims without authority | Generic caveat only | Clear reviewer and confirmation boundary for consequential action |

## Adversarial empty-evidence check

When no local facts are supplied, the response must not invent product names, customers, segments, metrics, targets, competitors, owners, dates, approvals, or market claims. It should provide a usable structure and ask for the smallest evidence needed next.

## Reviewer note

Do not award a high evidence score for elegant writing alone. A response that labels unsupported detail as fact fails the evidence-separation dimension.
