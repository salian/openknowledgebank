# Product Strategy & Vision Document

Use this bundle to draft a reviewable product direction from supplied evidence. Keep the desired future state distinct from the strategic choices, objectives, initiatives, and constraints used to move toward it.

## Evidence Boundary

Every substantive output separates `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification`. Do not invent customer evidence, market or competitor claims, metrics, targets, owners, dates, forecasts, resource commitments, or approval status. Show proposed consequential actions and obtain accountable confirmation before execution.

## Source Note

General structure is synthesized from Productboard product-strategy guidance and Atlassian product-management and vision-board guidance. Local product evidence is required for scenario-specific conclusions.
