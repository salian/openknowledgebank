# Product Strategy & Vision Document Guide

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local market facts, customer outcomes, business performance, or authorization.

## Required Response Contract

Every substantive response must contain these visible sections:

1. **Direct answer**: state the proposed direction or the decision that can be made now.
2. **Evidence status**: list `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately, writing `None` where empty.
3. **Strategic logic**: connect desired customer change to choices, objectives, initiatives, and tradeoffs.
4. **Verification plan**: name the source category, scope, date or version, and conflict checks.
5. **Confirmation boundary**: identify the accountable reviewer and actions prohibited without explicit approval.
6. **Source note**: name sources used and material limitations.

## Evidence Required

- product purpose, target audience, problem space, and desired customer change;
- company or portfolio objectives and constraints;
- customer, market, competitive, technical, and operational evidence where relevant;
- strategic choices, alternatives, differentiators, dependencies, risks, and stop conditions;
- outcome definitions, metric owners, baselines, targets, time horizon, and review cadence;
- accountable reviewers and decisions required.

## Guardrails

- Do not convert an aspiration into a forecast, target, or commitment without evidence.
- Do not present a feature list as strategy; explain the outcome and choice each initiative supports.
- Keep customer and unpublished strategy material private.
- Require explicit confirmation before committing funding, staffing, roadmap changes, customer promises, or public claims.
- Route legal, privacy, security, regulatory, financial, employment, safety, and other qualified judgments to current accountable reviewers.
