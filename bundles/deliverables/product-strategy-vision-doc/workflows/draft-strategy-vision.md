# Draft and Review a Product Strategy and Vision Document

## Direct answer

Produce a reviewable strategy document only after separating the desired future from the evidence-backed choices and outcomes that support it.

## Sequence

1. **Clarify the decision.** Identify product scope, audience, horizon, requested decision, accountable reviewer, and excluded decisions.
2. **Inspect the evidence.** Inventory product, customer, market, competitive, business, technical, operational, and policy sources. Record date/version and scope.
3. **Classify evidence.** Label each statement `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`. Preserve provenance and do not fill gaps.
4. **Draft the vision.** State the durable customer or user change. Avoid feature lists, unsupported market claims, and delivery promises.
5. **Make choices explicit.** Connect problems and context to strategic choices, alternatives, tradeoffs, dependencies, and revisit signals.
6. **Define outcomes.** State objectives and outcome measures. Include baseline, target, time horizon, owner, and definition only when supplied or verified.
7. **Add initiatives carefully.** Describe the smallest coherent set of bets that advances the strategy; route requirements and implementation detail downstream.
8. **Reconcile conflicts.** Compare definitions, time periods, segments, filters, versions, sources, and owners. Show the conflict and request resolution.
9. **Run the quality check.** Test clarity, coherence, evidence discipline, measurability, uncertainty handling, and approval boundaries.
10. **Confirm and maintain.** Obtain accountable confirmation for consequential commitments, then record context date, decisions, open questions, and review triggers.

## Verification plan

For each material claim, name the source category, scope, date or version, owner, and conflict check. For missing evidence, state the exact artifact or conversation needed next.

## Confirmation boundary

Do not commit funding, staffing, roadmap changes, customer promises, public claims, or changes to a live system from this document without explicit approval from the accountable owner. The document may propose options; it does not grant authority.

## Source note

This workflow synthesizes Productboard and Atlassian guidance. It is not a universal product-management standard; adapt it to local governance.
