# Product Strategy and Vision Source Checks

| Source category | Check | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product and customer evidence | Scope, segment, date, method, sample, and owner | Customer preference, adoption, retention, or outcome |
| Market and competitive evidence | Publication date, geography, definition, and source quality | Market size, competitor capability, or differentiation |
| Business objectives | Exact objective, period, status, and accountable owner | Alignment, funding, or priority |
| Product analytics | Event definition, denominator, time window, filters, and version | Baseline, trend, or target |
| Technical and operational evidence | System scope, version, dependency, and current state | Feasibility, capacity, reliability, or delivery date |
| Policy, legal, privacy, and security material | Current jurisdiction, policy version, and qualified reviewer | Compliance or legal conclusion |

## Minimum verification record

For each material claim, record source, scope, date/version, extraction or calculation rule, conflicts checked, and the person or team responsible for confirmation.

## Source note

Use the linked Productboard and Atlassian sources for general structure. Use current local evidence for product-specific conclusions.
