# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory organizational goals, user research, accessibility and language needs, content inventory and performance, channel and journey evidence, policy and brand requirements, rights and consent, workflow and tooling, taxonomy, measures, owners, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the content strategy document, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before collect user data, change taxonomy or workflow, delete or publish content, make regulated claims, use third-party material, spend funds, or represent user outcomes.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
