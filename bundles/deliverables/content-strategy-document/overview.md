# Content Strategy Document Overview

Define objectives and users from research, current content and channels, policy and brand constraints, accessibility and language needs, content roles and priorities, lifecycle and workflow, taxonomy, measurement, risks, ownership, decision rights, and review cadence.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes organizational goals, user research, accessibility and language needs, content inventory and performance, channel and journey evidence, policy and brand requirements, rights and consent, workflow and tooling, taxonomy, measures, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish user identity or need, content performance, channel role, accessibility compliance, rights, policy requirement, workflow capacity, outcome, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
