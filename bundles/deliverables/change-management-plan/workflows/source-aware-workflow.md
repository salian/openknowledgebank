# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory approved change scope, affected processes and roles, workforce and stakeholder impact assessment, consultation requirements, accessibility, communications, training and support needs, operational dependencies, measures, feedback, risks, owners, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the organizational change management plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before announce or implement change, alter employment terms or roles, identify resistant employees, monitor individuals, bypass consultation, spend funds, or claim adoption or benefits.

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
