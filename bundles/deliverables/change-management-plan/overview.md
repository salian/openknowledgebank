# Organizational Change Management Plan Overview

Define the change and decision rights, affected groups, impact evidence, accessibility, labor and policy constraints, participation, communications, training, support, resistance and feedback, measures, risks, sequencing, stop conditions, and governance.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes approved change scope, affected processes and roles, workforce and stakeholder impact assessment, consultation requirements, accessibility, communications, training and support needs, operational dependencies, measures, feedback, risks, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish stakeholder identity or readiness, employee sentiment, role impact, consultation duty, training need, adoption, resistance, benefit, legal compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
