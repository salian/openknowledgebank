# Quality Assurance Scorecard Overview

Define purpose, process and permitted use; derive criteria from current standards and customer or safety needs; specify observable anchors, critical failures, weights and missing data; design representative sampling and reviewer assignment; train and calibrate reviewers; measure agreement and uncertainty; govern disputes, privacy, fairness, changes, reporting, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes quality objectives and current standards, process and population, customer and safety requirements, criterion definitions and anchors, weighting rationale, sample frame and selection, reviewed records and provenance, reviewer training and calibration, agreement and uncertainty, privacy and fairness assessment, disputes, versioning, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish criterion applicability, sample representativeness, observed fact, score, weight, reviewer agreement, quality level, employee performance, fairness, compliance, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
