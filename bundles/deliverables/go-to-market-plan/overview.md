# Go-to-Market Plan Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware product launch and market execution planning analysis and a reviewable go-to-market plan.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering product, market, problem, segment, and research evidence, positioning, value proposition, differentiation, claims, and reasons to believe, offer, packaging, pricing, terms, and unit economics, channels, partners, sales process, enablement, and customer success readiness, launch scope, timeline, dependencies, supply, support, legal, privacy, and localization, and budget, forecast assumptions, measures, owners, risks, stop conditions, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer market demand, segment fit, positioning, price response, channel readiness, and launch outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before launching, publishing claims, changing price or terms, contacting customers, committing spend or forecasts, or entering markets without accountable approval.
