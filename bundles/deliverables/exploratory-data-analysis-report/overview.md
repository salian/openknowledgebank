# Exploratory Data Analysis Report Overview

Define the question and intended use, inspect data provenance and collection, population and sample, schema and units, missingness, duplicates, quality and transformations, distributions, outliers, relationships, subgroups, temporal effects, uncertainty, multiple exploration, sensitivity, privacy, limitations, reproducibility, and next tests.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes analysis question and prohibited uses, dataset version and provenance, collection and sampling process, population and period, schema and units, missingness and quality, transformations and code, descriptive outputs and graphics, subgroup and temporal checks, uncertainty and sensitivity, privacy controls, reproducible environment, review, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish data provenance, population, unit, quality, missingness mechanism, outlier meaning, relationship, causal explanation, significance, representativeness, model suitability, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
