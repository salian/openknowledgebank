# Organizational Design Proposal Overview

Confirm mandate and outcomes; diagnose work and current structure from evidence; define design principles, options, roles, spans, decision rights, interfaces, capacity and cost assumptions; assess employment, equality, accessibility, privacy and change impacts; document consultation, risks, transitions, measures, governance, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes business mandate and outcomes, current organization and work data, role and decision inventories, service and workload evidence, workforce and skills data, financial assumptions, employment terms and applicable law, equality and accessibility assessment, consultation records, options, transition risks, measures, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish performance gap, role necessity, workload, capacity, employee suitability, protected characteristic, consultation requirement, lawful process, cost, saving, impact, outcome, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
