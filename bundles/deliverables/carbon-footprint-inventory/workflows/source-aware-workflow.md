# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current standards, originator guidance, required sections, evidence expectations, audience needs, and local approval procedures.
3. Inventory reporting objective and entities, control or equity approach, facilities and value chain, period and base year, activity data and provenance, factors and GWP versions, calculations, estimates, exclusions, recalculation policy, data quality, uncertainty, controls, assurance, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the greenhouse gas emissions inventory and methodology report, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before collect restricted supplier data, select boundaries or factors without review, submit disclosures, buy offsets, set targets, claim conformance or reductions, or represent assurance.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
