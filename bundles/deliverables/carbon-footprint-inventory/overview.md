# Greenhouse Gas Emissions Inventory Overview

Verify the applicable standard edition and reporting objective; define organizational and operational boundaries, scopes, period, base year, gases, activity data, factors, global warming potentials, estimates, exclusions, recalculations, quality controls, uncertainty, and assurance.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes reporting objective and entities, control or equity approach, facilities and value chain, period and base year, activity data and provenance, factors and GWP versions, calculations, estimates, exclusions, recalculation policy, data quality, uncertainty, controls, assurance, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish organizational boundary, scope classification, activity quantity, emission factor, GWP value, calculated emissions, completeness, materiality, conformance, assurance, reduction, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
