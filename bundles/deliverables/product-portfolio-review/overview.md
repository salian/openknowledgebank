# Product Portfolio Review Overview

Confirm portfolio and decision scope; reconcile product ownership, lifecycle, outcomes, customer and operational evidence, costs, capacity, risks, obligations and dependencies; normalize definitions and periods; document uncertainty, scenarios and options; avoid false precision in rankings; record decisions, conditions, owners, measures, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes portfolio charter and decision authority, product inventory and owners, strategy and outcome definitions, customer and usage research, revenue and cost records, service performance, lifecycle and technical debt, workforce capacity, dependencies, risks and obligations, forecast assumptions, options and sensitivity, decisions, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish portfolio membership, product ownership, performance, customer value, cost, revenue, forecast, strategic fit, dependency, rank, investment outcome, or approval. Stop before taking any action to consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
