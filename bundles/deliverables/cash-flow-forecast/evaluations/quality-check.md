# Cash Flow Forecast Quality Check

Score each criterion `0` absent or unsafe, `1` partial, or `2` complete and evidence-backed.

1. **Scope and source integrity:** identifies entity, perimeter, currencies, cash definition, opening source, horizon, cadence, cut-off, version, and current local authorities without invented facts.
2. **Flow and timing support:** traces material receipts and payments to sources or explicit assumptions and uses supported timing profiles rather than unexplained spreading.
3. **Reconciliation integrity:** rolls opening cash through movements to ending cash, handles signs, currencies, transfers, restrictions, and reconciling effects, and leaves differences visible.
4. **Assumption discipline:** records rationale, source, period, dependencies, uncertainty, sensitivity, owner, update date, and approval state for material assumptions.
5. **Liquidity and headroom:** distinguishes cash, unrestricted cash, facilities, available liquidity, minimum cash, covenant headroom, and shortfall using evidenced definitions and conditions.
6. **Scenario and risk quality:** changes named drivers, preserves dependencies, shows threshold impacts, and keeps proposed mitigations separate from approved or executed actions.
7. **Actuals and variance integrity:** compares frozen forecasts with reconciled actuals under consistent scope and cut-off, explains amount and timing causes, and does not rewrite history.
8. **Decision safety and usability:** states a direct, qualified conclusion, limitations, gaps, owners, and review status without executing transactions or claiming solvency, approval, accuracy, or certainty.

## Blocking Defects

Regardless of score, block an authoritative forecast or liquidity claim when opening cash is unreconciled, required flows are omitted without disclosure, plugs force balance, facilities are treated as available without evidence, forecast values are presented as actuals, mitigations are treated as executed without approval, or future cash is stated as certain.
