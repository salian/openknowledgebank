# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan source-awareness check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name authoritative source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing critical activity, dependency, recovery objective, backup recoverability, role assignment, and plan effectiveness
- leave an unevidenced accountable reviewer as `Needs verification`
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before activating plans, declaring emergencies, contacting authorities or stakeholders, changing production recovery controls, or claiming readiness without tested and approved evidence
