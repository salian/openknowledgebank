# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware organizational continuity and technology recovery analysis and a reviewable business continuity and disaster recovery plan.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering organization, sites, services, processes, systems, people, and scenario scope, business impact analysis, criticality, dependencies, and maximum tolerable disruption, recovery time, recovery point, service level, and data assumptions, continuity, workaround, alternate-site, supplier, backup, restoration, and return strategies, activation, command, roles, succession, contact, communication, safety, and regulatory requirements, and exercise, test evidence, gaps, remediation, maintenance, distribution, access, and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer critical activity, dependency, recovery objective, backup recoverability, role assignment, and plan effectiveness.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before activating plans, declaring emergencies, contacting authorities or stakeholders, changing production recovery controls, or claiming readiness without tested and approved evidence.
