# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory channel creator editor and publication authority, brief audience and approved claims, raw gameplay voice face chat and source provenance, publisher platform music sound image font clip and mod rights, sponsorship affiliate and disclosure terms, privacy safety and moderation review, edit timeline and change approvals, captions text contrast and accessibility checks, export audio link metadata and upload tests, final approval and performance definitions.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the gaming video edit, rights log, and upload handoff, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access channels, use protected assets, expose personal data, fabricate gameplay or endorsements, upload, publish, monetize, change metadata, or represent views, revenue, or approval.
