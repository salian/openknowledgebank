# First-Line Supervisor of Sales Workers Source-Aware Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/41-1011.00
- https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/small-business/legal-requirements

O*NET grounds retail supervision and EEOC material is U.S.-focused; neither establishes local employment authority, policy, customer terms, performance, or lawful action.

## Evidence Required

- delegation, policy, staffing, schedule, and attendance evidence
- sales, service, returns, discounts, inventory, quality, and complaint records
- goal definitions, attribution, periods, comparisons, coaching, training, and accommodation evidence
- pricing, customer, employment, legal, HR, and approval boundaries

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable sales supervision decision brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before change schedules, approve discounts or refunds, contact customers, access personal data, issue discipline, change pay or goals, make employment decisions, or claim performance outcomes.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent employee intent, performance, misconduct, customer state, sales attribution, lawful employment action, pricing authority, or approval.
- Do not infer access, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
