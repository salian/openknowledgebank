# Source-aware copy and editorial brief

## Decision

State the direct answer, recommendation, or draft purpose.

## Evidence Table

Record source, scope, date/version, status, and limitation for each material input.

## Analysis

- Audience, objective, and channel.
- Claim-evidence table.
- Draft copy and alternatives.
- Editorial, rights, and approval checklist.

## Missing Evidence And Risks

List what could change the conclusion and what must be verified.

## Confirmation

Name the accountable reviewer and actions that require explicit approval.

## Source Note

Separate authoritative sources, local evidence, assumptions, and missing facts.
