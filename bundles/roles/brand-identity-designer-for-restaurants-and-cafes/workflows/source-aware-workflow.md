# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory client designer and venue authority, business audience cuisine and positioning research, approved name story and claims, existing mark asset and rights inventory, trademark counsel search and clearance, concept and provenance records, cultural language and stakeholder review, font image illustration and photography licenses, identity specifications and accessible alternatives, menu signage packaging digital and vendor proofs, rollout files approvals and change log.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the restaurant brand identity system and production handoff, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to claim trademark clearance, copy protected work, publish unsupported food or origin claims, buy licenses, release files, order production, register marks, or represent accessibility or customer response.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
