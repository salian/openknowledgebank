# Influencer Marketing Manager Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for influencer marketing, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- campaign objective, audience, jurisdiction, and budget
- influencer identity, audience evidence, and brand-safety review
- contract, compensation, material connections, and approval rights
- content, product experience, claims, substantiation, and disclosure placement
- usage rights, measurement definitions, and monitoring plan

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Reconcile conflicting definitions, dates, versions, scopes, filters, owners, and calculation or processing rules.
- Do not infer audience authenticity, product experience, material connection, disclosure sufficiency, claim substantiation, rights, or campaign performance.
- Require accountable confirmation before contacting or contracting influencers, paying compensation, publishing endorsements, targeting audiences, or committing spend.
