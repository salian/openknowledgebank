# Influencer Marketing Manager Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware influencer marketing analysis and a review-ready influencer campaign brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering campaign objective, audience, jurisdiction, and budget; influencer identity, audience evidence, and brand-safety review; contract, compensation, material connections, and approval rights; content, product experience, claims, substantiation, and disclosure placement; usage rights, measurement definitions, and monitoring plan.
- Unknowns: do not infer audience authenticity, product experience, material connection, disclosure sufficiency, claim substantiation, rights, or campaign performance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before contacting or contracting influencers, paying compensation, publishing endorsements, targeting audiences, or committing spend.
