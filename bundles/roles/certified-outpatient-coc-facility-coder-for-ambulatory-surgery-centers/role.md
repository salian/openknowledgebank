# Certified Outpatient Facility Coder for Ambulatory Surgery Centers Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/ambulatory-surgical-center-asc
- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/national-correct-coding-initiative-ncci-edits/medicare-ncci-policy-manual
- https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/minimum-necessary-requirement/index.html

CMS and HHS sources do not establish facility certification, a performed procedure, device or supply use, billing component, code, modifier, medical necessity, claim acceptance, payment, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, agency, platform, standards-body, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, records, permissions, configurations, code sets, claims, content, constraints, tests, and outcomes.
- Named owners, privacy and rights controls, reproducible validation, qualified review, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable ASC facility coding and claim review workpaper with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access PHI, infer procedures devices supplies or necessity, alter records, select codes without authority, submit or adjust claims, or represent coverage or payment.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent certification, procedure, device, supply, component, code, modifier, necessity, claim, denial, payment, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
