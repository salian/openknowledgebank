# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory patient payer facility provider and date identifiers, certification enrollment contract eligibility and authorization, authenticated order operative anesthesia nursing recovery implant drug and supply records, facility professional and supplier responsibility, current ASC covered-procedure payment payer licensed code and NCCI sources, code modifier and claim validation, submission acknowledgement remittance denial adjustment reconciliation, privacy controls and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the ASC facility coding and claim review workpaper, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access PHI, infer procedures devices supplies or necessity, alter records, select codes without authority, submit or adjust claims, or represent coverage or payment.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
