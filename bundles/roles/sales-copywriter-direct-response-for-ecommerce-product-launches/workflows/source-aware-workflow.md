# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory approved product and variant records, testing and claim substantiation, audience research, review and testimonial source consent and relationships, inventory source and timestamp, approved price discounts bundles and terms, shipping return warranty and support records, capacity and deadline evidence, creative and media rights, channel copy versions, consent and tracking design, disclosure link checkout and accessibility tests, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the ecommerce product-launch sales copy deck, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish unsupported claims, fabricate scarcity, use reviews or media without rights, change prices or terms, collect leads or payments, launch campaigns, or represent conversion, revenue, or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
