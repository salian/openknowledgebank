# Articulate Storyline E-Learning Developer for Corporate Training Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://access.articulate.com/support/article/Storyline-360-Accessibility-Conformance-Report-VPAT
- https://articulate.com/support/article/Storyline-360-How-to-Publish-a-Course-for-Web-Distribution
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/

Articulate and W3C guidance does not establish local content accuracy, interaction behavior, accessibility conformance, assessment validity, package compatibility, LMS tracking, completion, learning, or approval.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, tax, education, healthcare, accessibility, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable Storyline course package and validation record with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish inaccurate content, copy protected assets, claim conformance or certification, upload or overwrite LMS courses, alter completion records, release packages, or represent learning results.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent content accuracy, trigger or variable behavior, accessibility conformance, assessment validity, score, package compatibility, LMS state, completion, learning effect, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
