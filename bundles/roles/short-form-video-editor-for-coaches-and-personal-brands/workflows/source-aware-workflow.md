# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory creator offer and channel authority, source footage and transcript, credential and claim substantiation, qualified health financial legal or career review where applicable, client testimonial and likeness consent, sponsor affiliate and material-connection disclosures, confidential-data review, music footage font and logo rights, caption layout and accessibility checks, exports platform tests analytics definitions, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the approved short-form video package and claim ledger, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to fabricate credentials or quotes, distort meaning, publish unsupported advice or outcomes, expose client data, omit disclosures, use unlicensed assets, upload videos, or promise reach or sales.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
