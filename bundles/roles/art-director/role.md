# Art Director Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for art direction and creative governance, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- objective, audience, channels, budget, and decision criteria
- creative brief, concepts, references, and brand standards
- source assets, licenses, releases, and usage rights
- production specifications, accessibility, claims, disclosures, and approvals
- vendor capabilities, schedule, estimates, feedback, and measurement definitions

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer audience response, creative effectiveness, asset rights, subject consent, claim substantiation, approval, or campaign outcome.
- Do not invent an artifact owner, author, date, or version.
- Require accountable confirmation before publishing creative, licensing assets, engaging vendors, approving claims, or committing budget.
