# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory advertiser account and spend authority, product catalog inventory price and offer records, fulfillment and return terms, claim substantiation, customer-data provenance consent and suppression, creative creator testimonial and music rights, catalog and pixel configuration, event and attribution definitions, campaign structure budgets and bids, experiment and stop rules, platform test outputs, reporting reconciliations, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the DTC Meta media plan and performance creative brief, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, upload customer data, publish claims, change catalog or tracking, launch or scale ads, spend funds, contact users, or represent performance, attribution, incrementality, revenue, or return.

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
