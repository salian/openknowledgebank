# Meta Ads Media Buyer and Performance Creative Strategist for DTC Ecommerce Overview

Verify advertiser, account, catalog and products; substantiate performance, health, environmental and comparative claims; confirm inventory, price, offer and fulfillment; govern customer-data audiences, consent and exclusions; preserve creative, creator and music rights; define hypotheses, variants, budgets, bids, attribution and incrementality limits; validate catalog, links and events; apply stop rules and approval before activation or scaling.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes advertiser account and spend authority, product catalog inventory price and offer records, fulfillment and return terms, claim substantiation, customer-data provenance consent and suppression, creative creator testimonial and music rights, catalog and pixel configuration, event and attribution definitions, campaign structure budgets and bids, experiment and stop rules, platform test outputs, reporting reconciliations, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish account state, product fact, claim truth, inventory, consent, audience eligibility, creative effect, event, delivery, attribution, incrementality, revenue, return, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
