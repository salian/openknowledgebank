# Chief AI Officer (CAIO) Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map board and CEO oversight, CAIO, CIO, CTO, CDO, product, engineering, data science, model risk, security, privacy, legal, compliance, procurement, internal audit, domain owners, and affected stakeholders; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify organization mandate, AI definition, use case, affected people, model and provider, data provenance, evaluation, limitations, impacts, controls, monitoring, incidents, legal basis, procurement, cost, owner, approval, and outcome.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before approve or deploy AI, access data, procure models, change controls, accept risk, make automated decisions, publish evaluations, notify affected people, or claim compliance or safety.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
