# Instagram Social Media Manager for Realtors Overview

Verify account, brokerage and listing authority; define audience and content pillars; substantiate property, market and professional claims; preserve image, music, testimonial and client permissions; apply endorsement disclosures and fair-housing review; protect personal and location data; document moderation, records, accessibility, scheduling, measurement limits, escalation and publication approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes account and brokerage authority, listing agreements and current property records, market-data sources, brand and advertising rules, image music and testimonial rights, material-connection disclosures, fair-housing review, client and occupant consent, privacy and location controls, content calendar, moderation policy, accessibility review, analytics configuration, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish listing authority, property or market fact, media right, endorsement, disclosure sufficiency, protected trait, consent, audience identity, engagement, lead, fair-housing compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
