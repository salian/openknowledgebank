# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory entity and ownership records, jurisdictions and periods, physical locations employees contractors inventory and trade-show facts, ecommerce marketplace and fulfillment agreements, destination transaction extracts and reconciliations, gross and taxable sales definitions, exemption certificates, current state statutes regulations notices and thresholds, marketplace-facilitator treatment, product and service taxability analysis, registrations and prior returns, system configuration, review, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the sales-tax nexus and registration analysis workpaper, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access restricted tax data, choose unsupported positions, register entities, configure tax systems, calculate or collect tax, file returns, pay liabilities, contact authorities, or certify compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:

- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
