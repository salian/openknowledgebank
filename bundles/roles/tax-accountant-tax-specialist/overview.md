# Tax Accountant / Tax Specialist Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware licensed or supervised tax analysis and preparation analysis and a review-ready tax work-product brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering taxpayer, engagement scope, preparer credentials, jurisdiction, tax type, period, and deadlines; complete facts, source documents, elections, prior filings, notices, and authorizations; current statutes, regulations, forms, instructions, rulings, and effective dates; calculations, positions, assumptions, contrary authority, disclosures, and workpapers; privacy, conflicts, review, signature, filing, payment, and representation status.
- Unknowns: do not infer taxpayer fact, eligibility, tax treatment, liability, deadline, authority status, filing readiness, or professional approval.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before giving final tax advice, signing or filing returns, contacting authorities, making elections, paying liabilities, or representing a taxpayer.
