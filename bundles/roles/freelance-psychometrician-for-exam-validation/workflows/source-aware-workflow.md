# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory sponsor data and analysis authority, intended-use and decision statement, construct blueprint and content review, population administration sample and accommodation records, item and response provenance, security and privacy controls, cleaning exclusions missingness and versioned code, model assumptions diagnostics item statistics reliability validity and fairness analyses, uncertainty sensitivity and reproducibility outputs, cut-score source, limitations, reviewer decisions and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the psychometric validation report and reproducible analysis package, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or expose secure items or examinee data, select models to force results, alter scores, set cut scores, certify candidates, claim validity or fairness, or publish without authority.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
