# Freelance Psychometrician for Exam Validation Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.testingstandards.net/open-access-files.html
- https://www.ets.org/research/policy_research_reports/publications/report/2002/ibxf.html
- https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/civil-rights-laws/disability-discrimination

Assessment standards and civil-rights sources do not establish local construct coverage, sample quality, model fit, reliability, validity, fairness, cut score, certification fitness, or approval.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, credentialing, assessment, testing, platform, safety, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected identity, authority, source records, permissions, requirements, calculations, tests, exceptions, and outcomes.
- Named owners, qualified review, reproducible validation, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable psychometric validation report and reproducible analysis package with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or expose secure items or examinee data, select models to force results, alter scores, set cut scores, certify candidates, claim validity or fairness, or publish without authority.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent construct coverage, sample representativeness, model fit, item quality, reliability, validity, fairness, cut score, score meaning, certification fitness, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
