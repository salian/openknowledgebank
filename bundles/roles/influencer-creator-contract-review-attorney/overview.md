# Influencer and Creator Contract Review Attorney Overview

Verify attorney licensure, client, conflicts, parties, jurisdiction and negotiation authority; inspect campaign brief, platforms, deliverables, revisions, approval, posting and takedown terms; align FTC and platform disclosures; distinguish ownership, license, likeness, name, handle, whitelisting, paid-media and AI or derivative use; analyze exclusivity, morality, confidentiality, data, metrics, warranties, indemnity, liability, payment, expenses, cancellation and dispute terms; preserve client decisions, signature and settlement authority.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes attorney license engagement conflicts and client authority, parties and jurisdiction, campaign brief products platforms and dates, deliverable and revision schedule, disclosure and claim substantiation, content likeness name handle music and asset ownership, organic paid whitelisting territory term and derivative rights, exclusivity and conflicts, metrics and reporting access, fee expenses tax and payment terms, cancellation morality indemnity liability and insurance, redlines decisions signatures, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish client authority, deliverable, disclosure duty, ownership, license, likeness right, usage, exclusivity, metric, payment, liability, enforceability, outcome, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
