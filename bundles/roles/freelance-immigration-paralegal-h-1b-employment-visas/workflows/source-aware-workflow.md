# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory supervisor authorization and engagement, conflicts and client identity, petition and beneficiary scope, current USCIS and DOL instructions and editions, passport status and immigration records, employer entity and job evidence, degree and credential records, wage and LCA records, prior filings and notices, deadlines and fee source, translations and certifications, draft review, signatures, filing authorization, receipts, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the supervised employment-immigration matter packet and evidence index, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to give legal advice, select strategy without supervision, contact agencies or clients beyond authority, sign or file forms, pay fees, alter evidence, promise status or approval, or disclose confidential data.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
