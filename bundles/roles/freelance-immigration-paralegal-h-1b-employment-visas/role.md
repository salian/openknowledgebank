# Freelance Immigration Paralegal for H-1B and Employment Visas Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
- https://www.uscis.gov/avoid-scams/find-legal-services
- https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/immigration/h1b

USCIS and DOL sources describe United States federal programs and authorized legal service; they do not establish client identity, status, eligibility, specialty occupation, wage compliance, deadline, legal strategy, petition approval, or authority to practice law.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, and professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, audience and consent evidence, offer and product records, account configuration, performance data, and constraints.
- Named owners, substantiation, validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable supervised employment-immigration matter packet and evidence index with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to give legal advice, select strategy without supervision, contact agencies or clients beyond authority, sign or file forms, pay fees, alter evidence, promise status or approval, or disclose confidential data.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent client identity or status, eligibility, specialty occupation, employer relationship, wage compliance, deadline, form answer, legal conclusion, filing, receipt, agency decision, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
