# Quantitative Analyst (Quant) Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware quantitative finance model design, validation, risk analysis, and controlled use and a review-ready quantitative model review brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering use case, products, markets, date, and decision; market and reference data provenance, vendor rights, and timestamps; model mathematics, assumptions, calibration, backtest, validation, risk, limits, code, environment, governance, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer price, alpha, model validity, risk, forecast, trading result, or production fitness.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that trade, deploy a model, change limits, commit capital, or give personalized investment advice.
