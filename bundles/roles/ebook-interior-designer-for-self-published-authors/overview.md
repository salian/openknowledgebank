# Ebook Interior Designer for Self-Published Authors Overview

Verify author or publisher authority, final manuscript, edition, formats and channels; preserve text and editorial decisions while applying semantic headings, navigation, notes, tables, images and responsive typography; clear fonts, illustrations and third-party content; provide meaningful alternatives and reading order; avoid image-only text except where justified; generate target formats with controlled metadata; validate package structure and links; test representative apps, devices, themes and font sizes; document platform-specific differences; require author approval before upload and never promise acceptance or sales.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes author publisher and publication authority, final manuscript checksum edition and change control, style and structure map, heading navigation note table image and link review, font illustration image quotation and excerpt rights, alt text reading order language metadata and accessibility tests, EPUB and target-format validator outputs, app device theme and text-resize tests, source package backups and version history, upload decision and platform response, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish manuscript approval, editorial fact, structure, right, accessibility conformance, format validity, device behavior, platform acceptance, sale, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
