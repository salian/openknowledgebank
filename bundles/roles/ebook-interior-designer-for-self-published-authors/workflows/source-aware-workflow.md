# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory author publisher and publication authority, final manuscript checksum edition and change control, style and structure map, heading navigation note table image and link review, font illustration image quotation and excerpt rights, alt text reading order language metadata and accessibility tests, EPUB and target-format validator outputs, app device theme and text-resize tests, source package backups and version history, upload decision and platform response, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the validated ebook package, source files, and issue log, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to rewrite substantive content without authority, use unlicensed fonts or images, remove accessibility semantics, overwrite final manuscripts, upload books, claim conformance or acceptance, or promise sales.
