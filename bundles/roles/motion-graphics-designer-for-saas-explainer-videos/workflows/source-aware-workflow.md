# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory product owner and publication authority, product build and source recordings, audience and use-case brief, claim substantiation and approved testimonials, script storyboard animatic and review notes, brand system, footage illustration font music voice and logo rights, captions transcript and accessibility review, source files version history and export settings, platform playback tests, analytics definitions, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the source-linked SaaS explainer animation package, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent features or outcomes, fabricate UI, use protected assets without rights, publish unsupported claims, expose customer data, upload videos, claim conformance, or promise conversions.

List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
