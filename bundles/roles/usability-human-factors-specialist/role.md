# Usability / Human Factors Specialist Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for usability and human-factors research, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- research question, product, users, tasks, environment, hazards, and decision criteria
- participant population, recruitment, consent, incentives, accommodations, and safeguarding
- protocol, scenarios, measures, equipment, versions, and moderator instructions
- recordings, observations, errors, adverse events, data handling, coding, and analysis
- accessibility, ergonomics, limitations, validation, risk controls, review, and approval

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer participant consent, representativeness, task success, error cause, hazard severity, usability, accessibility, or safety conclusion.
- Do not invent an artifact owner, author, date, or version.
- Require accountable confirmation before recruiting or contacting participants, recording sessions, collecting sensitive data, changing safety controls, or claiming usability, accessibility, or safety.
