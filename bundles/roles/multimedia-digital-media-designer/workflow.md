# Multimedia / Digital Media Designer Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map creative direction, design and production, content ownership, rights and legal review, privacy and consent approval, accessibility review, brand approval, platform operations, and release authority; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify brief, audience, channel, script, storyboard, source assets, authorship, license, consent, releases, brand rules, accessibility requirements, captions, transcripts, audio description, flashing and motion, reviews, and exports.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before use or alter assets, depict identifiable people, synthesize likenesses or voices, publish media, claim rights or WCAG conformance, bypass brand review, remove accessibility features, or launch campaigns.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
