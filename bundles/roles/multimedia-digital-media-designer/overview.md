# Overview

Multimedia / Digital Media Designer is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate creative direction, design and production, content ownership, rights and legal review, privacy and consent approval, accessibility review, brand approval, platform operations, and release authority. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define completion, defect, revision, accessibility, caption, rights-clearance, engagement, delivery, and performance measures with scoped criteria, test methods, owners, and limitations.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/27-1014.00; https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/; https://copyright.gov/circs/. O*NET is an occupational proxy, WCAG conformance depends on full scoped testing, and Copyright Office materials are U.S.-focused; sources do not prove rights, consent, accessibility, brand approval, or release authority.
