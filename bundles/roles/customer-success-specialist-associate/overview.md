# Customer Success Specialist / Associate Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware customer success operations analysis and a review-ready customer success account brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering account, contract, products, stakeholders, and permissions; customer goals and agreed outcomes; adoption and health definitions with dated evidence; interaction and support history; risks, issues, and escalation ownership; renewal terms and personal-data handling.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer health, sentiment, adoption, contractual entitlement, renewal likelihood, or promised outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before contacting customers, changing CRM records, promising outcomes, granting concessions, or changing commercial terms.
