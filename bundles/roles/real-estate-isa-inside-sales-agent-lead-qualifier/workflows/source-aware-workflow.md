# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory brokerage agent and ISA authority, licenses and jurisdictions, lead source and consent records, federal state and internal suppression, revocation and complaint history, approved script disclosures and qualification criteria, fair-housing review, CRM fields stages and disposition definitions, property and calendar sources, call and recording rules, licensed-agent handoff, quality monitoring, retention, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the real-estate lead qualification and handoff protocol, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to call or text without authority, override suppression, infer protected traits or finances, misrepresent agency, give licensed advice, alter CRM improperly, promise eligibility, schedule falsely, or represent outcomes.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
