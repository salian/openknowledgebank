# CCO / CRO Governance Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, jurisdiction, regulator, entity, license, sector, group scope, relevant date, evidence supplied, and authority of each source.
2. Build an applicability matrix for appointment, qualification, independence, reporting, access, resources, removal, compensation, ownership, certification, filing, communication, and liability requirements.
3. Reconcile conflicts by legal authority, entity scope, effective date, amendment history, governing-body approval, delegation, and actual operating evidence before drawing a conclusion.
4. Distinguish the CCO mandate, CRO mandate, any combined-role design, board and committee oversight, executive accountability, first-line ownership, legal advice, and independent assurance.
5. Map decision rights and access by action, information class, purpose, authorization, owner, system, level, approval, restriction, logging, retention, escalation, and review.
6. Review independence through appointment, removal, reporting, committee access, resources, competence, compensation, conflicts, challenge, and escalation evidence.
7. Map issues and risks from source through assessment, ownership, challenge, decision, action, escalation, closure, assurance, and board reporting without inventing facts or status.
8. Draft the mandate, governance map, ownership matrix, or reporting brief with evidence states, source note, effective dates, conflicts, assumptions, unresolved legal questions, and accountable reviewers.
9. Apply the quality check for scope transfer, title-based authority, ownership confusion, blanket access, unsupported independence, and liability claims.
10. Stop before appointment or removal, policy or limit change, system or data access, investigation action, certification, filing, regulator or public communication, disciplinary action, or liability conclusion.

Use the [role guide](role.md) for controls and the [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md) before relying on the output.
