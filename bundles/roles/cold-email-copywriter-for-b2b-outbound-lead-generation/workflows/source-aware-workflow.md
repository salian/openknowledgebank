# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory sender legal identity and authority, recipient jurisdictions and business context, contact source and collection date, lawful-basis or consent analysis, suppression and objection records, approved audience criteria, account research provenance, offer and claim substantiation, approved proof and references, physical address and opt-out operation, domain and authentication records, cadence and retention, test outputs, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the B2B outbound email sequence and compliance brief, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to source or enrich contacts, infer intent, upload lists, send email, override suppression, track recipients, change domains, contact replies, or represent delivery, meetings, pipeline, or compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
