# Sales Compensation / Incentive Analyst Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware sales incentive plan analysis, crediting, payout reconciliation, and governance and a review-ready sales compensation analysis brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering plan, period, population, territory, quota, and eligibility; CRM, bookings, crediting, and source-system data; plan document and version; calculations, adjustments, disputes, protected employee data, pay-equity review, communication, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer eligibility, attainment, credit, payout, dispute outcome, fairness, or legal compliance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that change a plan, quota, credit, payout, or employee record, or communicate compensation decisions.
