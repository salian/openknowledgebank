# Legal Operations Manager Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory matter, client, engagement, privilege, confidentiality, hold, retention, vendor, invoice, budget, workflow, access, and service-performance. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate lawyer professional judgment, client authority, legal-operations administration, records and privacy control, security approval, procurement and finance control, vendor delivery, and independent review. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define matter cycle time, spend, forecast, invoice, workload, service, vendor, and knowledge measures with privilege-safe scope, formulas, periods, owners, and exclusions. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not give legal advice, waive privilege, disclose client information, open or close matters, change holds or retention, grant repository access, approve invoices, instruct counsel, or execute contracts without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
