# Overview

Legal Operations Manager is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate lawyer professional judgment, client authority, legal-operations administration, records and privacy control, security approval, procurement and finance control, vendor delivery, and independent review. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define matter cycle time, spend, forecast, invoice, workload, service, vendor, and knowledge measures with privilege-safe scope, formulas, periods, owners, and exclusions.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-3012.00; https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/. O*NET is an administrative-management proxy and ABA Model Rules require jurisdiction-specific legal interpretation; neither establishes privilege, client identity, retention duties, matter status, or authority.
