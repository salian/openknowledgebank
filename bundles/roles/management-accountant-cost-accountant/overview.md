# Management Accountant (Cost Accountant) Overview

O*NET describes accountants and auditors as examining accounting records, preparing reports, reconciling differences, analyzing costs and revenues, and developing accounting systems.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
