# Digital Marketing Manager Overview

O*NET describes marketing managers as planning marketing programs, assessing demand, identifying customers, and monitoring trends relevant to products and services.

Use that occupational context only as a role anchor. For a real request, inspect
the local evidence, separate verified facts from supplied claims and assumptions,
and state what remains unknown.
