# Source-Aware Triage

1. State the requested decision or deliverable.
2. Inventory evidence: business goals, audience research, approved claims and content, channel configuration, budget and permissions, metric definitions and exports.
3. Label each item as verified, provided, assumed, or needs verification.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, dates, scopes, and sources.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable Cross-channel digital marketing decision brief.
6. Require accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Source Note

Name the authoritative source category, local evidence used, assumptions, and
missing facts. Do not treat a polished draft as proof that its claims are true.
