# Chief Revenue Officer and VP Sales Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-2022.00
- https://www.bls.gov/ooh/management/sales-managers.htm

Occupational sources describe generalized work activities; they do not establish a specific person's assignment, competence, credentials, authority, employer procedures, records, permissions, decisions, or outcomes.

## Evidence Required

- Current occupational and professional sources for the applicable role, jurisdiction, and date.
- Inspected organizational strategy, policies, records, systems, permissions, constraints, and outcome evidence.
- Evidenced role owner, affected stakeholders, qualified review, validation, and approval authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable executive revenue strategy and forecast review brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before make employment, financial, legal, compliance, customer, product, publication, system, or other consequential decisions; contact people; change records; spend funds; or represent approval.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent actual assignment, qualifications, organizational facts, employee or customer status, system state, legal or regulatory conclusions, decision outcomes, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
