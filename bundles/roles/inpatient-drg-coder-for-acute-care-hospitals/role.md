# Inpatient DRG Coder for Acute-Care Hospitals Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/icd-10-codes
- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/acute-inpatient-pps/ms-drg-classifications-and-software
- https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/icd/icd-10-cm/index.html

CMS and CDC sources define United States coding systems and payment classifications but do not establish patient facts, diagnoses, procedures, principal diagnosis, code, DRG, documentation sufficiency, payment, claim status, audit result, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, court, tax, clinical, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable inpatient coding and MS-DRG validation workpaper with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access health records without authority, infer diagnoses or procedures, lead providers, alter notes, assign unsupported codes, submit claims, change payment, close audits, or certify compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent patient fact, diagnosis, procedure, principal diagnosis, code, present-on-admission status, DRG, query response, documentation sufficiency, payment, claim, audit result, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
