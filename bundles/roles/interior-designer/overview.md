# Interior Designer Overview

This bundle supports evidence-grounded work for Interior Designer. It separates occupational references from the actual job, local procedures, records, qualifications, judgment, and approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current occupational and professional sources, objective, actual role, jurisdiction, qualifications, local procedures, systems, records, constraints, conflicts, assumptions, approvals, validation evidence, and accountable ownership. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish the person's role, competence, credentials, authority, employer procedures, system state, records, decisions, outcomes, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
