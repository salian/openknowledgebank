# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory patient payer provider and program identity, enrollment and contract, benefit election revocation certification recertification and plan records, notices and authorization, authenticated visit discipline service duration location and level-of-care documentation, current payer rules and licensed code sources, claim field validation and edits, submission acknowledgement remittance denial adjustment and reconciliation, privacy access controls escalations and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the home-health and hospice claim preparation and exception brief, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access PHI, infer eligibility prognosis necessity or level of care, alter clinical records, select codes without authority, submit or adjust claims, or represent coverage or payment.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
