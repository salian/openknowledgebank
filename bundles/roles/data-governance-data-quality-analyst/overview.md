# Data Governance / Data Quality Analyst Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware data governance, quality assessment, issue triage, and remediation planning and a review-ready data governance and quality brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering data domains, owners, stewards, definitions, catalog, lineage, and source systems; quality rules, profiling methods, thresholds, results, and dates; privacy classification, retention, access, controls, issues, remediation, certification, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer ownership, business meaning, lineage, quality score, fitness for use, privacy status, or compliance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that change access, definitions, retention, or controls; expose data; or certify data quality.
