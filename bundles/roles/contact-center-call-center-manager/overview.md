# Contact Center / Call Center Manager Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware contact-center demand, staffing, service, and quality planning and a review-ready contact-center operating brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering service scope, channels, jurisdiction, and hours; contact history and demand forecast; workforce definitions, staffing, skills, and schedules; SLA and KPI definitions; QA samples and calibration; recording, consent, privacy, vendor, and budget rules.
- Unknowns: do not infer future volume, staffing need, SLA performance, QA result, consent, or compliance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that change staffing or schedules, contact or record customers, issue credits, or commit vendor spend.
