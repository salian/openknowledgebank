# Content Designer Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for content design for digital services, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- user need, task, research, journey, channel, and success criteria
- current content, analytics, search terms, feedback, and source dates
- service policy, legal basis, product behavior, claims, and approvals
- content model, hierarchy, language, localization, accessibility, and reading context
- prototypes, usability evidence, ownership, lifecycle, publication, and measurement

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer user need, policy meaning, product behavior, content accuracy, comprehension, accessibility, or performance.
- Do not invent an artifact owner, author, date, or version.
- Require accountable confirmation before publishing content, changing service instructions, making legal or product claims, or claiming usability or accessibility.
