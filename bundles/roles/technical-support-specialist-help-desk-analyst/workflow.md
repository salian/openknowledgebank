# Technical Support Specialist / Help Desk Analyst Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map requester, service desk triage, system ownership, engineering diagnosis, identity and access approval, security incident response, privacy review, business ownership, and change authority; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify request, requester identity, entitlement, affected service, symptoms, timestamps, environment, changes, logs, diagnostics, knowledge records, access approvals, recovery methods, actions, outcome, and escalation.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before reset credentials, recover accounts, grant access, run commands, change configurations, access devices or logs, disclose data, close tickets, contact users, or declare an incident resolved.
