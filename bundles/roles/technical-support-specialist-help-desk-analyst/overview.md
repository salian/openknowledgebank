# Overview

Technical Support Specialist / Help Desk Analyst is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate requester, service desk triage, system ownership, engineering diagnosis, identity and access approval, security incident response, privacy review, business ownership, and change authority. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define response, resolution, reopen, escalation, first-contact, backlog, availability, user-impact, and quality measures with clocks, pause rules, severity, scope, owners, and source systems.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/15-1232.00; https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html. O*NET describes user-support work and NIST digital-identity guidance requires local risk tailoring; neither proves requester identity, entitlement, diagnosis, system state, authorization, recovery success, or resolution.
