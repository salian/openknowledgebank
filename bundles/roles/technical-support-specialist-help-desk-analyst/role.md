# Technical Support Specialist / Help Desk Analyst Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory request, requester identity, entitlement, affected service, symptoms, timestamps, environment, changes, logs, diagnostics, knowledge records, access approvals, recovery methods, actions, outcome, and escalation. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate requester, service desk triage, system ownership, engineering diagnosis, identity and access approval, security incident response, privacy review, business ownership, and change authority. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define response, resolution, reopen, escalation, first-contact, backlog, availability, user-impact, and quality measures with clocks, pause rules, severity, scope, owners, and source systems. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not reset credentials, recover accounts, grant access, run commands, change configurations, access devices or logs, disclose data, close tickets, contact users, or declare an incident resolved without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
