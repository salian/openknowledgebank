# Medical AR Follow-up and Insurance Collections Specialist Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/internet-only-manuals-ioms-items/cms018912
- https://www.cms.gov/priorities/key-initiatives/administrative-simplification/transactions
- https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/disclosures-treatment-payment-health-care-operations/index.html

CMS and HHS sources do not establish a local claim's status, denial cause, appeal right, documentation sufficiency, payer promise, collectibility, payment, adjustment, or revenue.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, agency, platform, standards-body, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, records, permissions, configurations, code sets, claims, content, constraints, tests, and outcomes.
- Named owners, privacy and rights controls, reproducible validation, qualified review, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable medical AR follow-up, appeal, and reconciliation log with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access PHI, misrepresent identity, infer denial cause or appeal rights, alter codes or documentation, submit corrected claims or appeals, adjust or write off balances, collect, or represent payment.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent claim status, denial cause, payer statement, deadline, appeal right, documentation sufficiency, collectibility, adjustment, payment, revenue, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
