# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory advertiser and account authority, current policy and account state, provider credentials and offer records, curriculum price refund and support terms, claim substantiation and typicality, testimonial consent and disclosures, audience source and exclusions, creative and landing rights, consent pixel and data-flow review, event and attribution settings, budget and bid authority, experiment design and stop rules, tests, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the Meta ads launch strategy and experiment plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, upload audiences, infer sensitive traits, publish claims, install tracking, launch or alter ads, spend funds, contact leads, or represent approval, conversion, earnings, or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
