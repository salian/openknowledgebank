# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory brand account number and integration authority, jurisdictions and current messaging review, subscriber source disclosure consent timestamp scope and revocation, suppression and quiet-hour configuration, segment logic and data minimization, product inventory price discount shipping and testimonial substantiation, message sender opt-out link coupon personalization and accessibility tests, event delivery checkout and CRM reconciliation, budget experiment stop rules and send approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the e-commerce SMS campaign, consent, and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, import subscribers, infer consent, override suppression, publish false urgency, send messages, change automations, spend funds, or represent delivery, conversion, revenue, or return.
