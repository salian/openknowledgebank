# Commercial Real Estate Offering Memorandum Designer Overview

Confirm client, property, audience, brokerage or offering context and counsel boundaries; reconcile property, site, zoning, tenancy, leases, income, expenses, capital and debt facts to approved sources; date market and comparable data; distinguish actuals, estimates and projections with assumptions and risks; protect tenant and confidential data; preserve photo, map, logo, font and plan rights; avoid unsupported investment claims; route securities, brokerage, environmental and legal statements to qualified reviewers before controlled distribution.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes client brokerage and property authority, offering and audience context, title survey zoning and site records, building and environmental reports, rent roll leases and tenant-consent controls, operating statements and reconciliations, capital and debt records, market and comparable sources with dates, projection model assumptions and sensitivity, counsel-approved offering terms risks and disclaimers, photo map logo font and plan rights, confidentiality classification, accessibility review, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish property fact, tenant or lease, income, expense, projection, valuation, offering term, right, legal compliance, investment merit, return, distribution, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
