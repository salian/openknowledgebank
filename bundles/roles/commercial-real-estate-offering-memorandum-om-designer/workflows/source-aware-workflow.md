# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory client brokerage and property authority, offering and audience context, title survey zoning and site records, building and environmental reports, rent roll leases and tenant-consent controls, operating statements and reconciliations, capital and debt records, market and comparable sources with dates, projection model assumptions and sensitivity, counsel-approved offering terms risks and disclaimers, photo map logo font and plan rights, confidentiality classification, accessibility review, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the commercial real-estate offering memorandum and source ledger, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent property tenant or financial facts, make investment or legal claims, expose tenant data, use assets without rights, set offering terms, solicit investors, distribute the OM, or promise returns.

State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
