# Financial Reporting Accountant Overview

O*NET describes accountants and auditors as examining accounting records, preparing and analyzing financial statements, reconciling differences, reviewing controls, and assessing conformity with reporting standards.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
