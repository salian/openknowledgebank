# Overview

Inside sales is remote selling through approved channels. It can include research, outreach, qualification, discovery, objection handling, quoting, follow-up, and pipeline reporting, but scope differs by organization and market.

Separate marketing lead generation, SDR prospecting, ISR qualification and selling, account-executive closing, sales engineering, legal and finance review, sales operations, and customer-success handoff. Titles do not establish account ownership, discount, commitment, signature, recording, or CRM authority.

Before contact, verify identity, audience, channel, location, local time, consent or other approved basis, suppression status, approved sender, and current policy. FTC rules are scoped examples, not universal permission.

Use explicit evidence states and continue with the [role guide](role.md) and [workflow](workflow.md).
