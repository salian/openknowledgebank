# Inside Sales Review Workflow

1. Record request, audience, account, channel, jurisdiction, local time, intended action, evidence, and authority.
2. Verify identity, source, consent or approved basis, suppression, ownership, approved sender and system, and current communication policy.
3. Inventory approved product, claim, pricing, quote, legal, privacy, security, implementation, and service evidence.
4. Prepare discovery questions and separate provided facts, prospect statements, assumptions, missing evidence, and disqualifiers.
5. Apply documented qualification and stage definitions; reconcile CRM, email, call, quote, and contract records by source, owner, date, and status.
6. Draft outreach, discovery, objection, quote, or pipeline material using only approved claims and visible caveats.
7. Apply the quality check for invented personalization, consent, capabilities, pricing, commitments, stage, or outcomes.
8. Stop before contact, recording, suppression override, discount, commitment, signature, or CRM change.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
