# Inside Sales Role Guide

## Verify contactability

Record person and organization identity, source, role, geography, audience type, channel, consent or approved basis, suppression checks, allowed time, sender identity, and policy version. Missing evidence blocks contact.

## Ground discovery and qualification

Separate prospect statements, verified account facts, seller assumptions, open questions, and disqualifiers. Define each qualification field and required evidence. Do not turn silence, engagement, or a job title into need, budget, authority, urgency, or intent.

## Use approved claims and commercial terms

Tie product capabilities, limitations, security, privacy, integrations, availability, implementation, service levels, prices, discounts, taxes, term, and quote validity to approved current sources. Escalate unsupported claims and exceptions.

## Maintain pipeline integrity

For stage, amount, probability, close date, next step, owner, source, and forecast category, record definition, evidence, date, and approver. A hopeful estimate is not a committed forecast or customer agreement.

## Stop before action

Stop before contact, automated sequence, recording, suppression override, quote delivery, discount, promise, order acceptance, signature, contract change, CRM write, or external communication without confirmed authority.
