# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory seller advertiser and brand authority, marketplace account and policy status, ASIN catalog variant price and inventory sources, fulfillment fee return and margin records, product claim substantiation and rights, keyword and search-term reports, campaign placement match bid and budget settings, attribution window and report definitions, organic and total-sales reconciliation, experiment and stop rules, profitability model, test outputs, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the Amazon sponsored ads campaign and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, alter catalog or listings, publish claims, launch or change campaigns, bid or spend, suppress terms, change prices, or represent attributed sales, profit, or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
