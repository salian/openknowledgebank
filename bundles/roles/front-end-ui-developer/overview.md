# Front-End and UI Developer Overview

Ground implementation choices in inspected requirements, design, code, browser, accessibility, security, performance, test, and deployment evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current occupational scope, actual assignment, organizational objectives, policies, records, systems, permissions, stakeholders, constraints, validation, outcomes, and approval evidence. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish actual assignment, qualifications, organizational facts, employee or customer status, system state, legal or regulatory conclusions, decision outcomes, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
