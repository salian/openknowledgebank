# Computer and Information Research Scientist Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware computing and information research design, experimentation, and reproducibility and a review-ready computing research brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general behavior and authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- Required evidence: research question, decision, prior work, and novelty criteria; datasets, licenses, provenance, sampling, and preprocessing; algorithms, code, dependencies, hardware, environment, and versions; protocol, baselines, controls, metrics, uncertainty, ablations, and error analysis; reproducibility artifacts, peer review, security, ethics, limitations, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer novelty, causality, reproducibility, benchmark superiority, safety, or research validity.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that access restricted data, execute untrusted code, release code or data, publish a finding, or claim novelty or superiority.
