# Overview

Telemarketer / Telesales Representative is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate seller, telemarketer, list provider, campaign owner, compliance, privacy, legal, payment, fulfillment, customer service, quality monitoring, and authorized supervisor. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define eligible population, attempts, connects, dispositions, sales, cancellations, complaints, suppression, revenue, period, source, and exclusions before calculating performance.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/41-9041.00; https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/telemarketing-sales-rule; https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/qa-telemarketers-sellers-about-dnc-provisions-tsr-0. O*NET defines a broad occupation; FTC materials are scoped U.S. federal rules and guidance. Call type, audience, consent, exemptions, state law, and FCC requirements need current review.
