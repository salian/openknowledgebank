# Telemarketer / Telesales Representative Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map seller, telemarketer, list provider, campaign owner, compliance, privacy, legal, payment, fulfillment, customer service, quality monitoring, and authorized supervisor; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify seller, caller, recipient, number source, audience, location, local time, consent, relationship, suppression, exemption, script, disclosures, claim substantiation, price, payment, order, recording, owner, and approval.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before place calls, use prerecorded or automated technology, record, override suppression, change scripts, make claims, take payment, submit orders, schedule follow-up, or update systems.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
