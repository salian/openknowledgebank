# Telemarketer / Telesales Representative Role Guide

## 1. Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## 2. Build the evidence record

Inventory seller, caller, recipient, number source, audience, location, local time, consent, relationship, suppression, exemption, script, disclosures, claim substantiation, price, payment, order, recording, owner, and approval. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## 3. Preserve role boundaries

Separate seller, telemarketer, list provider, campaign owner, compliance, privacy, legal, payment, fulfillment, customer service, quality monitoring, and authorized supervisor. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## 4. Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define eligible population, attempts, connects, dispositions, sales, cancellations, complaints, suppression, revenue, period, source, and exclusions before calculating performance. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## 5. Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## 6. Stop before consequential action

Do not place calls, use prerecorded or automated technology, record, override suppression, change scripts, make claims, take payment, submit orders, schedule follow-up, or update systems without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
