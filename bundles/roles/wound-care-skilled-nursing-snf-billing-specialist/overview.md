# Wound Care and Skilled Nursing Facility Billing Specialist Overview

Verify patient, payer, provider, facility, stay and benefit-period facts; inspect authenticated wound assessment, orders, procedures, supplies, medications, practitioner and nursing records; distinguish facility, professional and supplier responsibilities and apply date-specific consolidated-billing rules; use current payer policy and licensed code sources; never infer wound stage, diagnosis, necessity or service; protect PHI and images; validate claim fields and edits; reconcile submissions, remittances, denials and adjustments; require clinical, coding and billing approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes patient payer provider facility stay and benefit records, eligibility authorization and enrollment, authenticated wound assessment images orders procedure nursing practitioner supply and medication records, facility professional supplier contracts and consolidated-billing analysis, current payer policy licensed code and edit sources, claim validation submission acknowledgement remittance denial adjustment and reconciliation, privacy image-access controls escalations and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish coverage, wound condition or stage, diagnosis, service, supply, necessity, billing responsibility, code, claim, denial, payment, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
