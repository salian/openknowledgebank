# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory patient payer provider facility stay and benefit records, eligibility authorization and enrollment, authenticated wound assessment images orders procedure nursing practitioner supply and medication records, facility professional supplier contracts and consolidated-billing analysis, current payer policy licensed code and edit sources, claim validation submission acknowledgement remittance denial adjustment and reconciliation, privacy image-access controls escalations and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the wound-care and SNF billing review and exception brief, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access PHI or wound images, infer condition stage diagnosis or necessity, create services or supplies, select codes without authority, submit or adjust claims, or represent payment.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
