# Overview

A CHRO role can coordinate enterprise people strategy and HR capability, but its mandate varies. It does not automatically confer board, manager, legal, payroll, benefits-fiduciary, investigation, filing, or individual employment authority.

Separate enterprise strategy, workforce analytics, organization design, talent and succession, rewards, culture, employee relations, labor relations, HR operations, legal advice, manager decisions, and board oversight. Map accountability from local charters and delegations.

Use explicit evidence states. With no local evidence, headcount, demographics, cost, attrition, performance, potential, pay, cases, findings, compliance, owners, and outcomes are `Needs verification`.

Sources: O*NET 11-3121.00 and scoped EEOC employer and recordkeeping guidance.
