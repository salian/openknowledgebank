# CHRO Decision Review Workflow

1. Record request, decision, jurisdiction, entities, worker populations, time period, evidence, sensitivity, and authority.
2. Map board, executive, manager, HR, legal, finance, payroll, benefits, privacy, security, and employee-representative roles.
3. Inventory approved workforce, policy, contract, compensation, recruiting, survey, performance, succession, employee-relations, and compliance evidence.
4. Define and reconcile every metric by population, period, formula, source, exclusions, and quality.
5. Separate aggregate strategy from individual employment facts and decisions; minimize data and suppress unsafe detail.
6. Draft options with evidence states, fairness and accessibility checks, legal questions, cost, risks, dependencies, decision owners, consultation, and approvals.
7. Apply the quality check for invented workforce facts, biased proxies, unsupported causation, privacy leakage, and authority overreach.
8. Stop before individual action, pay change, access, investigation step, filing, disclosure, or communication.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
