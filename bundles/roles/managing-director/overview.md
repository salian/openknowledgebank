# Overview

Managing Director is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate board or owner governance, managing-director accountability, functional ownership, finance and legal control, risk challenge, operational execution, and independent review. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define portfolio, financial, operational, client, workforce, risk, and outcome measures with formulas, currencies, periods, baselines, targets, owners, and adverse indicators.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-1011.00. Managing Director varies substantially by jurisdiction and organization; O*NET's Chief Executives profile is only a proxy and does not establish board delegation, officer status, fiduciary duty, portfolio scope, or authority.
