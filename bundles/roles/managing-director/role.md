# Managing Director Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory charter, delegation, governance, portfolio, strategy, financial, risk, workforce, client, operational, performance, and commitment. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate board or owner governance, managing-director accountability, functional ownership, finance and legal control, risk challenge, operational execution, and independent review. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define portfolio, financial, operational, client, workforce, risk, and outcome measures with formulas, currencies, periods, baselines, targets, owners, and adverse indicators. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not commit funds, sign agreements, direct regulated work, change strategy, restructure teams, alter compensation, make client commitments, publish disclosures, or claim outcomes without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
