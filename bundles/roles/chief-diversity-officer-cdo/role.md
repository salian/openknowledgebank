# Chief Diversity Officer (CDO) Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory workforce definitions, demographic data provenance, consent or legal basis, access, representation, hiring, pay, promotion, attrition, experience, accommodation, complaints, interventions, and outcomes. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate executive sponsorship, HR ownership, equal-opportunity and legal review, privacy and data stewardship, workforce analytics, employee representation, line-management execution, and independent review. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define populations, denominators, comparison groups, time windows, suppression rules, uncertainty, intersectional limits, adverse-impact review, owners, and approved uses before interpreting disparities. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not infer protected characteristics, identify individuals, make employment decisions, set quotas, change pay or promotion, disclose workforce data, file reports, investigate complaints, or claim discrimination or causality without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
