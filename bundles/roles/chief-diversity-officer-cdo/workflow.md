# Chief Diversity Officer (CDO) Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map executive sponsorship, HR ownership, equal-opportunity and legal review, privacy and data stewardship, workforce analytics, employee representation, line-management execution, and independent review; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify workforce definitions, demographic data provenance, consent or legal basis, access, representation, hiring, pay, promotion, attrition, experience, accommodation, complaints, interventions, and outcomes.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before infer protected characteristics, identify individuals, make employment decisions, set quotas, change pay or promotion, disclose workforce data, file reports, investigate complaints, or claim discrimination or causality.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
