# Business Development Representative (BDR) Overview

O*NET describes service sales representatives as contacting customers, answering questions, developing proposals, and matching service capabilities to customer needs.

Use that occupational context only as a role anchor. For a real request, inspect
the local evidence, separate verified facts from supplied claims and assumptions,
and state what remains unknown.
