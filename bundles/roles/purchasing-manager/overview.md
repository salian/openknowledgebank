# Overview

Purchasing management coordinates sourcing and buying activity, but a title does not establish budget, award, signature, ordering, payment, system, or legal authority.

## Decision boundaries

Separate business need and specification ownership; procurement process stewardship; technical, security, privacy, sustainability, finance, and legal evaluation; approval; contracting; ordering; receipt; and payment. Record who owns each step from evidence.

Preserve competition by recording solicitation version, bidder access, questions and answers, bid receipt, criteria and weights, evaluator conflicts, score rationale, exceptions, approvals, and communications. Do not fill gaps with plausible supplier facts.

FAR procurement-integrity and conflict rules are useful scoped examples, not universal procurement policy.

Use explicit evidence states and continue with the [role guide](role.md) and [workflow](workflow.md).
