# Purchasing Review Workflow

1. Record request, decision, demand, specification, jurisdiction, procurement regime, budget, timeline, evidence, and authority.
2. Map stakeholders and segregated roles from request through payment.
3. Inventory solicitation, amendments, bidder communications, bids, criteria, scorecards, conflicts, approvals, contracts, orders, receipts, invoices, and performance records.
4. Freeze criteria and normalize only evidence-supported scope, quantity, unit, currency, tax, freight, term, service level, and risk assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by source, version, date, authorization, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with criteria-level rationale, uncertainty, exclusions, risks, total-cost definition, decision owner, and approval path.
7. Apply the quality check for invented suppliers, changed criteria, hidden conflicts, unsupported savings, and authority overreach.
8. Stop before supplier contact, award, signature, order, payment, system change, or external communication.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
