# Provider Enrollment and Credentialing Specialist for CAQH and PECOS Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/enrollment-renewal/providers-suppliers/chain-ownership-system-pecos/enrollment-applications
- https://www.cms.gov/files/document/pecos-fact-sheet.pdf
- https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/guidance/index.html

CMS and HHS sources do not establish provider identity, licensure, sanctions status, application accuracy, payer participation, approval, effective date, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, credentialing, assessment, testing, platform, safety, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected identity, authority, source records, permissions, requirements, calculations, tests, exceptions, and outcomes.
- Named owners, qualified review, reproducible validation, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable provider enrollment and credentialing status record with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to impersonate providers, create credentials or practice facts, conceal sanctions, sign attestations, expose sensitive data, submit or change enrollments, accept contract terms, or claim approval.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent identity, credential, license, sanction or exclusion status, practice fact, payer requirement, attestation, submission, participation, effective date, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
