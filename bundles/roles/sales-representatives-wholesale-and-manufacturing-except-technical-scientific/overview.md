# Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing (Except Technical/Scientific) Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware wholesale and manufacturing sales analysis and a review-ready account and offer brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering account, contacts, consent, territory, and relationship history; product, inventory, eligibility, pricing, discount, and approval rules; customer needs, specifications, quantities, and timing; quote, order, tax, shipping, fulfillment, warranty, and return terms; CRM records, forecast definitions, commitments, and escalation paths.
- Unknowns: do not infer contact consent, customer need, inventory, price, discount authority, order status, delivery date, or forecast.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before contacting prospects, issuing quotes, granting discounts, placing orders, committing delivery, or changing CRM records.
