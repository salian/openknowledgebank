# Chief Legal Officer / General Counsel Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map organizational client authority, board governance, lawyer professional judgment, constituent interests, compliance and risk challenge, operational ownership, records and privacy control, and external counsel; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify client identity, engagement scope, facts, jurisdiction, law, privilege, confidentiality, conflicts, investigations, claims, contracts, governance, advice, approvals, and escalation.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before give or adopt legal advice, represent a person, waive privilege, disclose confidential information, resolve conflicts, file or sign documents, settle claims, instruct counsel, or make regulated decisions.
