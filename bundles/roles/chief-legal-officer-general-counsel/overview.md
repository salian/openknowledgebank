# Overview

Chief Legal Officer / General Counsel is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate organizational client authority, board governance, lawyer professional judgment, constituent interests, compliance and risk challenge, operational ownership, records and privacy control, and external counsel. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define legal-service and risk indicators without converting them into unsupported legal conclusions; record scope, denominator, period, source, owner, privilege status, and limitations.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/23-1011.00; https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_13_organization_as_client/; https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/. O*NET is occupational and ABA Model Rules are models, not jurisdiction-specific law; sources do not establish client identity, privilege, conflicts, legal conclusions, reporting duties, or authority.
