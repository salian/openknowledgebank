# Chief Legal Officer / General Counsel Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory client identity, engagement scope, facts, jurisdiction, law, privilege, confidentiality, conflicts, investigations, claims, contracts, governance, advice, approvals, and escalation. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate organizational client authority, board governance, lawyer professional judgment, constituent interests, compliance and risk challenge, operational ownership, records and privacy control, and external counsel. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define legal-service and risk indicators without converting them into unsupported legal conclusions; record scope, denominator, period, source, owner, privilege status, and limitations. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not give or adopt legal advice, represent a person, waive privilege, disclose confidential information, resolve conflicts, file or sign documents, settle claims, instruct counsel, or make regulated decisions without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
