# General and Operations Manager Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware general and operations management analysis and a review-ready operations decision brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering objectives, scope, owners, and decision rights; current processes, service levels, staffing, and capacity; budget, commitments, and vendor terms; KPI definitions and dated performance evidence; operational risks, controls, compliance obligations, and escalation paths.
- Unknowns: do not infer capacity, performance, cost, authority, staffing need, compliance status, or operational outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing staffing, approving budgets, engaging vendors, changing controls, or making external commitments.
