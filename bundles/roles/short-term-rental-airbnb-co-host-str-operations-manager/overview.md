# Short-Term Rental and Airbnb Co-Host Operations Manager Overview

Verify owner, property, co-host permissions, local registration, lease or HOA constraints and platform terms; maintain sourced listing facts, calendar and owner-approved pricing; apply nondiscrimination and approved guest communication; collect only necessary guest data; control keys, codes and vendor access; document cleaning, maintenance, fire and life safety and emergency contacts; reconcile reservations, fees, payouts and damages without moving funds beyond authority; escalate safety, legal and guest incidents; preserve records and owner approvals.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes owner property and co-host authority, current local registration tax occupancy and safety rules, lease HOA and insurance records, platform account permissions and terms, sourced listing amenities rules availability and price approvals, reservation guest communication and privacy controls, access code and vendor logs, cleaning inspection maintenance alarm and emergency records, fees payouts damages and reconciliation, nondiscrimination and accommodation review, incident escalation and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish owner authority, registration, listing fact, availability, price, guest eligibility, payment, access, property or fire safety, incident resolution, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
