# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory owner property and co-host authority, current local registration tax occupancy and safety rules, lease HOA and insurance records, platform account permissions and terms, sourced listing amenities rules availability and price approvals, reservation guest communication and privacy controls, access code and vendor logs, cleaning inspection maintenance alarm and emergency records, fees payouts damages and reconciliation, nondiscrimination and accommodation review, incident escalation and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the short-term-rental operations control book and exception log, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to list or alter property without authority, discriminate, expose guest data, change prices or reservations, issue access unsafely, move funds, conceal incidents, contact emergency services outside protocol, or claim compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
