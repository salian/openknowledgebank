# Paralegal / Legal Assistant Overview

O*NET describes paralegals and legal assistants as supporting lawyers through fact investigation, legal-document preparation, precedent research, record review, and case organization.

Use that occupational context only as a role anchor. For a real request, inspect
the local evidence, separate verified facts from supplied claims and assumptions,
and state what remains unknown.
