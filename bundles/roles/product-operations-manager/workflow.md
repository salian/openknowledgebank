# Role Review Workflow

1. Record the request, organization context, product model, evidence supplied, intended audience, and decision to support.
2. Inventory the current charter, stakeholders, workflows, systems, data definitions, cadences, deliverables, and approval paths without accessing unprovided systems.
3. Select relevant role archetypes as hypotheses and mark all unsupported local facts `Needs verification`.
4. Build a service map that separates administration, analysis, facilitation, recommendation, approval, and execution.
5. Map Product Operations boundaries and handoffs with Product Management and cross-functional partners.
6. Review workflows and feedback loops for observed friction while preserving source, scope, privacy, and system ownership.
7. Define or reconcile measures only from documented formulas, periods, filters, sources, owners, and caveats.
8. Draft the requested deliverable with evidence states, source note, open questions, decisions needed, and accountable review needs.
9. Apply the quality check and identify assumptions, access gaps, conflicts, and consequential actions.
10. Stop before live-system, workflow, dashboard, roadmap, access, vendor, customer-contact, or communication changes.

Use the [role guide](role.md) for controls and the [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md) before relying on the output.
