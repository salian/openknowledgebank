# Contract E-Discovery and Managed Document Review Attorney Overview

Verify attorney status, supervising counsel, matter, engagement, conflicts, protective orders and access; preserve collection, processing and chain-of-custody context; use the current review protocol, issue definitions, date ranges, custodians and coding rules; code only inspected content and metadata; escalate privilege, privacy, trade-secret, foreign-law and technical issues; apply calibration, sampling, disagreement and rework controls; maintain notes, audit trail and confidentiality; preserve counsel authority over privilege calls, redactions, clawback, production and certifications.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes reviewer license or authorization, supervising counsel and engagement, matter and jurisdiction, conflicts and confidentiality agreements, legal hold and preservation scope, protective and ESI orders, collection custodian source and chain-of-custody records, processing and deduplication settings, current review protocol issues and coding definitions, document content and metadata, privilege and privacy escalation rules, calibration and QC samples, disagreement and rework log, redactions production set and load files, counsel decisions, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish matter scope, custodian, collection completeness, responsiveness, issue code, privilege, confidentiality, redaction, waiver, production obligation, legal conclusion, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
