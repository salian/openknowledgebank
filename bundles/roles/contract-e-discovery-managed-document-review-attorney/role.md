# Contract E-Discovery and Managed Document Review Attorney Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_26
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_34
- https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_37
- https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/

Federal rules and ABA model rules do not establish a matter's governing law, collection completeness, responsiveness, privilege, confidentiality, coding, production obligation, waiver, legal conclusion, or attorney authority.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, court, tax, clinical, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable managed document review coding and quality-control record with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access matter data without authority, disclose documents, make final privilege or legal calls beyond authority, alter source data, waive protections, produce records, certify completeness, contact parties, or promise outcomes.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent matter scope, custodian, collection completeness, responsiveness, issue code, privilege, confidentiality, redaction, waiver, production obligation, legal conclusion, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
