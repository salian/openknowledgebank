# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory facility department and analyst authority, patient encounter and schedule, authenticated orders notes administration procedure and supply records, charge-master version, licensed code and payer edit sources, interface and batch logs, missing duplicate late and exception analysis, control totals, correction and approval history, privacy access and segregation controls, downstream claim acknowledgement and reconciliation.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the charge-capture reconciliation and exception log, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access PHI, create services or units, choose clinical codes without authority, change charge masters or prices, enter or reverse charges, release claims, or represent revenue or compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
