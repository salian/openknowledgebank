# Charge Capture and Charge Entry Analyst Overview

Define facility, department, payer and charge-source scope; reconcile schedules, authenticated documentation, orders, administrations, devices, supplies and departmental systems to the charge master and claim; use date-appropriate payer and licensed code sources; detect missing, duplicate, late and inconsistent charges without creating facts; protect PHI and segregate duties; route clinical, coding and pricing judgments; validate batches and control totals; retain corrections, approvals and downstream acknowledgement.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes facility department and analyst authority, patient encounter and schedule, authenticated orders notes administration procedure and supply records, charge-master version, licensed code and payer edit sources, interface and batch logs, missing duplicate late and exception analysis, control totals, correction and approval history, privacy access and segregation controls, downstream claim acknowledgement and reconciliation. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish encounter, service, supply, unit, charge, code, modifier, price, ownership, claim readiness, revenue, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
