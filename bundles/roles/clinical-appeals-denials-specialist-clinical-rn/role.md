# Clinical Appeals and Denials Specialist Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/appeals-grievances/fee-for-service/appeals
- https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html
- https://www.cms.gov/medical-billing/understanding-costs/appeal-a-decision

CMS and HHS sources describe selected United States appeal and privacy processes; they do not establish local plan terms, patient facts, medical necessity, code accuracy, deadline, payer error, appeal rights, submission, reversal, payment, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, court, tax, clinical, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable licensed-review clinical appeal packet and evidence index with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or disclose health records without authority, make clinical judgments without licensure, alter records or codes, sign or submit appeals, contact payers, waive rights, promise reversal or payment, or represent compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent patient fact, coverage, authorization, medical necessity, code, denial reason, deadline, appeal right, payer error, submission, reversal, payment, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
