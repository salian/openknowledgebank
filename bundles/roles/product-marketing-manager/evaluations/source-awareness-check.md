# Product Marketing Manager source-awareness check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing customer needs, market size, competitor claims, product capabilities, roadmap commitments, launch dates, channel performance, win-loss causes
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before external claims, launch commitments, pricing, roadmap disclosure, customer communications, or campaign spend
