# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory company identity and deck authority, intended audience and offering context, approved strategy and product records, market-source dates and methods, customer and logo consent, metric dictionary and source systems, financial statements and reconciliations, projection model and assumptions, cap table and fundraising terms from counsel-approved records, team credentials, risk and disclaimer review, asset and font rights, confidentiality classification, accessibility review, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the startup pitch deck and source ledger, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent or alter metrics, make investment or legal claims, disclose confidential data, use logos without consent, set valuation or terms, solicit investors, distribute the deck, or represent funding outcomes.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
