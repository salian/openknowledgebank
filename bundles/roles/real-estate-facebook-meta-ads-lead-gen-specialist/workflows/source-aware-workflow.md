# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory advertiser and brokerage authority, jurisdiction and housing-service scope, current Meta account and Special Ad Category settings, audience and exclusion design, property and service records, claim substantiation, creative and data rights, form notices and consent, CRM and follow-up controls, budget and bid authority, conversion and attribution settings, test evidence, reviews, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the real-estate Meta lead campaign brief, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, select or exclude protected audiences, upload customer data, publish housing claims, launch or alter ads, collect or contact leads, change budgets, or represent lead quality or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status

- Provided:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
