# Customer Service Representative Overview

O*NET describes customer service representatives as handling inquiries and complaints, providing information, reviewing accounts, recording actions, and resolving service problems.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
