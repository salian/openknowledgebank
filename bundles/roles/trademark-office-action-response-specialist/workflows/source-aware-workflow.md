# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory attorney license good standing engagement conflicts and client authority, applicant domicile and representation requirement, application serial number and complete TSDR record, office-action type issue date and deadline calculation, issue-by-issue matrix, current statute regulation TMEP and case sources, client factual declarations and authenticated evidence, draft and attorney review history, signature and filing authority, correct response form attachments fee submission receipt and status, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the attorney-approved office-action response and filing evidence packet, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to practice law without authorization, invent facts or evidence, miss or alter deadlines, sign for another person, disclose confidential data, file or pay without authority, claim acceptance, or guarantee registration.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
