# Content Creator / Social Media Producer Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware digital content creation and social production analysis and a review-ready content production brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering objective, audience, channel, jurisdiction, and success criteria; creative brief, script, claims, and approvals; source assets, copyright, licenses, releases, and usage rights; sponsorships, material connections, disclosure requirements, and brand safety; accessibility, schedule, distribution, budget, and measurement definitions.
- Unknowns: do not infer asset rights, subject consent, claim substantiation, disclosure sufficiency, audience response, or content performance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before publishing content, using unclear-rights assets, targeting minors, entering sponsorships, or committing spend.
