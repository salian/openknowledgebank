# Content Creator / Social Media Producer Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for digital content creation and social production, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- objective, audience, channel, jurisdiction, and success criteria
- creative brief, script, claims, and approvals
- source assets, copyright, licenses, releases, and usage rights
- sponsorships, material connections, disclosure requirements, and brand safety
- accessibility, schedule, distribution, budget, and measurement definitions

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Reconcile conflicting definitions, dates, versions, scopes, filters, owners, and calculation or processing rules.
- Do not infer asset rights, subject consent, claim substantiation, disclosure sufficiency, audience response, or content performance.
- Require accountable confirmation before publishing content, using unclear-rights assets, targeting minors, entering sponsorships, or committing spend.
