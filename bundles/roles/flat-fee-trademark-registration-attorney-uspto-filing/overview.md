# Flat-Fee Trademark Registration Attorney for USPTO Filing Overview

Verify attorney licensure, client identity, engagement, conflicts and flat-fee scope; establish mark, owner, goods and services, territories and business context; define and document search scope and limitations; provide qualified registrability and risk advice; verify filing basis, specimens, dates and declarations; confirm current forms and fees; protect confidential records; obtain client decisions and signatures; docket filing, receipts, deadlines and later-stage exclusions.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes attorney license and engagement, client and owner legal identity, conflicts, flat-fee inclusions and exclusions, mark formats and translations, goods services and business evidence, territory and search strategy, search databases date and results, legal analysis, filing basis and use records, specimens and dates, declarations, current forms and fee schedule, client decisions, signatures, filing receipt, and docket. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish client or owner identity, mark ownership, use, search completeness, conflict, registrability, class or goods, filing basis, specimen validity, fee, signature, filing, registration, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
