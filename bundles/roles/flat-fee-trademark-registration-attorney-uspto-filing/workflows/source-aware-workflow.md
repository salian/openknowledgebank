# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory attorney license and engagement, client and owner legal identity, conflicts, flat-fee inclusions and exclusions, mark formats and translations, goods services and business evidence, territory and search strategy, search databases date and results, legal analysis, filing basis and use records, specimens and dates, declarations, current forms and fee schedule, client decisions, signatures, filing receipt, and docket.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the trademark clearance and application review packet, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to form an attorney-client relationship without authority, give unqualified legal advice, clear a mark automatically, sign or file, pay fees, make declarations, contact third parties, promise registration, or miss deadlines.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
