# Flat-Fee Trademark Registration Attorney for USPTO Filing Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-hire-private-trademark-attorney
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/application-filing-basis
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply

USPTO guidance describes United States application processes and benefits of private counsel; it does not establish ownership, use, search completeness, clearance, registrability, filing basis, specimen validity, fee, signature authority, registration, or legal outcome.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, and professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, audience and consent evidence, offer and product records, account configuration, performance data, and constraints.
- Named owners, substantiation, validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable trademark clearance and application review packet with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to form an attorney-client relationship without authority, give unqualified legal advice, clear a mark automatically, sign or file, pay fees, make declarations, contact third parties, promise registration, or miss deadlines.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent client or owner identity, mark ownership, use, search completeness, conflict, registrability, class or goods, filing basis, specimen validity, fee, signature, filing, registration, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
