# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory current product catalog and variants, inventory source and timestamp, approved prices discounts and terms, shipping return and fulfillment rules, claim substantiation, review and testimonial consent, creative rights, audience consent and suppression, sender identity and address, accessibility and localization review, links codes and rendering tests, campaign and attribution settings, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the DTC ecommerce email campaign copy deck, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to change prices or inventory, publish claims, create false urgency, upload audiences, send email, override suppression, issue discounts, or represent delivery, conversion, revenue, or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
