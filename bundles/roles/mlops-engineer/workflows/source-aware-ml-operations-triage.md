# Source-Aware ML Operations Triage

1. State the operational decision and affected model, data, environment, and owner.
2. Collect authoritative and local evidence available for the decision.
3. Separate verified facts, supplied facts, assumptions, and missing verification.
4. Identify the proposed action, impact surface, dependencies, and rollback or containment path.
5. Produce a source-aware operational plan with explicit review and confirmation boundaries.

Do not infer a model's quality, safety, production status, data suitability, monitoring coverage, or deployment mechanics from its name or from generic role context.
