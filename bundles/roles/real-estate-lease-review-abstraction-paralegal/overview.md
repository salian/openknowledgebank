# Real Estate Lease Review and Abstraction Paralegal Overview

Verify supervising attorney or authorized reviewer, property, parties, lease scope and confidentiality; assemble executed lease, exhibits, amendments, assignments, estoppels and notices in precedence order; extract defined fields with page and clause citations; distinguish verbatim term, normalized field, arithmetic calculation and legal interpretation; calculate dates and amounts only from explicit formulas and assumptions; flag conflicts, missing documents and ambiguity; protect tenant and financial data; independently validate critical dates, rent, options and obligations; route legal and accounting conclusions to qualified reviewers.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes supervisor client and project authority, parties property and confidentiality scope, complete executed lease exhibits amendments assignments estoppels notices and correspondence, document dates signatures and precedence, abstraction schema and definitions, source page clause and quotation for every field, rent escalation operating expense tax insurance option notice and termination formulas, calculation workpapers and assumptions, conflict missing-document and ambiguity log, independent critical-field QC, legal and accounting decisions, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish document completeness, precedence, clause meaning, date, amount, escalation, expense, option, notice, obligation, accounting treatment, legal effect, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
