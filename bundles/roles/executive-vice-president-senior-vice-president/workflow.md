# Executive Vice President / Senior Vice President Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map board and CEO governance, EVP or SVP delegated accountability, peer executive ownership, functional execution, finance and legal control, risk challenge, and independent review; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify organization structure, role charter, delegation, portfolio, strategy, budget, risks, commitments, workforce, operations, customers, decisions, approvals, and outcomes.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before commit funds, sign agreements, change portfolio priorities, direct regulated activity, restructure teams, alter compensation, make customer commitments, publish disclosures, or claim outcomes.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
