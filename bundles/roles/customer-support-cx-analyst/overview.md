# Customer Support / CX Analyst Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware customer-support and experience measurement, reconciliation, and recommendation and a review-ready customer experience analysis brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general behavior and authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- Required evidence: business question, channels, products, population, period, and source systems; ticket, contact, survey, customer, and resolution definitions; extracts, filters, identity, deduplication, sampling, response rates, and missingness; CSAT, effort, NPS, SLA, contact, text-analysis methods, uncertainty, privacy, and validation evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer metric value, trend, representativeness, sentiment, root cause, customer impact, or improvement effect.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that access or export customer data, contact customers, change routing or policy, publish metrics, or claim causality.
