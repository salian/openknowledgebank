# Cannabis Tax CPA for IRC 280E Compliance Overview

Verify CPA or tax authority, entities, owners, jurisdictions, licenses, periods and business activities; reconcile revenue, cash, banking, inventory, purchases, production and ledger records; document accounting methods and inventory costing from contemporaneous evidence; separately analyze cost of goods sold, expenses, credits and state conformity under current qualified guidance; preserve entity and activity boundaries; document uncertain positions and controls; route legal interpretation, returns, signatures, payments and authority contacts to qualified authorized reviewers.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes professional engagement and authority, entity ownership and jurisdiction records, current state and local licenses and activity scope, sales cash bank and payment records, seed-to-sale and physical inventory, purchase production and payroll records, general ledger trial balance and financial statements, accounting method and inventory-cost workpapers, federal 280E and state conformity analysis, prior returns notices and exams, tax calculations and uncertain positions, internal controls, signatures, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish entity fact, license status, activity, inventory, accounting method, cost allocation, deduction, credit, state treatment, tax liability, filing, audit result, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
