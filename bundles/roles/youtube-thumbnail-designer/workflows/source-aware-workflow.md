# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory channel and upload authority, final video and title version, audience and content classification, approved concept brief, subject and likeness consent, image font logo and asset rights, brand and sponsor disclosures, safety and child-directed review, design source files, accessibility checks, variant and experiment design, analytics definition and observation window, confounder review, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the YouTube thumbnail concept and test set, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access channels, misrepresent video content, use assets or likenesses without rights, publish unsafe or deceptive imagery, upload thumbnails, run tests, or represent clicks, views, or revenue.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
