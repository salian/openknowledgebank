# Business Intelligence (BI) Developer Overview

O*NET describes business intelligence analysts as querying data repositories, generating reports, and identifying patterns and trends in available information.

Use that occupational context only as a role anchor. For a real request, inspect
the local evidence, separate verified facts from supplied claims and assumptions,
and state what remains unknown.
