# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory clinic provider account and publication authority, licenses jurisdictions and service scope, verified location hours and contact records, health-claim substantiation, review provenance authorization and disclosures, PHI and intake controls, domain crawl index canonical and structured-data evidence, profile ownership categories services and media, analytics call and appointment definitions, change log, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the healthcare local-search content and profile plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish unsupported health claims, expose patient data, create or solicit deceptive reviews, change profiles or websites, collect intake data, claim rankings, or represent treatment or business outcomes.

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
