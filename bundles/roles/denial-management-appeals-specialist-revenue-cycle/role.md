# Denial Management and Appeals Specialist for Revenue Cycle Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/appeals-grievances/fee-for-service/appeals
- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/administrative-simplification/hipaa-aca
- https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html

CMS and HHS sources describe selected United States appeal, transaction and privacy requirements; they do not establish local coverage, authorization, code, denial cause, deadline, correction, appeal right, submission, payment, write-off, outcome, or compliance.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, tax, education, healthcare, accessibility, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable denial root-cause and appeal tracking workpaper with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or disclose health records without authority, alter clinical records, assign codes without qualification, submit claims or appeals, contact payers, write off balances, promise reversal or payment, or certify compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent patient fact, coverage, authorization, eligibility, code, denial cause, deadline, correction, appeal right, submission, payment, write-off, outcome, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
