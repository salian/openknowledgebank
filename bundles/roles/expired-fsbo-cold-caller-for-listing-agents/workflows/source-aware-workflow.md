# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory agent brokerage and caller authority, licenses and jurisdictions, listing source status and timestamp, ownership and contact provenance, vendor license and permitted use, federal state and internal suppression checks, number type and consent records, approved identity brokerage and purpose disclosures, call-time and recording rules, fair-housing and script review, objection revocation and complaint process, disposition definitions, licensed-agent handoff, retention, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the expired and FSBO outreach script and disposition protocol, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to acquire or enrich lists, call or text, override suppression or revocation, record calls, misrepresent brokerage or listing status, infer motivation, advise on agency or price, book appointments falsely, or promise outcomes.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
