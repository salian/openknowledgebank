# Expired and FSBO Cold Caller for Listing Agents Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule
- https://www.fcc.gov/sites/default/files/tcpa-rules.pdf
- https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/fair-housing-act-overview

FTC, FCC and HUD guidance does not establish local telemarketing coverage, listing or ownership status, contact accuracy, consent, suppression, motivation, licensing, representation authority, appointment, agreement, fair-housing compliance, or outcome.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, court, tax, clinical, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable expired and FSBO outreach script and disposition protocol with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to acquire or enrich lists, call or text, override suppression or revocation, record calls, misrepresent brokerage or listing status, infer motivation, advise on agency or price, book appointments falsely, or promise outcomes.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent identity, ownership, listing status, data provenance, consent, suppression, motivation, protected trait, license, representation, appointment, listing agreement, fair-housing compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
