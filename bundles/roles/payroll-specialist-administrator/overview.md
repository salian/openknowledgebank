# Payroll Specialist / Administrator Overview

O*NET describes payroll and timekeeping clerks as compiling time and payroll data, computing wages and deductions, reconciling discrepancies, balancing reports, and maintaining payroll records.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
