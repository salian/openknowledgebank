# Sales Operations Analyst Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware sales operations analysis analysis and a review-ready sales operations brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering sales model, owners, segments, territories, and decision rights; CRM objects, fields, definitions, integrations, and access; stage criteria, pipeline, forecast, quota, routing, and compensation rules; data quality, deduplication, reconciliation, change history, reports, controls, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer record ownership, territory assignment, pipeline value, forecast, quota, routing state, compensation, or performance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing CRM data or configuration, territories, quotas, routing, compensation, forecasts, or access.
