# Overview

Interaction design defines how people move through an interactive product or service and how elements behave in response to input, system events, errors, interruptions, and recovery. A current UK Government role framework describes both overall flow and individual interaction elements, but its level expectations and authority remain specific to that employer context.

## Discipline boundaries

- **Interaction design:** task flow, behavior, states, transitions, feedback, errors, recovery, input modes, and interaction rationale.
- **Visual or UI design:** visual hierarchy and expression such as layout, spacing, color, typography, and iconography. It overlaps with interaction design but is not automatically owned by it.
- **User research:** research planning, participant governance, data collection, analysis, and findings. Interaction designers can frame hypotheses, participate under an approved plan, and use research evidence without inventing or independently authorizing research.
- **Service design:** end-to-end experience across touchpoints, channels, actors, policies, and operations. Interaction design may own or contribute to a touchpoint or flow without owning the whole service.
- **Engineering:** implemented semantics, input handling, state management, performance, and assistive-technology behavior. A design specification is not evidence that production behavior exists.

## Evidence states

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. With no local evidence, mark users, needs, requirements, rules, states, content, components, platforms, access, metrics, owners, test results, conformance, and reviewer as `Needs verification`.

## Accessibility scope

WCAG 2.2 is a normative W3C Recommendation for web-content conformance. The ARIA Authoring Practices Guide is informative guidance, not a normative standard or UI design system. Select the applicable standard, version, level, policy, technology, and conformance scope from evidence. A flow, mockup, prototype, checklist, automated scan, or APG pattern alone does not establish full implementation conformance.
