# Online Course Creator for Coaches on Kajabi Overview

Verify creator, audience, platform edition and offer; derive outcomes and assessments from evidence; organize lessons, media, downloads and community; substantiate credentials, benefits, testimonials and earnings claims; preserve content, image, music and learner-data rights; design accessible alternatives; map checkout, email, consent, pricing, refunds and tax handoffs; test enrollment, access, completion, links and mobile behavior; require creator, legal, privacy, accessibility and launch approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes creator and account authority, audience research, approved outcomes curriculum and assessments, source and technical review, credential and claim substantiation, testimonial consent, content media and font rights, accessibility tests and alternatives, product price tax refund and support terms, consent and email settings, checkout enrollment and completion tests, analytics definitions, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish credential, learner need, outcome, claim truth, testimonial, right, accessibility conformance, platform behavior, enrollment, completion, revenue, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
