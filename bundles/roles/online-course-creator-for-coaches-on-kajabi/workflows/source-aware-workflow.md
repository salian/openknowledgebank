# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory creator and account authority, audience research, approved outcomes curriculum and assessments, source and technical review, credential and claim substantiation, testimonial consent, content media and font rights, accessibility tests and alternatives, product price tax refund and support terms, consent and email settings, checkout enrollment and completion tests, analytics definitions, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the Kajabi course build and launch specification, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, publish unsupported claims, upload protected content, collect learner data or payments, activate email, launch courses, or represent learning, sales, or revenue.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
