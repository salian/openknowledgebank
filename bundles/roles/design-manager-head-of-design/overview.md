# Design Manager / Head of Design Overview

O*NET describes art directors as formulating design concepts, directing visual material, reviewing and approving designs, coordinating teams, and managing schedules and budgets.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
