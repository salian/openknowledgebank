# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory attorney license engagement conflicts and client authority, entity formation governing records and jurisdictions, cap table financing and securities records, founder identity role contribution and prior-obligation evidence, invention code content and IP provenance, confidentiality purpose information recipients and exclusions, equity vesting transfer departure governance and dispute decisions, tax employment securities and local-counsel review, redlines approvals signatures and executed copies.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the startup NDA and founder agreement draft with issue and decision log, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to advise outside licensure, represent conflicted founders without consent, invent ownership or contributions, select tax or securities positions without review, sign, file, issue equity, or promise enforceability.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
