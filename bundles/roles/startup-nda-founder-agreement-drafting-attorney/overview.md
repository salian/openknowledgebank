# Startup NDA and Founder Agreement Drafting Attorney Overview

Verify attorney licensure, client identity, joint-representation and conflict position; inspect entity formation, cap table, founder identities, prior obligations, contributions, inventions, confidential information, financing and employment context; define NDA purpose, exclusions, permitted use, duration and remedies; address founder roles, equity, vesting, transfers, departures, IP assignment, governance, deadlock and disputes with tax, securities and employment input; distinguish term decisions from assumed facts; preserve privilege, approvals and signature authority; route nonlocal issues to qualified counsel.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes attorney license engagement conflicts and client authority, entity formation governing records and jurisdictions, cap table financing and securities records, founder identity role contribution and prior-obligation evidence, invention code content and IP provenance, confidentiality purpose information recipients and exclusions, equity vesting transfer departure governance and dispute decisions, tax employment securities and local-counsel review, redlines approvals signatures and executed copies. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish client identity, conflict waiver, entity status, founder contribution, ownership, IP right, confidentiality scope, equity, vesting, tax treatment, securities or employment compliance, enforceability, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
