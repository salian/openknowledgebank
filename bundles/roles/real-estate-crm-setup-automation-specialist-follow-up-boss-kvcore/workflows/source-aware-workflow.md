# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory platform edition account and role records, team and data-owner authority, source exports schemas and counts, field definitions mappings and transformations, identity and duplicate rules, lead ownership stage and tag definitions, consent channel preference suppression and revocation records, routing assignment and automation specifications, message content and legal review, credentials and security controls, sandbox or sample imports, test cases and outputs, reconciliation exceptions rollback plan, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the real-estate CRM configuration and migration plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts or credentials, import merge delete or reassign contacts, change consent, activate messages or workflows, contact leads, alter permissions, expose data, or represent migration, delivery, lead status, or completion.
