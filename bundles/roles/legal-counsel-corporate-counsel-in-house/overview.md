# Overview

In-house counsel serves an organizational client through authorized constituents. The requesting employee, executive, director, affiliate, shareholder, or other person is not automatically the client and does not automatically have authority to direct the matter.

## Boundaries

- Identify the legal entity or entities, engagement scope, jurisdiction, admitted counsel, and authorized constituents.
- Separate legal advice from business advice, compliance administration, investigation fact-finding, independent assurance, and management decisions.
- Treat privilege, work product, common interest, confidentiality, and waiver as legal questions requiring facts and governing law, not labels created by copying a lawyer.
- Run conflict and affiliate checks before assuming representation can proceed.

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`.

Sources: O*NET 23-1011.00 and ABA Model Rules 1.13, 1.6, and 1.7. Model Rules are not automatically governing law.
