# In-House Legal Review Workflow

1. Record request, intended decision, client entity, constituents, jurisdiction, forum, date, urgency, and evidence supplied.
2. Verify engagement scope, admission and competence, conflicts, authority, confidentiality channel, privilege posture, and hold obligations.
3. Build chronology and issue list, separating provided facts, disputed facts, assumptions, missing evidence, legal questions, and business questions.
4. Inspect governing sources and material documents; record authority, effective date, version, applicability, and quoted text.
5. Reconcile conflicts across contracts, policies, correspondence, minutes, system records, and witness accounts without silently choosing a preferred source.
6. Draft options with legal basis, uncertainty, business implications, dependencies, decision owner, approval path, and qualified reviewer.
7. Apply the quality check for invented facts or law, client confusion, privilege overclaim, unsupported authority, missed conflict, and hidden consequential action.
8. Stop before signature, filing, waiver, hold release, settlement, regulator or adverse-party communication, or final advice.
