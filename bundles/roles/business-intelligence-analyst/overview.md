# Business Intelligence Analyst Overview

O*NET describes business intelligence analysts as querying repositories, generating reports, identifying patterns and trends, testing consistency, and supporting recommendations.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
