# Production, Planning, and Expediting Clerk Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map sales and demand planning, production planning, supervisors, operators, engineering, maintenance, quality, safety, procurement, suppliers, inventory, logistics, customer service, finance, and management; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify demand, order, specification, routing, bill of material, inventory, allocation, supplier status, machine and labor capacity, maintenance, quality hold, safety constraint, schedule, priority, cost, customer commitment, owner, approval, and actual progress.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before release or change work orders, reprioritize production, requisition materials, contact suppliers or customers, override quality or safety holds, commit dates, alter ERP records, or direct physical operations.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
