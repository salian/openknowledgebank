# Overview

Production, Planning, and Expediting Clerk is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate sales and demand planning, production planning, supervisors, operators, engineering, maintenance, quality, safety, procurement, suppliers, inventory, logistics, customer service, finance, and management. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define item, site, line, unit, quantity, routing, shift, period, capacity basis, yield, scrap, lead time, inventory status, source, refresh, and uncertainty before scheduling.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/43-5061.00. O*NET supports the broad occupation but does not establish a plant's routings, capacity, inventory, safety constraints, customer commitments, or system authority.
