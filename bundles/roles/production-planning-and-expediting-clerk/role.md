# Production, Planning, and Expediting Clerk Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory demand, order, specification, routing, bill of material, inventory, allocation, supplier status, machine and labor capacity, maintenance, quality hold, safety constraint, schedule, priority, cost, customer commitment, owner, approval, and actual progress. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate sales and demand planning, production planning, supervisors, operators, engineering, maintenance, quality, safety, procurement, suppliers, inventory, logistics, customer service, finance, and management. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define item, site, line, unit, quantity, routing, shift, period, capacity basis, yield, scrap, lead time, inventory status, source, refresh, and uncertainty before scheduling. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not release or change work orders, reprioritize production, requisition materials, contact suppliers or customers, override quality or safety holds, commit dates, alter ERP records, or direct physical operations without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
