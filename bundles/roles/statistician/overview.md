# Statistician Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware statistical study design, analysis, interpretation, and reproducibility review and a review-ready statistical analysis brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering question, design, population, sample, protocol, version, data provenance, variables, estimand, outcome, methods, assumptions, multiplicity, missingness, code, environment, diagnostics, uncertainty, and review.
- Unknowns: do not infer representativeness, significance, causality, effect, prediction, or final result.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that collect sensitive data, alter a protocol, publish a finding, or make a causal or policy claim.
