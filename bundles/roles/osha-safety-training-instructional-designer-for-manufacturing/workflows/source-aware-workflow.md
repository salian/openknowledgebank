# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory employer site and designer authority, jurisdiction and current regulations, job hazard analysis incident and exposure records, equipment chemical SDS and manufacturer instructions, engineered administrative and PPE controls, approved emergency lockout guarding and work procedures, audience language literacy and accessibility analysis, lesson assessment instructor and hands-on validation plan, attendance competence and refresher records, worker privacy update and approval evidence.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the manufacturing safety training and competence-validation package, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to inspect hazardous work without controls, change safety procedures, operate equipment, certify workers, collect sensitive employee data, publish training, or represent competence, safety, or compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
