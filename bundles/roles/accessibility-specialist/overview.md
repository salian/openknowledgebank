# Accessibility Specialist Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware digital accessibility assessment and remediation planning and a review-ready accessibility assessment brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering users and platforms; WCAG version, level, and criterion-level tests; code, design, browser, assistive-technology, and test-tool versions; manual and automated findings with severity and reproduction steps; exceptions, remediation, retest evidence, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer conformance, user impact, criterion pass or fail, legal compliance, or remediation completion.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before actions that publish an ACR or VPAT, approve an exception, change production, or claim conformance.
