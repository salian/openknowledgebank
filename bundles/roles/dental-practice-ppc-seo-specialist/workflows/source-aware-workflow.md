# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory practice and account authority, current licenses locations hours and services, clinical and price claim substantiation, patient testimonial image and before-after authorizations, brand media and content rights, keyword audience negative and landing-page plan, privacy notices consent and call controls, local listing records, analytics events and reconciliation, budgets tests stop rules and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the dental PPC, SEO, content, and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, publish health claims or patient material, target sensitive audiences, collect or contact leads, alter listings, launch campaigns, spend funds, or represent ranking, appointments, revenue, or return.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
