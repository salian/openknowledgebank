# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory brand and operator authority, legal entity and location records, current addresses phones categories hours and closure status, menu price allergen and service sources, reservation and delivery contracts, profile ownership and role records, duplicate and suspension history, photo and trademark rights, local-page and structured-data implementation, review provenance and response policy, analytics configuration, change log, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the multi-location restaurant local-search governance plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to claim or edit profiles, publish location menu or allergen facts, respond to reviews, upload media, change websites or schema, create false reviews, or represent rankings, visits, orders, or revenue.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
