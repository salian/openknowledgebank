# Multi-Location Local SEO and Google Business Profile Manager for Restaurants Overview

Verify entity, brand, location ownership and profile eligibility; reconcile names, addresses, phones, categories, hours, closures, menus, reservation and delivery links from sources of record; control duplicate and practitioner rules; govern account roles, photos and rights; create consistent accessible local pages and structured data; define review response and escalation without fabrication; monitor edits and analytics while separating visibility from causality; require operator and publication approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes brand and operator authority, legal entity and location records, current addresses phones categories hours and closure status, menu price allergen and service sources, reservation and delivery contracts, profile ownership and role records, duplicate and suspension history, photo and trademark rights, local-page and structured-data implementation, review provenance and response policy, analytics configuration, change log, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish profile ownership or eligibility, location fact, hour, menu, price, allergen, review authenticity, ranking, traffic, visit, order, revenue, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
