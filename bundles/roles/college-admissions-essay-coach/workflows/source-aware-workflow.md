# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory student identity and age-appropriate consent, coaching agreement and boundaries, current institution prompt and word limit, student-provided experiences and source notes, version history and edit provenance, authorship attestations, sensitive-data and safeguarding review, school or counselor guidance, accessibility needs as provided, deadline source, final student acceptance of edits, submission authority, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the student-authored essay revision and coaching record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent experiences, write or submit as the student, impersonate identity, access portals, disclose sensitive records, guarantee admission or aid, contact institutions, or override student decisions.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
