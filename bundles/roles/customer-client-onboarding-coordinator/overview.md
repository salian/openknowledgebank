# Overview

Customer / Client Onboarding Coordinator is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate account ownership, onboarding coordination, implementation, support, security and privacy approval, finance and billing control, customer acceptance, and escalation ownership. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define time-to-milestone, readiness, completion, adoption, issue age, rework, and satisfaction measures with start and stop events, exclusions, owners, and source systems.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/43-4051.00; https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework. O*NET describes customer-service work and NIST provides voluntary privacy-risk guidance; neither establishes contract scope, identity, access, consent, completion, or local authority.
