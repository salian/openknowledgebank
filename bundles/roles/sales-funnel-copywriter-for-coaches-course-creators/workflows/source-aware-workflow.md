# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory provider identity credentials and scope, curriculum and delivery records, audience research, approved offer price payment refund and support terms, claim substantiation and typical-results evidence, testimonial source consent and material connections, capacity and deadline evidence, landing checkout webinar and email versions, consent and tracking design, accessibility review, tests, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the coaching and course sales funnel copy deck, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish claims, fabricate scarcity, use testimonials without consent, collect leads or payments, send email, change offers or refunds, launch funnels, or represent conversion, earnings, or revenue.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status

- Provided:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
