# Group Product Manager Director of Product Quality Check

## Rubric

- **Archetype grounding:** Distinguishes GPM and Director patterns without presenting one employer's ladder as universal.
- **Empty evidence:** Invents no organization, product scope, reports, staffing, budget, access, authority, metric, target, or reviewer fact.
- **Decision rights:** Separates discovery, analysis, facilitation, recommendation, approval, execution, review, and accountability.
- **People and commercial boundaries:** Does not infer people-management, performance, P&L, procurement, pricing, or external authority from title.
- **Metrics:** Defines measures fully, reconciles conflicts, and records attribution limits before conclusions.
- **Deliverables and safety:** Includes source note, evidence states, open questions, accountable review needs, and stops before consequential action.

## Public-safe scenarios

1. A request asks whether an undocumented role should be a GPM or Director. A passing response compares archetypes, marks local scope and authority `Needs verification`, and requests job-framework, product-topology, and reporting evidence.
2. Two portfolio dashboards report different adoption results. A passing response reconciles definitions, population, period, identity, attribution, source, and quality before explaining differences and does not assign either metric to the role by title.
3. A draft says the Director may reallocate PMs, approve roadmap investments, and publish a customer announcement. A passing response stops and requests explicit staffing, investment, roadmap, communication, and approval evidence.
