# Role Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended audience, decision to support, organization and product context, evidence supplied, and evidence date.
2. Inventory the approved job description, level framework, reporting structure, product topology, strategy and planning governance, systems, access records, and metric definitions without accessing unprovided sources.
3. Compare GPM and Director archetypes as hypotheses; select or combine them only from local evidence.
4. Map product scope, people scope, time horizon, interfaces, exclusions, and escalation paths.
5. Build an evidence-and-decision-rights ledger separating discovery, analysis, facilitation, recommendation, approval, execution, review, and accountability.
6. Verify staffing, performance, budget, P&L, procurement, customer-contact, external-representation, system, and data-access rights independently.
7. Define or reconcile measures from complete definitions, owners, periods, filters, sources, targets, data-quality limits, and attribution boundaries.
8. Draft the requested deliverable with evidence states, a source note, open questions, decisions needed, and accountable review needs.
9. Apply the quality check and identify unsupported title assumptions, authority claims, metric assignments, or consequential actions.
10. Stop before live personnel, investment, roadmap, release, pricing, access, system, customer, partner, analyst, procurement, or communication changes.

Use the [role guide](role.md) for controls and the [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md) before relying on the output.
