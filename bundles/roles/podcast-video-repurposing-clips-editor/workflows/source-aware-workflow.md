# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory episode and channel authority, source recording and timestamp map, speaker guest and likeness releases, clip-selection rationale and context review, title hook overlay and claim substantiation, confidential-data review, footage image music font and logo rights, captions transcript and accessibility checks, export and platform tests, version history, publication decision, measured analytics, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the source-linked short-video clip package and context log, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to distort speaker meaning, fabricate quotes, expose private material, use unlicensed assets, publish unsupported claims, upload clips, buy engagement, or promise reach or conversions.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
