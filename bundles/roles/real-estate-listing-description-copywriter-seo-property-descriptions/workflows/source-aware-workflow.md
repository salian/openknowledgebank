# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory brokerage seller and writer authority, executed listing scope, deed assessor survey measurement and inspection records, seller feature improvement and condition records, amenity and location sources with dates, approved price and showing facts, fair-housing and advertising review, MLS portal and web limits, image map quote and trademark rights, keyword observations, copy versions and source ledger, disclosures, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the source-backed property listing copy set, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent property or neighborhood facts, imply protected-class preference, make safety or investment claims, use assets without rights, publish, alter price or status, or promise ranking or sale.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
