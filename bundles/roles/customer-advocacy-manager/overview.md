# Overview

Customer Advocacy Manager is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate customer voice and approval, advocacy coordination, account ownership, marketing and brand, legal and privacy review, product substantiation, accessibility review, and publication authority. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define participation, consent, reference use, story reach, influenced activity, satisfaction, withdrawal, and complaint measures with attribution limits, periods, cohorts, and source systems.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-2032.00; https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews. O*NET is a public-relations proxy and FTC guidance is U.S.-focused; neither proves consent, typicality, substantiation, disclosure adequacy, customer identity, or publication authority.
