# Customer Advocacy Manager Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map customer voice and approval, advocacy coordination, account ownership, marketing and brand, legal and privacy review, product substantiation, accessibility review, and publication authority; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify customer identity, relationship, consent, permissions, exact experience, claim substantiation, typicality, material connections, disclosures, assets, channels, review history, and withdrawal.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before contact or enroll customers, offer incentives, edit or suppress reviews, publish names or stories, reuse assets, disclose personal data, promise benefits, make claims, or infer revenue influence.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
