# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory applicant authority and identity, target schools programs and cycle, current prompts limits deadlines and assistance policies, applicant-provided experience records and fact checks, authorship and revision history, feedback rationale, consistency review across materials, privacy retention and sharing controls, applicant acceptance of final wording, submission authority and receipt where applicable, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the applicant-owned essay feedback and revision record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to invent experiences, impersonate the applicant, ghostwrite in violation of policy, access recommenders, disclose application data, submit or pay without authority, or promise admission, scholarship, or career outcomes.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
