# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory approved business objective and audience research, search and support demand, product documentation and version, subject-matter interviews, claim substantiation, approved customer evidence and consent, dated competitor sources, keyword and SERP observations, editorial and brand standards, rights and citation records, distribution and conversion design, analytics definitions, review cadence, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the B2B SaaS content strategy and article brief set, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish content, make unsupported claims, use customer or competitor material without rights, change product positioning, collect leads, alter websites, or represent rankings, attribution, pipeline, or revenue.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
