# Account Executive (Closer) Overview

O*NET describes wholesale and manufacturing sales representatives as answering product questions, matching products to customer needs, preparing terms, and supporting customers after sale.

Use that occupational context only as a role anchor. For a real request, inspect
the local evidence, separate verified facts from supplied claims and assumptions,
and state what remains unknown.
