# Bookkeeper / Accounting Clerk Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware bookkeeping and accounting operations analysis and a review-ready bookkeeping review brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering entity, books, period, accounting basis, chart of accounts, and authority; invoices, receipts, bank records, purchase orders, payroll feeds, and source provenance; transaction dates, amounts, currencies, taxes, coding, and approvals; ledgers, subledgers, reconciliations, aging, close status, and exceptions; access, segregation of duties, retention, review, and correction history.
- Unknowns: do not infer transaction validity, account coding, balance, reconciliation status, tax treatment, close completeness, or approval.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before posting or reversing entries, paying vendors, changing master data, closing periods, or certifying financial records.
