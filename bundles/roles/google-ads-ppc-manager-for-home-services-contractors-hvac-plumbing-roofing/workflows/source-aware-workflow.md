# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory contractor legal identity and authority, current licenses insurance and jurisdictions, service areas hours and capacity, Google account and Local Services eligibility, approved services prices financing guarantees and terms, claim and review substantiation, keyword location and exclusion plan, landing and call assets, consent recording and CRM controls, budget and bid authority, conversion and attribution settings, test outputs, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the home-services Google Ads campaign and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, represent licensing, publish claims, target locations, record calls, collect or contact leads, launch or alter campaigns, spend funds, or represent lead quality, conversion, revenue, or return.

State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
