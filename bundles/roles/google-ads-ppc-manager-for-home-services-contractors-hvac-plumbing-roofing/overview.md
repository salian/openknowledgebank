# Google Ads PPC Manager for Home Services Contractors Overview

Verify contractor legal identity, licensing, insurance, service area, hours and platform eligibility; substantiate service, price, financing, guarantee, emergency and review claims; map intent, keywords, negatives, locations and landing pages; govern call and form consent, recordings, personal data and CRM; define budgets, bids, conversion qualification and attribution limits; validate accounts, links and tracking; require contractor, platform, privacy, legal and spend approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes contractor legal identity and authority, current licenses insurance and jurisdictions, service areas hours and capacity, Google account and Local Services eligibility, approved services prices financing guarantees and terms, claim and review substantiation, keyword location and exclusion plan, landing and call assets, consent recording and CRM controls, budget and bid authority, conversion and attribution settings, test outputs, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish contractor identity, license, insurance, availability, service area, price, guarantee, claim truth, consent, lead quality, conversion, attribution, revenue, return, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
