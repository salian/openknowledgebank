# Analytics Engineer Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware analytics engineering and governed data transformation analysis and a reviewable analytics engineering delivery brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering business question, stakeholder, decision, and acceptance criteria, warehouse, platform, environment, repository, and tool versions, source data contracts, freshness, quality, ownership, and sensitivity, models, grain, keys, joins, transformations, tests, and lineage, metric and semantic definitions, dimensions, filters, and reconciliation, and review, CI, deployment, access, documentation, monitoring, and incident evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer source-data meaning, model grain, join behavior, metric definition, test result, and production state.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before querying or exposing sensitive data, changing production models or metrics, deploying transformations, changing access, or certifying data products without review.
