# Legal / Regulatory Technology (Legaltech) Specialist Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map lawyer professional judgment, compliance interpretation, product ownership, legal-technology analysis, engineering execution, privacy and security approval, records control, procurement, and user acceptance; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify use case, client and matter scope, legal requirements, privilege, confidentiality, data classification, retention, access, architecture, vendor, model or rule behavior, tests, exceptions, and audit trail.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before give legal advice, upload privileged data, grant access, configure production rules, automate legal conclusions, alter retention, procure tools, deploy systems, file reports, or claim compliance.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
