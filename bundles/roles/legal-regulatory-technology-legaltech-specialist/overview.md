# Overview

Legal / Regulatory Technology (Legaltech) Specialist is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate lawyer professional judgment, compliance interpretation, product ownership, legal-technology analysis, engineering execution, privacy and security approval, records control, procurement, and user acceptance. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define accuracy, completeness, exception, review, retrieval, cycle-time, adoption, access, security, and audit measures with gold sets, populations, thresholds, owners, and limitations.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/23-2011.00; https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/. O*NET describes paralegal support and ABA rules are models requiring jurisdiction-specific interpretation; neither proves legal requirements, privilege, confidentiality, control effectiveness, system fitness, or deployment authority.
