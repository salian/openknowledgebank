# Customer Education / Training Manager Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map customer education, product, customer success, support, implementation, instructional design, subject-matter experts, accessibility, localization, legal, privacy, platform administration, and credential governance; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify customer segments, tasks, product version, learning need, prerequisites, curriculum, content sources, accessibility needs, assessment items, scoring, completion, adoption, support outcomes, consent, owner, approval, and reviewer.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before enroll learners, access customer data, publish content, issue credentials, change product guidance, send communications, alter LMS records, or claim learning or business impact.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
