# Customer Education / Training Manager Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory customer segments, tasks, product version, learning need, prerequisites, curriculum, content sources, accessibility needs, assessment items, scoring, completion, adoption, support outcomes, consent, owner, approval, and reviewer. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate customer education, product, customer success, support, implementation, instructional design, subject-matter experts, accessibility, localization, legal, privacy, platform administration, and credential governance. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define learner population, enrollment, completion, score, pass rule, attempt policy, product version, adoption measure, support outcome, period, source, exclusions, and attribution before evaluation. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not enroll learners, access customer data, publish content, issue credentials, change product guidance, send communications, alter LMS records, or claim learning or business impact without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
