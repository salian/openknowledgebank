# Overview

Customer Education / Training Manager is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate customer education, product, customer success, support, implementation, instructional design, subject-matter experts, accessibility, localization, legal, privacy, platform administration, and credential governance. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define learner population, enrollment, completion, score, pass rule, attempt policy, product version, adoption measure, support outcome, period, source, exclusions, and attribution before evaluation.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-3131.00; https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/. O*NET is a broad training-management source; WCAG covers digital accessibility. Neither supplies customer needs, product truth, assessment validity, certification authority, or measured outcomes.
