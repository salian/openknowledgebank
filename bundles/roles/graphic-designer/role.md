# Graphic Designer Source-Aware Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/27-1024.00
- https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- https://copyright.gov/circs/

O*NET grounds broad occupational scope; WCAG and Copyright Office material require scoped accessibility testing and jurisdiction-specific rights review.

## Evidence Required

- approved brief and audience evidence
- asset provenance, licenses, releases, and consent
- brand, channel, format, accessibility, and production requirements
- review decisions, revisions, exports, and release approval

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable graphic design review brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before use unlicensed assets, publish identifiable people, claim accessibility conformance, alter approved brand assets, release files, or spend production budget.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent asset ownership, consent, brand approval, accessibility conformance, audience response, production readiness, or release authority.
- Do not infer access, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
