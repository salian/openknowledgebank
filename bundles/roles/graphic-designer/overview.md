# Graphic Designer Overview

Graphic designers develop visual concepts and layouts for defined audiences and channels. A brief, brand rule, asset license, consent, accessibility target, and release approval must be evidenced rather than inferred.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes brief, audience, message, channel, dimensions, source assets, authorship, licenses, consent, brand rules, accessibility criteria, production specifications, review comments, and release approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish asset ownership, consent, brand approval, accessibility conformance, audience response, production readiness, or release authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
