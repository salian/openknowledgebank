# Audio and Video Technician Source-Aware Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/27-4011.00
- https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

O*NET grounds occupational scope and WCAG informs digital accessibility; neither proves equipment capability, venue safety, signal integrity, media rights, or readiness.

## Evidence Required

- production brief, run of show, venue, audience, and accessibility target
- equipment inventory, versions, signal path, power, network, and compatibility evidence
- media provenance, licenses, releases, consent, captions, and transcripts
- test results, fault logs, backup plan, safety review, and go-live authority

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable AV production readiness brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before connect or energize equipment, change venue systems, record people, publish media, disable accessibility features, bypass safety controls, or declare production ready.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent equipment availability, compatibility, signal quality, rights, consent, accessibility, safety, test results, or readiness.
- Do not infer access, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
