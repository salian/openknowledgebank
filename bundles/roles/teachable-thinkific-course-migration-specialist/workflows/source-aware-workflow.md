# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory owner account and release authority, source and target plan version and settings, full content product user enrollment progress grade and integration inventories, field and feature mapping, unsupported-item log, content media and font rights, privacy retention and payment boundaries, exports backups and checksums, sample and batch migration logs, count value and relationship reconciliation, accessibility functional commerce email domain and redirect tests, rollback support and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the course migration map, reconciliation report, and cutover record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts or learner data without authority, copy protected content, expose payment data, alter grades or completion, delete source records, change domains, send email, launch products, or claim complete migration.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
