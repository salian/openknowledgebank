# Teachable and Thinkific Course Migration Specialist Overview

Verify owner, platforms, plans, environments and migration authority; inventory courses, lessons, media, quizzes, certificates, products, prices, coupons, users, enrollments, progress, domains, email and integrations; map supported and unsupported fields explicitly; clear rights and protect learner and payment data; export and back up through approved methods; migrate controlled samples before batches; test accessibility and learner workflows; reconcile counts, values and exceptions; plan redirects, communications, cutover and rollback; never infer completion or overwrite records without approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes owner account and release authority, source and target plan version and settings, full content product user enrollment progress grade and integration inventories, field and feature mapping, unsupported-item log, content media and font rights, privacy retention and payment boundaries, exports backups and checksums, sample and batch migration logs, count value and relationship reconciliation, accessibility functional commerce email domain and redirect tests, rollback support and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish scope, ownership, compatibility, user, enrollment, progress, grade, payment, accessibility conformance, migration completeness, cutover success, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
