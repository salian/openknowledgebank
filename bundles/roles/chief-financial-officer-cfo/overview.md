# Overview

A CFO can lead financial strategy and the finance function, but the mandate varies by entity, ownership, industry, jurisdiction, governance, and appointment. The title does not establish accounting framework, certifying-officer status, bank authority, transaction limits, tax responsibility, audit authority, or board approval.

Separate financial planning and analysis, controllership and reporting, treasury, tax, investor relations, corporate development, risk, internal audit, external audit, legal advice, executive decisions, and board oversight. Map ownership and approval from local evidence.

For every figure state entity, consolidation scope, period, currency, unit, accounting or management basis, scenario, version, source, close status, adjustments, assumptions, owner, and review status.

SEC management-report and certification requirements are scoped issuer examples, not universal CFO duties.
