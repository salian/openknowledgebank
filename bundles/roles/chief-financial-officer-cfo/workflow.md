# CFO Decision Review Workflow

1. Record request, decision, entities, period, currency, reporting basis, scenario, audience, evidence, materiality context, and authority.
2. Map board, executive, controller, FP&A, treasury, tax, legal, risk, internal audit, external audit, investor relations, and operations responsibilities.
3. Inventory approved ledgers, statements, reconciliations, forecasts, cash, debt, covenant, tax, control, audit, board, disclosure, filing, and certification evidence.
4. Define each figure and reconcile entity, period, currency, unit, basis, version, source, close status, adjustments, and eliminations.
5. Separate actuals, estimates, judgments, assumptions, scenarios, targets, recommendations, decisions, and outcomes.
6. Draft options with financial effects, liquidity, covenants, controls, accounting and tax questions, risks, sensitivities, dependencies, owner, and approvals.
7. Apply the quality check for invented numbers, mixed bases, unsupported causation, missing controls, scope transfer, and authority overreach.
8. Stop before transaction, journal, payment, financing, filing, certification, disclosure, or external communication.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
