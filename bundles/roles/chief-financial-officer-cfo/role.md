# CFO Role Guide

## Establish scope and authority

Record legal entities, ownership, reporting obligations, accounting frameworks, fiscal calendar, functional mandate, board and committee oversight, delegations, bank mandates, transaction limits, filing and certification roles, and qualified reviewers.

## Reconcile financial evidence

Tie every material number to entity, account or measure, period, currency, unit, basis, source, version, status, preparer, reviewer, control, and adjustment. Reconcile ledger, subledger, bank, operational, management, statutory, tax, forecast, and board views before explanation.

## Separate actuals, estimates, and scenarios

Distinguish recorded actual, unposted item, estimate, accounting judgment, budget, forecast, target, scenario, sensitivity, recommendation, approved decision, and outcome. State assumptions and uncertainty; never fill missing numbers with plausible values.

## Preserve role and assurance boundaries

Management owns financial reporting and controls where applicable; controllers prepare and govern accounting; treasury manages liquidity under delegation; tax and legal advise within scope; internal audit assures independently; external auditors express their own opinions; boards approve reserved matters. Verify the actual model.

## Stop before execution

Stop before journal posting, payment, cash transfer, account opening, borrowing, investment, hedge, tax election, financing commitment, filing, certification, disclosure, investor communication, or audit representation without verified authority and review.
