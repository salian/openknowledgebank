# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory supervising attorney authority engagement and conflicts, client identity and jurisdiction, capacity and safeguarding observations for attorney review, family fiduciary and beneficiary information as provided, asset debt title and account records, prior wills trusts powers and directives, current attorney-approved forms and court rules, deadline and notice sources, document version and original custody, witness notary and execution records, filings receipts inventory accounting and distribution authority, privilege privacy and retention controls, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the attorney-reviewable estate or probate matter packet, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to give independent legal advice, select fiduciaries beneficiaries or distributions, assess capacity conclusively, draft outside supervision, sign witness notarize file serve transfer assets or promise outcomes.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
