# Lawyer / Attorney Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware licensed legal research, analysis, and drafting support analysis and a review-ready legal work-product brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering client, matter, engagement scope, licensed supervisor, jurisdiction, and deadlines; complete facts, record, evidence, and adverse facts; current governing authority and citation status; privilege, confidentiality, conflicts, and protective handling; procedural posture, options, risks, and draft-review status.
- Unknowns: do not infer client relationship, jurisdiction, legal authority, privilege, conflict status, deadline, legal conclusion, or filing readiness.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before giving final legal advice, filing or signing documents, contacting parties or tribunals, waiving rights, or representing a client.
