# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory advertiser and account authority, product and offer records, claim substantiation and approved customer evidence, audience definitions and source provenance, customer-list rights consent and suppression, creative and asset rights, form notices and lawful-basis review, landing and tracking configuration, CRM schema mapping deduplication and stage definitions, budget and bid authority, experiment and stop rules, platform and CRM reconciliation, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the LinkedIn demand-generation campaign and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, upload customer lists, infer personal traits or intent, publish claims, collect or contact leads, change CRM, launch or scale campaigns, spend funds, or represent pipeline, revenue, or return.
