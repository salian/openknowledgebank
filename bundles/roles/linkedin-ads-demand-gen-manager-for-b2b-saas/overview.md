# LinkedIn Ads Demand Generation Manager for B2B SaaS Overview

Verify advertiser, account, product and offer; substantiate capabilities, customer outcomes and competitive claims; define company, role and list audiences from permitted data without sensitive inference; preserve customer-match rights and consent; align ads, forms and landing pages; govern CRM field mapping, deduplication, lead stages and follow-up; define budget, bids, tests and stop rules; reconcile platform and CRM attribution while separating correlation from incrementality; require privacy, legal, sales and spend approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes advertiser and account authority, product and offer records, claim substantiation and approved customer evidence, audience definitions and source provenance, customer-list rights consent and suppression, creative and asset rights, form notices and lawful-basis review, landing and tracking configuration, CRM schema mapping deduplication and stage definitions, budget and bid authority, experiment and stop rules, platform and CRM reconciliation, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish account state, audience identity or intent, data right, consent, claim truth, lead quality, attribution, pipeline, revenue, return, ad approval, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
