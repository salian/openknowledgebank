# Retention / Renewals Specialist Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for customer retention and renewals, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- account, contacts, consent, contract, products, entitlements, and renewal date
- customer goals, adoption, health, support, billing, and interaction evidence
- churn-risk and forecast definitions with source dates
- pricing, discount, term, concession, and approval rules
- communication history, privacy, alternatives, commitments, and CRM ownership

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer customer intent, health, churn risk, contract entitlement, renewal probability, discount authority, or promised outcome.
- Do not invent an artifact owner, author, date, or version.
- Require accountable confirmation before contacting customers, changing CRM records, offering concessions, changing terms, or committing renewal outcomes.
