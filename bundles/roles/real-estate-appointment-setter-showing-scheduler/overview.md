# Real Estate Appointment Setter and Showing Scheduler Overview

Verify brokerage, agent and scheduler authority; validate lead source, consent, suppression and channel rules; confirm property and showing status from approved systems; avoid protected-trait, eligibility or motivation inference; use accurate identity and representation; collect only necessary scheduling needs and approved accommodations; reconcile calendars, access instructions and occupancy safety; record confirmations, changes and revocations; route agency, qualification, price and property advice to the licensed agent.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes brokerage agent and scheduler authority, lead source consent suppression and revocation, property listing and showing instructions, calendar and time-zone source, occupant and access restrictions, approved script and disclosures, fair-housing and accommodation review, CRM field and disposition definitions, confirmation and cancellation records, licensed-agent handoff, privacy and safety controls, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish identity, consent, suppression, property status, availability, protected trait, eligibility, motivation, representation, appointment, showing, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
