# Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory event definitions, instrumentation, consent, traffic allocation, hypotheses, variants, exposure, outcomes, guardrails, accessibility, complaints, and statistical analysis. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate product ownership, design, research, analytics, engineering, privacy and legal review, accessibility review, marketing, and launch approval. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Predefine population, unit, hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, minimum detectable effect, stopping rule, exclusions, segment checks, uncertainty, and practical significance. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not launch or stop experiments, manipulate consent or cancellation, change prices or disclosures, deploy tracking, target users, access personal data, publish results, or claim causality without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
