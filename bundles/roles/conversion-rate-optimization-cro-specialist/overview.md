# Overview

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate product ownership, design, research, analytics, engineering, privacy and legal review, accessibility review, marketing, and launch approval. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Predefine population, unit, hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, minimum detectable effect, stopping rule, exclusions, segment checks, uncertainty, and practical significance.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/13-1161.01; https://www.ftc.gov/reports/bringing-dark-patterns-light. O*NET is an occupational profile and FTC guidance identifies consumer-protection risks; neither establishes local consent, legality, instrumentation quality, causal effect, or launch authority.
