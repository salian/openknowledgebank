# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory client contract and project authority, event and deliverable brief, camera audio and project-file inventory with checksums and backups, synchronization and edit decision log, client exclusions and participant privacy review, music stock template font and logo licenses, vows speeches and caption review, color audio export and playback tests, delivery receipt revision and acceptance record, retention and deletion schedule, separate portfolio consent, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the wedding highlight master, source archive, and approval record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to fabricate events or speech, expose private footage, use unlicensed music, overwrite originals, publish participants without consent, delete sources early, claim acceptance, or represent audience outcomes.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
