# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory role and performance analysis, approved competencies and outcomes, current product security pricing and roadmap boundaries, claim substantiation and approved customer evidence, competitive source dates, lesson practice and manager-coaching design, assessment blueprint rubric calibration and pilot, employee and customer privacy controls, accessibility and localization tests, LMS and completion definitions, outcome measurement and confounders, update cadence, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the SaaS sales onboarding curriculum and assessment plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish unsupported product claims, expose customer data, score or certify staff without authority, change enablement systems, claim conformance or performance causality, or launch training.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
