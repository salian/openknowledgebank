# Design Researcher Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware design research analysis and a review-ready design research brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering research question and decision; participant population, recruitment criteria, consent, and incentives; current protocol, script, and method; recordings, notes, data handling, and retention; analysis method, coding, synthesis, and contrary evidence; accessibility, safeguarding, limitations, and stakeholder review.
- Unknowns: do not infer participant identity or consent, representativeness, finding prevalence, user intent, research saturation, or usability outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before recruiting or contacting participants, recording sessions, collecting sensitive data, or publishing findings.
