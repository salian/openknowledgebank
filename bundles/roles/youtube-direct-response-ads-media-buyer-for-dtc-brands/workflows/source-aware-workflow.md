# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory advertiser account and spend authority, product catalog formula tests and claim substantiation, creator testimonial and disclosure records, footage music likeness and asset rights, audience and customer-data provenance consent and suppression, inventory price offer shipping and return terms, landing checkout and tracking configuration, conversion and attribution definitions, budget bids experiments and stop rules, test outputs, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the DTC YouTube media and creative experiment plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, publish unsupported claims, use creators or assets without rights, upload audiences, install tracking, launch or scale ads, spend funds, or represent conversion, revenue, or return.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
