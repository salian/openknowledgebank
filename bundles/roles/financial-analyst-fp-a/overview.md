# Financial Analyst (FP&A) Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware financial planning and analysis analysis and a review-ready FP&A decision brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering entity, business units, reporting period, decision, and accounting basis; actuals, ledger extracts, reconciliations, close status, and source dates; budget, forecast, drivers, assumptions, scenarios, and model versions; KPI and variance definitions, allocations, currencies, tax, and intercompany treatment; risks, sensitivities, approvals, narrative, and disclosure context.
- Unknowns: do not infer actual result, forecast, variance cause, driver relationship, liquidity, accounting treatment, or management approval.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing budgets or forecasts, posting entries, approving spend, distributing sensitive results, or making public financial claims.
