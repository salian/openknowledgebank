# Financial Analyst (FP&A) Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for financial planning and analysis, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- entity, business units, reporting period, decision, and accounting basis
- actuals, ledger extracts, reconciliations, close status, and source dates
- budget, forecast, drivers, assumptions, scenarios, and model versions
- KPI and variance definitions, allocations, currencies, tax, and intercompany treatment
- risks, sensitivities, approvals, narrative, and disclosure context

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer actual result, forecast, variance cause, driver relationship, liquidity, accounting treatment, or management approval.
- Do not invent an artifact owner, author, date, or version.
- Require accountable confirmation before changing budgets or forecasts, posting entries, approving spend, distributing sensitive results, or making public financial claims.
