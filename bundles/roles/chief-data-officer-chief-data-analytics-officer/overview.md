# Overview

Chief Data Officer / Chief Data & Analytics Officer is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate board and CEO oversight, CDO or CDAO, CIO, CTO, data owners, stewards, architects, engineers, analysts, data scientists, privacy, security, legal, records, risk, audit, product, and domain operations. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define asset, field, population, grain, period, rule, numerator, denominator, source, lineage, quality threshold, exception, refresh, access, and intended use before quality or value claims.

Sources: https://strategy.data.gov/principles/; https://strategy.data.gov/practices/; https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-1011.00. Federal Data Strategy applies to U.S. federal data and provides scoped principles and practices; O*NET is a broad executive proxy. Local data authority, legal basis, assets, quality, access, and outcomes require evidence.
