# Chief Data Officer / Chief Data & Analytics Officer Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory business question, data domain, asset inventory, owner and steward, schema, lineage, provenance, quality, legal basis, consent, classification, access, retention, sharing agreement, analytics method, model, platform, cost, decision, approval, and outcome. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate board and CEO oversight, CDO or CDAO, CIO, CTO, data owners, stewards, architects, engineers, analysts, data scientists, privacy, security, legal, records, risk, audit, product, and domain operations. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define asset, field, population, grain, period, rule, numerator, denominator, source, lineage, quality threshold, exception, refresh, access, and intended use before quality or value claims. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not grant access, copy or share data, change retention, publish datasets, alter production schemas, approve analytics or models, procure platforms, commit budget, or claim quality, privacy, or compliance without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
