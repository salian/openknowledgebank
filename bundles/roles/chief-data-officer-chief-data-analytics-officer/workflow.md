# Chief Data Officer / Chief Data & Analytics Officer Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map board and CEO oversight, CDO or CDAO, CIO, CTO, data owners, stewards, architects, engineers, analysts, data scientists, privacy, security, legal, records, risk, audit, product, and domain operations; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify business question, data domain, asset inventory, owner and steward, schema, lineage, provenance, quality, legal basis, consent, classification, access, retention, sharing agreement, analytics method, model, platform, cost, decision, approval, and outcome.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before grant access, copy or share data, change retention, publish datasets, alter production schemas, approve analytics or models, procure platforms, commit budget, or claim quality, privacy, or compliance.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
