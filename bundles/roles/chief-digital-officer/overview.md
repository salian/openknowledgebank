# Overview

Chief Digital Officer is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate board and CEO oversight, chief digital officer, CIO, CTO, CDO, product and service owners, operations, design, engineering, data, security, privacy, finance, HR, procurement, change leadership, and assurance. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define service, user population, channel, process, baseline, benefit, cost, period, source, target, attribution, dependency, quality, and owner before transformation claims.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-1011.00; https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/government-functional-standard-govs-005-digital/government-functional-standard-govs-005-digital-html. O*NET is a broad executive proxy; GovS 005 applies to UK government organizations. Neither establishes another organization's digital mandate, portfolio, baseline, benefits, or authority.
