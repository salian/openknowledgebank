# Chief Digital Officer Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map board and CEO oversight, chief digital officer, CIO, CTO, CDO, product and service owners, operations, design, engineering, data, security, privacy, finance, HR, procurement, change leadership, and assurance; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify strategy, user need, service and process baseline, product portfolio, technology and data, accessibility, privacy, security, workforce, supplier, cost, benefit, dependency, risk, owner, approval, implementation, and outcome.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before change policy or operating processes, reprioritize products, deploy technology, access data, restructure teams, instruct suppliers, commit funding, publish services, or claim realized benefits.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
