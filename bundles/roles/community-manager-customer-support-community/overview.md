# Community Manager (Customer / Support Community) Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware online community operations analysis and a review-ready community operations brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering community purpose, platform, audience, and jurisdiction; current guidelines and moderation policy; user reports and conversation context; consent, privacy, and retention rules; escalation paths and safety criteria; content calendar and metric definitions.
- Unknowns: do not infer user intent, policy violation, identity, consent, moderation history, or community outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before posting, messaging users, deleting content, banning accounts, or exporting personal data.
