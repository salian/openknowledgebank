# Real Estate Wholesaling Acquisitions Cold Caller Overview

Verify caller, principal, jurisdictions, licensing and permitted business model; document list source, lawful purpose, do-not-call and internal suppression checks; avoid sensitive-trait, distress or motivation inference; use accurate identity, purpose and representation; distinguish interest from commitment; obtain and record channel-specific consent where required; protect property and personal data; define disclosures, recording rules, dispositions, escalation and licensed handoff; require legal and campaign approval before calls.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes principal and caller authority, jurisdictions and current telemarketing rules, real-estate and wholesaling licensing review, list source license date and fields, lawful-purpose analysis, federal state and internal suppression checks, number type and consent records, approved identity disclosures and script, call-time and recording rules, property sources, disposition definitions, complaint and revocation process, licensed escalation, retention controls, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish person identity, ownership, list provenance, lawful purpose, consent, suppression, distress, motivation, property fact, license, representation, offer, appointment, contract, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
