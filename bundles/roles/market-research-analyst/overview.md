# Market Research Analyst Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware market research analysis and a review-ready market research brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering decision, market definition, population, geography, period, and hypotheses; sampling frame, recruitment, consent, incentives, and fieldwork; questionnaire, interview guide, method, and version; data provenance, cleaning, weighting, coding, and analysis; uncertainty, limitations, contrary evidence, privacy, and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer market size, representativeness, respondent identity, statistical significance, preference, causality, or forecast.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before recruiting or contacting participants, buying data, collecting sensitive data, publishing findings, or claiming market size.
