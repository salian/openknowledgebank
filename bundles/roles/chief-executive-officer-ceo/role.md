# Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Role Guide

## Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## Build the evidence record

Inventory governance, strategy, financial, risk, workforce, customer, operational, and performance. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## Preserve role boundaries

Separate board governance, executive accountability, functional ownership, legal and risk challenge, finance control, operational execution, and independent review. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define measures with owners, formulas, periods, baselines, targets, data lineage, review cadence, and adverse-impact indicators; distinguish activity, output, outcome, and realized benefit. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## Stop before consequential action

Do not approve policy, commit capital, enter agreements, publish disclosures, restructure teams, change compensation, access restricted data, direct regulated activity, or claim outcomes without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
