# Overview

Chief Executive Officer (CEO) is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate board governance, executive accountability, functional ownership, legal and risk challenge, finance control, operational execution, and independent review. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define measures with owners, formulas, periods, baselines, targets, data lineage, review cadence, and adverse-impact indicators; distinguish activity, output, outcome, and realized benefit.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-1011.00. O*NET describes a broad occupation and does not establish a local charter, board delegation, fiduciary conclusion, or authority.
