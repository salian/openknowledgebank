# Overview

Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate sales ownership, marketing contribution, customer-success retention, finance recognition and control, legal approval, operations support, executive accountability, and independent review. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define funnel, bookings, recognized revenue, retention, margin, acquisition cost, cycle time, and forecast measures with formulas, cohorts, currencies, periods, owners, and reconciliation rules.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-2022.00. O*NET's Sales Managers profile is an occupational proxy and does not establish enterprise-wide revenue authority, local targets, contract terms, or forecast accuracy.
