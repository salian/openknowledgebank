# Paid Social / Social Media Advertising Specialist Overview

O*NET describes search marketing strategists as using research, data, and technology to understand user intent, conduct paid online initiatives, manage social campaigns, and measure outcomes for optimization.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
