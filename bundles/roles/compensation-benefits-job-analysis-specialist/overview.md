# Compensation, Benefits & Job Analysis Specialist Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware compensation, benefits, and job analysis analysis and a review-ready job and compensation analysis brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering job purpose, duties, scope, decision rights, and jurisdiction; current job descriptions, architecture, levels, and evaluation method; compensation surveys, effective dates, peer groups, and matching rationale; pay, benefits, protected-characteristic, privacy, and access evidence; internal and external equity analyses, classification rules, approvals, and appeal process.
- Unknowns: do not infer job equivalence, level, exemption or classification, market match, pay equity, discriminatory effect, or employee entitlement.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before reclassifying jobs, changing pay or benefits, accessing identifiable employee data, or making legal employment conclusions.
