# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory advertiser brokerage and account authority, current licenses jurisdictions and service areas, property and service records, market and claim substantiation, approved testimonials and rights, keyword negative location and audience plan, landing call and form assets, consent recording and CRM controls, conversion and attribution settings, budget and bid authority, experiment and stop rules, test outputs, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the real-estate Google Ads campaign and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, publish claims, target or exclude protected audiences, collect or contact leads, record calls, launch or scale campaigns, spend funds, or represent conversion, revenue, or return.

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
