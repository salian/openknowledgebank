# Real Estate Drone and Matterport 3D Tour Media Specialist Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/small-unmanned-aircraft-systems-uas-regulations-part-107
- https://matterport.com/legal
- https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/FHEO/documents/FHEO_Guidance_on_Advertising_through_Digital_Platforms.pdf

FAA, Matterport and HUD sources do not establish pilot eligibility, airspace authorization, site safety, access, property condition or dimensions, media rights, listing accuracy, platform output, or approval.

## Evidence Required

- Current official regulator, platform, standards-owner, education, healthcare, accessibility, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, exceptions, tests, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable property capture plan and source-linked media delivery package with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to fly without authority, enter property, capture people or private material without consent, alter structural facts deceptively, claim measurements, upload or transfer models, publish listings, or represent compliance.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent pilot status, registration, airspace authorization, weather or site safety, access, consent, property condition, measurement, right, listing fact, platform behavior, compliance, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
