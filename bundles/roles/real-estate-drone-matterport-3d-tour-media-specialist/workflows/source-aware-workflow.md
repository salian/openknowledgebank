# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory client property and publication authority, pilot certificate aircraft registration and recurrent training, airspace weather waiver and local-rule checks, access occupant privacy and hazard plan, capture checklist and originals, Matterport account model and transfer settings, measurement provenance, edit and staging log, media music and release rights, listing fact source, fair-housing review, delivery acceptance, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the property capture plan and source-linked media delivery package, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to fly without authority, enter property, capture people or private material without consent, alter structural facts deceptively, claim measurements, upload or transfer models, publish listings, or represent compliance.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
