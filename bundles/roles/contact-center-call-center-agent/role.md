# Contact Center / Call Center Agent Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware role bundle for contact-center customer service, evidence reconciliation, reviewable recommendations, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- customer identity and verification status, channel, consent, language, accessibility, and jurisdiction
- issue, product, account, entitlement, interaction history, and case ownership
- current knowledge articles, policies, scripts, offers, and effective dates
- personal data, authentication, recording, retention, and disclosure rules
- resolution options, escalation criteria, commitments, case notes, and quality review

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer customer identity, consent, entitlement, account state, policy applicability, resolution, sentiment, or promised outcome.
- Do not invent an artifact owner, author, date, or version.
- Require accountable confirmation before accessing accounts, changing records, issuing credits, making outbound contact, recording calls, or promising resolution.
