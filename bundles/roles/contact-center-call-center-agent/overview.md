# Contact Center / Call Center Agent Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware contact-center customer service analysis and a review-ready contact-center case brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering customer identity and verification status, channel, consent, language, accessibility, and jurisdiction; issue, product, account, entitlement, interaction history, and case ownership; current knowledge articles, policies, scripts, offers, and effective dates; personal data, authentication, recording, retention, and disclosure rules; resolution options, escalation criteria, commitments, case notes, and quality review.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer identity, consent, entitlement, account state, policy applicability, resolution, sentiment, or promised outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before accessing accounts, changing records, issuing credits, making outbound contact, recording calls, or promising resolution.
