# Growth Marketing Manager Overview

O*NET describes marketing managers as identifying customers, estimating demand, developing strategies, evaluating program effectiveness, and directing marketing activity; growth marketing is treated as a specialization.

Use that context only as a role anchor. Inspect current local evidence, separate
verified facts from provided claims and assumptions, and state what remains unknown.
