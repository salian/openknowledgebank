# Growth strategy and experiment decision brief

## Direct Answer

State the decision, recommendation, or draft purpose.

## Evidence Status

List `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.

## Evidence Table

Record source, scope, date or version, evidence status, owner, and limitation.

## Analysis

- Growth hypothesis and decision criteria.
- Funnel, channel, identity, and attribution evidence.
- Experiment design, guardrails, budget, and expected decision.
- Risks, approvals, rollout, and monitoring.

## Verification Plan

Name the source category, scope, date or version, and conflict checks.

## Confirmation Boundary

Name the accountable reviewer and actions requiring explicit approval.

## Source Note

Name sources used, assumptions, missing facts, and material limitations.
