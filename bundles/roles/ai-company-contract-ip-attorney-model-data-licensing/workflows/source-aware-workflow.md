# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory attorney license engagement conflicts and client authority, transaction parties and jurisdictions, asset inventory and versions, dataset model code and output provenance, chain of title licenses consents and restrictions, supplier and open-source terms, intended training evaluation deployment and output uses, personal confidential and regulated data flows, model documentation and tests, security and incident controls, regulatory analysis, insurance and risk allocation, redlines negotiation record, signatures, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the AI model and data licensing issue and redline memorandum, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to give advice outside licensure, assert ownership without evidence, access or transfer data or models, train or deploy systems, waive rights, accept terms, sign, close transactions, or promise enforceability or compliance.
