# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory clinic clinician and account authority, licenses supervision locations treatments devices and product records, current clinical regulatory and advertising review, claim substantiation, patient testimonial before-after and media consent, creative and music rights, audience exclusion and disclosure plan, lead form privacy notice consent and CRM controls, booking and clinical handoff, analytics budget experiment stop rules tests and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the med-spa Instagram campaign, lead, and measurement plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access accounts, publish health claims or patient media, infer sensitive traits, diagnose or recommend treatment, collect or contact leads, launch or scale ads, spend funds, or represent outcomes.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
