# GDPR and CCPA Data Processing Agreement Attorney Source-Aware Role Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa
- https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/

GDPR and California sources do not establish local applicability, controller or processor roles, data flows, lawful basis, transfer mechanism, security, compliance, enforceability, or transaction outcome.

## Evidence Required

- Current official platform, regulator, agency, tax, education, healthcare, accessibility, or professional sources with date, version, jurisdiction, and scope.
- Inspected client inputs, source records, permissions, configurations, calculations, constraints, alternatives, and outcomes.
- Named owners, substantiation, reproducible validation, qualified review where required, approval, and action authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable privacy DPA redline and processing schedule with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to give advice outside licensure, invent processing facts, claim compliance, select roles or transfer mechanisms without review, accept liability, sign, execute, or promise enforceability.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent applicability, party role, data category, purpose, lawful basis, instruction, transfer, subprocessor, security, rights response, compliance, enforceability, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
