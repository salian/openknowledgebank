# GDPR and CCPA Data Processing Agreement Attorney Overview

Verify attorney licensure, client, parties, jurisdictions and agreement set; separately analyze GDPR and California applicability and terminology; inspect actual products, data subjects, categories, purposes, roles, instructions, retention, subprocessors and transfers; map assistance for rights, impact assessments, incidents, audits, deletion and return; align security exhibits and liability with evidence; preserve conflicts, privilege, negotiation and signature authority; route nonlocal-law issues to qualified counsel.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes attorney license engagement conflicts and client authority, parties jurisdictions and master agreement, product and service versions, data-flow inventory and processing records, data subjects categories purposes and retention, controller processor business and service-provider analysis, subprocessor list and contracts, transfer locations and mechanisms, rights and incident workflows, security controls and reports, audit deletion and return capability, liability and insurance input, redlines decisions signatures, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish applicability, party role, data category, purpose, lawful basis, instruction, transfer, subprocessor, security, rights response, compliance, enforceability, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
