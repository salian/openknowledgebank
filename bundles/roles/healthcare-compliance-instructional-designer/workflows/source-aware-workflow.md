# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory organization audience and jurisdiction scope, current authoritative requirements and effective dates, local policies procedures and owners, role and risk analysis, incident audit and training-need evidence, source-to-objective map, scenario provenance and de-identification, clinical legal and compliance review, assessment blueprint rubric validation and remediation, accessibility tests and accommodations, LMS completion attestation and retention settings, change monitoring and version history, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the healthcare compliance curriculum and evidence map, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to give legal or clinical advice, expose patient data, declare policy or compliance without authority, certify staff, alter records, publish training, or claim conformance or risk reduction.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
