# Concierge and Guest Services Representative Overview

This bundle supports evidence-grounded work for Concierge and Guest Services Representative. It separates authoritative sources, prompt-supplied facts, local records, professional judgment, and actions requiring approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current authoritative sources, objective, scope, local records, constraints, decision criteria, conflicts, assumptions, approvals, validation evidence, and accountable ownership. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish the person's role, competence, credentials, authority, employer procedures, system state, records, decisions, outcomes, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
