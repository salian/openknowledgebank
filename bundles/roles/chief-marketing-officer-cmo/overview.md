# Overview

Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate board and CEO oversight, product and commercial ownership, marketing strategy, brand, product marketing, demand generation, sales, finance, privacy, legal review, and agency execution. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define spend, reach, response, lead, pipeline, revenue, incrementality, attribution, period, population, currency, source, exclusions, and uncertainty before comparison.

Sources: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-2021.00; https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/advertising-marketing-basics. O*NET is an occupational source; FTC guidance is scoped U.S. advertising guidance and does not establish campaign facts, substantiation, approvals, or applicability elsewhere.
