# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory account and integration authority, Shopify and Klaviyo versions and settings, profile identity and merge rules, event catalog and reconciliation, email and SMS consent source timestamp and disclosure, suppression objections and quiet-hour logic, segment and flow specifications, catalog inventory offer and claim records, sender and template settings, links coupons and rendering tests, holdout and measurement plan, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the Klaviyo email and SMS retention flow specification, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to access or merge profiles, change consent or suppression, import lists, activate flows, send email or SMS, alter offers, issue coupons, or represent delivery, retention, attribution, revenue, or compliance.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status
- Verified:
- Provided:
- Assumed:
- Needs verification:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
