# Brand / Digital Brand Manager Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware brand strategy and governance analysis and a review-ready brand decision brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering business objective and audience research; positioning and message architecture; current identity, voice, and channel standards; asset ownership and usage rights; product-claim substantiation and approvals; campaign plan, budget, and measurement definitions.
- Unknowns: do not infer audience preference, brand fit, asset rights, claim substantiation, approval, or campaign performance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before publishing claims or assets, changing brand standards, engaging vendors, or committing spend.
