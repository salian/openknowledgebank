# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory firm attorney and office authority, licenses jurisdictions and practice scope, current local ethics and advertising rules, approved credentials specialization awards and affiliations, claim and result substantiation, client testimonial case and logo consent, confidentiality and privilege review, service and intake process, domain profile and technical configuration, page and structured-data outputs, content media and trademark rights, analytics and intake definitions, change log, and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the law-firm SEO content and technical plan, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to publish legal claims or guarantees, expose client data, imply specialization without basis, create false reviews, change profiles or websites, collect intake data, or represent rankings, clients, or revenue.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Evidence Status

- Provided:

### Verification Plan
List exact sources, records, checks, conflicts, and reviewers.

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
