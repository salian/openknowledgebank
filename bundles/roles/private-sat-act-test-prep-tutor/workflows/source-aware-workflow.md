# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify current occupational and professional sources, jurisdiction, actual role, qualifications, and local procedures.
3. Inventory student parent and tutor authority, target exam date and current official format rules, diagnostic source conditions and results, student goals schedule and constraints, approved accommodation evidence, skill and content-domain plan, authorized practice-material provenance and licenses, session work attempts feedback and retest records, privacy and retention controls, academic-integrity boundaries, trend calculations limitations and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the student-owned test preparation plan and progress record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to diagnose disabilities, seek or use leaked items, impersonate students, register or change accommodations without authority, expose student data, take tests, fabricate progress, or guarantee scores admission or scholarships.

## Required Output

### Direct Answer
State what can and cannot be concluded.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary
Name the evidenced authorized reviewer and prohibited actions.

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
