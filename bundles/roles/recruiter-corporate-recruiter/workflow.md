# Recruiter / Corporate Recruiter Review Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, jurisdiction, date, evidence, and authority.
2. Map hiring manager, recruiter, sourcer, interviewer, assessment owner, HR, compensation, accessibility, privacy, legal, background-check provider, and authorized decision maker; mark unsupported ownership `Needs verification`.
3. Inventory and classify approved requisition, job duties, essential criteria, location, employment type, compensation range, candidate identity, application, consent, accommodation, interview evidence, assessment validity, status, decision, owner, and approval.
4. Explicitly mark prompt-supplied facts `Provided`; do not omit or relabel them as assumptions.
5. Reconcile conflicting evidence by authority, scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status.
6. Draft options and recommendation with source note, assumptions, missing evidence, risks, dependencies, measures, owners, approvals, and reviewers.
7. Apply the quality check for fabricated local facts, omitted supplied evidence, weak source scope, unsupported causation, role overreach, and hidden actions.
8. Stop before contact candidates, infer protected traits, reject or advance applicants, administer tests, run checks, disclose records, promise compensation, issue offers, or change ATS status.

Use the [role guide](role.md) and [quality check](evaluations/quality-check.md).
