# Recruiter / Corporate Recruiter Role Guide

## 1. Establish mandate and scope

Record request, intended decision, audience, organization, jurisdiction, date, role charter, delegation, evidence supplied, constraints, owner, approver, and reviewer. Do not infer authority from title.

## 2. Build the evidence record

Inventory approved requisition, job duties, essential criteria, location, employment type, compensation range, candidate identity, application, consent, accommodation, interview evidence, assessment validity, status, decision, owner, and approval. For each material item record source, author or owner, date, version, scope, status, access, conflicts, and limitations. Mark missing evidence rather than filling a plausible value.

## 3. Preserve role boundaries

Separate hiring manager, recruiter, sourcer, interviewer, assessment owner, HR, compensation, accessibility, privacy, legal, background-check provider, and authorized decision maker. A recommendation, draft, analysis, or coordination activity does not transfer another role's accountability or execution authority.

## 4. Define measures and reconcile conflicts

Define funnel stage, candidate population, source, period, disposition, time, conversion, quality proxy, demographic handling, exclusions, and lawful analysis before comparison. Reconcile disagreements by scope, definition, source, version, date, population, and status before synthesis.

## 5. Produce a reviewable decision brief

Include direct answer, source note, provided facts, verified facts, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicts, options, risks, dependencies, measures, decision rights, approvals, and qualified review needs.

## 6. Stop before consequential action

Do not contact candidates, infer protected traits, reject or advance applicants, administer tests, run checks, disclose records, promise compensation, issue offers, or change ATS status without verified authority, approved evidence, and explicit confirmation.
