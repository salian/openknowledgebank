# Overview

Recruiter / Corporate Recruiter is a role archetype whose mandate varies by organization, level, industry, jurisdiction, operating model, and local charter. Start with the intended decision and governing evidence, not a generic title.

## Role boundaries

Separate hiring manager, recruiter, sourcer, interviewer, assessment owner, HR, compensation, accessibility, privacy, legal, background-check provider, and authorized decision maker. Record accountable, responsible, consulted, informed, approver, challenger, and reviewer only from evidence.

## Evidence contract

Use `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, `Needs verification`, `Not applicable pending review`, or `Not assessed`. Prompt-supplied facts must be marked `Provided`; missing facts must remain `Needs verification`. Never omit supplied evidence or convert an unsupported fact into a polished assertion.

Define funnel stage, candidate population, source, period, disposition, time, conversion, quality proxy, demographic handling, exclusions, and lawful analysis before comparison.
