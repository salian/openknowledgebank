# Realistic Job Preview Source-Aware Framework Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1744-6570.1989.tb01553.x
- https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/other-assessment-methods/realistic-job-previews/

Framework sources describe generalized concepts and methods; they do not establish local applicability, inputs, classifications, calculations, thresholds, decisions, authority, or outcomes.

## Evidence Required

- Current primary, controlling, or originator source and the applicable edition, scope, definitions, and method.
- Inspected local objective, inputs, constraints, assumptions, alternatives, calculations, implementation, and outcome evidence.
- Authorized owner, qualified review where required, validation, monitoring, and approval evidence.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify framework definitions, applicability, assumptions, and current source context.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable job-preview evidence, fairness, and candidate-information brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before collect applicant or employee data, publish job claims, discourage or screen candidates, alter hiring, disclose confidential information, or represent fit, fairness, retention, or approval.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent job duty or condition, candidate identity or fit, employee experience, accessibility, fairness, self-selection effect, turnover, hiring outcome, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
