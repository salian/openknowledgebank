# Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware market segmentation and positioning strategy analysis and a reviewable stp strategy and evidence brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering market definition and research sources, segmentation variables, method, data, and recency, segment measurability, differentiation, accessibility, substance, and actionability, targeting criteria, resources, risks, and exclusions, positioning, value proposition, alternatives, and reasons to believe, and ethics, privacy, fairness, testing, and approval evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer segment validity, segment size, customer need, target attractiveness, competitive position, and message effect.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before launching targeting or campaigns, using sensitive traits, committing spend, making unsupported claims, or applying discriminatory exclusions.
