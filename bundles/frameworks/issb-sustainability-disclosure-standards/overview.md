# IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware sustainability-related financial disclosure analysis and a reviewable issb disclosure research brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering reporting entity, jurisdiction, period, and adoption basis, applicable IFRS S1, IFRS S2, amendments, reliefs, and effective dates, sustainability-related risks and opportunities, governance, strategy, risk-management, metrics, and targets evidence, financial effects, data definitions, controls, estimates, and boundaries, and review, assurance, and publication approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer applicability, effective requirement, material information, reporting boundary, metric accuracy, and assurance status.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before asserting compliance, publishing disclosures, making assurance or regulatory claims, or changing reported data without qualified review.
