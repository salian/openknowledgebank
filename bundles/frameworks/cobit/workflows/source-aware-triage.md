# COBIT Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for applicable COBIT edition and licensed publications, enterprise strategy and stakeholder needs, governance scope and design factors, enterprise and alignment goals, selected objectives and practices, current processes and evidence, roles and decision rights, risk and control mappings, metrics and capability method, assurance evidence, tailoring rationale, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that approve governance design, assign accountability, change objectives, controls, policies, risk acceptance, metrics, assurance conclusions, or claims of COBIT alignment or maturity.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
