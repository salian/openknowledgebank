# COBIT Source-Awareness Check

A passing response contains all five required sections, names a source URL, preserves prompt evidence, avoids invented provenance and reviewer ownership, and names these prohibited actions: approve governance design, assign accountability, change objectives, controls, policies, risk acceptance, metrics, assurance conclusions, or claims of COBIT alignment or maturity.
