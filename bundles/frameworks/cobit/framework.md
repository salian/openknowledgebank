# COBIT Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for enterprise information-and-technology governance, goals cascade, design factors, governance and management objectives, components, performance, and assurance review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.isaca.org/resources/cobit
- https://www.isaca.org/store2/product/CB19FGM

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- applicable COBIT edition and licensed publications, enterprise strategy and stakeholder needs, governance scope and design factors, enterprise and alignment goals, selected objectives and practices, current processes and evidence, roles and decision rights, risk and control mappings, metrics and capability method, assurance evidence, tailoring rationale, owners, and approvals

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer applicable objective, governance design, capability level, control effectiveness, assurance result, conformity, or legal compliance.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that approve governance design, assign accountability, change objectives, controls, policies, risk acceptance, metrics, assurance conclusions, or claims of COBIT alignment or maturity.
