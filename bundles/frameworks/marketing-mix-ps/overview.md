# Marketing Mix Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware marketing-mix strategy analysis and a reviewable marketing-mix decision brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering market, segment, customer need, and research scope, selected marketing-mix model and definitions, product or service evidence, price, costs, willingness-to-pay, and constraints, place, channel, availability, and partner evidence, and promotion, claims, creative, media, measurement, budget, and approvals.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer need, product fit, price response, channel availability, message effect, and commercial outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing product, pricing, distribution, campaigns, claims, customer targeting, or spend without accountable approval.
