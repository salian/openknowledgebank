# Design Thinking Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for problem and decision scope, participant and stakeholder groups, recruitment and consent, research plan and raw observations, synthesis trace, point of view, ideas and selection criteria, prototype fidelity, test protocol and results, accessibility, privacy, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that recruit or contact participants, record sessions, collect or share personal data, publish findings, change a product or roadmap, spend research budget, or claim validation.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
