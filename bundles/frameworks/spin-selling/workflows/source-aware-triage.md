# SPIN Selling Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for source version and local sales policy, account and contact context, buyer-provided situation, problems and consequences, need-payoff language, question plan, call notes or recording with consent, opportunity criteria, product evidence, pricing authority, owner, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that contact or record a prospect, store sensitive buyer data, change CRM or forecast state, advance or disqualify an opportunity, commit pricing or terms, or make unsupported product or outcome claims.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
