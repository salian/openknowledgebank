# BANT Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for source version and local qualification policy, account and contact scope, stated budget evidence, decision process and authority evidence, need and impact evidence, timing and trigger evidence, uncertainty, CRM fields, consent, disqualification policy, owner, and review history.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that qualify or disqualify a lead, change CRM or forecast state, contact a prospect, record sensitive buyer information, route ownership, commit pricing or timing, or make unsupported claims.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
