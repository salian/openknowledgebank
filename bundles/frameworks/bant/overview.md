# BANT Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current source material and inspected local evidence. It does not prove local state, authorization, outcomes, or compliance.

## Scope

- budget, authority, need, timing, qualification evidence, buyer context, fairness, privacy, and sales handoff review

## Key Limitation

- Do not infer budget, authority, need, timing, buyer intent, qualification, priority, forecast, consent, or owner.
