# COSO Enterprise Risk Management Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing risk scope, risk appetite, likelihood, impact, response effectiveness, and portfolio interaction
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before accepting risk, changing strategy or controls, making regulatory or assurance claims, or reporting risk conclusions without accountable governance
