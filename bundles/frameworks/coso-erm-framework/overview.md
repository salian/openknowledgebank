# COSO Enterprise Risk Management Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware enterprise risk management and strategy analysis and a reviewable coso erm risk and strategy brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering framework edition and official guidance, mission, strategy, and business objectives, risk appetite, tolerances, and governance, risk inventory, definitions, assessment method, and evidence, responses, owners, dependencies, and portfolio view, and performance, review, change, and reporting evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer risk scope, risk appetite, likelihood, impact, response effectiveness, and portfolio interaction.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before accepting risk, changing strategy or controls, making regulatory or assurance claims, or reporting risk conclusions without accountable governance.
