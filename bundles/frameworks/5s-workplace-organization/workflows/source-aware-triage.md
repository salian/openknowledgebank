# 5S Workplace Organization Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for work area and process scope, people and shifts, item inventory and use evidence, red-tag criteria and disposition authority, point-of-use needs, cleaning and inspection records, visual standards, standard work, safety and ergonomic review, baseline observations, audit method, owners, approvals, and sustainment evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that discard, move, label, or restrict items; change layout, storage, cleaning, inspection, visual controls, standard work, access, or safety procedures; stop operations.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
