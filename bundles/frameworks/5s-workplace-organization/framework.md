# 5S Workplace Organization Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for sort, straighten, shine, standardize, sustain, workplace flow, visual control, safety, ownership, and improvement review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/five-s/

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- work area and process scope, people and shifts, item inventory and use evidence, red-tag criteria and disposition authority, point-of-use needs, cleaning and inspection records, visual standards, standard work, safety and ergonomic review, baseline observations, audit method, owners, approvals, and sustainment evidence

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer item necessity, safe location, defect cause, standard suitability, audit status, employee agreement, safety, or improvement result.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that discard, move, label, or restrict items; change layout, storage, cleaning, inspection, visual controls, standard work, access, or safety procedures; stop operations.
