# Customer Success Methodology Overview

Source-aware guidance for customer outcomes, segmentation, success plans, health evidence, engagement, renewal risks, and governed action.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes customer contract and objectives, stakeholders, products, adoption and support records, outcome definitions, segments, lifecycle stage, success plan, health inputs, risk signals, communications, renewal dates, commercial terms, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Customer identity, contract terms, objectives, adoption, health validity, risk, stakeholder sentiment, outcome attainment, renewal likelihood, attribution, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
