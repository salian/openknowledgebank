# DevOps Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware software delivery and operational performance analysis and a reviewable devops delivery and reliability brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering product and value-stream scope, repositories, pipelines, environments, and deployment process, change, release, and rollback evidence, reliability, incident, recovery, and service telemetry, security, access, secrets, and supply-chain controls, and metric definitions, source systems, periods, and current DORA guidance.
- Unknowns: do not infer deployment state, pipeline result, metric definition, incident cause, reliability impact, and change authorization.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before deploying or rolling back production, changing pipelines or infrastructure, handling credentials, changing access, or declaring performance improvements without comparable evidence.
