# Double Diamond Overview

Source-aware guidance for divergent and convergent discovery, definition, development, delivery, evidence, and decision gates.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes challenge, affected people, research plan, consent, evidence, assumptions, insights, problem definitions, alternatives, prototypes, accessibility criteria, test methods, feedback, decision criteria, constraints, risks, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Problem existence, participant representativeness, consent, insight validity, problem framing, alternative coverage, prototype behavior, accessibility, test result, desirability, feasibility, viability, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
