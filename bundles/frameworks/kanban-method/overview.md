# Kanban Method Overview

Source-aware guidance for visualizing work, limiting WIP, managing flow, feedback loops, explicit policies, and evolutionary change.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes service and demand, work-item types, workflow states, entry and exit criteria, current WIP, capacity, classes of service, blockers, aging, lead and cycle times, throughput, policies, feedback cadence, risks, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Demand, capacity, workflow state, WIP, blocker cause, flow metrics, service-level expectation, policy effect, bottleneck, forecast, improvement outcome, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
