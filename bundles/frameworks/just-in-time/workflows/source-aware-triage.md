# Just-In-Time Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for customer demand and takt calculation, product mix, process and value-stream map, cycle and changeover times, work-in-process and buffers, quality and downtime, replenishment signals, supplier lead time and reliability, capacity, safety stock rationale, trial results, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that change inventory targets, reorder points, kanban, production or replenishment schedules, supplier orders, staffing, buffers, or customer commitments; stop or restart production.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
