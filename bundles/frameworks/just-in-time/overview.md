# Just-In-Time Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current source material and inspected local evidence. It does not prove local state, authorization, outcomes, or compliance.

## Scope

- takt time, continuous flow, pull, kanban, inventory, replenishment, quality, supplier, capacity, and resilience review

## Key Limitation

- Do not infer demand stability, takt, capacity, kanban quantity, inventory sufficiency, supplier reliability, quality, service risk, or improvement result.
