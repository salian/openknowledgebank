# Just-In-Time Source-Aware Application Framework

Source-aware framework bundle for takt time, continuous flow, pull, kanban, inventory, replenishment, quality, supplier, capacity, and resilience review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply the framework as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources

- https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/just-in-time-production/
- https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/pull-production/
- https://global.toyota/en/company/vision-and-philosophy/production-system/

Name the applicable source URL in every substantive Source Note. Verify its current version, date, product or method scope, and applicability. Where a source is secondary or proprietary material is unavailable, state that limitation rather than presenting the summary as canonical.

## Evidence Required

- customer demand and takt calculation, product mix, process and value-stream map, cycle and changeover times, work-in-process and buffers, quality and downtime, replenishment signals, supplier lead time and reliability, capacity, safety stock rationale, trial results, owners, and approvals

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory evidence and label it as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source behavior and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Preserve prompt facts under `Provided`; distinguish them from verified facts, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Do not infer demand stability, takt, capacity, kanban quantity, inventory sufficiency, supplier reliability, quality, service risk, or improvement result.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or an accountable reviewer.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that change inventory targets, reorder points, kanban, production or replenishment schedules, supplier orders, staffing, buffers, or customer commitments; stop or restart production.
