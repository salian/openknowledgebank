# Customer Success QBR Framework Overview

Source-aware guidance for evidence-backed quarterly business reviews, outcome tracking, risks, decisions, and follow-through.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes review period, contract and objectives, stakeholders, prior commitments, product adoption, support and service records, outcome measures, health inputs, risks, opportunities, roadmap evidence, decisions, actions, owners, dates, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Customer facts, contract terms, measure definitions, outcome attainment, attribution, health, risk, opportunity, roadmap status, commitment ownership, and decision authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
