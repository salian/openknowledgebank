# G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance Source-Aware Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/g20-oecd-principles-of-corporate-governance-2023_ed750b30-en.html
- https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2023/09/g20-oecd-principles-of-corporate-governance-2023_60836fcb.html

The sources establish general framework concepts only; verify the current edition, definitions, license, jurisdiction, and local applicability.

## Evidence Required

- edition and publication
- jurisdiction
- legal entity
- ownership and control
- securities and markets
- shareholder rights
- institutional investors
- disclosures
- audit
- board structure and duties
- stakeholder rules
- sustainability matters
- policies
- records
- gaps
- owners
- and approvals

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable Corporate-governance principles mapping brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before claim legal compliance, change governance documents, disclose confidential information, advise boards or investors, file with regulators, vote securities, or adopt policy.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent Edition, jurisdictional adoption, entity facts, ownership, rights, legal duties, disclosure accuracy, audit status, board independence or effectiveness, stakeholder treatment, sustainability claims, compliance, and authority.
- Do not infer access, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
