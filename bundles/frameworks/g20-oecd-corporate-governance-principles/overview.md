# G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance Overview

Source-aware guidance for mapping the 2023 Principles to jurisdiction, ownership, disclosure, boards, stakeholders, sustainability, and review evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes edition and publication, jurisdiction, legal entity, ownership and control, securities and markets, shareholder rights, institutional investors, disclosures, audit, board structure and duties, stakeholder rules, sustainability matters, policies, records, gaps, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Edition, jurisdictional adoption, entity facts, ownership, rights, legal duties, disclosure accuracy, audit status, board independence or effectiveness, stakeholder treatment, sustainability claims, compliance, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
