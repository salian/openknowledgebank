# Zero-Based Budgeting Overview

Source-aware guidance for decision packages, baseline reconstruction, cost-benefit review, ranking, allocation, and governed budget approval.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity, budget period, currency, accounting basis, strategic objectives, current commitments, contracts, legal minimums, cost centers, activities, volumes, decision packages, alternatives, benefits, dependencies, rankings, scenarios, controls, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Baseline, unavoidable commitments, cost values, benefit estimates, package dependencies, ranking criteria, scenario effects, accounting or tax treatment, affordability, and approval authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
