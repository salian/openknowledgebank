# Growth Loops Overview

Source-aware guidance for mapping loop inputs, actions, outputs, reinvestment, constraints, measurement, experiments, and governed scaling.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes product and market, actors, value event, loop steps, input and output units, reinvestment mechanism, conversion and cycle-time measures, cohorts, channels, costs, constraints, counter-metrics, experiment design, baseline, results, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Loop causality, event definitions, measurement integrity, cohort effects, incrementality, cycle time, saturation, cost, harms, experiment results, forecast, and growth outcome. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
