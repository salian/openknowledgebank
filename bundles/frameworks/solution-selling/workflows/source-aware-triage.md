# Solution Selling Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for licensed method source or local methodology, account and stakeholders, buyer-provided problem and impact evidence, current approach, desired outcomes, capability and limitation evidence, alternatives, value assumptions, decision criteria and process, mutual actions, pricing authority, CRM state, owner, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that contact or record a buyer, store sensitive information, change CRM or forecast state, recommend or commit a solution, price, discount, term, timeline, or make unsupported claims.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
