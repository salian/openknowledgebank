# Solution Selling Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for buyer problem, pain, impact, vision, capability fit, value, decision process, mutual plan, ethics, and opportunity review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.amazon.com/Solution-Selling-Creating-Difficult-Markets/dp/0786303158

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- licensed method source or local methodology, account and stakeholders, buyer-provided problem and impact evidence, current approach, desired outcomes, capability and limitation evidence, alternatives, value assumptions, decision criteria and process, mutual actions, pricing authority, CRM state, owner, and approvals

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer buyer pain, impact, vision, value, solution fit, authority, intent, opportunity stage, forecast, or commitment.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that contact or record a buyer, store sensitive information, change CRM or forecast state, recommend or commit a solution, price, discount, term, timeline, or make unsupported claims.
