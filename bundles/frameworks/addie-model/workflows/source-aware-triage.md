# ADDIE Model Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for source interpretation, business and learner needs, audience and context, task analysis, measurable objectives, instructional and assessment strategy, content sources and rights, prototype and review evidence, delivery environment, accessibility, pilot data, evaluation design, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that publish or deploy training, enroll or contact learners, collect learner data, change assessments, issue credentials, spend budget, or claim compliance, competence, or causal impact.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
