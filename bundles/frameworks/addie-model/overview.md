# ADDIE Model Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current source material and inspected local evidence. It does not prove local state, authorization, outcomes, or compliance.

## Scope

- analysis, design, development, implementation, evaluation, learner need, objective, assessment, accessibility, and training review

## Key Limitation

- Do not infer learner need, objective alignment, content accuracy, accessibility, assessment validity, transfer, training effect, compliance, or approval.
