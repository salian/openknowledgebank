# First Contact Resolution Overview

Source-aware guidance for defining, calculating, validating, interpreting, and improving first-contact resolution without gaming or unsupported benchmarks.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes service and channels, population, contact and issue identifiers, resolution definition, repeat-contact window, transfers, reopenings, exclusions, bot and human contacts, source systems, timestamps, customer confirmation, segmentation, calculation, uncertainty, counter-metrics, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Contact and issue linkage, resolution status, repeat window, exclusion validity, channel coverage, transfer handling, data completeness, score calculation, uncertainty, comparability, gaming, customer outcome, and causality. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
