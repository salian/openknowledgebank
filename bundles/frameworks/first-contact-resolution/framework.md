# First Contact Resolution Source-Aware Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.sqmgroup.com/resources/library/blog/fcr-metric-operating-philosophy
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/guidance/agent-business-value-metrics-reference

The sources establish general framework concepts only; verify the current edition, definitions, license, jurisdiction, and local applicability.

## Evidence Required

- service and channels
- population
- contact and issue identifiers
- resolution definition
- repeat-contact window
- transfers
- reopenings
- exclusions
- bot and human contacts
- source systems
- timestamps
- customer confirmation
- segmentation
- calculation
- uncertainty
- counter-metrics
- owners
- and approvals

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, scope, environment or organization, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify the current official source, edition, version, license, feature surface, jurisdiction, and applicability.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting definitions, records, dates, scopes, filters, transformations, settings, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable First-contact resolution measurement brief with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before access customer or employee data, close cases to improve scores, publish benchmarks, rank staff, change service policy, automate resolution, or make employment decisions.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent Contact and issue linkage, resolution status, repeat window, exclusion validity, channel coverage, transfer handling, data completeness, score calculation, uncertainty, comparability, gaming, customer outcome, and causality.
- Do not infer access, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
