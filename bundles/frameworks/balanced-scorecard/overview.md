# Balanced Scorecard Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware strategy execution and performance measurement analysis and a reviewable balanced scorecard design and review brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering strategy, scope, and framework interpretation, objectives and perspective assignment, causal hypotheses and dependencies, measure definitions, formulas, sources, baselines, and targets, initiatives, owners, resources, and timing, and review cadence, decisions, and observed results.
- Unknowns: do not infer strategic objective, causal relationship, metric definition, baseline, target feasibility, and initiative effect.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before committing targets or resources, publishing performance claims, changing strategy, or making personnel decisions without accountable review.
