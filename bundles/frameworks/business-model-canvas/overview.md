# Business Model Canvas Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware business-model design and validation analysis and a reviewable business model canvas evidence brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering business or initiative scope, customer segments and observed needs, value propositions and supporting evidence, channels and customer relationships, key activities, resources, and partners, and revenue streams, cost structure, assumptions, experiments, and owners.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer need, value proposition fit, channel performance, partner commitment, revenue viability, and cost viability.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before committing strategy, pricing, partnerships, resources, investment, or public business claims without accountable review.
