# Toyota Production System and Kaizen Overview

Source-aware guidance for jidoka, just-in-time, pull, flow, standard work, problem solving, and safe continuous improvement.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes process and purpose, customer demand, takt assumptions, workflow, standard work, cycle and wait times, WIP, defects, stops, equipment, people, ergonomics, hazards, quality checks, supply constraints, countermeasures, trials, measures, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Demand, takt, cycle time, WIP, defect cause, equipment condition, worker capability, hazard, quality effect, countermeasure result, flow improvement, sustainability, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
