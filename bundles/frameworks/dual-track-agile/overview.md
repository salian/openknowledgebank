# Dual-Track Agile Overview

Source-aware guidance for continuous discovery and delivery, assumption testing, backlog evidence, integration, and controlled product decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes product objective, users, outcomes, assumptions, discovery questions, research evidence, consent, opportunities, solution ideas, prototypes, feasibility evidence, risks, backlog items, acceptance criteria, delivery capacity, release evidence, measures, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish User need, representativeness, consent, assumption status, desirability, feasibility, viability, usability, backlog readiness, delivery result, outcome, causality, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
