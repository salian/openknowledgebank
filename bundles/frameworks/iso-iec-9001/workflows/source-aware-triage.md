# ISO 9001 Quality Management Systems Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for applicable ISO 9001 edition and amendment, licensed standard text, QMS scope and exclusions, interested parties, process map and criteria, risks and objectives, controlled documented information, competence, operational evidence, monitoring and audit records, nonconformities, management review, certification body, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that approve or alter the QMS, controlled documents, objectives, controls, audit findings, corrective actions, management-review conclusions, or claims of ISO conformity or certification.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
