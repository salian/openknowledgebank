# ISO 9001 Quality Management Systems Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for quality-management-system scope, context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement, and certification-readiness review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html
- https://www.iso.org/iso-9001-quality-management.html

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- applicable ISO 9001 edition and amendment, licensed standard text, QMS scope and exclusions, interested parties, process map and criteria, risks and objectives, controlled documented information, competence, operational evidence, monitoring and audit records, nonconformities, management review, certification body, owners, and approvals

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer applicable requirement, conformity, exclusion validity, control effectiveness, audit result, certification status, or legal compliance.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that approve or alter the QMS, controlled documents, objectives, controls, audit findings, corrective actions, management-review conclusions, or claims of ISO conformity or certification.
