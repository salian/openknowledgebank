# The Challenger Sale Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for licensed method or official source version, account and stakeholder context, buyer-provided evidence, commercial insight source and substantiation, relevance by stakeholder, alternative explanations, conversation plan, consent and call notes, solution capabilities, pricing authority, opportunity stage, owner, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that contact or pressure a buyer, record or store sensitive information, change CRM or forecast state, advance an opportunity, commit pricing or terms, or make unsupported commercial claims.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
