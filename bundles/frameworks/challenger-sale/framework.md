# The Challenger Sale Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for commercial insight, teach, tailor, take control, constructive tension, buyer evidence, ethics, and deal progression review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://challengerinc.com/what-is-challenger-sales-methodology/
- https://challengerinc.com/challenger-sales/

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- licensed method or official source version, account and stakeholder context, buyer-provided evidence, commercial insight source and substantiation, relevance by stakeholder, alternative explanations, conversation plan, consent and call notes, solution capabilities, pricing authority, opportunity stage, owner, and approvals

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer buyer belief, problem, receptivity, insight validity, urgency, solution fit, opportunity stage, forecast, consent, or commitment.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that contact or pressure a buyer, record or store sensitive information, change CRM or forecast state, advance an opportunity, commit pricing or terms, or make unsupported commercial claims.
