# IFRS Accounting Standards Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing applicable requirement, effective date, entity fact, accounting conclusion, materiality, and disclosure sufficiency
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before finalizing accounting conclusions, posting journal entries, issuing financial statements, or making audit, tax, legal, valuation, or investment claims
