# MEDDIC and MEDDPICC Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing metric validity, buying authority, decision criteria, decision process, pain, champion strength, and competition
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before contacting stakeholders, changing CRM fields or stage, committing forecasts, making customer claims, or advancing an opportunity without accountable approval
