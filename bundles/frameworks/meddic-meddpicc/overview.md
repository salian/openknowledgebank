# MEDDIC and MEDDPICC Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware complex B2B opportunity qualification analysis and a reviewable meddic or meddpicc opportunity brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering framework variant and organization definitions, opportunity stage and source dates, metrics and supporting business evidence, economic buyer, decision criteria, and decision process, pain and champion evidence, and paper process and competition when the selected variant includes them.
- Unknowns: do not infer metric validity, buying authority, decision criteria, decision process, pain, champion strength, and competition.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before contacting stakeholders, changing CRM fields or stage, committing forecasts, making customer claims, or advancing an opportunity without accountable approval.
