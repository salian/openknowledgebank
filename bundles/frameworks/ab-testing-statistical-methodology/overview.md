# A/B Testing Statistical Methodology Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware controlled experiment design and statistical decision-making analysis and a reviewable a/b test design and analysis brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering hypothesis and decision outcome, experimental unit, randomization, and allocation, population, eligibility, and exposure, power, sample-size, variance, and effect assumptions, alpha, multiplicity, and stopping rule, and instrumentation, exclusions, and analysis plan.
- Unknowns: do not infer randomization integrity, sample adequacy, metric validity, treatment exposure, statistical significance, and practical significance.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before launching or changing exposure, stopping an experiment, shipping a treatment, collecting personal data, or claiming causality without the planned analysis.
