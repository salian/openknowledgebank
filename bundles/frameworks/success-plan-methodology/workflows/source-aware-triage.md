# Joint Success Plan Methodology Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for customer-provided outcomes and priorities, baseline and metric definitions, target and date, milestone acceptance criteria, action owners and dependencies, product capability and limitations, adoption or usage source, risks and blockers, meeting notes and consent, plan version, review cadence, commercial terms, customer approval, and change history.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that contact customers, change success-plan or CRM state, assign commitments, share customer data, promise outcomes or product changes, alter commercial terms, trigger escalation, or claim value realization.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
