# Nine-Box Talent Grid Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for purpose and population, role and level comparability, performance period and criteria, potential definition and validated indicators, evidence sources and data quality, manager ratings, calibration participants and rationale, bias and adverse-impact checks, employee notice and access, privacy and retention, development options, appeal and reassessment process, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that place or label employees, change ratings or talent records, disclose sensitive assessments, make promotion, compensation, succession, development, performance, or termination decisions, or deny appeal.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
