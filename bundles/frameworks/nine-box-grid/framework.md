# Nine-Box Talent Grid Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for performance and potential definitions, evidence, calibration, bias, privacy, development, succession, appeal, and reassessment review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.shrm.org/mena/topics-tools/tools/toolkits/organizational-leadership-development
- https://www.gallup.com/workplace/647864/avoid-getting-boxed-conventional-succession-planning-methods.aspx

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- purpose and population, role and level comparability, performance period and criteria, potential definition and validated indicators, evidence sources and data quality, manager ratings, calibration participants and rationale, bias and adverse-impact checks, employee notice and access, privacy and retention, development options, appeal and reassessment process, owners, and approvals

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer employee performance, potential, readiness, aspiration, placement, fairness, succession suitability, or employment outcome.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that place or label employees, change ratings or talent records, disclose sensitive assessments, make promotion, compensation, succession, development, performance, or termination decisions, or deny appeal.
