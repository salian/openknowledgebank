# Net Promoter Score Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware customer loyalty measurement and follow-up analysis and a reviewable nps measurement and interpretation brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering survey wording, scale, channel, and timing, population, sample, response rate, and nonresponse evidence, scoring definition and method version, data cleaning, exclusions, weighting, and privacy controls, segment, benchmark, period, and uncertainty, and qualitative feedback and closed-loop follow-up evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer respondent representativeness, score calculation, segment comparability, benchmark validity, driver, and customer identity.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before contacting customers, exposing personal data, publishing scores or benchmarks, changing incentives, or claiming growth or causality without supporting analysis.
