# Net Promoter Score Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing respondent representativeness, score calculation, segment comparability, benchmark validity, driver, and customer identity
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before contacting customers, exposing personal data, publishing scores or benchmarks, changing incentives, or claiming growth or causality without supporting analysis
