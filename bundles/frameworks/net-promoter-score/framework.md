# Net Promoter Score Source-Aware Application Framework

Source-aware framework bundle for designing, calculating, interpreting, and acting on Net Promoter Score with explicit instrument, sample, scoring, segment, and uncertainty evidence.

Apply the framework as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- survey wording, scale, channel, and timing
- population, sample, response rate, and nonresponse evidence
- scoring definition and method version
- data cleaning, exclusions, weighting, and privacy controls
- segment, benchmark, period, and uncertainty
- qualitative feedback and closed-loop follow-up evidence

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Reconcile conflicting definitions, dates, versions, scopes, filters, owners, and calculation or processing rules.
- Do not infer respondent representativeness, score calculation, segment comparability, benchmark validity, driver, and customer identity.
- Require accountable confirmation before contacting customers, exposing personal data, publishing scores or benchmarks, changing incentives, or claiming growth or causality without supporting analysis.
