# SCOR Digital Standard Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for supply-chain process, performance, practice, people, hierarchy, metric, benchmarking, and transformation review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.ascm.org/corporate-solutions/standards-tools/scor-ds/

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- current licensed SCOR DS version, business and supply-chain scope, process hierarchy and mappings, metric dictionary and formulas, periods and units, benchmark cohort and rights, systems and source records, practices and people skills, constraints, baselines, targets, owners, approvals, and implementation evidence

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer SCOR mapping, metric definition, benchmark comparability, performance gap, practice maturity, causal improvement, or conformity.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that change process design, metric targets, supplier or customer commitments, inventory or capacity policy, staffing, transformation priorities, or claims of SCOR performance or conformity.
