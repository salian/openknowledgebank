# SCOR Digital Standard Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for current licensed SCOR DS version, business and supply-chain scope, process hierarchy and mappings, metric dictionary and formulas, periods and units, benchmark cohort and rights, systems and source records, practices and people skills, constraints, baselines, targets, owners, approvals, and implementation evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that change process design, metric targets, supplier or customer commitments, inventory or capacity policy, staffing, transformation priorities, or claims of SCOR performance or conformity.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
