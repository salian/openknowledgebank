# DORA Metrics Overview

Source-aware guidance for defining, collecting, validating, interpreting, and improving software-delivery performance metrics.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes services and teams, deployment definition, lead-time start and end events, failure definition, recovery definition, source systems, timestamps, filters, exclusions, incident links, aggregation, distributions, baselines, changes, survey context, counter-metrics, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Service and team scope, event definitions, timestamp integrity, deployment and failure classification, recovery linkage, aggregation, comparability, causality, performance, wellbeing, and improvement outcome. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
