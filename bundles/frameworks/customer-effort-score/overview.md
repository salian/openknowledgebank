# Customer Effort Score Overview

Source-aware guidance for defining, collecting, calculating, interpreting, and acting on customer-effort evidence.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes decision question, customer population, interaction type, survey wording, scale direction, channels, invitation logic, consent, response records, exclusions, dates, segments, calculation, uncertainty, benchmarks, follow-up evidence, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Question wording, scale direction, sample coverage, response integrity, exclusions, score calculation, uncertainty, segment differences, benchmark comparability, causality, and outcome. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
