# Work Sample Testing Overview

Start from current job analysis and representative tasks; define administration, scoring, assessor training, accessibility, security, validation, fairness, monitoring, and decision boundaries.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current job analysis, representative tasks, competency definitions, candidate instructions, accommodations, scoring anchors, assessor training, validation studies, adverse-impact monitoring, privacy controls, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish job requirements, candidate identity or competence, authorship, accommodation need, score validity, fairness, disparate impact, hiring outcome, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
