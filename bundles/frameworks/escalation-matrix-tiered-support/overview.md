# Escalation Matrix and Tiered Support Overview

Source-aware guidance for severity, impact, urgency, ownership, functional and hierarchical escalation, communications, and safe resolution.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes services, customers, support scope, issue categories, impact and urgency criteria, severity levels, tiers, capabilities, contacts, schedules, response and update targets, security and safety triggers, handoff requirements, communication templates, exceptions, tests, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Issue facts, impact, urgency, severity, customer or safety effect, ownership, responder availability, handoff quality, SLA state, escalation need, resolution, communication accuracy, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
