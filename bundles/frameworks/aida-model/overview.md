# AIDA Model Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current source material and inspected local evidence. It does not prove local state, authorization, outcomes, or compliance.

## Scope

- attention, interest, desire, action, audience, message, channel, evidence, measurement, and ethical marketing review

## Key Limitation

- Do not infer audience intent, stage, causal effect, claim validity, conversion attribution, performance, consent, or approval.
