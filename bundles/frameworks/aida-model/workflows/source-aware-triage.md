# AIDA Model Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for source interpretation and model variant, audience and need evidence, offer and substantiation, message and creative, channel and placement, stage definitions, conversion event, attribution window, baseline, experiment design, consent and policy constraints, approvals, and results.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that publish content, launch or change campaigns, spend budget, contact audiences, collect or export personal data, make unsupported claims, or alter live conversion tracking.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
