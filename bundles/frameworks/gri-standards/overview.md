# GRI Standards Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware impact-focused sustainability reporting analysis and a reviewable gri reporting and disclosure brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering standard edition, effective date, and reporting claim, organization, reporting period, entities, activities, and boundary, material-topic determination and stakeholder process, applicable Universal, Sector, and Topic Standards, disclosure requirements, data definitions, consolidation, and omissions, and review, assurance, GRI content index, and publication approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer applicable standard, reporting boundary, material topic, disclosure completeness, data quality, and assurance status.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before publishing sustainability claims, asserting compliance or assurance, omitting required information, or changing reported data without accountable review.
