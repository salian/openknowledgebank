# Lean Manufacturing Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware lean operational improvement analysis and a reviewable lean current-state and improvement brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering customer and value definition, process scope and current-state value stream, demand, takt, flow, pull, inventory, and queue evidence, observed waste and root causes, standard work, roles, people, and safety constraints, and experiment, metric, owner, and review cadence.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer value, process time, demand, waste cause, capacity, safety effect, and improvement outcome.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing production work, staffing, inventory, layouts, equipment, standard work, or safety controls without accountable approval.
