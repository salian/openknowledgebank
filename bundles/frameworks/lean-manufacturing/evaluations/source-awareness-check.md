# Lean Manufacturing Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing customer value, process time, demand, waste cause, capacity, safety effect, and improvement outcome
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before changing production work, staffing, inventory, layouts, equipment, standard work, or safety controls without accountable approval
