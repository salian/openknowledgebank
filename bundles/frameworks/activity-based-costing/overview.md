# Activity-Based Costing Overview

Source-aware guidance for resources, activities, cost pools, drivers, allocations, validation, and governed costing decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes entity, period, accounting basis, general-ledger totals, resources, activities, cost pools, driver definitions, driver quantities, capacities, objects, allocation rules, reconciliations, sensitivities, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Ledger values, activity definitions, causal driver relationships, quantities, capacity, allocation results, reconciliation, unit costs, profitability, accounting treatment, and decision outcome. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
