# Sales and Operations Planning Overview

Source-aware guidance for demand, supply, inventory, capacity, financial reconciliation, scenarios, and executive decisions.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes planning horizon, calendar, products and locations, demand history, forecast, orders, promotions, supply, capacity, inventory, lead times, constraints, costs, margins, budgets, scenarios, assumptions, risks, decisions, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Demand and supply values, forecast method, capacity, inventory, constraints, costs, margins, scenario assumptions, service effects, safety effects, financial reconciliation, and approval authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
