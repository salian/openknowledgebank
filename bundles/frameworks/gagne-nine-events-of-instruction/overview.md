# Gagne's Nine Events of Instruction Overview

Source-aware guidance for sequencing attention, objectives, recall, content, guidance, practice, feedback, assessment, and transfer.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes learners, context, prerequisites, learning objectives, content, accessibility needs, language, technology, attention strategy, prior-knowledge evidence, guidance, practice, feedback, assessment criteria, transfer context, timing, facilitator capability, pilot evidence, owners, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Learner needs, prerequisites, accessibility, objective alignment, content accuracy, sequence suitability, practice authenticity, feedback quality, assessment validity, transfer, learning outcome, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
