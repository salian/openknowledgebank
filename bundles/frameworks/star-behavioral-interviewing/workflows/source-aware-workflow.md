# Source-Aware Workflow

1. Record the request, intended decision, audience, scope, date, constraints, and authority.
2. Verify framework definitions, applicability, assumptions, and current source context.
3. Inventory framework and rights review, hiring and decision authority, current job analysis competencies and criteria, structured question probe and order design, anchored rubric and weighting rationale, accommodation and accessibility process, candidate consent and privacy controls, interviewer training and calibration, candidate response and source notes, independent ratings disagreements and adjudication, subgroup adverse-impact and validation review where appropriate, decision audit and approvals.
4. Preserve `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, and `Needs verification` separately.
5. Define included and excluded work, records, systems, permissions, dependencies, validation, and rollback or stop conditions.
6. Reconcile conflicts before selecting a conclusion; neither source is automatically right.
7. Draft the job-linked STAR interview guide, anchored scorecard, and calibration record, including alternatives, risks, dependencies, owners only when evidenced, review points, and stop conditions.
8. Require explicit confirmation before taking any action to copy proprietary systems without rights, invent candidate evidence, ask prohibited questions, deny accommodations, change scores to fit outcomes, automate final decisions, claim bias removal or predictive validity, hire reject or publish without authority.

### Verification Plan

### Confirmation Boundary

### Source Note
List authoritative sources used, local evidence used, and missing sources.
