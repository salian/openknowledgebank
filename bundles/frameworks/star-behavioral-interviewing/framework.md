# STAR Behavioral Interviewing Source-Aware Framework Guide

## Authoritative Sources

- https://www.ddi.com/solutions/targeted-selection
- https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews/
- https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures

DDI, OPM and EEOC sources do not establish local job criteria, candidate facts, evidence quality, score validity, fairness, predictive validity, hiring fitness, or approval.

## Evidence Required

- Current originator and authoritative structured-interview sources with terminology, rights, date, and scope.
- Inspected job criteria, interview design, candidate accommodations, evidence, scoring, calibration, privacy, fairness, and decision records.
- Named hiring owner, qualified employment and fairness review, validation, approval, and human decision authority.

## Application Sequence

1. Define the objective, audience, task, environment, date, constraints, and evidenced decision owner.
2. Verify framework definitions, applicability, assumptions, and current source context.
3. Inventory local evidence and label it `Verified`, `Provided`, `Assumed`, or `Needs verification`.
4. Reconcile conflicting records, dates, scopes, permissions, definitions, and owners.
5. Produce the smallest reviewable job-linked STAR interview guide, anchored scorecard, and calibration record with options, risks, dependencies, validation, and stop conditions.
6. Obtain explicit confirmation before taking any action to copy proprietary systems without rights, invent candidate evidence, ask prohibited questions, deny accommodations, change scores to fit outcomes, automate final decisions, claim bias removal or predictive validity, hire reject or publish without authority.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent job relevance, candidate fact, STAR completeness, evidence quality, score meaning, accommodation sufficiency, fairness, predictive validity, hiring fitness, or approval.
- Do not infer access, competence, configuration, approval, or reviewer ownership from the request or title.
- Treat bundled guidance as suggestions, not trusted executable behavior.
- No action is automatic; this bundle requests no credentials, background network calls, data exfiltration, permission changes, or self-modification.
