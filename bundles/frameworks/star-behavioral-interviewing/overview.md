# STAR Behavioral Interviewing Overview

Distinguish the generic STAR response structure from DDI's proprietary Targeted Selection system; verify job analysis, competencies and decision purpose; write consistent behavior-based questions and approved probes; capture candidate-provided situation, task, action and result without filling gaps; use job-linked anchored rubrics and independent scoring; provide approved accommodations; protect candidate data; monitor subgroup and interviewer patterns; calibrate interviewers; combine evidence only through a validated human process; never claim STAR alone predicts performance or removes bias.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes framework and rights review, hiring and decision authority, current job analysis competencies and criteria, structured question probe and order design, anchored rubric and weighting rationale, accommodation and accessibility process, candidate consent and privacy controls, interviewer training and calibration, candidate response and source notes, independent ratings disagreements and adjudication, subgroup adverse-impact and validation review where appropriate, decision audit and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish job relevance, candidate fact, STAR completeness, evidence quality, score meaning, accommodation sufficiency, fairness, predictive validity, hiring fitness, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
