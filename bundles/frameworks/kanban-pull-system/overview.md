# Kanban Pull System Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware pull-based workflow and replenishment analysis and a reviewable kanban pull-system design brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering customer demand and service expectations, workflow states, entry and exit policies, capacity, work-in-progress, queue, and lead-time evidence, replenishment point, quantity, signal, and ownership, blocked work, defects, dependencies, and exceptions, and experiment, safety, quality, and review evidence.
- Unknowns: do not infer demand, capacity, work state, replenishment need, flow constraint, and change effect.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing production flow, inventory, work limits, staffing, service commitments, or safety and quality controls without accountable approval.
