# PRINCE2 Source-Awareness Check

A passing response includes all five required sections, names an applicable source URL, preserves prompt facts under Provided, identifies exact missing evidence, avoids invented provenance or reviewer ownership, and names these prohibited actions: authorize initiation, project or stage work; approve exception or change; commit budget or resources; assign governance roles; or claim PRINCE2 conformity or certification.
