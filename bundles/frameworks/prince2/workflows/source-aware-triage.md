# PRINCE2 Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for PRINCE2 edition and licensed source, project mandate and business case, organization and role appointments, product descriptions, plans and stages, tolerances, risks, issues, quality controls, progress evidence, tailoring decisions, approvals, and lessons.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that authorize initiation, project or stage work; approve exception or change; commit budget or resources; assign governance roles; or claim PRINCE2 conformity or certification.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
