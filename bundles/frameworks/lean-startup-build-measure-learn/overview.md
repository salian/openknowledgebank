# Lean Startup Build-Measure-Learn Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware startup experimentation and validated learning analysis and a reviewable build-measure-learn experiment brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering vision, problem, customer, and riskiest assumption, hypothesis and falsifiable learning question, minimum test or product scope, population, exposure, instrumentation, and actionable metrics, baseline, success, failure, and stopping criteria, and results, confounders, learning, pivot or persevere recommendation, and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer customer problem, hypothesis validity, metric meaning, experiment effect, validated learning, and pivot need.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before launching experiments, exposing customers, collecting personal data, committing product direction, or claiming validation without adequate evidence.
