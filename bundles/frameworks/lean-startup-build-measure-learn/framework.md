# Lean Startup Build-Measure-Learn Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for Build-Measure-Learn experiments with explicit hypotheses, minimum test scope, actionable metrics, learning criteria, ethics, and pivot boundaries.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Evidence Required

- vision, problem, customer, and riskiest assumption
- hypothesis and falsifiable learning question
- minimum test or product scope
- population, exposure, instrumentation, and actionable metrics
- baseline, success, failure, and stopping criteria
- results, confounders, learning, pivot or persevere recommendation, and approval

## Application Sequence

1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and applicable source version.
2. Inventory the required evidence and label its status.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile conflicts in definitions, periods, scope, data, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails

- Verify source version and local evidence before naming state or result.
- Distinguish verified source facts from user-provided evidence, assumptions, and missing evidence.
- Reconcile conflicting definitions, dates, versions, scopes, filters, owners, and calculation or processing rules.
- Do not infer customer problem, hypothesis validity, metric meaning, experiment effect, validated learning, and pivot need.
- Require accountable confirmation before launching experiments, exposing customers, collecting personal data, committing product direction, or claiming validation without adequate evidence.
