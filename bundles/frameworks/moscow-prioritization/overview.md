# MoSCoW Prioritization Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware timeboxed requirement prioritization analysis and a reviewable moscow priority decision brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering decision scope, deadline, outcome, and constraints, agreed Must, Should, Could, and Won't definitions, requirements and acceptance criteria, dependencies, compliance, safety, and minimum viable outcome, effort, capacity, uncertainty, and contingency, and decision owner, rationale, change control, and review cadence.
- Unknowns: do not infer requirement necessity, effort, dependency, deadline feasibility, priority agreement, and tradeoff effect.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before committing scope, deadlines, resources, contractual obligations, or dropping safety, compliance, or critical requirements without accountable approval.
