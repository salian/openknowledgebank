# Economic Order Quantity Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware inventory order-quantity analysis analysis and a reviewable eoq assumptions and sensitivity brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering item, location, period, and decision scope, demand rate, source, variability, and seasonality, ordering or setup cost definition, holding-cost components, rate, unit value, and period, lead time, service level, replenishment, and stockout assumptions, and discounts, capacity, perishability, minimums, sensitivity, and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer demand, ordering cost, holding cost, lead time, model applicability, and recommended quantity.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before placing purchase or production orders, changing inventory policy, committing capacity or cash, or presenting a point estimate without assumptions and sensitivity.
