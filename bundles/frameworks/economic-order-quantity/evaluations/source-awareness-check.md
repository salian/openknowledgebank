# Economic Order Quantity Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing demand, ordering cost, holding cost, lead time, model applicability, and recommended quantity
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before placing purchase or production orders, changing inventory policy, committing capacity or cash, or presenting a point estimate without assumptions and sensitivity
