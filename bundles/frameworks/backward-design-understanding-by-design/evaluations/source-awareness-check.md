# Backward Design and Understanding by Design Source-Awareness Check

Check that responses:

- answer directly using the required visible sections
- name official or primary source categories and local evidence
- separate verified, provided, assumed, and missing information
- avoid inventing learner need, desired result, assessment validity, instructional alignment, accessibility requirement, and learning outcome
- define verification steps for conflicts and source-of-record checks
- require confirmation before publishing curriculum, making high-stakes assessment or credential decisions, or claiming learning effectiveness without valid evidence
