# Toyota Production System Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect the current source version and exact local evidence for customer demand and takt context, process map and standard work, cycle and lead times, work-in-process and buffers, defect and abnormality records, stop and escalation rules, kanban design, supplier capability, staffing, safety, trial results, owners, and approvals.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that stop or restart production, change standard work, staffing, inventory buffers, kanban, schedules, supplier commitments, quality controls, or safety procedures.
7. End with a Source Note naming source URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
