# Customer Journey Mapping Source-Aware Triage

1. State the decision and direct answer possible now.
2. Record Verified, Provided, Assumed, and Needs verification separately.
3. Inspect current sources and exact local evidence for decision scope and journey boundary, research participants and consent, actor or segment evidence, scenario and goal, phases and touchpoints, observed actions and quotes, channels and systems, pain points and emotions with provenance, service metrics, accessibility and inclusion evidence, opportunity criteria, owners, approvals, and research limitations.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, dates, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Record alternatives, stop conditions, and an independent cross-check.
6. Require explicit approval before actions that recruit or contact participants, record research, collect or share personal data, publish quotes or maps, change services or roadmaps, assign owners, or claim customer validation.
7. End with a Source Note naming URLs, user evidence, and missing sources.
