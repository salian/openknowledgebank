# Customer Journey Mapping Source-Aware Guide

Source-aware framework bundle for persona or actor, scenario, phases, actions, thoughts, emotions, channels, touchpoints, evidence, opportunities, and ownership review, evidence reconciliation, reviewable decisions, and controlled consequential actions.

Apply this guidance as a decision aid, not as proof of local facts, outcomes, compliance, professional judgment, or authorization.

## Authoritative and Identified Sources
- https://www.nngroup.com/articles/journey-mapping-101/

Name an applicable URL in every Source Note. Verify current version, date, scope, and applicability. Do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary methods; disclose when a source is secondary or a licensed primary text is still required.

## Evidence Required
- decision scope and journey boundary, research participants and consent, actor or segment evidence, scenario and goal, phases and touchpoints, observed actions and quotes, channels and systems, pain points and emotions with provenance, service metrics, accessibility and inclusion evidence, opportunity criteria, owners, approvals, and research limitations

## Application Sequence
1. Define the decision, scope, owner, date, and source version.
2. Inventory evidence as verified, provided, assumed, or needing verification.
3. Apply only source-supported concepts to inspected local evidence.
4. Reconcile definitions, identifiers, versions, periods, scope, permissions, processing, and ownership.
5. Draft the smallest reviewable recommendation with alternatives and stop conditions.
6. Obtain accountable confirmation before consequential action.

## Guardrails
- Do not infer customer identity, need, emotion, behavior, touchpoint experience, journey stage, pain cause, priority, or outcome.
- Do not invent artifact provenance, access, execution, approval, or reviewer ownership.
- Require accountable confirmation before actions that recruit or contact participants, record research, collect or share personal data, publish quotes or maps, change services or roadmaps, assign owners, or claim customer validation.
