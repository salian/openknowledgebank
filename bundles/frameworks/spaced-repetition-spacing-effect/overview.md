# Spacing Effect and Spaced Practice Overview

Translate research into testable schedules for verified objectives, learners, material, prior knowledge, desired retention interval, retrieval difficulty, feedback, accessibility, workload, privacy, and measured outcomes.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current source definitions and scope, local objective and context, inspected inputs, assumptions, alternatives, calculations, constraints, implementation, outcomes, validation, decision ownership, and approval evidence. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish learner memory or need, optimal interval, forgetting rate, item difficulty, accessibility need, mastery, retention, causal effect, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
