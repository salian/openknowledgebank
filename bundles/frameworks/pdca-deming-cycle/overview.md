# PDCA and PDSA Improvement Cycle Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware iterative process improvement and learning analysis and a reviewable pdca or pdsa experiment brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current authoritative sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering selected PDCA or PDSA interpretation and source, problem, aim, theory, prediction, and scope, baseline, measures, data plan, and operational definitions, small-scale test, owner, timing, and safeguards, observed results, variation, comparison, and learning, and adopt, adapt, abandon, standardize, or repeat decision and approval.
- Unknowns: do not infer problem cause, baseline, prediction, test result, learning, and wider-scale effect.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing production processes, standard work, staffing, controls, or safety conditions, or scaling a change without reviewed evidence.
