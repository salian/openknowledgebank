# LeSS (Large-Scale Scrum) Overview

Verify framework source and protected-material boundaries; define one product, customers, Product Owner and Product Backlog; inspect team skills, feature-team feasibility, dependencies, architecture, integration, events, coordination, management roles, sites, vendors, regulations, incentives, employment effects, measures and constraints; distinguish LeSS rules from local choices; design reversible experiments, training, safety and feedback; require people, product, engineering, delivery, finance, privacy and legal approval before reorganization.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes current LeSS source and licensed-use review, product and customer definition, Product Owner and backlog authority, team skills and feature-team evidence, dependency architecture integration and release data, event and coordination design, management role and policy impacts, site vendor and regulatory constraints, employee consultation and fairness review, baseline measures, experiment rollback and learning plan, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish product boundary, team capability, feature-team readiness, role assignment, employee impact, dependency, architecture readiness, delivery effect, productivity, quality, cost, compliance, or approval. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
