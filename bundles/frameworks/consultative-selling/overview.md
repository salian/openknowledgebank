# Consultative Selling Overview

Use this bundle to reason from current sources and inspected local evidence.

## Scope
- buyer discovery, business problem, value hypothesis, evidence, fit, recommendation, ethics, and sales progression review

## Key Limitation
- Do not infer buyer need, impact, intent, authority, solution fit, value, opportunity stage, consent, forecast, or commitment.
