# Six Sigma DMAIC Overview

Use this bundle to prepare source-aware structured process improvement analysis and a reviewable dmaic project and evidence brief.

## Operating Principle

Start with the decision and evidence, not a presumed answer. Use current official or primary sources for general concepts and user-provided or authorized artifacts for local facts. Keep missing evidence visible, reconcile conflicts, and stop before consequential action without accountable approval.

## Scope

- General context: use the sources listed in the bundle metadata.
- Local conclusions: require current artifacts covering problem, scope, customer requirements, and critical-to-quality measures, process map, baseline, and operational definitions, measurement-system and data-collection evidence, analysis and root-cause evidence, improvement options, pilot design, and results, and control plan, owner, cadence, and response limits.
- Unknowns: do not infer baseline performance, measurement reliability, root cause, pilot effect, process stability, and control ownership.
- Action boundary: require confirmation before changing production work, equipment, staffing, quality controls, or safety procedures, or declaring an improvement without monitored evidence.
