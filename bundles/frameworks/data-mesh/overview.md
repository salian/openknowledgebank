# Data Mesh Overview

Source-aware guidance for domain ownership, data products, self-service platforms, federated governance, and controlled adoption.

## Evidence Contract

Relevant evidence includes business domains, data products, producers, consumers, owners, source systems, schemas, semantics, SLAs, quality evidence, access policies, classifications, lineage, platform capabilities, interoperability standards, governance decisions, costs, risks, and approvals. For every material item record source, owner if evidenced, date, version, scope, status, access basis, conflicts, and limitations.

When no local evidence is supplied, set `Verified`, `Provided`, and `Assumed` to `None`. Put exact missing artifacts under `Needs verification`. A general disclaimer is not a substitute for requesting evidence.

## Boundary

Analysis and drafting do not establish Domain boundaries, ownership, data contents, product quality, semantic compatibility, effective access, lineage, policy enforcement, platform capability, cost, adoption outcome, and authority. Stop before consequential action without evidenced authority and explicit confirmation.
